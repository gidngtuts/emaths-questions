## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 12

**(a)** Factorise completely.

**(i)** $24a^{2}b+12ab^{2}-ab$

**(ii)** $mn-18-9m+2n$

**(b)** Expand and simplify $(-2x+3q)(x-2q)$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $24a^{2}b+12ab^{2}-ab=ab(24a+12b-1)$ (B1)

**(a)(ii)** $mn-18-9m+2n$

$=mn-9m+2n-18$

$=m(n-9)+2(n-9)$ (M1, grouping)

$=(m+2)(n-9)$ (A1)

**(b)** $(-2x+3q)(x-2q)$

$=-2x^{2}+4xq+3qx-6q^{2}$ (M1)

$=-2x^{2}+7xq-6q^{2}$ (A1)
