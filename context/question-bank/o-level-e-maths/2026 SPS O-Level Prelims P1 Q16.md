## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 12

The figure shows a region $ABCD$ bounded by two arcs, that share the same center $O$, and the straight edges $AD$ and $BC$.
$OA=2\,OD$, angle $DOB=1.2$ radians, and the perimeter of $ABCD$ is $44.8\text{ cm}$.
Find the area of the figure $ABCD$. [4]

![Question 16 region bounded by two arcs](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/SPS/images/SPS-EM-2026-4G3-P1-Final-pages/page-12.png)

## Answer Scheme

Let $OD=r$, so $OA=2r$.

$$
\text{Perimeter}=2r(1.2)+r(1.2)+r+r=5.6r
$$

$$
5.6r=44.8
$$

$$
r=8
$$

$$
\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}(1.2)(16^{2})-\frac{1}{2}(1.2)(8^{2})=153.6-38.4=115.2\text{ cm}^{2}
$$
