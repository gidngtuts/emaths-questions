## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [2]; total [3]
> **Page:** 6

$$
c = \frac{k^{2}+h^{2}}{h}
$$

**(a)** Find $c$ when $k = 2.81$ and $h = 3.67$. Give your answer correct to 2 significant figures. [1]

**(b)** Rearrange the formula to make $k$ the subject. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
c = \frac{(2.81)^{2}+(3.67)^{2}}{3.67} = 5.8215 = 5.8 \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(b)**

$$
c = \frac{k^{2}+h^{2}}{h}
$$

$$
hc = k^{2}+h^{2}
$$

$$
k^{2} = hc-h^{2} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
k = \pm \sqrt{hc-h^{2}} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
