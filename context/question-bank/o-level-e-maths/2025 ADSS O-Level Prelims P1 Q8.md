## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 5

Given that $8^{1-b} = 16^{b-3}$, find the value of $b$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
8^{1-b} = 16^{b-3}
$$

$$
2^{3(1-b)} = 2^{4(b-3)} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

By comparing, $3(1-b) = 4(b-3)$ [M1]

$$
3-3b = 4b-12
$$

$$
15 = 7b
$$

$$
b = \frac{15}{7} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
