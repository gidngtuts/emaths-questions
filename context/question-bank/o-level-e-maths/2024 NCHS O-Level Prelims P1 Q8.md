## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 7

Explain whether $x = 22$ and $y = 1$ is a possible solution of the equation $9x+2.5y = 3y-2x$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
9x+2.5y &= 3y-2x \\
11x &= 0.5y \\
22x &= y \\
\frac{x}{y} &= \frac{1}{22}
\end{aligned}
$$

Hence, $x = 22$ and $y = 1$ is not a solution, as $22(22)\neq 1$.

Or

LHS $= 9(22)+2.5(1) = 200.5$

RHS $= 3(1)-2(22) = -41$

Since LHS $\neq$ RHS, $x = 22$ and $y = 1$ is not a solution.
