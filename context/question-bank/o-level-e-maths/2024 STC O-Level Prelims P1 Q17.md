## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

The diagram shows three semi-circles with diameters $2r$, $3r$ and $4r$ respectively. Find the ratio of the unshaded region to that of the shaded region.

![Three semicircles of diameters 2r, 3r and 4r with a shaded band](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/STC/images/CHIJ-STC-2024-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-13.png)

## Answer Scheme

Area of shaded region:

$$
\frac{\pi(1.5r)^{2}}{2}-\frac{\pi(r)^{2}}{2}=\frac{5}{8}\pi r^{2}
$$

Area of unshaded region:

$$
\frac{\pi(2r)^{2}}{2}-\frac{5}{8}\pi r^{2}=\frac{11}{8}\pi r^{2}
$$

$$
\text{Area of unshaded region}:\text{Area of shaded region}=\frac{11}{8}\pi r^{2}:\frac{5}{8}\pi r^{2}=11:5
$$
