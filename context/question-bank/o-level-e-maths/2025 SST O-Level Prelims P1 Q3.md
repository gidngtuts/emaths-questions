## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 5

**(a)** Given that $2^{m-1}\times 3^{m} = 1$, find the value of $36^{m}$. [2]

**(b)** Simplify $\dfrac{(2xy)^{-2}}{12}\div\dfrac{x^{3}}{4y^{2}}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Given that $2^{m-1}\times 3^{m}=1$, find the value of $36^{m}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^{m-1}\times 3^{m}&=1\\
2^{m}\times 3^{m}&=2\\
6^{m}&=2\\
36^{m}&=2^{2}\\
36^{m}&=4
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b)** Simplify $\dfrac{(2xy)^{-2}}{12}\div\dfrac{x^{3}}{4y^{2}}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{(2xy)^{-2}}{12}\div\dfrac{x^{3}}{4y^{2}}&=\dfrac{1}{48x^{2}y^{2}}\times\dfrac{4y^{2}}{x^{3}}\\
&=\dfrac{1}{12x^{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$
