## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 13

$x$ and $y$ are related such that $x$ is inversely proportional to the square of $y$.
If $y$ is increased by 400%, find the percentage change in $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$y$ becomes $5y$, so $x$ becomes $\dfrac{k}{25y^{2}}$.

$$
\text{Percentage change} = \frac{\dfrac{k}{y^{2}}-\dfrac{k}{25y^{2}}}{\dfrac{k}{y^{2}}}\times 100\% = 96\%
$$
