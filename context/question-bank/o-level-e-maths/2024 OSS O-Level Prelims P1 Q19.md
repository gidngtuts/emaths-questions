## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 16

$y$ is proportional to the square root of $x$.
If the value of $x$ is increased by 300%, the value of $y$ will be increased by $r\%$.
Find the value of $r$.

## Answer Scheme

$y=k\sqrt{x}$. Increasing $x$ by 300% gives $4x$.

$y=k\sqrt{4x}=2k\sqrt{x}$, so $y$ is doubled, an increase of 100%.

$r=100$
