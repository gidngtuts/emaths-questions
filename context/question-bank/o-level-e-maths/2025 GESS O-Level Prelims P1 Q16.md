## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 14

**(a)** Simplify $\dfrac{x^{2}+xy}{x^{2}+2xy+y^{2}}$. [2]

**(b)** Factorise completely $10yz-5y^{2}-2xz+xy$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{x^{2}+xy}{x^{2}+2xy+y^{2}}=\frac{x(x+y)}{(x+y)^{2}}=\frac{x}{x+y}
$$

**(b)**

$$
10yz-5y^{2}-2xz+xy=5y(2z-y)-x(2z-y)=(5y-x)(2z-y)
$$
