## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Simplify $\dfrac{a^{2}-3a+2}{9a^{2}-1}\div\dfrac{2a-2}{6a-2}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{(a-2)(a-1)}{(3a-1)(3a+1)}\times\frac{2(3a-1)}{2(a-1)}=\frac{a-2}{3a+1}
$$
