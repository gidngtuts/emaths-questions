## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 5

$$
a=\frac{5-b^{2}}{5c}
$$

**(a)** Find $a$ when $b=4.23$ and $c=-204$.
Give your answer correct to 2 significant figures. [2]

**(b)** Rearrange the formula to make $b$ the subject. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
a=\frac{5-(4.23)^{2}}{5(-204)}=0.013\text{ (2 s.f.)}
$$

[M1 correct substitution o.e. without rounding to 2 s.f., A1]

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
5ca&=5-b^{2}\\
b^{2}&=5-5ca\\
b&=\pm\sqrt{5-5ca}
\end{aligned}
$$

[M1 simplify to one line, can be implied; M1 make $b^{2}$ the subject, can be implied; A1 must have $\pm$]
