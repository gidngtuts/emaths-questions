## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 22

The diagram shows the map of a park in the shape of a quadrilateral $ABCD$. The scale of the map is $1\text{ cm}$ to $10\text{ m}$.

![Map of quadrilateral park ABCD, scale 1 cm to 10 m](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E-Math-Prelim-Paper-1-FINAL-pages/page-22.png)

(a) Construct the bisector of angle $ABC$. [1]

(b) Construct the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. [1]

(c) Shade the region inside $ABCD$ that is closer to $AB$ than to $BC$ and closer to $B$ than to $A$. [1]

(d) A tree $T$ is located inside the park $ABCD$ such that it is $30\text{ m}$ from $D$ and equidistant from $A$ and $B$. Mark and label the exact position of $T$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) B1 for the angle bisector constructed accurately with construction arcs.

(b) B1 for the perpendicular bisector of $AB$ constructed accurately, with construction arcs on both sides of $AB$.

(c) B1 for the correct region shaded.

(d) B1 for the correct position of $T$.
