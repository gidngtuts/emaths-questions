## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 9

Simplify $\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{2}{9m}\div\sqrt[4]{81m^{16}}$, giving your answer as a single fraction.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4}{3}+\frac{2}{9m}\div\sqrt[4]{81m^{16}} &= \frac{4}{3}+\frac{2}{9m}\div 3m^{4} \\
&= \frac{4}{3}+\frac{2}{9m}\times\frac{1}{3m^{4}} \\
&= \frac{4}{3}+\frac{2}{27m^{5}} \\
&= \frac{36m^{5}+2}{27m^{5}}
\end{aligned}
$$
