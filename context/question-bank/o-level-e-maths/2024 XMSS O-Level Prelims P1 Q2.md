## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

(a) Express $540$ as a product of its prime factors. [1]

(b) The number $\dfrac{540x}{y}$ is a perfect square, where $x$ and $y$ are prime numbers and $x > y$. Find the value of $x$ and of $y$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $540 = 2^{2}\times 3^{3}\times 5$ [B1]

(b) $x = 5$, $y = 3$ [B1]
