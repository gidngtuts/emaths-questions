## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 8–9

The position vector of point $C$ is $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. The position vector of point $D$ is $\begin{pmatrix}4\\h\end{pmatrix}$. $|\overrightarrow{CD}|=\sqrt{20}$.

Find the two possible values of $h$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

The position vector of point $C$ is $\begin{pmatrix}2\\-3\end{pmatrix}$. The position vector of point $D$ is $\begin{pmatrix}4\\h\end{pmatrix}$. $|\overrightarrow{CD}|=\sqrt{20}$.

Find the two possible values of $h$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{CD}&=\overrightarrow{CO}+\overrightarrow{OD}\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\3\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}4\\h\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\begin{pmatrix}2\\3+h\end{pmatrix}\\
\sqrt{2^{2}+(3+h)^{2}}&=\sqrt{20}\\
\sqrt{4+9+6h+h^{2}}&=\sqrt{20}\\
h^{2}+6h+13&=20\\
h^{2}+6h-7&=0\\
(h+7)(h-1)&=0\\
h&=-7\text{ or }1
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** for $\overrightarrow{CD}$. **M1** to form quadratic equation. **A1**
