## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

(a) Find the area of a regular hexagon with each side $7\text{ cm}$. [2]

(b) Find the interior angle of a regular 15-sided polygon. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\text{Area of hexagon} = 6\times \frac{1}{2}\times 7\times 7\times \sin 60^{\circ} = 127.3057344\text{ cm}^{2} = 127\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

M1, A1.

(b) $\dfrac{(15-2)\times 180}{15} = 156^{\circ}$ — B1.
