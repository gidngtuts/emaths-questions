## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

A six-faced die was thrown $28$ times. The table shows the number of times that each possible score occurred.

| Score | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency | 8 | 6 | 6 | 2 | 4 | 2 |

The die was thrown $2$ more times.
Amanda calculated the mean score of all the $30$ times mentally and gave the answer as $3.1$.
Is Amanda’s calculation correct? State your reasons clearly. [3]
