## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 4

(a) Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{18b^{7}}{5c^{2}} \div \dfrac{3b^{4}}{81}$. [2]

(b) Use the laws of indices to show that $6^{4}\times 100+116\times 36^{2}$ can be expressed as a single power of six. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\frac{18b^{7}}{5c^{2}}\div \frac{3b^{4}}{81} = \frac{18b^{7}}{5c^{2}}\times \frac{81}{3b^{4}} = \frac{486b^{3}}{5c^{2}}
$$

M1 for $\dfrac{486}{5}$. A1 for the full answer.

(b)

$$
6^{4}\times 100+116\times 36^{2} = 6^{4}\times 100+116\times 6^{4} = 6^{4}(100+116) = 6^{4}\times 216 = 6^{7}
$$

M1, A1 (shown).
