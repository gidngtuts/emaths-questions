## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 14

$X$, $Y$ and $Z$ lie on a circle with centre $O$ and radius $4$ cm. Angle $XOY = 2.5$ radians.

![Circle centre O with points X, Y and Z](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/WGSS/images/WGSS_2024_Prelim_EMath_4052-1-_QP-pages/page-14.png)

(a) Find the area of minor sector $XOY$. [1]

(b) (i) Write down, in term of $\pi$, for reflex angle $XOY$. [1]

(ii) Find the length of the arc $XZY$, in term of $\pi$. [1]

(iii) The major sector $XZYO$ is used to make a cone. Calculate the base radius of the cone. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Area of sector $= \dfrac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}(4)^{2}\times 2.5 = 20\text{ cm}^{2}$ [B1]

(b) (i) $2\pi-2.5$ [B1]

(ii) $4(2\pi-2.5) = (8\pi-10)$ [B1]

(iii) $4(2\pi-2.5) = 2\pi r$ [M1]

$r = \dfrac{4(2\pi-2.5)}{2\pi} = 2.41$ [A1]
