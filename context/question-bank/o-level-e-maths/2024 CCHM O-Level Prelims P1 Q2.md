## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Given that $0 < x < 180$, find the possible values of $x$ for $\sin x^{\circ} = 0.985$.
Give your answers correct to $1$ decimal place.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\sin x^{\circ} = 0.985
$$

$$
x = \sin^{-1} 0.985 \text{ or } 180-\sin^{-1} 0.985 = 80.1 \text{ or } 99.9
$$
