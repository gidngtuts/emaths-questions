## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 3, (d) 2; total 10
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Solve the inequality $-\dfrac{2x+1}{3}\leq\dfrac{3x-1}{-5}$. [2]

**(b)** Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $-\dfrac{x}{1-3x}+\dfrac{7}{(3x-1)^{2}}$. [3]

**(c)** Solve the simultaneous equations.

$$
5x=49-4y
$$

$$
4x-5y=-10
$$

[3]

**(d)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{h^{15}}{64f^{12}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
-\frac{2x+1}{3}\leq\frac{3x-1}{-5}
$$

$$
-\frac{2x+1}{3}\leq-\frac{3x-1}{5}
$$

$$
5(2x+1)\geq 3(3x-1)
$$

$$
10x+5\geq 9x-3
$$

$$
x\geq -8
$$

**(b)**

$$
-\frac{x}{1-3x}+\frac{7}{(3x-1)^{2}}=\frac{x}{3x-1}+\frac{7}{(3x-1)^{2}}
$$

$$
=\frac{x(3x-1)}{(3x-1)^{2}}+\frac{7}{(3x-1)^{2}}=\frac{x(3x-1)+7}{(3x-1)^{2}}=\frac{3x^{2}-x+7}{(3x-1)^{2}}
$$

Alternative method:

$$
-\frac{x}{1-3x}+\frac{7}{(3x-1)^{2}}=-\frac{x}{1-3x}+\frac{7}{(1-3x)^{2}}
$$

$$
=\frac{7}{(1-3x)^{2}}-\frac{x(1-3x)}{(1-3x)^{2}}=\frac{7-x+3x^{2}}{(3x-1)^{2}}=\frac{3x^{2}-x+7}{(3x-1)^{2}}
$$

**(c)**

$$
5x=49-4y \quad (1)
$$

$$
4x-5y=-10 \quad (2)
$$

From (1): $5x+4y=49$ (3)

(3) $\times 4$: $20x+16y=196$ (4)

(2) $\times 5$: $20x-25y=-50$ (5)

(4) $-$ (5):

$$
16y-(-25y)=196-(-50)
$$

$$
41y=246
$$

$$
y=6
$$

Sub $y=6$ into (1):

$$
5x=49-4(6)=25
$$

$$
x=5
$$

Ans $x=5$, $y=6$

**(d)**

$$
\left(\frac{h^{15}}{64f^{12}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}=\left(\frac{64f^{12}}{h^{15}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}=\frac{16f^{8}}{h^{10}}
$$
