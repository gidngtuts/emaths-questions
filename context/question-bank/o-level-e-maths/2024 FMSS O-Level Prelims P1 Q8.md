## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 6

The force, $F$, between two particles is inversely proportional to the square of the distance, $d$, between them.
Calculate the percentage change in force when the distance between the two particles is increased by $350\%$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
F = \frac{k}{d^{2}}, \quad k = Fd^{2}.
$$

New distance $= 4.5d$.

$$
\text{new } F = \frac{k}{(4.5d)^{2}} = \frac{Fd^{2}}{20.25d^{2}} = \frac{4}{81}F.
$$

$$
\text{percentage change} = \left(\frac{4}{81}-1\right)\times 100 = -\frac{7700}{81}\% \text{ or } -95\frac{5}{81}\% \text{ or } -95.1\% \text{ (3 s.f.)}.
$$

M1 for $\dfrac{1}{20.25}$ or $\dfrac{4}{81}$. A1.
