## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 8

Factorise completely.

(a) $abc-3c-6+2ab$ [2]

(b) $80x^{4}-5$ [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $abc-3c-6+2ab = abc-3c+2ab-6$

$= c(ab-3)+2(ab-3)$ [M1]

$= (ab-3)(c+2)$ [A1]

(b) $80x^{4}-5 = 5(16x^{4}-1)$ [M1]

$= 5(4x^{2}+1)(4x^{2}-1)$ [M1]

$= 5(4x^{2}+1)(2x+1)(2x-1)$ [A1]
