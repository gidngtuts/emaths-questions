## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** Solve the equation $x^{2}-4x-11 = 0$ by completing the square. Give your answer correct to $2$ decimal places. [3]

**(b)(i)** Factorise completely $4x^{2}+5x-6$. [1]

**(ii)** Hence, factorise completely $4(2t+2)^{2}+5(2t+2)-6$. Write your answer as simply as possible. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Solve the equation $x^{2}-4x-11=0$ by completing the square. Give your answer correct to 2 decimal places.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}-4x-11&=0\\
(x^{2}-4x+2^{2}-2^{2})-11&=0\\
(x-2)^{2}-4-11&=0\\
(x-2)^{2}&=15\\
x-2&=\pm\sqrt{15}\\
x&=2\pm\sqrt{15}\\
x&=5.87\ (2\text{ d.p.})\text{ or }x=-1.87\ (2\text{ d.p.})
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b) (i)** Factorise completely $4x^{2}+5x-6$.

$$
4x^{2}+5x-6=(4x-3)(x+2)
$$

**(ii)** Hence, factorise completely $4(2t+2)^{2}+5(2t+2)-6$. Write your answer as simply as possible.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(2t+2)^{2}+5(2t+2)-6&=[4(2t+2)-3][(2t+2)+2]\\
&=(8t+5)(2t+4)\\
&=2(8t+5)(t+2)
\end{aligned}
$$
