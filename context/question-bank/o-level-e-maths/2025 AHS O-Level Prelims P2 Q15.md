## Answer Scheme

The diagram shows a container with diameter $20$ cm and height $40$ cm. It contains water to a height of $24$ cm from the vertex of the container. A layer of oil of height $4$ cm floats on the water surface.

**(a)** Calculate the diameter of the water in the container.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\text{Diameter of water}}{\text{Diameter of container}}&=\dfrac{\text{Height of water}}{\text{Height of container}}\\
\text{Diameter of water}&=\dfrac{20\times 24}{40}\\
&=12\ \mathrm{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

**B1**

**(b)** The curved surface area of the container is $150\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}$.

Calculate the curved surface of the container in contact with the oil.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{\text{Curved surface area of container}}{\text{Curved surface area of water}}&=\left(\dfrac{10}{6}\right)^{2}\\
\text{Curved surface area of water}&=\dfrac{150\times 36}{100}\\
&=54\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}\\
\dfrac{\text{Curved surface area of container}}{\text{Curved surface area of water and oil}}&=\left(\dfrac{10}{7}\right)^{2}\\
\text{Curved surface area of water and oil}&=\dfrac{150\times 49}{100}\\
&=73.5\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}\\
\text{Hence, curved surface area of the container in contact with the oil}&=73.5\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}-54\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}\\
&=19.5\ \mathrm{cm}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** for finding the curved surface areas of water. **M1** for finding curved surface area of water and oil. **A1**
