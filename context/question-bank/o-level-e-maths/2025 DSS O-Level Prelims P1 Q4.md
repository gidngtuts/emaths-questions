## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

300 ml of liquid is poured into bottles A, B and C in the ratio $2:8:5$.
$n$ ml of liquid is spilled from bottle A.
The ratio of the amounts of liquid in A, B and C becomes $7:40:25$.
Find the value of $n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Amount of liquid in bottles B and C $=300\div 15\times 13=260$ ml

Amount of liquid in bottle A $=40$ ml

Amount of liquid left in bottle A $=260\div 65\times 7=28$ ml

$$
n=40-28=12
$$

Or

$2:8:5$ is equivalent to $10:40:25$

75 units represent 300 ml

1 unit represents 4 ml

$$
n=4\times(10-7)=12
$$
