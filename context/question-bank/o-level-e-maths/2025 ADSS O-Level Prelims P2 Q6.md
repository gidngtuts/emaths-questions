## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) [5]; (a)(ii) [3]; (b)(i) [2]; (b)(ii) [2]; total [12]
> **Page:** 11–12

**(a)** The diagram below is a container made up of a cylinder of diameter 24 cm and height 35 cm and a cone of height $h$ cm. The total volume of the container is $18\,850\text{ cm}^{3}$.

![Question 6 cylinder and cone](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P2-QP-pages/page-11.png)

**(i)** Calculate the total surface area of the container. [5]

**(ii)** Water is poured into a container from the top at a rate of $250\text{ cm}^{3}/\text{s}$. The tap is turned off when the water reaches 30 cm from the vertex of a cone. Find the time taken for the pouring of water. [3]

**(b)**

![Question 6 parallelogram ACDE](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P2-QP-pages/page-12.png)

Given that $ACDE$ is a parallelogram and $AB : BC = 1 : 4$.

**(i)** Ming claims that the ratio of the area of triangle $EBA$ to the area of triangle $ECA$ is $16 : 25$. Do you agree? Justify your answer with reference to given information. [2]

**(ii)** Given that area of triangle $EBA$ is $3\text{ cm}^{2}$, find the area of parallelogram $ACDE$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** Volume of cylinder $= \pi(12)^{2}(35) = 15833.62697\text{ cm}^{3}$ [B1]

Volume of cone $= \dfrac{1}{3}\pi(12)^{2}h = 150.7964474h$

$15833.62697+150.7964474h = 18850$, so $h = 20$ cm [B1]

Surface area of cylinder $= \pi(12)^{2}+2\pi(12)(35) = 3091.327\text{ cm}^{2}$ [M1]

Surface area of cone $= \pi(12)\sqrt{12^{2}+20^{2}} = 879.2868305\text{ cm}^{2}$ [B1]

Total surface area $= 3091.327+879.2868 = 3970\text{ cm}^{2}$ (3 sig fig) [A1]

**(ii)**

$$
(18850-15833.62697)+\pi(12)^{2}(30-20) = 7540.266451\text{ cm}^{3} \quad \text{[M1][B1]}
$$

Time taken $= \dfrac{7540.266451}{250} = 30.161 = 30.2$ s [A1]

**(b)(i)** I disagree that the area of triangle $EBA$ to the area of triangle $ECA$ is $16 : 25$.

$$
\frac{\text{area of }\triangle EBA}{\text{area of }\triangle ECA} = \frac{0.5 \times BA \times h}{0.5 \times CA \times h} = \frac{1}{5} \quad \text{[M1][A1]}
$$

**(ii)** Area of $\triangle ECA = 15\text{ cm}^{2}$ [B1]

Area of parallelogram $ACDE = 15 \times 2 = 30\text{ cm}^{2}$ [A1]
