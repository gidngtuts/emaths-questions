## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 3, (d) 3, (e) 2, (f) 2; total 12
> **Page:** 16–17

StreamGuru is a legal online media services provider that allows users to download music albums over the Internet.

It offers subscribers two choices of StreamGuru Plans, Standard and Elite.

**(a)** Under the Standard Plan, subscribers can download $x$ albums in one hour.

Write an expression, in terms of $x$, for the time taken, in minutes, to download one album under the Standard Plan. [1]

**(b)** Under the Elite Plan, subscribers can download 5 more albums in one hour compared to the Standard Plan.

Write an expression, in terms of $x$, for the time taken, in minutes, to download one album under the Elite Plan. [1]

**(c)** A StreamGuru subscriber can download an album under the Elite Plan 2 minutes faster than under the Standard Plan.

Write an equation in $x$ to represent this information and show that it reduces to

$$
x^{2}+5x-150=0.
$$

[3]

**(d)** Solve the equation $x^{2}+5x-150=0$. [3]

**(e)** How many minutes does it take for a Elite Plan subscriber to download 2 music albums? [2]

**(f)** Find the percentage of time saved by downloading one music album under the Elite Plan compared to under the Standard Plan. [2]
