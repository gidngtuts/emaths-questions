## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Simplify $\dfrac{2x^{2}+7x-15}{16x^{4}-81}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2x^{2}+7x-15}{16x^{4}-81}=\frac{(2x-3)(x+5)}{(4x^{2})^{2}-9^{2}}
$$

$$
=\frac{(2x-3)(x+5)}{(4x^{2}-9)(4x^{2}+9)}=\frac{(2x-3)(x+5)}{(2x-3)(2x+3)(4x^{2}+9)}
$$

$$
=\frac{x+5}{(2x+3)(4x^{2}+9)}
$$
