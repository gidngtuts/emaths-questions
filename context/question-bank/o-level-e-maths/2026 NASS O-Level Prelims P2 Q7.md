## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 2, (a)(ii) 3, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 2, (b)(iii) 3; total 11
> **Page:** 16–17

A café offers the following menu.

| Main Dish | Price | Drink | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken Burger | &#36;5.80 | Apple Juice | &#36;2.00 |
| Vegan Burger | &#36;4.80 | Orange Juice | &#36;2.00 |

| Soup | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Chicken Soup | &#36;2.20 |
| Mushroom Soup | &#36;2.80 |
| Onion Soup | &#36;3.00 |

| Set Meal | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Chicken Set Meal (Chicken Burger + Chicken Soup + a drink) | &#36;8.80 |
| Vegan Set Meal (Vegan Burger + Mushroom Soup + a drink) | &#36;8.50 |

**(a)** A customer orders the vegan set meal.

**(i)** Find the percentage saving when the customer orders the set meal instead of ordering the items separately. [2]

**(ii)** The customer receives an $x\%$ discount as a member of the café's loyalty programme.
The total bill, inclusive of a 10% service charge and 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST), is &#36;9.70.

Find $x$.
Give your answer to the nearest whole number. [3]

**(b)** Every month, the café owner reviews the cost and selling prices of the set meals.

The owner uses a $1\times 2$ matrix $D$ to represent the new cost prices of the two set meals.
$D=PC$, where

$$
P=\begin{pmatrix}1.2 & 1.4 & 1.3\end{pmatrix}
\quad\text{and}\quad
C=\begin{pmatrix}
2.20 & 1.80 \\
0.85 & 1.10 \\
0.40 & 0.40
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The columns of $C$ are Chicken and Vegan. The rows of $C$ are Main Dish, Soup and Drinks.

$C$ represents the original cost prices of the main dish, soup and drink for each set meal.

**(i)** State what the elements of $P$ represent. [1]

**(ii)** Evaluate the matrix $D$. [2]

**(iii)** The owner revises the selling price if the percentage profit from selling a set meal is less than 90%.

Determine whether the owner should revise the selling price of the Chicken Set Meal.
Justify your answer with working. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Cost without set meal} &= 4.80+2.80+2.00 \\
&= &#36;9.60 \\
\text{Percentage savings} &= \frac{9.60-8.50}{9.60}\times 100\% \\
&= 11.5\%\text{ (3 s.f.)}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Markers Report:** A very common mistake is to calculate the savings based on the set meal cost instead.

**(a) (ii)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(1-\frac{x}{100}\right)(1.1)(1.09)(8.5) &= 9.7 \\
1-\frac{x}{100} &= \frac{9.7}{10.1915} \\
\frac{x}{100} &= 1-\frac{9.7}{10.1915} \\
x &= 4.8226 \\
x &= 5\text{ (nearest whole number)}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Markers Report:** Majority of the students were not aware of how the service charge and GST is calculated. Most assumed that it is 119% of the cost of the set meal.

**(b) (i)** The elements of $P$ suggest that the cost of main dish, soup and drinks have increased by 20%, 40% and 30% respectively.

> **Markers Report:** Most students gave the answers based on the product of the two matrices and were not able to focus on the matrix $P$ which represents the percentage increase.

**(b) (ii)**

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1.2 & 1.4 & 1.3\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
2.20 & 1.80 \\
0.85 & 1.10 \\
0.40 & 0.40
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}4.35 & 4.22\end{pmatrix}
$$

> **Markers Report:** Generally okay, with some giving the wrong order as $(2\times 1)$.

**(b) (iii)** Percentage profit

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \frac{8.8-4.35}{4.35}\times 100\% \\
&= 102\%\text{ (3 s.f.)}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $102\%>90\%$, the owner should not revise the selling price of the Chicken Set Meal.

> **Markers Report:** A significant number of students either misread/misunderstood the question and did not use 8.80 in their calculation. Hence, ended up calculated wrongly. A common mistake is students failed to give the comparison ($102\%>90\%$) and just mentioned that the owner should not revise the selling price.
