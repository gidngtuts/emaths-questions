## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 3, (c) 5; total 11
> **Page:** 4–5

**(a)** Solve the inequality $2x-1<\dfrac{5+9x}{2}$. [2]

**(b)** It is given that $b=\dfrac{2(n-5p^{2})}{n-p^{2}}$.

**(i)** Find $b$ when $n=1$ and $p=-2$. [1]

**(ii)** Express $p$ in terms of $b$ and $n$. [3]

**(c)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{3x}{2x-1}-\dfrac{2}{5-x}=7$. [5]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
2x-1<\frac{5+9x}{2}
$$

$$
4x-2<5+9x
$$

$$
-7<5x
$$

$$
x>-\frac{7}{5}\text{ or }x>-1.4
$$

**(b) (i)**

$$
b=\frac{2}{3}
$$

**(ii)**

$$
b(n-p^{2})=2(n-5p^{2})
$$

$$
bn-bp^{2}=2n-10p^{2}
$$

$$
10p^{2}-bp^{2}=2n-bn
$$

$$
p^{2}(10-b)=2n-bn
$$

$$
p^{2}=\frac{2n-bn}{10-b}
$$

$$
p=\pm\sqrt{\frac{2n-bn}{10-b}}
$$

**(c)**

$$
\frac{3x}{2x-1}-\frac{2}{5-x}=7
$$

$$
\frac{3x(5-x)-2(2x-1)}{(2x-1)(5-x)}=7
$$

$$
15x-3x^{2}-4x+2=7(10x-2x^{2}-5+x)
$$

$$
-3x^{2}+11x+2-77x+14x^{2}+35=0
$$

$$
11x^{2}-66x+37=0
$$

$$
x=\frac{-(-66)\pm\sqrt{(-66)^{2}-4(11)(37)}}{2(11)}=\frac{66\pm\sqrt{2728}}{22}
$$

$$
x=5.37\text{ (3 s.f.) or }0.626\text{ (3 s.f.)}
$$
