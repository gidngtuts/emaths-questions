## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 16

Two teams played 16 basketball matches.
Their scores are shown in the stem-and-leaf diagram.

| Team A | Stem | Team B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 0 1 1 | 3 | 6 6 |
| 0 1 2 6 8 8 | 4 | 2 3 3 7 |
| 2 3 3 4 5 | 5 | 0 2 4 6 7 |
|  | 6 | 1 6 8 9 |
| 9 | 7 | 3 |

Key: $0 \mid 3 \mid 6$ means a score of 30 by Team A and a score of 36 by Team B.

(a) Find the median score of Team A. [1]

(b) Find the interquartile range of the scores of Team B. [1]

(c) Use your answers to part (a) and (b) to make two comments comparing the scores of the two teams. [2]

(d) Explain why the mean may not be an appropriate average to use to summarise the scores of Team A. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $47$. B1

(b) $20.5$. B1

(c) 1. On average Team B scored more points than Team A as their median score was 53 which was higher than the median score of 47 achieved by Team A. B1

2. The interquartile range of Team B was higher than that of Team A which was 17.5. Hence the scores for Team B was more widely spread out. B1

(d) There is an outlier in the distribution scores of Team A which is significantly greater than the rest of the scores. B1
