## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [3]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 15

The table represents the weekly salary of workers in a company.

| Weekly salary | ＄150 | ＄200 | ＄300 | ＄400 | ＄500 | ＄2400 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Number of workers | 12 | 10 | 8 | $x$ | 5 | 1 |

**(a)** Given that the weekly mean salary of the workers is ＄325, calculate the total number of workers in the company.

**(b)** Explain which measures of central tendency is a better gauge of workers’ salary.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
325 &= \frac{12(150)+10(200)+8(300)+x(400)+5(500)+2400}{12+10+8+x+5+1} \\
325 &= \frac{11100+400x}{36+x} \\
11700+325x &= 11100+400x \\
600 &= 75x \\
x &= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

Total number of workers $= 12+10+8+8+5+1 = 44$.

**(b)** Median is a better gauge of workers’ salary as there is a salary that is an extreme value of ＄2400 compared to the rest.
