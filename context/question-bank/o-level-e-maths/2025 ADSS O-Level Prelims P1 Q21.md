## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [3]; total [4]
> **Page:** 11

![Question 21 towns A, B and C](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P1-QP-pages/page-11.png)

The diagram shows three towns, $A$, $B$ and $C$. $C$ is 110 km due east of $A$. Angle $ABC = 90^{\circ}$ and $AB = 70$ km.

**(a)** Calculate the distance $BC$. [1]

**(b)** Calculate the bearing of $B$ from $A$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** By Pythagoras’ Thm, $AC^{2} = BC^{2}+AB^{2}$

$BC^{2} = 110^{2}-70^{2} = 7200$

$BC = 84.8528 = 84.9$ km [A1]

**(b)**

$$
\text{Angle } BAC = \cos^{-1}\left(\frac{70}{110}\right) = 50.479^{\circ} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

The bearing of $B$ from $A = 90^{\circ}-$ Angle $BAC = 039.5^{\circ}$ [M1][A1]
