## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 5

Simplify $\dfrac{a}{(2a-1)^{2}}-\dfrac{2a}{1-2a}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{a}{(2a-1)^{2}}-\frac{2a}{1-2a}
$$

$$
= \frac{a}{(2a-1)^{2}}+\frac{2a}{2a-1} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{a+2a(2a-1)}{(2a-1)^{2}}
$$

$$
= \frac{a+4a^{2}-2a}{(2a-1)^{2}} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{4a^{2}-a}{(2a-1)^{2}} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
