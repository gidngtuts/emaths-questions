## Question

> **Marks:** 7
> **Page:** 20–21

There are 12 boys and 13 girls in a class.
Two of the children are selected at random to participate in a forum.

(a) Draw a tree diagram to show the probabilities of the possible outcomes.

(b) Find, as a fraction in its simplest form, the probability that

(i) two boys are selected,

(ii) one boy and one girl are selected.

(c) As a standby, a third student is selected.
Find the probability that out of the three students, there will be at least one boy and one girl selected.

## Answer Scheme

(a) Tree diagram. First child: boy $\dfrac{12}{25}$, girl $\dfrac{13}{25}$.

Second child, after a boy: boy $\dfrac{11}{24}$, girl $\dfrac{13}{24}$.

Second child, after a girl: boy $\dfrac{12}{24}$, girl $\dfrac{12}{24}$.

(b) (i)

$$
\frac{12}{25}\times\frac{11}{24} = \frac{11}{50}
$$

(ii)

$$
\frac{12}{25}\times\frac{13}{24}\times 2 = \frac{13}{25}
$$

(c)

$$
1-\frac{12}{25}\times\frac{11}{24}\times\frac{10}{23}-\frac{13}{25}\times\frac{12}{24}\times\frac{11}{23}
$$

or

$$
P(BBG)+P(BGB)+P(GBB)+P(GGB)+P(GBG)+P(BGG)
$$

$$
= \frac{12}{25}\times\frac{11}{24}\times\frac{13}{23}\times 3+\frac{13}{25}\times\frac{12}{24}\times\frac{12}{23}\times 3 = 0.78 \text{ or } \frac{39}{50}
$$
