## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 4; total 7
> **Page:** 20–21

The diagram shows a cylindrical solid with a hemispherical top with the same radius $r$ cm.
The height of the cylinder is $2r$ cm and the volume of the entire solid is $576\pi\text{ cm}^{3}$.

![Question 7 solid](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/MGS/images/MGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-20.png)

**(a)** Show that $r=6$. [3]

**(b)** If the entire solid is melted to form a solid cone such that its radius and height are the same, find the total surface area of the cone. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Show that $r=6$.

$$
\frac{2}{3}\pi r^{3}+\pi r^{2}(2r)=576\pi
$$

M1

$$
\frac{8}{3}r^{3}=576
$$

$$
r^{3}=216
$$

M1

$$
r=6
$$

A1

**(b)** If the entire solid is melted to form a solid cone such that its radius and height are the same, find the total surface area of the cone.

$$
\frac{1}{3}\pi r_{c}(r_{c}^{2})=576\pi
$$

M1

$$
\frac{1}{3}r_{c}^{3}=576
$$

$$
r_{c}^{3}=1728
$$

$$
r_{c}=12
$$

M1

$$
\pi r_{c}l+\pi r_{c}^{2}=\pi(12)\sqrt{12^{2}+12^{2}}+\pi(12)^{2}=1090\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

M1, A1
