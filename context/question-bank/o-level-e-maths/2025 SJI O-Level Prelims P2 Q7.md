## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 4; total 9
> **Page:** 16–17

![Question 7 field ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/SJI/images/SJI-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-16.png)

The diagram shows a field $ABCD$ on horizontal ground crossed by a path $BD$.
$D$ is due east of $A$.
$AD=200$ m, $BC=48$ m and angle $ABD=95^{\circ}$.
The bearing of $B$ from $A$ is $045.8^{\circ}$.

**(a)** Calculate $BD$. [2]

**(b)** The area of triangle $BCD$ is $3300\text{ m}^{2}$.
Calculate the bearing of $C$ from $B$. [3]

**(c)** A vertical tower of height 80 m stands at $C$.
Sally walks from $B$ to $D$ and reaches a point $P$ where the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from $P$ is the greatest.
Find the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from $P$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{BD}{\sin 44.2^{\circ}}=\frac{200}{\sin 95^{\circ}}
$$

$$
BD=\frac{200}{\sin 95^{\circ}}\times\sin 44.2^{\circ}\approx 139.965\approx 140\text{ m}
$$

**(b)**

$$
3300=\frac{1}{2}(48)(139.965)\sin\angle CBD
$$

$$
\sin\angle CBD=\frac{3300\times 2}{48(139.965)}
$$

$$
\angle CBD\approx 79.230^{\circ}
$$

$$
\text{bearing of }C\text{ from }B=360^{\circ}-(180^{\circ}-45.8^{\circ})-95^{\circ}-79.230^{\circ}\approx 051.6^{\circ}\text{ (to 1 dp)}
$$

**(c)** Let $h$ be the height of the vertical tower.

$$
\sin 79.230^{\circ}=\frac{\text{opp}}{48}
$$

$$
\text{opp}=48\times\sin 79.230^{\circ}\approx 47.1544
$$

$$
\tan\theta_{e}=\frac{80}{47.1544}
$$

$$
\theta_{e}=59.5^{\circ}\text{ (to 1 dp)}
$$
