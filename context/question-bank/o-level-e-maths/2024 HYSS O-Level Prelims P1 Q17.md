## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

The diagram represents a plot of land, $EFGH$, which is to be used for an observatory.

![Plot of land EFGH](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/HYSS/images/2024-HYSS_S4-5-G3-MA-Prelim_Paper-1_QP-pages/page-13.png)

(a) Construct the bisector of the angle $EHG$. [1]

(b) Construct the perpendicular bisector of $EF$. [1]

(c) A café is to be built in the observatory, nearer to $E$ than to $F$ and nearer to $GH$ than to $EH$. Shade the region where the café is to be built. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) B1 for the angle bisector of $EHG$, with construction arcs shown clearly.

(b) B1 for the perpendicular bisector of $EF$, with construction arcs shown clearly.

(c) A1 for the shaded region nearer to $E$ than to $F$ and nearer to $GH$ than to $EH$. If (a) or (b) is wrong but the shading follows those lines, award A1 with error carried forward.
