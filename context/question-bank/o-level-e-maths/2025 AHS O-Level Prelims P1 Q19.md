## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 15–16

The diagram shows the speed-time graph for a car's journey when it approaches a red traffic light and then drives off when the traffic light turns green. The area beneath each speed-time graph represents the distance travelled by the car.

![Question 19 speed-time graph](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/AHS/images/AHS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Calculate the acceleration of the car for the first 20 minutes. [2]

**(b)** Given that the distance travelled in the first 20 minutes is the same as the distance travelled between 20 minutes and 32 minutes, calculate the value of $V$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Calculate the acceleration of the car for the first 20 minutes.

$$
\text{Acceleration}=-36\ \mathrm{km/h}\div\dfrac{20}{60}\ \mathrm{h}=-108\ \mathrm{km/h}^{2}
$$

or

$$
\text{Acceleration}=\dfrac{0-36}{\dfrac{20}{60}}=-108\ \mathrm{km/h}^{2}
$$

**M1** find gradient. **A1**

**(b)** Given that the distance travelled in the first 20 minutes is the same as the distance travelled between 20 minutes and 32 minutes, calculate the value of $V$.

Same distance means the two areas of the triangles are the same.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{32-20}{60}\times V&=\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{20}{60}\times 36\\
\dfrac{V}{10}&=6\\
V&=60
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** **A1**
