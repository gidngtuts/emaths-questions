## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]; (c) [2]; (d) [4]; total [10]
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Simplify $\dfrac{5x^{2}-20}{5x^{2}+5x-10}$. [2]

**(b)** Simplify $\dfrac{4a^{2}b}{21c^{3}} \div \dfrac{12a}{7c}$. [2]

**(c)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{27a^{12}}{b^{9}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}}$. [2]

**(d)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{2y(1-y)+1}{3} = 1-\left(\dfrac{8}{3}-y\right)$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{5x^{2}-20}{5x^{2}+5x-10} = \frac{5(x^{2}-4)}{5(x^{2}+x-2)} = \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x+2)(x-1)} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{x-2}{x-1} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\frac{4a^{2}b}{21c^{3}} \div \frac{12a}{7c} = \frac{4a^{2}b}{21c^{3}} \times \frac{7c}{12a} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{ab}{9c^{2}} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(c)**

$$
\left(\frac{27a^{12}}{b^{9}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \left(\frac{b^{9}}{27a^{12}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{b^{3}}{3a^{4}} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(d)**

$$
\frac{2y(1-y)+1}{3} = 1-\left(\frac{8}{3}-y\right)
$$

$$
2y-2y^{2}+1 = 3\left(\frac{5}{3}+y\right) \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
2y^{2}+y-6 = 0
$$

$$
(2y-3)(y+2) = 0 \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
y = \frac{3}{2} \text{ or } -2 \quad \text{[A1][A1]}
$$
