## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Sketch the graph of $y=(4-x)(2+x)$.
State clearly the coordinates of the points where the graph crosses the axes and the turning point on the curve.

A blank pair of axes is provided, with origin $O$.

## Answer Scheme

An inverted parabola (coefficient of $x^{2}$ is negative) through $(-2,0)$ and $(4,0)$, with $y$-intercept $(0,8)$ and turning point $(1,9)$.

Shape with correct $y$-intercept $(0,8)$ and $x$-intercepts $(-2,0)$ and $(4,0)$: B2

Coordinates of turning point $(1,9)$: B1
