## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 10

**(a)** Express $x^{2}+5x+7$ in the form $(x+a)^{2}+b$.

**(b)** Hence, explain why the expression will never be negative.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
x^{2}+5x+7 = \left(x+\frac{5}{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{3}{4}
$$

**(b)** Since $\left(x+\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{2}\geq 0$ for all $x$, $\left(x+\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{2}+\dfrac{3}{4}\geq\dfrac{3}{4}$. Therefore the expression will never be negative.
