## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 7–8

The diagram shows a rectangular cardboard $ABCD$ with $AB = (3x+1)$ cm and $AD = (x+13)$ cm. A square of side 3 cm is cut from each corner. The remaining cardboard is then folded along the dotted lines to form an open rectangular box with base $PQRS$ and height 3 cm. The volume of the tray is 930 cm$^{3}$.

(a) Form an equation, in terms of $x$, to represent this information and show that it simplifies to $3x^{2}+16x-345 = 0$. [4]

(b) Solve the equation $3x^{2}+16x-345 = 0$.
Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [3]

(c) Find the length of the diagonal $SQ$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Length of box $= 3x+1-3-3 = (3x-5)$ cm. Width of box $= x+13-3-3 = (x+7)$ cm. Height $= 3$ cm. B1 for length and width.

Volume $= 3(3x-5)(x+7)$. M1, follow through.

$$
930 = 3(3x^{2}+21x-5x-35) \quad \text{M1 (expansion, follow through)}
$$

$$
310 = 3x^{2}+16x-35
$$

$$
3x^{2}+16x-345 = 0 \quad \text{A1 (equation established)}
$$

(b)

$$
x = \frac{-16 \pm \sqrt{16^{2}-4(3)(-345)}}{2(3)} \quad \text{B1}
$$

$x = 8.38$ or $x = -13.72$. B1, B1

(c) Length of box $= 3(8.38)-5 = 20.14$ cm. Width $= 8.38+7 = 15.38$ cm.

M1, follow through, for either length or width from the Q3(b) value.

$$
SQ = \sqrt{20.14^{2}+15.38^{2}} = 25.3 \text{ cm}
$$

M1 and A1, follow through.
