## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

Simplify

(a) $5-2(4x-3)$, [1]

(b) $\left(\dfrac{27x^{3}}{y^{6}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
5-2(4x-3)=5-8x+6=-8x+11
$$

(b)

$$
\left(\frac{27x^{3}}{y^{6}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}=\left(\frac{y^{6}}{27x^{3}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}=\frac{y^{4}}{9x^{2}}
$$
