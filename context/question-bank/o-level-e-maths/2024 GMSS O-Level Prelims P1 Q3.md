## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 3

Solve $4-\dfrac{1}{3}x = 2$.

## Answer Scheme

$4-\dfrac{1}{3}x = 2$, so $\dfrac{1}{3}x = 2$ and $x = 6$.
