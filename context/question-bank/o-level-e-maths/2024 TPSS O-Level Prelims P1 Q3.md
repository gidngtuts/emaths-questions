## Question

Factorise $4x^{6}-100y^{2}$ completely.

## Answer Scheme

$4(x^{3}-5y)(x^{3}+5y)$.

B1 for $4(x^{3}-5y)(x^{3}+5y)$ or $(2x^{3}-10y)(2x^{3}+10y)$ seen.
