## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 15

The diagram shows an irregular pentagon $ABCDE$.

![Question 19 pentagon ABCDE](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/SPS/images/SPS-EM-2026-4G3-P1-Final-pages/page-15.png)

**(a)** Find the value of $x$. [2]

**(b)** Show that the points $ABCDE$ lie on the circumference of a circle.
You are expected to state any circle properties used. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Interior angles: $A=110^{\circ}$, $B=30^{\circ}+70^{\circ}=100^{\circ}$, $E=40^{\circ}+80^{\circ}=120^{\circ}$, $D=30^{\circ}+80^{\circ}=110^{\circ}$.

$$
110+100+120+110+x=540
$$

$$
x=100
$$

**(b)** $\angle ABE=\angle ADE=30^{\circ}$, so $A$, $B$, $D$, $E$ lie on a circle (equal angles subtended by $AE$, angles in the same segment).

In $\triangle AED$: $\angle DAE=180^{\circ}-120^{\circ}-30^{\circ}=30^{\circ}$, so $\angle DAB=110^{\circ}-30^{\circ}=80^{\circ}$.

For $ABCD$: $\angle DAB+\angle BCD=80^{\circ}+100^{\circ}=180^{\circ}$, so $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.

Hence all five points $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ lie on the same circle.
