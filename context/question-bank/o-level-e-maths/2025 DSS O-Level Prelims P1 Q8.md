## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3

Factorise completely.

**(a)** $16a^{2}b-8ab^{2}$ [1]

**(b)** $3c-4d+3cd-4$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $16a^{2}b-8ab^{2}=8ab(2a-b)$

**(b)** $3c-4d+3cd-4=3c(1+d)-4(1+d)=(3c-4)(1+d)$
