## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 8

The pie chart below shows the sales for four different brands of potato chips.

![Pie chart of potato chip sales, titled Brand B is the Best Seller](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/STC/images/CHIJ-STC-2024-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-08.png)

(a) State one misleading feature of the pie chart. [1]

(b) Explain how this feature affects the reader's interpretation of the pie chart. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Percentage of Brand B $=100\%-22\%-34\%-10\%=34\%$. Brand B and Brand D have equal percentages but their proportions in the pie chart are not equal.

Or: the title is biased.

(b) It may mislead readers into believing that Brand B is selling better than Brand D.

Or: it does not allow the reader to make his/her own judgement.
