## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 11–12

**(a)** $y$ is directly proportional to the cube root of $(x+1)$.
It is given that $x=7$ when $y=1$. Find the value of $y$ when $x=124$.

**(b)** $F$ is inversely proportional to the square of $d$.

Explain what happens to $F$ when $d$ is halved.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $y=k\sqrt[3]{x+1}$

$1=k\sqrt[3]{7+1}$ M1

$k=0.5$

$y=0.5\sqrt[3]{124+1}=2.5$ A1

**(b)** $F=\dfrac{k}{d^{2}}$

New $F=\dfrac{k}{(0.5d)^{2}}$ B1

$=4\left(\dfrac{k}{d^{2}}\right)=4F$

The scheme states: new value of $y$ becomes 4 times of the original value. B1

Other accepted answers: $F$ increases to 400% of the original value; $F$ increases by 300%; $F$ increases by 3 times; $F$ is increased by a factor of 4; $F$ is multiplied by 4.
