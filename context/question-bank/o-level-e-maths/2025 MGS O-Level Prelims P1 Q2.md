$4^{m-3}\times 8^{3m-1}=4$

Use the laws of indices to find the value of $m$. Show your working. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$4^{m-3}\times 8^{3m-1}=4$

Use the laws of indices to find the value of $m$. Show your working.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4^{m-3}\times 8^{3m-1}&=4\\
2^{2(m-3)}\times 2^{3(3m-1)}&=2^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1 for expressing terms in base 2

$$
\begin{aligned}
2^{2m-6+9m-3}&=2^{2}\\
2^{11m-9}&=2^{2}\\
11m-9&=2\\
11m&=11\\
m&=1
\end{aligned}
$$
