## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 13

**(a)** Factorise completely $49x^{2}-(x+3)^{2}$. [2]

**(b)** One solution of the equation $6x^{2}+kx-14 = 0$, where $k$ is an integer, is $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

Find

**(i)** the value of $k$, [1]

**(ii)** the other solution to the equation. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $3(8x+3)(2x-1)$

**(b) (i)** $k = 17$

**(ii)** $x = -\dfrac{7}{2}$
