## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]; total [4]
> **Page:** 9

![Question 19 shaded sectors](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P1-QP-pages/page-09.png)

The diagram shows the arcs $AB$ and $CD$ of two circles, centre $O$, with radii 4 cm and 8 cm respectively.

**(a)** Calculate the perimeter of the shaded region. [2]

**(b)** Calculate the area of the shaded region. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\text{Perimeter of shaded region} = \frac{60^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}} \times 2\pi(4)+4+4+\frac{60^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}} \times 2\pi(8) \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= 20.566\text{ cm} = 20.6\text{ cm} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\text{Area of shaded region} = \frac{60^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}} \times \pi(8)^{2}-\frac{60^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}} \times \pi(4)^{2} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= 25.133\text{ cm}^{2} = 25.1\text{ cm}^{2} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
