## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [4]
> **Page:** 21

**(a)** Underline the following ratios that are equivalent to the ratio $a:b$.

$\dfrac{1}{b}:\dfrac{1}{a}$

$a^{3}:b^{3}$

$3a:3b$

$a+1:b+1$

**(b)** John and Sam each have an amount of money.
The ratio John’s amount : Sam’s amount $= 6:2$.

John gives Sam ＄13.
The new ratio John’s amount : Sam’s amount $= 2:5$.

Find how much money John has now.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{1}{b}:\dfrac{1}{a}$ and $3a:3b$.

**(b)** Scale $6:2$ to $42:14$ and $2:5$ to $16:40$.

$26$ units correspond to ＄13, so 1 unit is ＄0.50 and John now has $16\times 0.50 = \text{＄}8$.

Or $\dfrac{6x-13}{2x+13} = \dfrac{2}{5}$, $x = 3.5$, and $6(3.5)-13 = 8$.
