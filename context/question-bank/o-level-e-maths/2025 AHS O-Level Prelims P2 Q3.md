## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 5–6

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form

$$
\dfrac{2}{9x^{2}-1}+\dfrac{1}{1-3x}.
$$

[3]

## Answer Scheme

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{2}{9x^{2}-1}+\dfrac{1}{1-3x}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{2}{9x^{2}-1}+\dfrac{1}{1-3x}&=\dfrac{2}{(3x-1)(3x+1)}-\dfrac{1}{3x-1}\\
&=\dfrac{2-1(3x+1)}{(3x-1)(3x+1)}\\
&=\dfrac{-(3x-1)}{(3x-1)(3x+1)}\\
&=\dfrac{-1}{3x+1}
\end{aligned}
$$

**M2** for factorising $9x^{2}-1$ and switching operation sign. **A1**
