## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 10

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{7y}{y+3}-\dfrac{y+9}{3-y}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{7y(3-y)-(y+9)(y+3)}{(y+3)(3-y)}=\frac{9y-8y^{2}-27}{(y+3)(3-y)}
$$

or $\dfrac{8y^{2}-9y+27}{(y+3)(y-3)}$
