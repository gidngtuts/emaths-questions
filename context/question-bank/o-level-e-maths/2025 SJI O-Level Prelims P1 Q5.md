## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

A polygon has $n$ sides.
When the number of sides is doubled, the interior angle is increased by $20^{\circ}$.
Find the value of $n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{(2n-2)\times 180}{2n}-\frac{(n-2)\times 180}{n}=20
$$

$$
(2n-2)(180)-(2n-4)(180)=40n
$$

$$
360=40n
$$

$$
n=9
$$
