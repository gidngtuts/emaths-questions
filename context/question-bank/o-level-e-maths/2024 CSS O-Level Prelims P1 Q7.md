## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 5

**(a)** Expand and simplify $(3x-y)(y+x)$. [2]

**(b)** Factorise completely $3-12a^{2}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
(3x-y)(y+x) = 3xy+3x^{2}-y^{2}-xy = 3x^{2}+2xy-y^{2}
$$

**(b)**

$$
3-12a^{2} = 3(1-4a^{2}) = 3(1-2a)(1+2a)
$$
