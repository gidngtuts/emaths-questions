## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]; (c) [3]; (d) [3]
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Solve $\dfrac{x}{5}+\dfrac{2x-3}{4} = -6$.

**(b)** Simplify $\dfrac{2(p-3q)}{10r}\div\dfrac{(3q-p)^{2}}{r}$.

**(c)** Solve the inequality $x+1 \le \dfrac{7x-2}{4} < \dfrac{5x+19}{5}$.

**(d)** Simplify $\dfrac{2y^{2}+7y-9}{4y^{2}-81}$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{5}+\frac{2x-3}{4} &= -6 \\
\frac{4x+5(2x-3)}{20} &= -6 \\
4x+10x-15 &= -120 \\
14x &= -105 \\
x &= -7.5
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\frac{2(p-3q)}{10r}\times\frac{r}{(3q-p)^{2}} = \frac{1}{5(p-3q)}
$$

Also accepted: $\dfrac{-1}{5(3q-p)}$ or $\dfrac{-1}{15q-5p}$.

**(c)** $2 \le x < 5\dfrac{11}{15}$, or $2 \le x < \dfrac{86}{15}$.

No marks if the inequality is reversed as $5\dfrac{11}{15} > x \ge 2$. Deduct 1 mark for $2 \le x < 5.73$.

**(d)**

$$
\frac{2y^{2}+7y-9}{4y^{2}-81} = \frac{(2y+9)(y-1)}{(2y+9)(2y-9)} = \frac{y-1}{2y-9}
$$
