## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [1]; (c) [3]; (d) [3]; (e) [2]; total [10]
> **Page:** 8–9

Two water taps, X and Y, together can fill a tank in $6$ hours. Tap X takes $x$ hours to fill the tank alone. Tap Y takes $25$ hours less than the Tap X to fill the tank alone.

**(a)** Write down an expression, in terms of $x$, for the fraction of the tank that Tap X will fill up in one hour. [1]

**(b)** Write down an expression, in terms of $x$, for the fraction of the tank that Tap Y will fill up in one hour. [1]

**(c)** Write down an equation to represent the rate of filling the tank by the $2$ taps and show that it reduces to $x^{2}-37x+150=0$. [3]

**(d)** Solve the equation $x^{2}-37x+150=0$. [3]

**(e)** Calculate how long it would take to fill the tank from empty using Tap Y. Give your answer in hours and minutes, correct to the nearest minute. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{1}{x}$

**(b)** $\dfrac{1}{x-25}$

**(c)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x-25}&=\dfrac{1}{6}\\
\dfrac{x+x-25}{x(x-25)}&=\dfrac{1}{6}\\
6(2x-25)&=x(x-25)\\
12x-150&=x^{2}-25x\\
x^{2}-37x+150&=0\quad(\text{shown})
\end{aligned}
$$

**(d)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\dfrac{-(-37)\pm\sqrt{(-37)^{2}-4(1)(150)}}{2(1)}\\
x&=32.365\quad\text{or}\quad x=4.634\\
x&=32.4\quad\text{or}\quad x=4.63\quad(3\text{ s.f.})
\end{aligned}
$$

Must show substitution of values.

**(e)** Reject $x=4.63$ as the time taken, $x-25$, cannot be negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Time Tap Y takes}&=32.365-25\\
&=7.365\\
&=7\text{ hours }22\text{ minutes}\ (\text{nearest minute})
\end{aligned}
$$
