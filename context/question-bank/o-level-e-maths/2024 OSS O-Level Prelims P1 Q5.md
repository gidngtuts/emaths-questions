Zen invests ＄42000 at a rate of 1.6% per annum compounded monthly.
Calculate the value of the investment at the end of 3 years.

## Answer Scheme

$$
42000\left(1+\frac{1.6}{12\times 100}\right)^{36}=\text{＄}44063.76
