## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 21

The scale of a map is $1:n$.
A school garden measuring $8\text{ cm}^{2}$ on a map has an actual area of $2048\text{ m}^{2}$.
Find $n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
8\text{ cm}^{2}:2048\text{ m}^{2}
= 1\text{ cm}^{2}:256\text{ m}^{2}
= 1\text{ cm}^{2}:2560000\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

$$
= 1\text{ cm}:1600\text{ cm}
$$

$$
n=1600
$$
