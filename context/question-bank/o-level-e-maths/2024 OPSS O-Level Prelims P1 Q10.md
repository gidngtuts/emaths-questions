## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 10

Sketch the graph in the spaces below. Blank axes, with origin marked 0, are provided for (a) and (b).

**(a)** $y=\dfrac{4}{x}$

**(b)** $y=-2x^{3}$

**(c)** Hence, state the number of solutions for the equation $\dfrac{4}{x}+2x^{3}=0$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Rectangular hyperbola $y=\dfrac{4}{x}$ in the first and third quadrants.

**(b)** Cubic $y=-2x^{3}$, decreasing through the origin.

**(c)** $\dfrac{4}{x}=-2x^{3}$ has no intersection, so 0 solutions.
