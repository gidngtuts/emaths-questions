## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 13

In the figure, $AB=12\text{ cm}$, $BC=5\text{ cm}$ and $\angle ABC=90^{\circ}$.

![Question 14 triangle ABC and D](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-13.png)

**(a)** Find the value of $\cos\angle ACD$. [2]

**(b)** If $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on the circumference of a circle, write down the radius of the circle. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
AC=\sqrt{12^{2}+5^{2}}=13
$$

$$
\cos\angle ACD=-\cos\angle ACB=-\frac{5}{13}
$$

**(b)** Using angles in a semicircle, $AC$ is the diameter.

$$
\text{Radius}=\frac{13}{2}=6.5
$$
