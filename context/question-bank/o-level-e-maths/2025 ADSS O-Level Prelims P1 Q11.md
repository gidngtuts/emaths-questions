## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [2]; total [3]
> **Page:** 5–6

**(a)** Factorise $20ab-4b$. [1]

**(b)** Factorise completely $3xy-2ab-ay+6xb$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $20ab-4b = 4b(5a-1)$ [A1]

**(b)**

$$
3xy-2ab-ay+6xb = 3x(2b+y)-a(2b+y) \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= (2b+y)(3x-a) \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
