**(a)** $\angle BDC=\angle FED$ (corresponding angles, $BD\parallel AE$).

$$
\frac{BD}{FE}=\frac{15}{10}=\frac{3}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{CD}{DE}=\frac{12}{8}=\frac{3}{2}
$$

[B1]

Therefore $\triangle CDB$ and $\triangle DEF$ are similar (ratio of corresponding sides and included angle are equal / SAS similarity test). [B1]

**(b)** $(12:8)^{2}=9:4$ [B1]

**(c)** $\angle CBD=\angle DFE$ (from part (a), $\triangle CDB$ and $\triangle DEF$ are similar). [B1]

Therefore $AB\parallel FD$ and hence $ABDF$ is a parallelogram.
