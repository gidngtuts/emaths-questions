## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 20

In the diagram below, $PQRST$ is a trapezium made up of a parallelogram $PQRS$ and an isosceles triangle $PST$ where $PT = PS$. It is given that $\overrightarrow{RS} = \mathbf{a}$, $\overrightarrow{RQ} = \mathbf{b}$ and $SR = ST$.

![Trapezium PQRST with vectors a and b](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/CCHM/images/CCHM-2024-S4-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-20.png)

**(a)** Show that $\overrightarrow{QS} = \overrightarrow{PT}$.

**(b)** Hence or otherwise, prove that the trapezium is made up of three isosceles triangles $PST$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\overrightarrow{QS} = \overrightarrow{QR}+\overrightarrow{RS} = -\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{a}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{PT} = \overrightarrow{PS}+\overrightarrow{ST} = -\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{a}
$$

Therefore $\overrightarrow{QS} = \overrightarrow{PT}$.

**(b)** Since $PQRS$ is a parallelogram, $PS \parallel QR$, $PQ \parallel SR$, $PS = QR$ and $PQ = SR$.

From (a), $PT = QS$. Also $PT = QS = PS = QR$ and $PQ = SR = TS$.

By the SSS congruency test, triangles $PTS$, $PQS$ and $QSR$ are congruent, therefore the trapezium is made up of three isosceles triangles.
