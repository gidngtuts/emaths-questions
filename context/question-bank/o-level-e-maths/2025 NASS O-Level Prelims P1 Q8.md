## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** The equation of a straight line is $2y=3-6x$.

Find the gradient of the line. [1]

**(b)** Another line has gradient $-3$ and passes through the point $(0, 1)$.

Explain why this line will not intersect the line in part (a). [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
2y=3-6x
$$

$$
y=-3x+\frac{3}{2}
$$

Gradient is $-3$.

**(b)** The line in part (a) is parallel to the line in part (b) since they have the same gradient.

Also, for the line in part (a), when $x=0$, $y=\dfrac{3}{2}$.

The point $(0, 1)$ does not lie on the line in part (a).

Therefore, the two lines will not intersect.
