## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 9

$X$, $Y$ and $Z$ are three points on horizontal ground.
Angle $ZXY=35^{\circ}$, angle $XYZ=90^{\circ}$ and $XZ=20$ cm.

**(a)** Calculate the distance $YZ$. [2]

**(b)** Calculate the area of triangle $XYZ$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Calculate the distance $YZ$.

$$
\sin 35^{\circ}=\frac{YZ}{20}
$$

M1 for setting up the correct equation.

$$
YZ=20\sin 35^{\circ}=11.472\text{ cm}=11.5\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}
$$

A1.

**(b)** Calculate the area of triangle $XYZ$.

$$
\cos 35^{\circ}=\frac{XY}{20}
$$

$$
XY=20\cos 35^{\circ}=16.383\text{ cm}=16.4\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}
$$

M1 for finding $XY$.

$$
\text{Area of triangle }XYZ=94.0\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

A1.

Alternative solution.

$$
\angle XZY=55^{\circ}
$$

M1 for finding angle $XZY$.

$$
\text{Area of triangle}=\frac{1}{2}\times 20\times 11.472\times\sin 55^{\circ}=94.0\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

A1.
