## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 7

The table below shows a multiple-choice test Sam and Roger took.

|  | Correct | No attempt | Incorrect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sam | 14 | 5 | 1 |
| Roger | 15 | 0 | 5 |

(a) Represent this information in a $2\times 3$ matrix, $S$. [1]

(b) The marks are awarded as follow:

Correct $= 2$ marks

No attempt $= 0$ mark

Incorrect $= -1$ mark

Represent the information in a $3\times 1$ matrix, $T$.

Evaluate $ST$ [2]

(c) Explain what your answer to (b) represents. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $S = \begin{pmatrix} 14 & 5 & 1 \\ 15 & 0 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$ [B1]

(b) $T = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 0 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$ [M1]

$ST = \begin{pmatrix} 27 \\ 25 \end{pmatrix}$ [A1]

(c) Total score Sam and Roger get respectively. [B1]
