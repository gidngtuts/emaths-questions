## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Given that $\dfrac{5^{x}}{2^{2x}\times 5^{3-x}}=2^{m}5^{n}$, express $m$ and $n$ in terms of $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{5^{x}}{2^{2x}\times 5^{3-x}}=2^{m}5^{n}$

$2^{-2x}5^{x-(3-x)}=2^{m}5^{n}$

$m=-2x$ (B1)

$n=2x-3$ (B1)
