## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 21

![Question 24 triangles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/DSS/images/Dunman-2026-Emaths-Prelims-Paper-1-pages/page-21.png)

In the diagram, $AE=8.5$ cm, $DE=7.5$ cm and angle $ABC=90^{\circ}$.
$AEB$ and $ADC$ are straight lines.
Triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $ADE$.

**(a)** Show that $AD=4$ cm. [2]

**(b)** Find the exact value of $\sin\angle AED+\cos\angle BED$. [3]

---

**END OF PAPER**

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Triangle $ABC$ is similar to triangle $ADE$.

$\therefore \angle ADE=\angle ABC=90^{\circ}$

By Pythagoras' Theorem,

$$
AD=\sqrt{8.5^{2}-7.5^{2}}=4\text{ cm}
$$

[M1, A1]

**(b)**

$$
\sin\angle AED=\frac{4}{8.5}=\frac{8}{17}
$$

$$
\cos\angle BED=-\cos\angle AED=-\frac{7.5}{8.5}=-\frac{15}{17}
$$

$$
\sin\angle AED+\cos\angle BED=\frac{8}{17}-\frac{15}{17}=-\frac{7}{17}
$$

[M1, M1, A1]
