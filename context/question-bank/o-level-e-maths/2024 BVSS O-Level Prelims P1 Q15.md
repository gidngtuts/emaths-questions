## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

Simplify $\left(\dfrac{9p^{3}q^{-2}}{p^{-1}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}}$, leaving your answer in positive index.

## Answer Scheme

One route:

$$
\left(\frac{9p^{3}q^{-2}}{p^{-1}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\frac{9p^{4}}{q^{2}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{2}} = \left(\frac{q^{2}}{9p^{4}}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}} = \frac{q^{3}}{27p^{6}}
$$

M1 for rewriting the base, M1 for the reciprocal power, A1.

An equivalent route gives $\dfrac{1}{27}p^{-6}q^{3} = \dfrac{q^{3}}{27p^{6}}$. B1 for each term is accepted.
