## Question

> **Marks:** 9
> **Page:** 12–13

$A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are four points on level ground, with $B$ due east of $A$.
$AC = 43$ m, $CD = 32$ m, $AD = 67$ m, angle $CAB = 52^{\circ}$ and angle $ABC = 33^{\circ}$.

![Points A, B, C, D with lengths 67 m, 43 m, 32 m and angles 52 degrees and 33 degrees](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/TPSS/images/2024-TPSS-EMATH-P2-PRELIM-QP-pages/page-12.png)

(a) Calculate the bearing of $B$ from $C$. [2]

(b) Calculate $AB$. [2]

(c) Calculate angle $CDA$. [3]

(d) A drone hovers 60 m vertically above $D$.
Find the angle of depression of $A$ from the drone. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Bearing of $B$ from $C = 180-(90-33) = 123^{\circ}$. M1, A1

The scheme redraws the figure with a north arrow at $C$ and at $B$.

(b)

$$
\frac{AB}{\sin(180-52-33)^{\circ}} = \frac{43}{\sin 33^{\circ}} \quad \text{M1}
$$

$AB = 78.65 = 78.7$ m (3 s.f.). A1

(c)

$$
\cos \angle CDA = \frac{67^{2}+32^{2}-43^{2}}{2(67)(32)} \quad \text{M2}
$$

$\angle CDA = 31.297^{\circ} = 31.3^{\circ}$ (1 d.p.). A1

(d)

$$
\tan \theta = \frac{60}{67} \quad \text{M1}
$$

$\theta = 41.84^{\circ} = 41.8^{\circ}$ (1 d.p.). A1
