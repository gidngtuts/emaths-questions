## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 13

$AOB$ is a sector of a circle, centre $O$. $OA = 10$ cm and the sector angle is $216^{\circ}$. The diagram shows the major sector.

(a) Calculate the perimeter of this sector. Give your answer in terms of $\pi$. [2]

(b) A cone is made from this sector by joining $OA$ to $OB$.
Calculate the volume of the cone. [4]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\text{Perimeter} = \frac{216}{360} \times 2 \times \pi \times 10 + 20 = 12\pi+20
$$

M1 for $\dfrac{216}{360} \times 2 \times \pi \times 10$.

(b) $2\pi r = 12\pi$, so $r = 6$. M1

Height of the cone $= \sqrt{10^{2}-6^{2}} = 8$. M1

$$
\text{Volume of the cone} = \frac{1}{3} \times \pi \times 6^{2} \times 8 = 301.59 \approx 302 \quad \text{M1, A1}
$$
