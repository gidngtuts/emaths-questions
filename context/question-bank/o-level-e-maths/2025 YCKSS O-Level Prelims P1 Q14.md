> **Page:** 10

Indicate clearly the coordinates of the points where the graph crosses the axes and the maximum point on the curve. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Sketch the graph of $y=-(x-2)^{2}+16$.

Maximum point $(2,16)$.
$y$-intercept $(0,12)$.
$x$-intercepts $(-2,0)$ and $(6,0)$.
