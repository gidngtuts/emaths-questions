## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3, (c) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 12

$P$ is the point $(2,-5)$.

$$
\overrightarrow{QP}=\begin{pmatrix}-4\\-3\end{pmatrix}
$$

**(a)** Find the length of $PQ$. [1]

**(b)** Find the equation of $PQ$. [3]

**(c)** Another point, $R$, lies between $P$ and $Q$ on the line $PQ$ such that $PQ:PR=4:3$.
Find the coordinates of $R$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\sqrt{(-4)^{2}+(-3)^{2}}=5
$$

**(b)**

$$
\text{gradient}=\frac{-3}{-4}=\frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
y=\frac{3}{4}x+c
$$

$$
-5=\frac{3}{4}(2)+c
$$

$$
c=-\frac{13}{2}
$$

$$
y=\frac{3}{4}x-\frac{13}{2}
$$

or $y=\dfrac{3}{4}x-6\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $y=\dfrac{3}{4}x-6.5$

**(c)**

$$
x\text{-coordinate}=2+\frac{3}{4}(4)=5
$$

$$
y\text{-coordinate}=-5+\frac{3}{4}(3)=-2\frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
R=(5,-2.75)
$$
