## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 6

Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{5x}{6}-\dfrac{3(x-1)}{4}+\dfrac{2x+7}{12}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{5x}{6}-\frac{3(x-1)}{4}+\frac{2x+7}{12} &= \frac{10x}{12}-\frac{9(x-1)}{12}+\frac{2x+7}{12} \\
&= \frac{10x-9x+9+2x+7}{12} \\
&= \frac{3x+16}{12}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: common denominator (same form as here). A1.
