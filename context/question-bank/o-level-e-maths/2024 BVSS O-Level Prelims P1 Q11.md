## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

The difference between simple interest and compound interest for a period of 3 years at $5\%$ per annum is ＄366.

Find the principal amount.

## Answer Scheme

Compound interest minus simple interest is ＄366.

$$
\left[P\left(1+\frac{5}{100}\right)^{3}-P\right]-\frac{P\times 5\times 3}{100} = 366
$$

M1 for a correct compound-interest application. M1 for the difference being compound interest minus simple interest.

$$
[1.157625P-P]-0.15P = 366
$$

$$
0.007625P = 366
$$

$$
P = 48000 \quad\text{A1}
$$
