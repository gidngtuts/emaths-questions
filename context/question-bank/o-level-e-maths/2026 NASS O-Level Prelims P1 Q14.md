## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

Simplify $\dfrac{3x^{2}+7x-40}{18x^{3}-128x}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3x^{2}+7x-40}{18x^{3}-128x} &= \frac{(3x-8)(x+5)}{2x(9x^{2}-64)} \\
&= \frac{(3x-8)(x+5)}{2x(3x-8)(3x+8)} \\
&= \frac{x+5}{2x(3x+8)}\text{ or }\frac{x+5}{6x^{2}+16x}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: factorise numerator. M1: factorise denominator. A1.
