## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 10

In the diagram, two circles with centres $O$ and $P$ respectively intersects at $A$.
The two circles have the same radius and $AB\parallel OP$.
Show that $AB=OP$.

![Two equal circles, centres O and P, with chord AB parallel to OP](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BSS/images/Broadrick-EM-2024-Maths-Sec-Prelim-P1-pages/page-10.png)

## Answer Scheme

In triangle $OAP$ and $BPA$,

$\angle OPA=\angle BAP$ (alt angle, $AB\parallel OP$)

Since $OA=AP=PB$ (same radius).

$\angle AOP=\angle PBA$ (base angles of isosceles triangle)

$AP=PA$ (common side)

(B2, any 2 of the 3 statements)

Therefore $\triangle OPA\equiv\triangle BAP$ (AAS) (A1, conclude AAS and equal sides)

Hence $AB=OP$
