## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Simplify

$$
\left(\frac{3a^{-\frac{3}{2}}b^{2}}{4^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{3}}\right)^{-2}
$$

and leave your answer in positive index notation. [3]

**(b)** Simplify

$$
\frac{(a-5b)^{2}}{a^{2}-25b^{2}}\div\frac{a-5b}{5b}.
$$

[2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\left(\frac{4^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{3}}{3a^{-\frac{3}{2}}b^{2}}\right)^{2}
$$

[M1]

$$
=\frac{4b^{6}}{9a^{-3}b^{4}}
$$

[M1]

$$
=\frac{4a^{3}b^{2}}{9}
$$

[A1]

**(b)**

$$
\frac{(a-5b)^{2}}{(a-5b)(a+5b)}\times\frac{5b}{a-5b}
$$

[M1] for $(a-5b)(a+5b)$

$$
=\frac{5b}{a+5b}
$$

[A1]
