## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 6

Simplify.

(a) $5(3x-2y)-2(x+4y)$ [2]

(b) $12p^{2}+8-(3p-2)^{2}$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $5(3x-2y)-2(x+4y) = 15x-10y-2x-8y = 13x-18y$ [M1 either correct expansion, A1]

(b) $12p^{2}+8-(3p-2)^{2} = 12p^{2}+8-(9p^{2}-12p+4) = 3p^{2}+12p+4$ [M1, A1]
