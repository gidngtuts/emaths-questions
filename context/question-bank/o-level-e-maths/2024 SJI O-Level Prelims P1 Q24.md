## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 18

A ball is thrown vertically upwards. Its height, $h$ m, above ground at time $t$ seconds after being thrown is given by the formula $h = -1.44t^{2}+1.6t+1.5$.
Find the time taken for the ball to return to the ground.
