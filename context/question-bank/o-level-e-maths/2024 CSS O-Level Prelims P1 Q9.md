## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** $x^{2}-10x+27$ can be expressed in the form $(x+p)^{2}+q$.
Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [2]

**(b)** Hence write down the coordinates of the minimum point of the graph of $y = x^{2}-10x+27$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
x^{2}-10x+27 = x^{2}-10x+5^{2}-5^{2}+27 = (x-5)^{2}+2
$$

$p = -5$, $q = 2$.

**(b)** $(5, 2)$.
