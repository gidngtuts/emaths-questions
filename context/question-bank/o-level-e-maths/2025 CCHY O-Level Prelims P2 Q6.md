## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c)(i) 3, (c)(ii) 2; total 9
> **Page:** 12–13

$A$ is the point $(4,-2)$ and the line $AB$ is parallel to the line $\dfrac{1}{2}x-3y=9$.

**(a)** Find the equation of the line $AB$. [2]

**(b)** The equation of the line $BC$ is $2x+9y+52=0$.
Find the $x$-coordinate of $B$. [2]

**(c)** It is given that $\overrightarrow{AD}=\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\overrightarrow{AE}=\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$.

**(i)** By considering their magnitudes, show that $ADE$ is a right-angled triangle. [3]

**(ii)** $F$ is the point $(0,-6)$. Showing your working, determine if $F$ lies on $DA$ extended. [2]
