## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 5–6

**(a)** Simplify $\dfrac{(2x^{3}y^{2})^{-4}}{(10x^{-2}y^{3})^{2}}\div\sqrt[3]{27x^{-3}y^{6}}$.

**(b)** Solve $3^{x+2}\times 3(3^{5})=1$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{2^{-4}x^{-12}y^{-8}}{100x^{-4}y^{6}}\times\frac{1}{(27x^{-3}y^{6})^{1/3}}=\frac{1}{1600x^{8}y^{14}}\times\frac{1}{3x^{-1}y^{2}}=\frac{1}{4800x^{7}y^{16}}
$$

**(b)** $3^{x+2}\times 3^{1}\times 3^{5}=3^{0}$

$x+2+6=0$, so $x=-8$
