## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

Simplify $\dfrac{5c}{2}\div\dfrac{20c^{2}}{d}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{5c}{2} \div \frac{20c^{2}}{d} = \frac{5c}{2} \times \frac{d}{20c^{2}} = \frac{d}{8c}
$$
