## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 9

Factorise completely.

**(a)** $5-5x^{2}$ [2]

**(b)** $2xq-2yq-yp+xp$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

Factorise completely.

**(a)**

$$
5-5x^{2}=5(1-x^{2})=5(1-x)(1+x)
$$

M1, A1

**(b)**

$$
2xq-2yq-yp+xp=2q(x-y)+p(x-y)=(2q+p)(x-y)
$$

M1, A1
