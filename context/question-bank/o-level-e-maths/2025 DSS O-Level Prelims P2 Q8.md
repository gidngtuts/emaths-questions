## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 1, (c) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 18–19

A group of 200 teenagers in Singapore were asked how many hours of daily screen time they had, on average, in the last week.

The cumulative frequency curve shows the distribution of the hours.

![Question 8 cumulative frequency curve](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Use the curve to estimate

**(i)** the median hours, [1]

**(ii)** the interquartile range of the number of hours. [2]

**(b)** Find the probability that a teenager selected from this group had a daily screen time of at least 5 hours. [1]

**(c)** Another group of 200 teenagers in Country X were also asked how many hours of daily screen time they had, on average, in the last week.

The box-and-whisker plot shows the distribution of the hours.

![Question 8 box-and-whisker plot](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-19.png)

Make two comparisons between the daily screen time of teenagers in Singapore and in Country X. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)** 7.5 hours ($\pm 0.1$)

**(ii)** $Q_{1}=6$, $Q_{3}=9.4$

IQR $=9.4-6=3.4$ ($\pm 0.1$)

**(b)** 32 students spent 5 hours or less.

168 students spent at least 5 hours.

$$
\text{Probability}=\frac{168}{200}=\frac{21}{25}=0.84
$$

**(c)** In general, teenagers in Country X spent less daily screen time with median of 6.5 hours compared to Singapore's median of 7.5 hours.

The amount of daily screen time spent by teenagers in Country X has a larger spread with an IQR of 5.2 hours as compared to that of Singapore with IQR of 3.4 hours.
