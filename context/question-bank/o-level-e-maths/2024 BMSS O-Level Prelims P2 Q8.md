## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 16–17

In July, the price of durians was ＄$x$ per kg. In August, the price of durians dropped by ＄$4.50$ per kg. Don could buy $2.8\text{ kg}$ more durians in August than in July with ＄$200$.

**(a)** Write down an expression, in terms of $x$, to show the mass of durians that can be bought for ＄$200$ in July. [1]

**(b)** Write down an expression, in terms of $x$, to show the mass of durians that can be bought for ＄$200$ in August. [1]

**(c)** Form an equation in terms of $x$ and show that it reduces to $14x^{2}-63x-4500=0$. [3]

**(d)** Solve the equation $14x^{2}-63x-4500=0$. Give your answers corrected to $2$ decimal places. [3]

**(e)** Find the price of the durians per kg in August. [1]

**(f)** Find the percentage drop in price in August compared to July. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{200}{x}$ kg [B1]

**(b)** $\dfrac{200}{x-4.5}$ kg [B1]

**(c)**

$$
\frac{200}{x-4.5}-\frac{200}{x}=2.8
$$

[M1]

$$
200x-200(x-4.5)=2.8x(x-4.5)
$$

$$
900=2.8x^{2}-12.6x
$$

[M1]

$$
28x^{2}-126x-9000=0
$$

$$
14x^{2}-63x-4500=0\text{ (shown)}
$$

[A1]

**(d)**

$$
x=\frac{63\pm\sqrt{(-63)^{2}-4(14)(-4500)}}{2(14)}
$$

[M1]

$$
x=\frac{63\pm\sqrt{255969}}{2(14)}
$$

$$
x=-15.82\text{ or }20.32
$$

[A1], [A1]

**(e)** $20.3191-4.50=＄15.82$ (2 d.p.) [B1] (follow through)

**(f)**

$$
\frac{4.50}{20.3191}\times 100\%=22.1\%\text{ (3 s.f.)}
$$

[B1] (follow through)
