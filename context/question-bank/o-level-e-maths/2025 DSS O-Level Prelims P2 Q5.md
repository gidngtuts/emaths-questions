## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 2, (c) 2, (d) 4; total 11
> **Page:** 12–13

The diagram shows a solid made of a cuboid $ABCDEFGH$ and a triangular prism $EFGHIJ$, resting on level ground.
$AB=8$ m, $BC=12$ m, $AE=3$ m, $EI=3$ m and $FI=7$ m.

![Question 5 solid](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-12.png)

**(a)** Show that angle $EFI=21.8^{\circ}$, correct to 1 decimal place. [3]

**(b)** Find the volume of the solid, leaving your answer correct to 2 decimal places. [2]

**(c)** Find the vertical height of $I$ above the ground. [2]

**(d)** Find the angle of elevation of $J$ from $B$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
3^{2}=8^{2}+7^{2}-2(8)(7)\cos\angle EFI
$$

$$
\cos\angle EFI=\frac{8^{2}+7^{2}-3^{2}}{2(8)(7)}=\frac{13}{14}
$$

$$
\angle EFI=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{13}{14}\right)=21.7867\ldots=21.8^{\circ}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\text{vol of prism}=\left(8\times 3+\frac{1}{2}\times 7\times 8\times\sin 21.7867^{\circ}\right)\times 12=412.70717\ldots=412.71\text{ m}^{3}
$$

**(c)**

$$
\text{height}=3+7\sin 21.8^{\circ}=5.5995\ldots=5.60\text{ m (3 s.f.)}
$$

**(d)** Let $X$ be the point vertically below $J$.

$$
CX=7\cos 21.8^{\circ}=6.4994\ldots
$$

$$
BX=\sqrt{12^{2}+6.4994^{2}}=13.647\ldots
$$

$$
\tan\angle JBX=\frac{5.5995}{13.647}
$$

$$
\angle JBX=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{5.5995}{13.647}\right)=22.3^{\circ}\text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$
