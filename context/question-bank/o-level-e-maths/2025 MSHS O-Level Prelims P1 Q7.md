## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 8

Factorise

**(a)** $2+5x-3x^{2}$, [2]

**(b)** $3x^{2}-24y+4x-18xy$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Factorise

**(a)** $2+5x-3x^{2}$,

$$2+5x-3x^{2}=(3x+1)(-x+2)$$

> **Markers Report:** Generally well done, accept other forms eg. $-(3x+1)(x-2)$, $(-3x-1)(x-2)$

**(b)** $3x^{2}-24y+4x-18xy$.

$$3x^{2}-24y+4x-18xy$$

$$=x(3x+4)-6y(4+3x)$$

$$=(x-6y)(3x+4)$$

> **Markers Report:** Generally well done
