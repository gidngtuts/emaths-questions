## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 5

Given that the coordinates of point $A$ is $(-2, 11)$ and point $B$ is $(5, -11)$.

Find

(a) length $AB$, [2]

(b) equation of the straight-line $AB$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Length $AB = \sqrt{(-11-11)^{2}+(5-(-2))^{2}}$ [M1]

$= \sqrt{533} = 23.086 = 23.1$ (3 s.f.) [A1]

(b) Gradient $m = \dfrac{-11-11}{5-(-2)} = -\dfrac{22}{7}$ [M1]

$11 = -\dfrac{22}{7}(-2)+c$

$11 = \dfrac{44}{7}+c$

$c = -\dfrac{33}{7}$ [M1]

$y = -\dfrac{22}{7}x-\dfrac{33}{7}$ [A1]
