## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Rearrange the formula to make $p$ the subject.

$$
pr = \frac{6p^{2}-1}{p}
$$

[2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
pr = \frac{6p^{2}-1}{p}
$$

$$
p^{2}(6-r) = 1
$$

$$
p = \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{6-r}}
$$
