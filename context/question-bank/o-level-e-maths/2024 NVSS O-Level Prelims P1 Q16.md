## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 2, (b)(ii) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 10

**(a)** Expand and simplify $(2x+3y)(7x-5y)$.

**(b)** Factorise completely.

**(i)** $x^{3}y^{3}-xy^{3}$

**(ii)** $5ax-3ay-10cx+6cy$

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $(2x+3y)(7x-5y)=14x^{2}-10xy+21xy-15y^{2}$ M1

$=14x^{2}+11xy-15y^{2}$ A1

**(b)** **(i)** $x^{3}y^{3}-xy^{3}=xy^{3}(x^{2}-1)$ B1

$=xy^{3}(x+1)(x-1)$ B1

**(ii)** $5ax-3ay-10cx+6cy=a(5x-3y)-2c(5x-3y)$ M1

$=(5x-3y)(a-2c)$ A1
