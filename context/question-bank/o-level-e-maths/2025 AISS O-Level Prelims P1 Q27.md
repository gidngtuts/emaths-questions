## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 20

$OAC$ is a triangle and $B$ is a point on $OC$.
$\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OB}=\mathbf{b}$ and $OB:BC=2:3$.
$X$ is the point on $AB$ such that $AX:XB=1:2$.

**(a)** Express $\overrightarrow{AC}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible. [2]

**(b)** Express $\overrightarrow{XB}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible. [2]

**(c)** $Y$ is the point on $OC$ such that $AXYC$ is a trapezium.
Find, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, $\overrightarrow{XY}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Note: 1 mark is deducted from the overall mark of this question if students did not use proper vector notations.

$OAC$ is a triangle and $B$ is a point on $OC$. $\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OB}=\mathbf{b}$ and $OB:BC=2:3$. $X$ is the point on $AB$ such that $AX:XB=1:2$.

**(a)** Express $\overrightarrow{AC}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible.

$$
\overrightarrow{OC}=2.5\mathbf{b}
$$

M1 for correct $\overrightarrow{OC}$ in terms of $\mathbf{b}$.

$$
\overrightarrow{AC}=2.5\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}
$$

A1.

**(b)** Express $\overrightarrow{XB}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible.

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}
$$

M1 for correct $\overrightarrow{AB}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$.

$$
\overrightarrow{XB}=\frac{2}{3}(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})
$$

A1.

**(c)** $Y$ is the point on $OC$ such that $AXYC$ is a trapezium. Find, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, $\overrightarrow{XY}$.

For $AXYC$ to be a trapezium, triangle $BXY$ is similar to triangle $BAC$.

$$
\frac{XY}{AC}=\frac{BX}{BA}=\frac{2}{3}
$$

M1 for identifying triangle $BXY$ to be similar to triangle $BAC$.

$$
\overrightarrow{XY}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}=\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac{5}{2}\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}\right)=\frac{5}{3}\mathbf{b}-\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}
$$

A1.
