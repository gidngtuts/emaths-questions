## Question

> **Page:** 3

Simplify $x^{3}-x(x+2)(x-2)$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Simplify $x^{3}-x(x+2)(x-2)$.

$$x^{3}-x(x+2)(x-2)$$

$$=x^{3}-x(x^{2}-4)$$

$$=x^{3}-x^{3}+4x$$

$$=4x$$
