## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 15

$$
\sin\left(\frac{1}{2}x^{\circ}\right)=0.961
$$

Find two possible values of $x$ in the range $0\leq x\leq 360$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\sin\left(\frac{1}{2}x^{\circ}\right)=0.961
$$

Find two possible values of $x$ in the range $0\leq x\leq 360$.

$$
\frac{1}{2}x^{\circ}=73.946^{\circ}\quad\text{or}\quad 180^{\circ}-73.946^{\circ}
$$

$$
x=147.9\quad\text{or}\quad x=212.1
$$

B1, B1.

Note: 1 mark is deducted if both answers are left to 3 significant figures. Students are to follow instructions on the cover page.
