## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 9

**(a)** Express $7-10x+x^{2}$ in the form $(x+a)^{2}+b$. [2]

**(b)** Write down the equation of the line of symmetry of the graph of $y=7-10x+x^{2}$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
7-10x+x^{2}=x^{2}-10x+7=(x-5)^{2}-5^{2}+7=(x-5)^{2}-18
$$

**(b)** $x=5$
