## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 14

The revenue $y$ (in dollars) of a company for selling $x$ items is given by $y=80x-x^{2}$ for $0\leq x\leq 80$.

**(a)** Sketch the graph of $y=80x-x^{2}$ for $0\leq x\leq 80$, showing its turning point, $x$ and $y$-intercepts. [2]

![Question 15 axes](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/MGS/images/MGS-Prelim-2025-P1-pages/page-14.png)

**(b)** "The company claims that it is possible for them to make a revenue of &#36;2000."
Using your answer in (a), determine if the statement above is correct. [1]

## Answer Scheme

The revenue $y$ (in dollars) for selling $x$ items is given by $y=80x-x^{2}$ for $0\leq x\leq 80$.

**(a)** Sketch the graph of $y=80x-x^{2}$ for $0\leq x\leq 80$, showing its turning point, $x$ and $y$-intercepts.

3 m for correct shape and all 2 intercepts and turning point.
2 m for missing 1 of the above.
1 m for missing 2 of the above.

![Question 15 sketch](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/MGS/images/MGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-MS-pages/page-14.png)

**(b)** "Peter claims that it is possible for him to make a revenue of &#36;2000."
Using your answer in (a), determine if Peter's statement is correct.
