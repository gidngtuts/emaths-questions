## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [3]; (c) [3]; total [8]
> **Page:** 4–5

**(a)** A investor deposits &#36;18 000 with a finance company paying compound interest on a monthly basis at $6\%$ per annum.

Calculate the interest received at the end of 5 years, correct to 2 decimal places. [2]

**(b)** The price of a washing machine is &#36;900 before discount. After selling the machine at a $20\%$ discount, the seller still makes a profit of $25\%$.

Calculate the cost price of the washing machine. [3]

**(c)** The price of a gaming laptop is &#36;2 999. John buys this laptop on hire purchase after putting a down payment of &#36;600. For the remaining amount of the laptop, John has to pay the seller a monthly instalment of &#36;120 over 2 years.

Calculate the interest rate charged by the seller. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** A investor deposits SGD 18 000 with a finance company paying compound interest on a monthly basis at $6\%$ per annum.

Calculate the interest received at the end of 5 years, correct to 2 decimal places.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total Amount}&=18000\left(1+\dfrac{6\div 12}{100}\right)^{5\times 12}\\
&=24279.30\\
\text{Interest}&=\text{Total Amount}-\text{Principal}\\
&=24279.30-18000\\
&=SGD 6279.30
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** **A1**

**(b)** The price of a washing machine is SGD 900 before discount. After selling the machine at a $20\%$ discount, the seller still makes a profit of $25\%$.

Calculate the cost price of the washing machine.

Let $x$ be the cost price of the washing machine.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Selling price of washing machine}&=900\times 80\%\\
&=720\\
\text{Percentage profit}&=\dfrac{\text{Selling price}-x}{x}\times 100\%\\
25&=\left(\dfrac{720-x}{x}\right)\times 100\\
1&=4\left(\dfrac{720-x}{x}\right)\\
x&=2880-4x\\
5x&=2880\\
x&=SGD 576
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** for selling price of washing machine. **M1** **A1**

**(c)** The price of a gaming laptop is SGD 2 999. John buys this laptop on hire purchase after putting a down payment of SGD 600. For the remaining amount of the laptop, John has to pay the seller a monthly instalment of SGD 120 over 2 years.

Calculate the interest rate charged by the seller.

$$
\begin{aligned}
P,\ \text{remaining amount owed to seller}&=SGD 2999-SGD 600\\
&=SGD 2399\\
\text{Total amount paid to seller over 2 years}&=24\times SGD 120\\
&=SGD 2880\\
\text{Interest earned by seller}&=SGD 2880-SGD 2399\\
&=SGD 481\\
I&=\dfrac{PRT}{100}\\
481&=\dfrac{2399\times R\times 2}{100}\\
R&=10.025\\
&=10.0\%
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** for finding interest. **M1** apply formula. **A1**
