## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Expand and simplify $a(5a-2b)(5a+2b)$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
a(5a-2b)(5a+2b)
$$

$$
= a\left((5a)^{2}-(2b)^{2}\right)
$$

$$
= a(25a^{2}-4b^{2})
$$

M1 for the correct expansion.

$$
= 25a^{3}-4ab^{2}
$$
