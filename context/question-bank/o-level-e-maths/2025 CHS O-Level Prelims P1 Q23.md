## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 22

$ABCD$ is a square and angle $EDC =$ angle $ECD = 55^{\circ}$.

![Question 23 square](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CHS/images/CAT-HIGH-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-22.png)

Prove that triangle $AEB$ is an isosceles triangle. [4]

## Answer Scheme

Since $\angle EDC = \angle ECD = 55^{\circ}$, $\triangle EDC$ is isosceles, $ED = EC$.

$$
\angle ADE = \angle BCE = 90^{\circ}-55^{\circ} = 35^{\circ}
$$

$AD = BC$ (sides of square).

$\triangle ADE \equiv \triangle BCE$ (SAS).

Therefore $AE = BE$, so $\triangle AEB$ is an isosceles triangle.
