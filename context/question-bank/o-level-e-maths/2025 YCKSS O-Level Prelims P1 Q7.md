## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

In this sequence, the difference between any two consecutive terms is the same number.

$$
w \quad x \quad y \quad 23 \quad z \quad \ldots
$$

The sum of the first five terms is 80. Find the values of $w$, $x$, $y$ and $z$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

In this sequence, the difference between any two consecutive terms is the same number. The sum of the first five terms is 80. Find the values of $w$, $x$, $y$ and $z$.

$$
(23-3d)+(23-2d)+(23-d)+23+(23+d)=80
$$

$$
-5d+115=80
$$

$$
d=7
$$

$$
w=2,\quad x=9,\quad y=16,\quad z=30
$$

Deduct 1 mark for each error.
