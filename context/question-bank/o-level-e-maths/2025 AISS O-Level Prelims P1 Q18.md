## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 14

Alice borrowed a sum of money from the bank which charges a compound interest of $3.5\%$ per annum, compounded quarterly.
Given that Alice had to pay &#36;67.30 in interest at the end of the first year, find the original sum of money borrowed, giving your answer correct to the nearest dollar. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Alice borrowed a sum of money from the bank which charges a compound interest of $3.5\%$ per annum, compounded quarterly. Given that Alice had to pay &#36;67.30 in interest at the end of the first year, find the original sum of money borrowed, giving your answer correct to the nearest dollar.

Let the original sum of money Alice borrowed be &#36;P.

$$
P+67.30=P\left(1+\frac{0.875}{100}\right)^{4}
$$

M1 for setting up the correct equation.

$$
67.30=P\left[\left(1+\frac{0.875}{100}\right)^{4}-1\right]
$$

M1 for factorising, only awarded if the original set-up has total $=$ principal $+$ interest.

$$
P=\frac{67.30}{\left(1+\dfrac{0.875}{100}\right)^{4}-1}=&#36;1898
$$

(nearest dollar). A1.
