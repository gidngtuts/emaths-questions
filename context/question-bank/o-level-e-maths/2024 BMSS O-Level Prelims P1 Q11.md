## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 11

**(a)** Solve the inequality

$$
x-7\leq\frac{3x-5}{2}<8.
$$

Represent your solution on the number line below.

The number line is marked from $-10$ to $8$ in steps of $2$. [3]

**(b)** Hence, write down the largest prime number that satisfy the inequality

$$
x-7\leq\frac{3x-5}{2}<8.
$$

[1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
x-7\leq\frac{3x-5}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{3x-5}{2}<8
$$

$$
2x-14\leq 3x-5\implies -9\leq x
$$

[M1]

$$
3x-5<16\implies x<7
$$

[M1]

Hence $-9\leq x<7$. Number line: closed circle at $-9$, open circle at $7$, and the segment between them. [A1]

**(b)** Largest prime number $x=5$ [A1]
