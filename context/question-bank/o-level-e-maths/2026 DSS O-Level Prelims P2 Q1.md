## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 2, (c) 2, (d)(i) 2, (d)(ii) 3; total 12
> **Page:** 4–5

**(a)** Find all the integers that satisfy the inequality $5\leq 2x-7<11$. [3]

**(b)** Factorise $80x^{2}-45y^{2}$. [2]

**(c)** Solve $\dfrac{18}{5-3x}=4$. [2]

**(d) (i)** Express $(x-3)^{2}-4$ in the form $(x-a)(x-b)$. [2]

**(ii)** Sketch the graph of $y=(x-3)^{2}-4$ on the axes below.
Indicate clearly the coordinates of the points where the graph crosses the axes and the turning point. [3]

![Question 1 sketch axes](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/DSS/images/Dunman-2026-Emaths-Prelims-Paper-2-pages/page-05.png)

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
5\leq 2x-7\text{ and }2x-7<11
$$

$$
2x\geq 12,\quad 2x<18
$$

$$
x\geq 6,\quad x<9
$$

$$
6\leq x<9
$$

The integers are $6$, $7$ and $8$.

[M1, M1, A1]

**(b)**

$$
80x^{2}-45y^{2}=5(16x^{2}-9y^{2})=5(4x-3y)(4x+3y)
$$

[M1, A1]

**(c)** Rewrite to a non-fractional equation.

$$
\frac{18}{5-3x}=4
$$

$$
18=20-12x
$$

$$
12x=2,\quad x=\frac{1}{6}
$$

[M1, A1]

Do not accept $x=0.167$ or $0.1666$. Can accept $x=0.1\dot{6}$.

**(d) (i)** Show expansion and simplify to the general form of a quadratic expression. If no working step is shown, no marks.

$$
(x-3)^{2}-4=x^{2}-6x+9-4=x^{2}-6x+5=(x-1)(x-5)
$$

[M1, A1]
