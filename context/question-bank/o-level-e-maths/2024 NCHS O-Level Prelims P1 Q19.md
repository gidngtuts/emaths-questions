## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [1]
> **Page:** 16

The pie chart shows the results of votes for Student Council President election.

![Pie chart titled Candidate C received overwhelming support, with slices 40 percent, 35 percent and 20 percent](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NCHS/images/2024-NCHS-Prelim-Math-P1-QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Give one reason to explain why the pie chart shown is considered misleading.

**(b)** Propose one way that you could improve the statistical diagram.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $40\%+35\%+20\% = 95\%$.

As pie charts are used to visualize parts of a whole, all the parts should always add up to 100%.

Or the title exaggerated the responses of the support for Candidate C.

**(b)** To include uncounted / spoiled votes (5%) to show the full result of votes.

Or to re-calculate the votes according to the three candidates according to a base of 95%.

Or to recraft the title to be less bias and allow readers to analysis themselves.
