## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 11

By expressing $x^{2}-10x-2$ in the form $(x-h)^{2}+k$, where $h$ and $k$ are constants, write down the coordinates of the minimum point of the graph of $y=x^{2}-10x-2$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
x^{2}-10x-2
= (x-5)^{2}-25-2
= (x-5)^{2}-27
$$

The minimum point is $(5,-27)$.
