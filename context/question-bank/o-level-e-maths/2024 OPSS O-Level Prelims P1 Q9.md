## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 9

A set of Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) taken from different parts of Singapore is as follows.

28, 30, 48, 19, 70, 50, 32, 72.

Represent the data using a box-and-whisker plot.

## Answer Scheme

Ordered data: 19, 28, 30, 32, 48, 50, 70, 72.

Median $=\dfrac{32+48}{2}=40$

Lower quartile $=\dfrac{28+30}{2}=29$

Upper quartile $=\dfrac{50+70}{2}=60$

Box-and-whisker plot from 19 to 72, with the box from 29 to 60 and the median at 40.
