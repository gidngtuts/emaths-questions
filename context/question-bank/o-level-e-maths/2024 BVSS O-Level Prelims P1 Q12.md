## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

The following data shows the masses of 11 parcels in kg.

$$
1.8\quad 2.1\quad 2.4\quad 2.5\quad 2.6\quad 3.2\quad 3.3\quad 3.5\quad 3.8\quad 4.0\quad 4.9
$$

(a) Calculate the mean mass. [1]

(b) Find the median mass. [1]

(c) Two parcels (the $2.4\text{ kg}$ and the $4.9\text{ kg}$) were removed from the delivery van. Explain why the median mass remains unchanged. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\frac{2.6+3.2+2.4+3.3+1.8+4.0+4.9+3.5+3.8+2.5+2.1}{11} = 3.1 \quad\text{B1}
$$

Mean mass is $3.1\text{ kg}$.

(b) Ordered list:

$$
1.8,\ 2.1,\ 2.4,\ 2.5,\ 2.6,\ 3.2,\ 3.3,\ 3.5,\ 3.8,\ 4.0,\ 4.9 \quad\text{B1}
$$

The median is the middle value, $3.2\text{ kg}$.

(c) The two masses fall on the left and right of the median value, keeping the median weight unchanged in the middle. B1.
