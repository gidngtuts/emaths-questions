## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 16

$ABCDE$ is part of a regular polygon which has an exterior angle of $20^{\circ}$. $CDLM$ is a square.

![Regular polygon ABCDE with square CDLM](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/WGSS/images/WGSS_2024_Prelim_EMath_4052-1-_QP-pages/page-16.png)

Find

(a) the value of $x$, [2]

(b) the value of $y$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\angle BCD = 180-20 = 160$ [M1]

$x = 160-90 = 70$ [A1]

(b) $\angle MBC = \dfrac{180-70}{2} = 55$ [M1]

$y = 160-55 = 105$ [A1]
