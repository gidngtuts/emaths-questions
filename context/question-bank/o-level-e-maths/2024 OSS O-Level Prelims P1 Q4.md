## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1, (c) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 4

The number $A=2^{7}\times 5^{11}\times 7^{8}$.

**(a)** Prove that $A$ is divisible by 245.

**(b)** The number $A\times k$ is a perfect square.
Find the smallest possible integer value of $k$.

**(c)** The number $B=2^{5}\times 3^{6}\times 7^{3}$.
Find the highest common factor (HCF) and lowest common multiple (LCM) of $A$ and $B$ as a product of its prime factors.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $245=5\times 7^{2}$, which is a factor of $A$.

**(b)** $k=2\times 5=10$

**(c)** $\operatorname{HCF}=2^{5}\times 7^{3}$

$\operatorname{LCM}=2^{7}\times 3^{6}\times 5^{11}\times 7^{8}$
