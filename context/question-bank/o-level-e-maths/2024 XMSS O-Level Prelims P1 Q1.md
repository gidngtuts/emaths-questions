## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Evaluate

(a) $\dfrac{8.75}{\sqrt{4.86+0.982}}$, correct to 2 decimal places, [1]

(b) $(5.87\times 10^{6})\div(3.94\times 10^{-2})$, leaving your answer in standard form, correct to 3 significant figures. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\dfrac{8.75}{\sqrt{4.86+0.982}} = 2.7459 = 2.75$ [B1]

(b) $(5.87\times 10^{6})\div(3.94\times 10^{-2}) = 1.4898\times 10^{8} = 1.49\times 10^{8}$ [B1]
