## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 2, (d) 1, (e) 4; total 9
> **Page:** 16–17

A bakery makes cakes.
The bakery operates for $6$ days each week.
The matrix, $\mathbf{M}$, shows the number of cakes of different types that are made each day.

$$
\mathbf{M}=\begin{pmatrix} 8 & 5 & 2 \\ 6 & 4 & 3 \end{pmatrix}
\begin{matrix} \text{Chocolate} \\ \text{Blackforest} \end{matrix}
$$

Columns are Small, Medium and Large.

**(a)** Evaluate the matrix $\mathbf{P}=6\mathbf{M}$. [1]

**(b)** Small cakes cost &#36;10 to make.
Medium cakes cost &#36;15 to make.
Large cakes cost &#36;20 to make.
Represent these amounts in a $3\times 1$ matrix $\mathbf{N}$. [1]

**(c)** Evaluate the matrix $\mathbf{T}=\mathbf{PN}$. [2]

**(d)** State what each of the elements of $\mathbf{T}$ represents. [1]

**(e)** The bakery sells each cake for $70\%$ more than it costs to make.
One week they sold $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of each size of the chocolate cakes and $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of each size of the blackforest cakes made that week.
The unsold cakes were given away.

Calculate the total amount of profit the bakery made that week. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\mathbf{P}=\begin{pmatrix} 48 & 30 & 12 \\ 36 & 24 & 18 \end{pmatrix}
$$

B1.

**(b)**

$$
\mathbf{N}=\begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 15 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix}
$$

B1.

**(c)**

$$
\mathbf{T}=\begin{pmatrix} 48 & 30 & 12 \\ 36 & 24 & 18 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 10 \\ 15 \\ 20 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1170 \\ 1080 \end{pmatrix}
$$

B1 for $1170$. B1 for $1080$.

**(d)** &#36;1170 represents the total cost of making the chocolate cakes in a week, while &#36;1080 represents the total cost of making the blackforest cakes in a week. B1.

Key points required: the numbers $1170$ and $1080$; units (&#36; or “in dollars”); “cost”; duration “in a week” or “six days”.

**(e)** Cakes sold that week:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 20 & 8 \\ 30 & 20 & 15 \end{pmatrix}
$$

M1 for the number of cakes of each type and size sold.

Sale price:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 17 \\ 25.50 \\ 34 \end{pmatrix}
$$

M1 for the sale price of each size.

$$
\begin{pmatrix} 32 & 20 & 8 \\ 30 & 20 & 15 \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} 17 \\ 25.50 \\ 34 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 1326 \\ 1530 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Total sales $=1326+1530=2856$.

$$
\text{Total profit}=2856-2250=&#36;606
$$

A1.

Alternative: $\dfrac{2}{3}(1170)(1.7)+\dfrac{5}{6}(1080)(1.7)=1326+1530=2856$, so profit $=&#36;606$.
