## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 1, (d) 2, (e) 1, (f) 2; total 8
> **Page:** 10–11

Salim owns three cafes $A$, $B$ and $C$ which sell burgers and salad.
The cafes operate 7 days a week. The sales on a weekend are on average half of that on a weekday.

**(a)** Suggest a reason why sales on a weekend are lower than sales on a weekday. [1]

The matrix, $F$, show the average number of food items that are sold on a weekday.

$$
F=\begin{pmatrix} 120 & 11 \\ 12 & 135 \\ 80 & 92 \end{pmatrix}
\begin{matrix} \text{Cafe } A \\ \text{Cafe } B \\ \text{Cafe } C \end{matrix}
$$

Columns are Burger and Salad, in that order.

**(b)** Evaluate the matrix $W=6F$. [1]

**(c)** In all of his three cafes, each burger costs SGD 12 and each plate of salad costs SGD 8.
Represent the costs of the food items in a $2 \times 1$ column matrix $C$. [1]

**(d)** Evaluate the matrix $R=WC$. [2]

**(e)** State what each of the elements of $R$ represents. [1]

**(f)** All the food items are sold for $40\%$ profit.
By writing down a row matrix $T$, find $TR$ which gives the total amount of profit all three cafes made in a week. [2]
