## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c)(i) 2, (c)(ii) 2, (d) 1; total 10
> **Page:** 14–15

$ABCD$ is a parallelogram. $A$ is the point $(-6, -2)$ and $B$ is the point $(-4, -7)$.
$\overrightarrow{BC}=\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$.

**(a)** Find the length of the line $AB$.

**(b)** Find the equation of the line $CD$.

**(c)** $X$ is the point where the diagonals of the parallelogram intersect.

**(i)** Find $\overrightarrow{XC}$.

**(ii)** Find the position vector of $X$.

**(d)** $P$ is the point on $BC$ such that $\dfrac{BP}{PC}=\dfrac{2}{1}$.
Find the ratio of the area of $\triangle ABP$ to the area of $\triangle ACP$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $AB=\sqrt{(-6-(-4))^{2}+(-2-(-7))^{2}}=\sqrt{(-2)^{2}+5^{2}}=5.39$

**(b)** $m_{CD}=m_{AB}=\dfrac{-2-(-7)}{-6-(-4)}=-\dfrac{5}{2}$

$\overrightarrow{OC}=\overrightarrow{BC}+\overrightarrow{OB}=\begin{pmatrix} 8 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ -7 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -9 \end{pmatrix}$, so $C(4, -9)$

$-9=-2.5(4)+c$, so $c=1$

$y=-\dfrac{5}{2}x+1$, or $2y=-5x+2$

**(c)** **(i)** $\overrightarrow{XC}=\dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AC}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -9 \end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix} -6 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}\right)=\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ -\dfrac{7}{2} \end{pmatrix}$

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{OX}=\overrightarrow{OC}-\overrightarrow{XC}=\begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -9 \end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ -\dfrac{7}{2} \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -\dfrac{11}{2} \end{pmatrix}$

**(d)** $\dfrac{2}{1}$, or $2:1$
