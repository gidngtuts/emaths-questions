## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 1, (d) 1; total 5
> **Page:** 19–20

In Bakery $A$, a chicken pie costs \$2.50, apple pie costs \$2.00 and a taro pie costs \$1.80. In Bakery $B$'s a chicken pie costs \$0.30 less, \$0.20 more for apple pie and \$0.10 more for taro pie.

This information can be represented by the matrix $\mathbf{X}$.

![Question 22 matrix X](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/AHS/images/AHS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-19.png)

**(a)** Peter wants to buy 5 chicken, 4 apple and 2 taro pies. Jane wants to buy 4 chicken and 7 apple pies. Represent their purchases in a $2\times 3$ matrix $\mathbf{Y}$. [1]

**(b)** Evaluate the matrix $\mathbf{W}=\mathbf{YX}$. [2]

**(c)** What does the last column of $\mathbf{W}$ represent? [1]

**(d)** Find the amount that Peter and Jane should spend in Bakery B. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Represent their purchases in a $2\times 3$ matrix $\mathbf{Y}$.

$$
\mathbf{Y}=\begin{pmatrix}5&4&2\\4&7&0\end{pmatrix}
$$

**B1**

**(b)** Evaluate the matrix $\mathbf{W}=\mathbf{YX}$.

$$
\mathbf{W}=\begin{pmatrix}5&4&2\\4&7&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2.5&-0.3\\2.0&0.2\\1.8&0.1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}24.1&-0.5\\24&0.2\end{pmatrix}
$$

**M1** form equation. **A1**

**(c)** What does the last column of $\mathbf{W}$ represent?

The last column represents the savings for Peter and extra cost for Jane if they buy from Bakery B.

**B1**

**(d)** Find the amount that Peter and Jane would spend in Bakery B.

$$
\begin{pmatrix}24.1&-0.5\\24&0.2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}23.6\\24.2\end{pmatrix}
$$

Peter would spend &#36;23.60 buying from Bakery B. Jane would spend &#36;24.20 buying from Bakery B.

**B1**
