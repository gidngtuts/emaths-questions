## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 3, (d) 3; total 12
> **Page:** 3–5

**(a)** Solve the simultaneous equations.

$$
4m+6n = 58
$$

$$
3m+5n = 46
$$

[3]

**(b)** Write as a single fraction in the simplest form $\dfrac{3x}{2x^{2}-50}-\dfrac{1}{x-5}$. [3]

**(c)** $x = \sqrt[3]{\dfrac{25y}{7z+2}}$

**(i)** Find $x$ when $y = 25$ and $z = -1$. [1]

**(ii)** Rearrange the formula to make $y$ the subject. [2]

**(d)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{2x+3}{(3x+1)(x-1)}-2 = 0$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
4m+6n = 58 \quad (1)
$$

$$
3m+5n = 46 \quad (2)
$$

$$
12m+18n = 174 \quad (3)
$$

$$
12m+20n = 184 \quad (4)
$$

$(3)-(4)$: $-2n = -10$, so $n = 5$ and $m = 7$.

**(b)**

$$
\frac{3x}{2x^{2}-50}-\frac{1}{x-5} = \frac{3x}{2(x-5)(x+5)}-\frac{1}{x-5} = \frac{3x-2(x+5)}{2(x-5)(x+5)} = \frac{x-10}{2(x-5)(x+5)}
$$

**(c)(i)** $-5$.

**(c)(ii)**

$$
x = \sqrt[3]{\frac{25y}{7z+2}}
$$

$$
x^{3} = \frac{25y}{7z+2}, \quad x^{3}(7z+2) = 25y, \quad y = \frac{x^{3}(7z+2)}{25}
$$

**(d)**

$$
\frac{2x+3}{(3x+1)(x-1)}-2 = 0
$$

$$
\frac{2x+3}{(3x+1)(x-1)} = 2
$$

$$
2x+3 = 2(3x+1)(x-1) = 6x^{2}-4x-2
$$

$$
6x^{2}-6x-5 = 0
$$

$$
x = 1.54 \text{ or } -0.541
$$
