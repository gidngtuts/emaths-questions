## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{4}{3-2y}-\dfrac{5}{y+3}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{4}{3-2y}-\dfrac{5}{y+3}$.

$$
\frac{4}{3-2y}-\frac{5}{y+3}=\frac{4(y+3)-5(3-2y)}{(3-2y)(y+3)}
$$

M1 for combining to a single fraction.

$$
=\frac{14y-3}{(3-2y)(y+3)}
$$

A1.
