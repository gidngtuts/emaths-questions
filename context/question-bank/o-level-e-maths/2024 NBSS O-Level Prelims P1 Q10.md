## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 8

Scale: 1 cm to 5 km.

![Scale drawing of rock bed R, ship A, port B and course AB, with north](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-_Prelim_P1_2024-v2_QP-pages/page-08.png)

In the scale drawing, $R$ is the centre of a rock bed, $A$ is a ship and $B$ is a port.
The line $AB$ shows the ship’s course.

**(a)** Measure the bearing of $B$ from $A$.

**(b)** The rock bed covers a 25 km radius.

If the ship were to stay on course, state the distance at which the ship will first hit the rock bed.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $310^{\circ}\pm 1^{\circ}$ (accepted $309^{\circ}$ to $311^{\circ}$).

**(b)** Construct a 5 cm radius. Accepted answers: 4 km, 4.5 km or 5 km.
