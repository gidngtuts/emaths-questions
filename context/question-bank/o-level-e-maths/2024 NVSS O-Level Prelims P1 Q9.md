## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

A bag contains some yellow and blue balls.
The ratio of the yellow balls to the blue balls is $1:4$.
5 yellow balls are removed from the bag and 10 blue balls are added to the bag.
The new ratio of yellow balls to blue balls is $1:6$.
Find the original number of yellow balls in the bag.

## Answer Scheme

Let the number of yellow balls be $x$. Number of blue balls is $4x$.

$\dfrac{x-5}{4x+10}=\dfrac{1}{6}$ M1

$6(x-5)=4x+10$

$6x-30=4x+10$ M1

$2x=40$, $x=20$

Number of yellow balls $=20$ A1

Other accepted methods include the ratio chains $1:4 \to 20:80 \to 15:90 \to 1:6$, and the unit method $1:4$ scaled to $4:16$ then $3:18$ with 1 unit $=5$, so 4 units $=20$.
