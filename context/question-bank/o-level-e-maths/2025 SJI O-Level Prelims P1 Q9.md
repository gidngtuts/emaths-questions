## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 8

The number $N$ is a multiple of 18 and is both a perfect square and a perfect cube.
Find the smallest possible value of $N$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
18=2\times 3^{2}
$$

$$
N=(2\times 3)^{6}=46\,656
$$
