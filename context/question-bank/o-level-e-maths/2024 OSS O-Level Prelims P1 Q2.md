## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{g^{12}}{256f^{6}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{4}}$.

**(b)** $5\times 9^{k-1}=15\times 81$. Find the value of $k$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\left(\dfrac{256f^{6}}{g^{12}}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{4f^{\frac{3}{2}}}{g^{3}}$

**(b)** $5\times 3^{2(k-1)}=3\times 5\times 3^{4}$

$3^{2(k-1)}=3^{5}$, so $2(k-1)=5$ and $k=3.5$
