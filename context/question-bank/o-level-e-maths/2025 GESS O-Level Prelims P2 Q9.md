## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 4, (b) 2, (c) 1, (d) 1; total 8
> **Page:** 22–23

Ali and Robert participated in the $42\text{ km}$ running event organised by SC Company.
Ali completed the race with an average speed of $x\text{ km/h}$. Robert completed the race at an average speed of $2\text{ km/h}$ slower than Ali.

**(a)** Given that Robert completed the race 30 minutes later than Ali, form an equation in $x$ and show that it reduces to $x^{2}-2x-168=0$. [4]

**(b)** Solve the equation $x^{2}-2x-168=0$. [2]

**(c)** Explain why one of the solutions in (b) must be rejected. [1]

**(d)** Calculate the time taken for Robert to complete the race. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\text{Time taken by Ali}=\frac{42}{x}\text{ h}
$$

$$
\text{Time taken by Robert}=\frac{42}{x-2}\text{ h}
$$

M1 for $\dfrac{42}{x}$ and $\dfrac{42}{x-2}$ seen.

$$
\frac{42}{x-2}-\frac{42}{x}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{42x-42(x-2)}{x(x-2)}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{84}{x(x-2)}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
x(x-2)=168
$$

$$
x^{2}-2x-168=0
$$

**(b)**

$$
x^{2}-2x-168=0
$$

$$
(x+12)(x-14)=0
$$

$$
x=-12\text{ or }x=14
$$

Accept by formula method.

**(c)** Since $x$ represents speed, $x$ cannot be negative.

**(d)**

$$
\text{Time taken}=\frac{42}{14-2}=3.5\text{ h}
$$
