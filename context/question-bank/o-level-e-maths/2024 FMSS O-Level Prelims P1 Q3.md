## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Tristen has written five positive integers.
The median of these numbers is $8$, the mode is $7$ and the mean is $13$.
The range of these numbers is $21$.

Find the five numbers. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$7, 7, 8, 15, 28$.

B1 for $7, 7, 8$. B1 for $15, 28$.
