## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 11

$\varepsilon = \{\text{integer } x : 0 < x \leq 12\}$

$A = \{\text{prime numbers}\}$

$B = \{\text{numbers that have at least 2 distinct factors}\}$

**(a)** Explain why $A$ is a proper subset of $B$ without listing down the elements.

**(b)** Draw a Venn diagram and list down the elements to illustrate the above information.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Prime numbers have $2$ distinct factors, therefore every element of set $A$ must also be an element of set $B$. However, some elements in $B$ are composites and therefore not in set $A$. Thus $A$ is a proper subset of $B$.

**(b)** $\mathcal{E} = \{1,2,\ldots,12\}$. Outside $A$ and $B$: $1$. In $B$ but not $A$: $4, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12$. In $A$ (and hence in $B$): $2, 3, 5, 7, 11$.
