## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 14

(a) $\dfrac{2^{a}\times\left(\sqrt[3]{64}\right)^{c}}{16^{b}} = 1$.

Find an expression for $a$ in terms of $b$ and $c$. [2]

(b) Simplify $\left(\dfrac{27y^{12}}{x^{6}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\sqrt[3]{64} = 4$, so $\dfrac{2^{a}\times 4^{c}}{16^{b}} = 1$, hence $2^{a}\times (2^{2})^{c} = (2^{4})^{b}$, $2^{a+2c} = 2^{4b}$ and $a = 4b-2c$. [2]

(b) $\left(\dfrac{27y^{12}}{x^{6}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \left(\dfrac{x^{6}}{27y^{12}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} = \dfrac{x^{2}}{3y^{4}}$. [2]
