## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Solve the equation $5+2x = \dfrac{20}{1+x}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
5+2x = \frac{20}{1+x}
$$

$$
(5+2x)(1+x) = 20
$$

$$
5+5x+2x+2x^{2} = 20
$$

$$
2x^{2}+7x-15 = 0
$$

$$
(2x-3)(x+5) = 0
$$

$$
x = 1.5\text{ or }x = -5
$$

M1, M1, A1 for both answers.
