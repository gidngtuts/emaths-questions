### Q10

> **Marks:** 3
> **Sub-topic:** Statistics
> **Page:** 7

Let the number of female athletes be $x$. Then the number of male athletes is $x+6$.

$$
60.8x+80.4(x+6)=72(x+x+6)
$$

$$
60.8x+80.4x+482.4=144x+432
$$

$$
-2.8x=-50.4
$$

$$
x=\frac{-50.4}{-2.8}=18
$$

Total number of athletes $=18+(18+6)=42$.

Alternative solution: let the number of male athletes be $x$. Then the number of female athletes is $x-6$.

$$
60.8(x-6)+80.4x=72(x+x-6)
$$

$$
60.8x-364.8+80.4x=144x-432
$$

$$
-2.8x=-67.2
$$

$$
x=\frac{-67.2}{-2.8}=24
$$

Total number of athletes $=24+(24-6)=42$.
