## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [3]; (b) [3]; (c) [3]
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Simplify $\dfrac{9a^{2}-4b^{2}}{3ap+6aq-4bq-2bp}$.

**(b)** Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{x}{6-7x-5x^{2}}+\dfrac{2}{5x-3}$.

**(c)** Solve the inequality $-\dfrac{1}{2} < \dfrac{2y}{5}-\dfrac{1+y}{3} \le \dfrac{5}{6}$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{9a^{2}-4b^{2}}{3ap+6aq-4bq-2bp} &= \frac{(3a+2b)(3a-2b)}{(3ap+6aq)-(2bp+4bq)} \\
&= \frac{(3a+2b)(3a-2b)}{3a(p+2q)-2b(p+2q)} \\
&= \frac{(3a+2b)(3a-2b)}{(p+2q)(3a-2b)} \\
&= \frac{3a+2b}{p+2q}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{6-7x-5x^{2}}+\frac{2}{5x-3} &= \frac{x}{-(5x-3)(x+2)}+\frac{2}{5x-3} \\
&= \frac{-x+2(x+2)}{(5x-3)(x+2)} \\
&= \frac{x+4}{(5x-3)(x+2)}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(c)**

$$
-\frac{1}{2} < \frac{2y}{5}-\frac{1+y}{3} \le \frac{5}{6}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
-\frac{1}{2} &< \frac{3(2y)-5(1+y)}{15} &&\text{and}&& \frac{3(2y)-5(1+y)}{15} \le \frac{5}{6} \\
-\frac{1}{2} &< \frac{y-5}{15} &&\text{and}&& \frac{y-5}{15} \le \frac{5}{6} \\
-15 &< 2(y-5) &&\text{and}&& y-5 \le \frac{25}{2} \\
-5 &< 2y &&\text{and}&& y \le 17\frac{1}{2} \\
-2\frac{1}{2} &< y \le 17\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
