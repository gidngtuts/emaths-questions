interpreting notations:
- $ab$ as $a \times b$
- $3y$ as $y + y + y$ or $3 \times y$
- $3(x + y)$ as $3 \times (x + y)$
- $\frac{a}{b}$ as $a \div b$ or $a \times \frac{1}{b}$
- $\frac{3+y}{5}$ as $(3 + y) \div 5$ or $\frac{1}{5} \times (3 + y)$
- $a^2$ as $a \times a$, $a^3$ as $a \times a \times a$, $a^2b$ as $a \times a \times b$, …
