## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{4-x}{x+2}-\dfrac{2}{3-x}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{4-x}{x+2}-\frac{2}{3-x} = \frac{(4-x)(3-x)-2(x+2)}{(x+2)(3-x)} = \frac{x^{2}-9x+8}{(x+2)(3-x)} = \frac{(x-1)(x-8)}{(x+2)(3-x)}
$$
