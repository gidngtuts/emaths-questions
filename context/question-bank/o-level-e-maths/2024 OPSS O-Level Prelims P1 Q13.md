## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

It is given that $\sqrt{3p^{3}+6r^{2}}=\dfrac{5r}{2}$.

Make $r$ the subject of the equation.

## Answer Scheme

$3p^{3}+6r^{2}=\dfrac{25r^{2}}{4}$

$12p^{3}+24r^{2}=25r^{2}$

$12p^{3}=r^{2}$

$r=\pm\sqrt{12p^{3}}$
