## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 15

12 men will take 54 days to complete a building project.

**(a)** Assuming that the men work at the same rate, how long will it take for 9 men to complete the project? [1]

**(b)** How many days can be saved if 6 more men are added to the team? [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $72$ days.

**(b)** $12$ men will take $54$ days.

$18$ men will take $\dfrac{54\times 12}{18} = 36$ days.

Number of days saved $= (54-36)$ days $= 18$ days.
