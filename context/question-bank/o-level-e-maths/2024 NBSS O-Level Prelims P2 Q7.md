## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [4]; (c) [3]
> **Page:** 17–19

$ABCDEFGH$ is a cuboid with dimensions 8 cm by 6 cm by 15 cm.
$V$ is the centre of the rectangular base.

![Cuboid 8 cm by 6 cm by 15 cm with V the centre of the base](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-2024-Prelim-P2-QP-pages/page-17.png)

**(a)** Show that $EV = 15.8$ cm, correct to 3 significant figures.

**(b)** Calculate angle $ACE$.

**(c)** A pyramid $EDAC$ is cut out from the cuboid.

Find the remaining volume of the cuboid.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $DV = VB = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{6^{2}+8^{2}} = 5$ cm.

$EV = \sqrt{15^{2}+5^{2}} = \sqrt{250} = 15.8$ cm (3 s.f.).

**(b)** Using $EC = 17$ cm, $AE = \sqrt{261}$ cm and $AC = 10$ cm,

$$
\cos\angle ACE = \frac{128}{340},\quad \angle ACE = 67.9^{\circ}\text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$

Using the rounded $EV = 15.8$ cm gives $67.8^{\circ}$.

**(c)** Volume of cuboid $= 8\times 6\times 15 = 720\text{ cm}^{3}$.

Volume of pyramid $= \dfrac{1}{3}\times\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\times 6\times 8\right)\times 15 = 120\text{ cm}^{3}$.

Remaining volume $= 600\text{ cm}^{3}$.
