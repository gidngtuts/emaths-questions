## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 10–11

The diagram shows the cross section of a cylinder, centre $O$, radius $r$, lying on its side. The cylinder contains water to a depth of $18\text{ cm}$. The width, $AB$, of the surface of the water is $24\text{ cm}$. The water surface is a chord $AB$ above the centre, and the shaded region is the water.

![Circular cross-section, chord AB = 24 cm, water depth 18 cm, centre O and bottom point P](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E5N-Prelim-Paper-2-as-on-5-Aug-pages/page-10.png)

(a) Use an algebraic method to show that $r = 13\text{ cm}$. [2]

(b) Show that angle $AOB$ is $134.76^{\circ}$ correct to 2 decimal places. [2]

(c) Hence calculate the area of the shaded segment. [4]

(d) The length of the cylinder is $40\text{ cm}$. The cylinder is turned so that it stands on one of its circular ends. In this position, the depth of the water is $h\text{ cm}$. Find the value of $h$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
(18-r)^{2}+12^{2} = r^{2}
$$

$$
324-36r+r^{2}+144 = r^{2}
$$

$$
36r = 468
$$

$$
r = 13\text{ cm}
$$

M1, A1.

(b)

$$
\text{Angle } AOB = 2\times \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{12}{5}\right) = 134.76^{\circ}
$$

Or $\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C$. M1, A1.

(c) Reflex angle $AOB = 360^{\circ}-134.76^{\circ} = 225.24^{\circ}$.

$$
\text{Area of major sector} = \frac{225.24}{360}\times \pi(13)^{2}
$$

$$
\text{Area of triangle } AOB = \frac{1}{2}\times 13^{2}\times \sin 134.76^{\circ}
$$

$$
\text{Area of segment} = \frac{225.24}{360}\times \pi(13)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\times 13^{2}\times \sin 134.76^{\circ} = 392.18 = 392\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

M1 for the reflex angle. A1 for the major sector. M1 for the triangle. A1.

(d) Volume of water $= 392.18\times 40 = 15687.2\text{ cm}^{3}$.

$$
h = \frac{15687.2}{\pi(13)^{2}} = 29.547\text{ cm} = 29.5\text{ cm}
$$

M1, A1.
