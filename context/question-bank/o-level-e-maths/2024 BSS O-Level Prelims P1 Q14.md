## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 9–10

A company divided a bonus among its employees $A$, $B$, and $C$ in the ratio $2:3:7$.
$C$'s bonus was $X\%$ more than the combined bonus of $A$ and $B$.
Find $X$.

## Answer Scheme

$X=\dfrac{7-5}{5}\times 100\%$ (M1)

$=40\%$ (A1)
