## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) [1]; (a)(ii) [2]; (a)(iii) [2]; (a)(iv) [2]; (b)(i) [1]; (b)(ii) [2]; total [10]
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a) (i)** The number of electric cars produced in country $Y$ by company $X$ in 2015 was $3\,857\,000$. Write this number in standard form. [1]

**(ii)** In 2005, $2.96$ million electric cars were produced in country $Y$. Calculate the percentage increase in the car production from 2005 to 2015. [2]

**(iii)** In 2015, $84.7\%$ of all cars produced in country $Y$ were non-electric cars. Calculate the total number of cars produced in 2015, giving your answer to the nearest million. [2]

**(iv)** In 2015, there were $420$ cars per $1000$ people in country $Y$. Estimate the population of the country in 2015. Give your answer in standard form correct to three significant figures. [2]

**(b)** A design team creates a scale model for a museum exhibit. The diameter of an asteroid is $253$ km. In a scale model, the diameter of the asteroid is $11$ m.

**(i)** Find the scale used for the model. Give your answer in the form $1:n$. [1]

**(ii)** Drawn to the same scale as the asteroid, the diameter of the asteroid's moon is $1.2$ m. Find the actual surface area of the moon, leaving your answer in terms of $\pi$. Assume that the moon is spherical. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)** The number of electric cars produced in country $Y$ by company $X$ in 2015 was $3\,857\,000$. Write this number in standard form.

$$
3.857\times 10^{6}
$$

**(ii)** In 2005, $2.96$ million electric cars were produced in country $Y$. Calculate the percentage increase in the car production from 2005 to 2015.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Percentage increase}&=\dfrac{3.857\times 10^{6}-2.96\times 10^{6}}{2.96\times 10^{6}}\times 100\%\\
&=30.3\%\quad(3\text{ significant figures})
\end{aligned}
$$

Show computation for percentage increase.

**(iii)** In 2015, $84.7\%$ of all cars produced in country $Y$ were non-electric cars. Calculate the total number of cars produced in 2015, giving your answer to the nearest million.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cars in }2015&=\dfrac{3.857\times 10^{6}}{100-84.7}\times 100\\
&=2.5209\times 10^{7}\\
&=25\text{ million cars}\ (\text{nearest million})
\end{aligned}
$$

Or $25\,000\,000$.

**(iv)** In 2015, there were $420$ cars per $1000$ people in country $Y$. Estimate the population of the country in 2015. Give your answer in standard form correct to three significant figures.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Population}&=\dfrac{2.5209\times 10^{7}}{420}\times 1000\\
&=6.00\times 10^{7}\quad(3\text{ significant figures})
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b) (i)** Find the scale used for the model. Give your answer in the form $1:n$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
11\ \mathrm{m}&:253\ \mathrm{km}\\
1\ \mathrm{m}&:23\ \mathrm{km}\\
1&:23000
\end{aligned}
$$

Be careful with conversion to km.

**(ii)** Drawn to the same scale as the asteroid, the diameter of the asteroid's moon is $1.2$ m. Find the actual surface area of the moon, leaving your answer in terms of $\pi$. Assume that the moon is spherical.

$$
\begin{aligned}
1\ \mathrm{m}&:23\ \mathrm{km}\\
1.2\ \mathrm{m}&:27.6\ \mathrm{km}\\
\text{Actual surface area}&=4\pi\left(\dfrac{27.6}{2}\right)^{2}\\
&=761.76\pi\ \mathrm{km}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
