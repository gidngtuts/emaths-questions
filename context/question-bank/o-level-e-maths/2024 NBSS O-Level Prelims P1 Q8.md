## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 6

Raj has 120 one-centimetre cubes.
He arranges all of the cubes into a cuboid.
The perimeter of the top of the cuboid is 20 cm.
Each side of the cuboid has a length greater than 3 cm.

Find the height of the cuboid.

## Answer Scheme

Perimeter of the top is 20 cm, so length $+$ breadth $= 10$.

Pairs with both sides greater than 3 cm: $4$ and $6$, area of top $= 24$, height $= \dfrac{120}{24} = 5$ cm.

$5$ and $5$ is rejected because the solid would be a cube. Sides of 3 cm or less are rejected.
