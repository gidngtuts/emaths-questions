## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 15

**(a)** Write $0.0000000357$ in standard form. [1]

**(b)** Write $5.7\times 10^{97}$ in the form $A\times 10^{98}$. [1]

**(c)** Hence work out $(100.2\times 10^{98})-(5.7\times 10^{97})$.
Give your answer in standard form. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Write $0.0000000357$ in standard form.

$$
3.57\times 10^{-8}
$$

B1.

**(b)** Write $5.7\times 10^{97}$ in the form $A\times 10^{98}$.

$$
0.57\times 10^{98}
$$

B1.

**(c)** Hence work out $(100.2\times 10^{98})-(5.7\times 10^{97})$. Give your answer in standard form.

$$
100.2\times 10^{98}-0.57\times 10^{98}=99.63\times 10^{98}=9.963\times 10^{99}
$$

B1.
