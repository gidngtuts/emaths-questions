## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

Lee has two pieces of string.
Their lengths are in the ratio $5:3$ and the total length of the two pieces of string is $8x$ cm.
Lee cuts $6$ cm from each piece of string.
The ratio of their lengths is now $9:5$.
Find the value of $x$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Lee has two pieces of string. Their lengths are in the ratio $5:3$ and the total length of the two pieces of string is $8x$ cm. Lee cuts $6$ cm from each piece of string. The ratio of their lengths is now $9:5$. Find the value of $x$.

The first piece of string is $5x$ cm.
The second piece of string is $3x$ cm.

$$
\frac{5x-6}{3x-6}=\frac{9}{5}
$$

M1 for setting up the right equation.

$$
25x-30=27x-54
$$

M1 for manipulation of algebraic terms.

$$
24=2x
$$

$$
x=12
$$
