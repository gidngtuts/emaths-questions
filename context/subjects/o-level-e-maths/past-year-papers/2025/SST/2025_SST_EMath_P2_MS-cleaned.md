**(a) (i)** The number of electric cars produced in country $Y$ by company $X$ in 2015 was $3\,857\,000$. Write this number in standard form.

**(ii)** In 2005, $2.96$ million electric cars were produced in country $Y$. Calculate the percentage increase in the car production from 2005 to 2015.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Percentage increase}&=\dfrac{3.857\times 10^{6}-2.96\times 10^{6}}{2.96\times 10^{6}}\times 100\%\\
&=30.3\%\quad(3\text{ significant figures})
\end{aligned}
$$

Show computation for percentage increase.

**(iii)** In 2015, $84.7\%$ of all cars produced in country $Y$ were non-electric cars. Calculate the total number of cars produced in 2015, giving your answer to the nearest million.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Total cars in }2015&=\dfrac{3.857\times 10^{6}}{100-84.7}\times 100\\
&=2.5209\times 10^{7}\\
&=25\text{ million cars}\ (\text{nearest million})
\end{aligned}
$$

Or $25\,000\,000$.

**(iv)** In 2015, there were $420$ cars per $1000$ people in country $Y$. Estimate the population of the country in 2015. Give your answer in standard form correct to three significant figures.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Population}&=\dfrac{2.5209\times 10^{7}}{420}\times 1000\\
&=6.00\times 10^{7}\quad(3\text{ significant figures})
\end{aligned}
$$

**(c)** It is given that $p=\dfrac{r^{2}q-2}{q-r^{2}}$.

**(i)** Find $p$ when $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $q=-1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p&=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{2}(-1)-2}{-1-\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}\\
&=\dfrac{-\dfrac{9}{4}}{-\dfrac{5}{4}}\\
&=\dfrac{9}{5}=1\dfrac{4}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Or $1.8$. Show substitution of values.

**(ii)** Express $r$ in terms of $p$ and $q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(q-r^{2})&=r^{2}q-2\\
pq-pr^{2}&=qr^{2}-2\\
pq+2&=r^{2}(p+q)\\
r^{2}&=\dfrac{pq+2}{p+q}\\
r&=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{pq+2}{p+q}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(d)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{x+3}{2x^{2}-8x+6}+\dfrac{5x}{3-x}=\dfrac{2}{x-1}$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{x+3}{2(x-3)(x-1)}-\dfrac{5x}{x-3}&=\dfrac{2}{x-1}\\
\dfrac{x+3}{2(x-3)(x-1)}-\dfrac{5x(2)(x-1)}{2(x-3)(x-1)}&=\dfrac{2(2)(x-3)}{2(x-3)(x-1)}\\
x+3-10x(x-1)&=4(x-3)\\
10x^{2}-7x-15&=0\\
x&=\dfrac{-(-7)\pm\sqrt{(-7)^{2}-4(10)(-15)}}{2(10)}\\
x&=1.62\quad\text{or}\quad -0.92\quad(2\text{ d.p.})
\end{aligned}
$$

Note: $2x^{2}-8x+6=2(x-3)(x-1)$ and $3-x=-(x-3)$.
