## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Alex buys a shirt at a price of £14.75.
Paul buys a shirt at a price of ＄21.99.
The exchange rate is ＄1 = £0.73.
Calculate how much more Paul pays than Alex.

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{14.75}{0.73}=20.2054$ M1

$21.99-20.2054 \approx \text{＄}1.78$ A1 (with units)

or

$21.99\times 0.73=16.0527$ M1

$16.0527-14.75=\text{£}1.30$ A1 (with units)
