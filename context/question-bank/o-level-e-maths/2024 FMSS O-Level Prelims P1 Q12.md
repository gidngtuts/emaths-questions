## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 9

Three points $A$, $B$ and $C$ are shown below. $A$ is at the lower left, $B$ is at the lower right, and $C$ is above the segment $AB$, closer to $A$ than to $B$.

![Points A, B and C for the constructions](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/FMSS/images/2024-FMSS-EMATH-P1-PRELIM-QP-pages/page-09.png)

**(a)** Construct the perpendicular bisector of $BC$. [1]

**(b)** Construct the angle bisector of angle $ACB$. [1]

**(c)** These two bisectors meet at $P$.
Complete the statement below.

The point $P$ is equidistant from the lines ………………… and …………………
and equidistant from the points ………………… and ………………… . [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Construction of the perpendicular bisector of $BC$. B1.

**(b)** Construction of the angle bisector of $\angle ACB$. B1.

**(c)** The point $P$ is equidistant from the lines $AC$ and $BC$ and equidistant from the points $B$ and $C$. B1 if all correct.
