## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 20–21

The triangle with vertices $A(2, 5)$, $B(-2, -3)$ and $C(2, -2)$ is shown in the diagram below. $AC$ is vertical.

Find

(a) the area of triangle $ABC$, [1]

(b) the coordinates of point $D$ such that $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, [1]

(c) the area of parallelogram $ABCD$, [1]

(d) angle $BAC$, [1]

(e) the gradient of line $AC$, [1]

(f) the cosine value of angle $BCA$ in terms of $p$, where $p = BC$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Area of triangle $ABC = 0.5\times 4\times 7 = 14\text{ unit}^{2}$ — B1.

(b) $(6, 6)$ — B1.

(c) Area of parallelogram $= 14\times 2 = 28\text{ unit}^{2}$ — B1.

(d) Angle $BAC = \tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{4}{8}\right) = 26.6^{\circ}$ (1 d.p.) — B1.

(e) undefined — B1.

(f) $-\dfrac{1}{p}$ — B1. Using the cosine rule to get $\dfrac{p^{2}-31}{14p}$ is also accepted.
