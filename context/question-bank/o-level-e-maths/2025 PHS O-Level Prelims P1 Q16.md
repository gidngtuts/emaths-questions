## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 14

$A$, $B$ and $C$ are points $(-4,3)$, $(1,9)$ and $(0,2)$ respectively.

**(a)** Find the length of $AB$. [2]

**(b)** Find the equation of the line that passes through $B$ and has the same gradient as $AC$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $AB=7.81$ units

**(b)** $y=-0.25x+9.25$
