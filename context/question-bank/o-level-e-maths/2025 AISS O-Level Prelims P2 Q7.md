## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 2, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 5; total 9
> **Page:** 14–15

**(a)** The diagram shows two geometrically similar bottles, containing cooking oil.
The height of the smaller bottle is $15$ cm.
The height of the larger bottle is $25$ cm.

**(i)** The diameter of the base of the larger bottle is $10$ cm.
Calculate the diameter of the base of the smaller bottle. [2]

**(ii)** The smaller bottle has a capacity of $400$ ml.
The manufacturer makes another geometrically similar bottle that holds $1.2$ litres.
Calculate the height of this bottle. [2]

**(b)** Two congruent semicircles with radius $4$ cm and centres $P$ and $Q$ overlap as shown below.

![Question 7 overlapping semicircles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/AISS/images/AISS-Prelim-EMath-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-15.png)

Calculate the area of the shaded region. [5]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)**

$$
\frac{d}{10}=\frac{15}{25},\quad d=6\text{ cm}
$$

M1 for the correct equation. A1.

**(a)(ii)** $400$ ml and $1.2$ litres $=1200$ ml, so the volume ratio is $1:3$.

$$
15\times\sqrt[3]{3}=21.6\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}
$$

M1 for the $1:\sqrt[3]{3}$ ratio. A1.

Alternative: $\left(\dfrac{h}{15}\right)^{3}=\dfrac{1200}{400}$, so $h=21.6$ cm.

**(b)** $PR=PQ=QR$ (radii). Triangle $PQR$ is equilateral, so $\angle PQR=\angle PRQ=\angle RPQ=60^{\circ}$.

M1 for recognising that triangle $PQR$ is equilateral.

$$
\text{Area of sector}=\frac{60}{360}\times\pi\times 4^{2}=\frac{8}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

M1 for the area of a sector.

$$
\text{Area of triangle}=\frac{1}{2}(4)(4)\sin 60^{\circ}=8\sin 60^{\circ}\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

M1 for the area of the triangle.

$$
\text{Area of shaded region}=\frac{8}{3}\pi+\frac{8}{3}\pi-8\sin 60^{\circ}=9.83\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

M1 for relating the areas of the sectors and the triangle to the shaded region. A1.
