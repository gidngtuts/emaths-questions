## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 4; total 6
> **Page:** 19

A glass in the shape of a cone on a stem is filled to the brim with water.
The diameter of the top of the glass is $11$ cm.
The height of the cone is $8$ cm.
The thickness of the glass is negligible.

**(a)** Calculate the curved surface area of the inside of the glass. [2]

**(b)** Ethan pours $40\%$ of the water in this glass into a second glass.
The second glass is in the shape of a cylinder.
The depth of water in the cylindrical glass is $2.5$ cm.
Calculate the diameter of the cylindrical glass. [4]

## Answer Scheme

A glass in the shape of a cone on a stem is filled to the brim with water. The diameter of the top of the glass is $11$ cm. The height of the cone is $8$ cm. The thickness of the glass is negligible.

**(a)** Calculate the curved surface area of the inside of the glass.

$$
l^{2}=5.5^{2}+8^{2}=94.25
$$

$$
l=\sqrt{94.25}
$$

M1 for finding the slant height of the cone.

$$
\text{Curved surface area}=\pi\times 5.5\times\sqrt{94.25}=168\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

A1.

**(b)** Ethan pours $40\%$ of the water in this glass into a second glass. The second glass is in the shape of a cylinder. The depth of water in the cylindrical glass is $2.5$ cm. Calculate the diameter of the cylindrical glass.

$$
\text{Volume of water in the glass}=\frac{1}{3}\pi(5.5)^{2}(8)=\frac{242}{3}\pi\text{ cm}^{3}
$$

M1 for finding the volume of water in the glass.

$$
\pi r^{2}(2.5)=\frac{242}{3}\pi\times 0.4
$$

M1 for the correct formula for the volume of a cylinder and for taking $40\%$ of the volume of the cone.

$$
r=\sqrt{\frac{\dfrac{242}{3}\times 0.4}{2.5}}
$$

M1 for correct $r$.

$$
\text{diameter}=2r=7.19\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}
$$

A1.
