## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 2; total 7
> **Page:** 16

Three points $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on a horizontal ground are such that $AB = 24\text{ m}$ and $BC = 53\text{ m}$.
Point $B$ is due north of $C$. The bearing of $A$ from $B$ is $122^{\circ}$.

![Triangle ABC with B due north of C, AB 24 m, BC 53 m](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/CCHM/images/CCHM-2024-S4-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Find the distance $AC$.

**(b)** Find the bearing of $C$ from $A$.

**(c)** Find the area of the triangular plot $ABC$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
AC^{2} = 53^{2}+24^{2}-2(53)(24)\cos 58^{\circ}
$$

$$
AC = 45.13186\ldots = 45.1\text{ m (3 s.f.)}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\frac{\sin\angle BAC}{53} = \frac{\sin 58^{\circ}}{45.13186\ldots}
$$

$$
\angle BAC = 84.8060\ldots^{\circ}
$$

Bearing of $C$ from $A = 360^{\circ}-58^{\circ}-84.8060\ldots^{\circ} = 217.2^{\circ}$.

**(c)**

$$
\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(53)(24)\sin 58^{\circ} = 539.35\ldots = 539\text{ m}^{2}
$$
