## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 15

$x$ is a number between $200$ and $300$.
The highest common factor of $x$ and $126$ is $21$.
Find the smallest possible value of $x$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$x$ is a number between $200$ and $300$. The highest common factor of $x$ and $126$ is $21$. Find the smallest possible value of $x$.

$$
126=2\times 3^{2}\times 7
$$

M1 for writing $126$ in terms of its prime factors.

$$
\text{HCF}=3\times 7
$$

$$
x=3\times 7\times 11=231
$$

A1.
