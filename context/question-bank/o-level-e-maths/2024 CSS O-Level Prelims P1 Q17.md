## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 12

**(a)**

**(i)** Express 1500 as the product of its prime factors.
Leave your answer in index notations. [1]

**(ii)** $p = 2^{m}\times 3^{2}\times n$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers.
Find the values of $m$ and $n$ if the HCF of 1500 and $p$ is 6, given that $100 < p < 150$. [2]

**(b)** In a race, Car A, Car B and Car C took 3 minutes, 6 minutes and 8 minutes respectively to complete one lap around the racing track.
If the race started at 7 pm, at what time will the cars be back at the starting point together again? [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $2^{2}\times 3\times 5^{3}$.

**(a)(ii)** $1500 = 2^{2}\times 3\times 5^{3}$.

$p = 2^{m}\times 3^{2}\times n$.

$\operatorname{HCF} = 2\times 3$, so $m = 1$ and $n = 7$.

**(b)** $\operatorname{LCM} = 2^{3}\times 3 = 24$.
