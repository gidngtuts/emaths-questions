## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

School students were surveyed on their favourite brand of drink. The results are represented in the pie chart below.

![Pie chart of favourite drink brands, with sector sizes that do not match the printed percentages](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E-Math-Prelim-Paper-1-FINAL-pages/page-04.png)

The printed percentages are Brand A $41\%$, Brand B $9\%$, Brand C $39\%$ and Brand D $21\%$.

(a) State one error in the pie chart above. [1]

(b) State one way to correct the error stated in part (a). [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Brand C’s and Brand D’s sectors add up to $60\%$ but are shown as half of the pie chart (which should be $50\%$). Or the totals add up to $110\%$ instead of $100\%$. B1.

(b) Recalculate or check the percentages for Brand C and Brand D so that the sectors of the pie chart are proportional to the actual percentages. Accept “recalculate all values to get the correct percentages.” B1.
