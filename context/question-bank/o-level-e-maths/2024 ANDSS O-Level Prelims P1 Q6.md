## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 7

**(a)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{y^{9}}{27x^{-6}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$, leaving your answer in positive index. [2]

**(b)** Given $\dfrac{25^{2-x}}{125}=5^{y}$, express $x$ in terms of $y$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\left(\frac{y^{9}}{27x^{-6}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}
= \frac{y^{9\times\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)}}{3^{3\times\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)}\,x^{-6\times\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)}}
$$

M1 for multiplying $-\dfrac{2}{3}$ to each index.

$$
= \frac{y^{-6}}{3^{-2}\,x^{4}}
= \frac{9}{x^{4}y^{6}}
$$

$\dfrac{9}{x^{4}y^{6}}$ is also accepted.

**(b)**

$$
\frac{25^{2-x}}{125}=5^{y}
$$

$$
\frac{(5^{2})^{2-x}}{5^{3}}=5^{y}
$$

Any other equivalent form of $5^{m}=5^{n}$ is accepted.

$$
5^{2-(6-3x)}=5^{y}
$$

$$
5^{2-6+3x}=5^{y}
$$

$$
5^{3x-4}=5^{y}
$$

Comparing indices,

$$
3x-4=y
$$

$$
x=\frac{y+4}{3}
$$
