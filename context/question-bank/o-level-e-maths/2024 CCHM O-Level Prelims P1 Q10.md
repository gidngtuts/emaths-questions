## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 7

The gradient of the line joining the points $(-3+2a, 7)$ and $(a+1, 2)$ is $-\dfrac{2}{3}$. Find the value of $a$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2-7}{a+1-(-3+2a)} = \frac{-5}{-a+4} = -\frac{2}{3}
$$

$$
-15 = -2(-a+4), \quad a = -\frac{7}{2}
$$
