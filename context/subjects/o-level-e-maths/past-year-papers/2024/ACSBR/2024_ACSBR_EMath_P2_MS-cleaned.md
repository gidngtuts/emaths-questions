(a) One possible answer:

- $ET = AT$ (tangents from an external point are equal)
- $TO$ is common
- $OE = OA$ (radii of the circle)

Triangle $TOA$ is congruent to triangle $TOE$ (SSS).

Another possible answer:

- $\angle TAO = \angle TEO = 90^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius)
- $TO$ is common
- $OE = OA$ (radii of the circle)

Triangle $TOA$ is congruent to triangle $TOE$ (RHS).

(b) (i) $\angle AOT = (180-90-32)^{\circ} = 58^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius).

$\angle ABF = \dfrac{1}{2}\angle AOT = \dfrac{1}{2}(58^{\circ}) = 29^{\circ}$ (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference).

$\angle OFG = \angle ABF = 29^{\circ}$ (alternate angles, $OF \parallel BA$).

(ii) $\angle ACF = \angle ABF = 29^{\circ}$ (angles in the same segment).

$\angle CAE = 180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-29^{\circ} = 61^{\circ}$ ($OT$ is the perpendicular bisector of chord $AE$).

$\angle CDE = 180^{\circ}-61^{\circ} = 119^{\circ}$ (angles in opposite segments).

(c) As $\angle OET$ and $\angle OAT$ are right angles, by the property of the angle in a semicircle, $OT$ is a diameter and points $E$ and $A$ lie on the circumference. $O$, $E$, $T$ and $A$ are thus four points on the circumference of this circle.

Or $\angle AOE+\angle ATE = (58 \times 2)+(32 \times 2) = 180^{\circ}$, and $\angle OET+\angle OAT = 180^{\circ}$. By the property of angles in opposite segments, $O$, $E$, $T$ and $A$ are four points on the circumference of this circle.

$$

(c) Volume of water remaining after dispensing 250 cups

$$
= 8666\frac{2}{3}\pi-\left(250 \times \frac{1}{3}\pi(3^{2})(5.3)\right) = 4691\frac{2}{3}\pi \text{ or } 14739 \text{ cm}^{3}.
$$

Volume of water in the cylinder

$$
= 4691\frac{2}{3}\pi-\frac{2}{3}\pi(10^{3}) = 4025\pi \text{ or } 12645 \text{ cm}^{3}.
$$

Height of water in the cylindrical section

$$
= \frac{4025\pi}{\pi(10^{2})} \text{ or } \frac{12645}{\pi(10^{2})} = 40.25.
$$

Height of water remaining in the dispenser $= 40.25+10 = 50.25$ cm or $50.3$ cm.

Alternative: volume dispensed for 250 cups $= 250 \times 15.9\pi$. Height of water dispensed $= \dfrac{250 \times 15.9\pi}{100\pi} = \dfrac{125}{\pi}$. Height remaining $= 90-\dfrac{125}{\pi} = 50.2$ cm (3 s.f.).

(d) Slant height of a cup $= \sqrt{3^{2}+5.3^{2}} = 6.0902$ cm.

Curved surface area $= \pi(3)(6.0902) = 57.399$ cm$^{2}$.

### Q9

> **Marks:** 11
> **Sub-topic:** Real-world context
> **Page:** 18–21

(a)

$$
4100\left(1+\frac{4}{100}\right)^{5} = 4988
$$

which is ＄5000 correct to the nearest thousand.

(b) Justification: choose the highest COE price recorded in the past 12 months to cover the worst-case scenario.

| | EV 60 kWh | 1998 cc petrol car |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost price | $100000+105000-30000 =$ ＄175000 | $110000+150000 =$ ＄260000 |
| Minimum downpayment | $40\% \times 175000 =$ ＄70000 | $30\% \times 260000 =$ ＄78000 |

6 months of Lee’s salary $= 6 \times 5000 =$ ＄30000.

Savings after the downpayment:

- EV: $105000-70000 =$ ＄35000
- Petrol: $105000-78000 =$ ＄27000

Lee can only afford the downpayment for the EV 60 kWh ($35000-30000 =$ ＄5000 left above the 6-month reserve).

(c) To determine if Lee can afford the car in January 2024.

(i) Road tax

$$
[250+3.75(60-30)] \times 0.7826 \div 6 = 47.246
$$

(ii) Loan amount $= 175000-70000 =$ ＄105000.

Interest $= \dfrac{105000 \times 2.78 \times 7}{100} =$ ＄20433.

(iii) Monthly instalment $= (105000+20433) \div 84 =$ ＄1493.25.

(iv) Other costs on a monthly basis $= \dfrac{4700}{12}+600 =$ ＄991.667.

Total monthly expense $= $2532.1625.

Spare cash for the month $= 5000-2700 =$ ＄2300.

(v) $2300-2532.1625 = -232.1625$.

Lee would not be able to afford the car.

However, if the ＄5000 remaining after the downpayment in part (b) is taken into account, with ＄30000 already set aside, he would be able to afford the car.
