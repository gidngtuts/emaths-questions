## Answer Scheme

**(a)** The question-paper equation is not visible. The scheme starts from

$12x-8x^{2}=6-8x^{2}$ (M1)

$12x=6$

$x=\dfrac{1}{2}$ (A1)

**(b)** $1-3p\geq 5$

$-3p\geq 4$

$p\leq -\dfrac{4}{3}$ (B1)

**(c)** $3A=\dfrac{Ap+h}{1-h}$

$3A(1-h)=Ap+h$ (M1, make linear)

$3A-3Ah=Ap+h$

$3A-3Ah-Ap=h$

$A(3-3h-p)=h$ (M1, factorise)

$A=\dfrac{h}{3-3h-p}$ (A1)

**(d)** The question-paper equation is not visible. The scheme solves

$\dfrac{x}{(x-4)^{2}}-\dfrac{3}{4-x}=2$

$\dfrac{x}{(x-4)^{2}}+\dfrac{3}{x-4}=2$ (M1, combine)

$\dfrac{x+3(x-4)}{(x-4)^{2}}=2$

$x+3x-12=2(x^{2}-8x+16)$ (M1, expand)

$4x-12=2x^{2}-16x+32$

$0=2x^{2}-20x+44$

$0=x^{2}-10x+22$

$x=\dfrac{-(-10)\pm\sqrt{(-10)^{2}-4(1)(22)}}{2(1)}$ (M1, quadratic formula using their found equation)

$=\dfrac{10\pm\sqrt{12}}{2}$

$=6.73$ or $3.27$ (2dp) (A1)
