## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Simplify $\dfrac{x^{2}-9}{3x^{2}-9x}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Simplify $\dfrac{x^{2}-9}{3x^{2}-9x}$.

$$
\frac{x^{2}-9}{3x^{2}-9x}=\frac{(x-3)(x+3)}{3x(x-3)}=\frac{x+3}{3x}
$$

B2, factorising numerator and denominator correctly. A1.
