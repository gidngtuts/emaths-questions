## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 10

Sketch the graph of $y=3^{-x}$ on the axes below.
Indicate clearly the coordinates of the points where the graph crosses the axes. [1]

## Answer Scheme

Both the shape and the $y$-intercept must be drawn and indicated correctly. The curve of $y=3^{-x}$ passes through $(0,1)$ and decreases towards the positive $x$-axis.
