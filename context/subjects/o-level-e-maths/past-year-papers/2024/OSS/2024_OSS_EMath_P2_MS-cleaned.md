### Q9

> **Marks:** (a) 5, (b) 5; total 10
> **Sub-topic:** Real-world context
> **Page:** 20–22

**(a)** For 300 sets: 600 eggs, 600 slices of bread, 300 sausages, 300 slices of ham and 300 cups of coffee.

Lowest choices:

- Pasar 30 eggs: 20 trays, ＄138
- Gardenia-style 14-slice bread: 43 loaves, ＄116.10
- Chef sausages with the ＄0.35 discount: ＄151.25
- SC ham: 30 packs, ＄99
- Indocafe: 12 packs, ＄47.40

Lowest total $=138+116.10+151.25+99+47.40=\text{＄}551.75$

**(b)** Let the selling price be ＄$x$. Total sales $=300x$.

To cover expenses, 40% of sales $=551.75$, so 100% of sales $=551.75\div 0.4=1379.375$ and 60% of sales $=827.625$, which meets the donation criterion of at least ＄600.

Selling price $=1379.375\div 300=4.5979\approx\text{＄}4.60$ to the nearest ten cents. The scheme writes $2.75875$ and then $\approx\text{＄}4.60$.

Alternative using the ＄200 funding: $1379.375-200=1179.375$, and $1179.375\div 300\approx 3.93$, about ＄4.

Assumptions accepted by the scheme: all 300 sets can be made without loss of ingredients; all 300 sets are sold at the minimum price; funding is not included because it is uncertain.
