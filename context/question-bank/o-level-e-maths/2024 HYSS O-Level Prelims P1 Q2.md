## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 3

(a) Express 1400 as the product of its prime factors. [1]

(b) Write down the smallest positive integer $k$ such that $1400k$ is a perfect cube. [1]

(c) $n$ is a number between 300 and 400. The highest common factor of $n$ and 1400 is 35. Find the largest possible value for $n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $1400 = 2^{3}\times 5^{2}\times 7$. B1.

(b) $1400k = 2^{3}\times 5^{2}\times 7\times k = 2^{3}\times 5^{2}\times 7\times(5\times 7^{2})$, so $k = 245$. B1.

(c) $n = 2^{?}\times 5\times 7\times ?$ and $1400 = 2^{3}\times 5^{2}\times 7$, so the HCF is $5\times 7 = 35$. By guess-and-check, $n = 5\times 7\times 11 = 385$. B2. The largest possible value between 300 and 400 is required.
