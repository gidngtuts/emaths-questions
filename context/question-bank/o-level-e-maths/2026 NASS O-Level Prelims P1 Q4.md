## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

The solution for the equation $\dfrac{3x}{4}=k-\dfrac{6x-5}{5}$ is twice the value of $k$ and $k$ is a constant.

Find the value of $k$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{3(2k)}{4} &= k-\frac{6(2k)-5}{5} \\
30k &= 20k-48k+20 \\
58k &= 20 \\
k &= \frac{10}{29}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: substitute $x$ with $2k$. A1.
