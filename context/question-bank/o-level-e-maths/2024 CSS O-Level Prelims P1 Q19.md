## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 14

The coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ are $(0, 3)$ and $(15, 12)$ respectively.
$O$ is the origin.

**(a)** Find the equation of $PQ$. [2]

**(b)** Find the perimeter of trapezium $OPQR$, where $O$ is the origin and $R$ is the point of reflection of $Q$ along the $x$-axis. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\text{Gradient} = \frac{12-3}{15-0} = \frac{3}{5}
$$

Equation of $PQ$: $y = \dfrac{3}{5}x+3$.

**(b)** Gradient of $R = (15, -12)$.

Perimeter of $OPQR$

$$
= 3+\sqrt{(15-0)^{2}+(12-3)^{2}}+(12\times 2)+\sqrt{15^{2}+12^{2}} = 63.70223 \approx 63.7
$$
