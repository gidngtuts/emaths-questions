## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

Expand and simplify $(x-y)^{2}-(x+1)(x-2y)$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Expand and simplify $(x-y)^{2}-(x+1)(x-2y)$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x-y)^{2}-(x+1)(x-2y)&=x^{2}-2xy+y^{2}-(x^{2}-2xy+x-2y)\\
&=x^{2}-2xy+y^{2}-x^{2}+2xy-x+2y\\
&=y^{2}-x+2y
\end{aligned}
$$
