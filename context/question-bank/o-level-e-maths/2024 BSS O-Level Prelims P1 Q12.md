## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3; total 4
> **Page:** 8–9

**(a)** Simplify $\left(16y^{3}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}$.

**(b)** $5^{k}=125\sqrt[3]{5}\sqrt{5}$.
Use laws of indices to find the value of $k$.
Show your working.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\left(16y^{3}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}=64y^{\frac{9}{2}}$ (B1)

**(b)** $5^{k}=125\sqrt[3]{5}\sqrt{5}$ (M1, change either to power of 3)

$5^{k}=5^{3}\left(5(5)^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}$

$5^{k}=5^{3}\left(5^{\frac{3}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}}$

$5^{k}=5^{3}\left(5^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)$ (M1, combine to $5^{\frac{1}{2}}$ or to a single power of 3)

$k=3\dfrac{1}{2}$ (A1)
