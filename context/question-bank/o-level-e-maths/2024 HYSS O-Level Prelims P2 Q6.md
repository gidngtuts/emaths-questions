## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 12–13

The diagram shows a semicircle, centre $O$, radius $16\text{ cm}$. A chord $AB$ cuts off a segment of height $3.2\text{ cm}$. The shaded region is that segment.

![Semicircle radius 16 cm with a shaded segment of height 3.2 cm](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/HYSS/images/2024-HYSS_S4-5-G3-MA-Prelim_Paper-2_QP-pages/page-12.png)

(a) Show that angle $AOB = 1.287$ radians, correct to 3 decimal places. [2]

(b) Calculate the area of the shaded region. [3]

(c) The semicircle is the cross section of a water trough of length $2.8\text{ m}$, standing on level ground. The shaded area represents the water in the trough.

(i) Calculate the volume of water, in $\text{cm}^{3}$, in the trough. Leave your answer in standard form. [3]

(ii) Calculate the number of litres of water that must be added to fill the trough. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Triangle $OAB$ is isosceles, so the perpendicular from $O$ to $AB$ meets $AB$ at $P$, and $OP = 16-3.2 = 12.8\text{ cm}$.

$$
\cos \angle AOP = \frac{12.8}{16}
$$

Angle $AOP = 36.86989^{\circ} = 0.643501\text{ rad}$. M1. Angle $AOB = 2\times 0.643501 = 1.287\text{ rad}$. M1. Shown.

(b) Area of sector $OAB = \dfrac{1}{2}\times 16^{2}\times 1.287 = 164.736\text{ cm}^{2}$. M1.

Area of triangle $OAB = \dfrac{1}{2}\times 16^{2}\times \sin 1.287 = 122.87992\text{ cm}^{2}$. M1.

Shaded segment $= 164.736-122.87992 = 41.9\text{ cm}^{2}$. A1.

(c) (i) Volume $= 41.85608\times 280 = 1.17\times 10^{4}\text{ cm}^{3}$. M1 for the conversion of $2.8\text{ m}$ to $280\text{ cm}$. M1, with error carried forward, for the expression. A1. The maximum mark from error carried forward is 1.

(ii) Volume of the trough $= \dfrac{1}{2}\times \pi\times 16^{2}\times 280 = 112594.6807\text{ cm}^{3}$. M1.

Additional water $= 112594.6807-11719.7024 = 100874.9788\text{ cm}^{3} = 101$ litres (3 s.f.). M1 with error carried forward. A1. The maximum mark from error carried forward is 1.
