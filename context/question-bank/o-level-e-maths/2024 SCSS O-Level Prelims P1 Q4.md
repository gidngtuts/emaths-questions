## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{2x}{3}-\dfrac{3(x-2)}{4}$.

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{2x}{3}-\dfrac{3(x-2)}{4}=\dfrac{8x-9(x-2)}{12}$ (M1, common denominator)

$=\dfrac{8x-9x+18}{12}=\dfrac{18-x}{12}$ (A1)
