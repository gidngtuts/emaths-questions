## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 3

In the quadrilateral $ABCD$, $BC = 5.25\text{ cm}$, $AC = 4.32\text{ cm}$.
Angle $ADC = 90^{\circ}$. Angle $ACD = 35^{\circ}$.

![Question 2 quadrilateral ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-03.png)

**(a)** Calculate $AD$. [1]

**(b)** Given that the area of triangle $ABC = 3.18\text{ cm}^{2}$, find the obtuse angle $ACB$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $AD = 4.32\sin 35^{\circ} = 2.48\text{ cm}$

**(b)**

$$
3.18 = \frac{1}{2}(5.25)(4.32)\sin\angle ACB
$$

Obtuse angle $ACB = 163.7^{\circ}$
