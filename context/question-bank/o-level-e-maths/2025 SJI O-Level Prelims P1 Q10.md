## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 9

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{1}{2-x}-\dfrac{3x+2}{x^{2}-7x+10}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{1}{2-x}-\frac{3x+2}{x^{2}-7x+10}=\frac{-1}{x-2}-\frac{3x+2}{(x-2)(x-5)}
$$

$$
=\frac{-x+5-3x-2}{(x-2)(x-5)}=\frac{-4x+3}{(x-2)(x-5)}
$$
