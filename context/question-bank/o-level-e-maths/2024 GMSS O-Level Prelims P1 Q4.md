## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

$10,\ 23,\ 34,\ 40,\ 25,\ 35,\ 17,\ 44,\ 23$

(a) Find the median of the set of numbers. [1]

(b) Find the range of the set of numbers. [1]

## Answer Scheme

Ascending order: $10$, $17$, $23$, $23$, $25$, $34$, $35$, $40$, $44$.

(a) Median $= 25$. [1]

(b) Range $= 44-10 = 34$. [1]
