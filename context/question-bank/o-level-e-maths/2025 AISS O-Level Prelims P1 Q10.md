## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 7

Five positive integers have a mean of $6$, a median of $7$ and a mode of $10$.
Find the five numbers. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Five positive integers have a mean of $6$, a median of $7$ and a mode of $10$. Find the five numbers.

$1,\ 2,\ 7,\ 10,\ 10$

B2 for getting all the numbers correct. B1 for getting $7,\ 10,\ 10$.
