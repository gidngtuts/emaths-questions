## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 6

![Question 8 windscreen wiper](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/YCKSS/images/YCKSS-EM-PRELIM-QP-2025-P1-pages/page-06.png)

The diagram shows the coverage of a car windscreen wiper $ABCD$. $AB$ and $DC$ are arcs of circles centre $O$ with radii $28\text{ cm}$ and $44\text{ cm}$ respectively. The perimeter of $ABCD$ is $72\pi-76\text{ cm}$.

**(a)** Calculate angle $AOB$ in radians, leaving your answer in terms of $\pi$. [2]

**(b)** Calculate the area of the windscreen that the car wiper wipes through. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Calculate angle $AOB$ in radians, leaving your answer in terms of $\pi$.

$$
2(44-28)+28\theta+44\theta=72\pi-76
$$

$$
72\theta=72\pi-76-32
$$

$$
\theta=\frac{72\pi-108}{72}
$$

$$
\text{angle }AOB=\pi-1.5
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Poorly done as many used the formula for degree

**(b)** Calculate the area of the windscreen that the car wiper wipes through.

$$
\text{area}=\frac{1}{2}\times 44^{2}(\pi-1.5)-\frac{1}{2}\times 28^{2}(\pi-1.5)
$$

$$
=946\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$
