## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 3, (a)(ii) 3, (b)(i) 2, (b)(ii) 2, (b)(iii) 2; total 12
> **Page:** 14–15

**(a)** The position vector of point $P$ is $\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$ and the position vector of point $Q$ is $\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ m \end{pmatrix}$, where $m<0$.

**(i)** $PQ=13$ units. Find $m$.

**(ii)** Given that $\overrightarrow{QP}=2\overrightarrow{PR}$, find the coordinates of point $R$.

**(b)** The coordinates of $B$ and $C$ are $(5, 7)$ and $(5, -2)$ respectively. Lines $AB$ and $AC$ intersect the $y$-axis at $P$ and $Q$ respectively.

**(i)** The gradient of $AB$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Find the equation of the line $AB$.

**(ii)** The $y$-coordinate of $A$ is 1. Find the area of triangle $ABC$.

**(iii)** Explain why triangle $APQ$ and triangle $ABC$ are similar.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** **(i)** $\overrightarrow{PQ}=\begin{pmatrix} 5 \\ m-4 \end{pmatrix}$

$25+(m-4)^{2}=169$, so $m^{2}-8m-128=0$

$m=-8$ or $m=16$ (rejected because $m<0$). $m=-8$

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{QP}=\begin{pmatrix} -5 \\ 12 \end{pmatrix}$

$\dfrac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{QP}=\overrightarrow{OR}-\overrightarrow{OP}$

$\overrightarrow{OR}=\begin{pmatrix} -2.5 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix} -3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix} -5.5 \\ 10 \end{pmatrix}$

$R(-5.5,\ 10)$

**(b)** **(i)** $y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+C$. At $(5, 7)$, $C=\dfrac{11}{3}$

$y=\dfrac{2}{3}x+\dfrac{11}{3}$

**(ii)** When $y=1$, $x=-4$. Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 9\times 9=40.5$ units$^{2}$

**(iii)** $PQ$ and $BC$ are parallel. Angle $PAQ=$ angle $BAC$ (common). Angle $APQ=$ angle $ABC$ (corresponding angles). Triangles $APQ$ and $ABC$ are similar.
