## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Express $\dfrac{8}{3x-2}-\dfrac{3}{2x+1}$ as a single fraction in its simplest form.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{8}{3x-2}-\frac{3}{2x+1}=\frac{8(2x+1)-3(3x-2)}{(3x-2)(2x+1)}=\frac{16x+8-9x+6}{(3x-2)(2x+1)}=\frac{7x+14}{(3x-2)(2x+1)}
$$
