## Question

> **Marks:** [4]
> **Page:** 16

Solve the equation $x^{2}+5x-16 = 0$ by completing the square.
Give your solutions correct to two decimal places.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}+5x-16 &= 0 \\
(x+2.5)^{2}-(2.5)^{2} &= 16 \\
(x+2.5)^{2} &= 22.25 \\
x &= -2.5\pm\sqrt{22.25} \\
x &= 2.22 \text{ or } x = -7.22
\end{aligned}
$$
