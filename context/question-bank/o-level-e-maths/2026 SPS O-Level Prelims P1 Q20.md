## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1, (c) 1; total 4
> **Page:** 16

**(a)** Solve the inequality $-15<3x-3\leq 15$. [2]

**(b)** Represent your solution to part (a) on the number line below. [1]

![Question 20 number line](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/SPS/images/SPS-EM-2026-4G3-P1-Final-pages/page-16.png)

**(c)** It is given that $y$ is such that $2\leq y\leq 8$.
If $x$ and $y$ are integers, find the smallest possible value of $y^{2}-x^{2}$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
-15<3x-3\leq 15
$$

$$
-12<3x\leq 18
$$

$$
-4<x\leq 6
$$

**(b)** Open circle at $-4$, filled circle at $6$, joined by a line.

**(c)** Smallest $y^{2}-x^{2}$: take smallest $y^{2}$ ($y=2$) and largest $x^{2}$ ($x=6$).

$$
y^{2}-x^{2}=2^{2}-6^{2}=-32
$$
