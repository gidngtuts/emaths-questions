## Question

> **Marks:** 7
> **Page:** 3

A cone has a radius of 24 cm and a total surface area of $1536\pi\text{ cm}^{2}$.

**(a)** Show that the volume of the cone is $6144\pi\text{ cm}^{3}$. [3]

**(b)** The cone is recasted into a solid sphere. Find

**(i)** the radius of the sphere, [2]

**(ii)** the total surface area of the sphere. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Since the total surface area is $1536\pi$,

$$
1536\pi=\pi(24)^{2}+\pi(24)l
$$

$$
l=40\text{ cm}
$$

$$
\text{height}=\sqrt{40^{2}-24^{2}}=32\text{ cm}
$$

$$
\text{Volume of cone}=\frac{1}{3}\times(24)^{2}\times\pi\times 32=6144\pi\text{ cm}^{3}
$$

(shown)

**(b) (i)**

$$
6144\pi=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}
$$

$$
r=\sqrt[3]{\frac{6144\times 3}{4}}=16.641=16.6\text{ cm (3 s.f.)}
$$

**(b) (ii)**

$$
\text{Surface area}=4\pi(16.641)^{2}=3479.91555=3480\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$
