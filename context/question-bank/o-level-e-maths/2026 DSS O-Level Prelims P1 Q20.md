## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 16

In a regular polygon, the ratio interior angle : exterior angle $=3:2$.

Calculate the number of sides of the polygon. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$5x=180^{\circ}$

Exterior angle $=72^{\circ}$

$$
\text{no. of sides}=\frac{360}{72}=5
$$
