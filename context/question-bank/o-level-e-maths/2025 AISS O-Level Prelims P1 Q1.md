## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Expand $(2p+5q)(3p^{2}-q+5r)$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Expand and simplify $(2p+5q)(3p^{2}-q+5r)$.

$$
6p^{3}-2pq+10pr+15p^{2}q-5q^{2}+25qr
$$

B2 if all terms are correct. B1 if at least 3 terms are right.
