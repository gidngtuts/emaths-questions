## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 6

A car travels at an average speed of $74.5\text{ km/h}$ for $2.25$ hours.

(a) Convert $75\text{ km/h}$ to m/s. [1]

(b) By rounding the numbers correct to 1 significant figure, find an estimate of the distance travelled by the car. Show your working clearly. [2]

(c) Without doing any calculation, explain why the actual distance travelled by the car is greater than the answer to (b). [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\dfrac{75000}{3600} = 20\dfrac{5}{6} = 20.8\text{ m/s}$. B1. Either form is accepted.

(b) Distance estimate: round $74.5$ to $70$ and $2.25$ to $2$, so $70\times 2 = 140\text{ km}$. M1 for showing each rounding to 1 significant figure. A1.

(c) Both the average speed and the time are greater than the 1-significant-figure rounded values. Thus the actual distance travelled is greater than the answer in part (b). B1.
