## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 15

$C$ is a point on $BD$ such that $CD=25$ cm. $\angle ABD=90^{\circ}$, $\angle ADB=28^{\circ}$ and $AD=40$ cm. Calculate $\angle ACB$.

![Right-angled triangle ABD with point C on BD, AD = 40 cm, CD = 25 cm and angle ADB = 28 degrees](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/STC/images/CHIJ-STC-2024-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-15.png)

## Answer Scheme

$$
\sin 28^{\circ}=\frac{AB}{40}
$$

$$
AB=40\sin 28^{\circ}=18.77886251
$$

$$
BD=\sqrt{40^{2}-(40\sin 28^{\circ})^{2}}=35.31790371
$$

$$
BC=\sqrt{40^{2}-(40\sin 28^{\circ})^{2}}-25=10.31790371
$$

$$
\tan\angle ACB=\frac{18.77886251}{10.31790371}
$$

$$
\angle ACB=\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{18.77886251}{10.31790371}\right)=61.21373934^{\circ}\approx 61.2^{\circ}\text{ (2 d.p.)}
$$
