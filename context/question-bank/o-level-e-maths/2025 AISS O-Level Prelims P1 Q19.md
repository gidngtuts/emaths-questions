## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1; total 2
> **Page:** 14

The Venn diagram shows the universal set and the number of elements in each of its subsets.

![Question 19 Venn diagram](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/AISS/images/AISS-Prelim-EMath-Paper-1-QP-pages/page-14.png)

Find the value of

**(a)** $\mathrm{n}\left((X'\cap Y)'\right)$, [1]

**(b)** $\mathrm{n}\left((X'\cup Y)\cap(X\cup Y)'\right)$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\mathrm{n}\left((X'\cap Y)'\right)$

$$
=17+8+2=27
$$

B1.

**(b)** $\mathrm{n}\left((X'\cup Y)\cap(X\cup Y)'\right)$

$$
=17
$$

B1.
