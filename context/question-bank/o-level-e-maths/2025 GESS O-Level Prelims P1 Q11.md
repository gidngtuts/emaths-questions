## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 10

Charles drew the bar graph below for his Element of Business Studies project.

![Question 11 bar graph](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-10.png)

Explain how the bar graph may be misleading. [1]

## Answer Scheme

B1 for a misleading feature and the effect of that feature on interpretation of the data.

1. The title is biased. It does not allow the reader to make his/her own judgement.

OR

2. The vertical axis does not start from zero. This exaggerates the differences between the data.
