## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 3, (a)(ii) 1, (b) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 16

$$
\sqrt[3]{\frac{x^{3}+2y}{y}}=2x
$$

**(a) (i)** Rearrange the formula to make $y$ the subject. [3]

**(ii)** Find the value of $y$ when $x$ is $-2$. [1]

**(b)** $p=\dfrac{3}{q}+2$

Sue claims that the value of $q$ is undefined when $p = 3$.

Explain why she may be wrong. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt[3]{\frac{x^{3}+2y}{y}} &= 2x \\
\frac{x^{3}+2y}{y} &= (2x)^{3} \\
x^{3}+2y &= 8x^{3}y \\
x^{3} &= 8x^{3}y-2y \\
x^{3} &= y(8x^{3}-2) \\
y &= \frac{x^{3}}{8x^{3}-2}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: Cube on both sides. M1: Terms with $y$ on one side and factorise. A1.

> **Markers Report:** Generally well done and most can get the first method mark. However, many did not know how to make $y$ the subject.

**(a) (ii)**

$$
y=\frac{(-2)^{3}}{8(-2)^{3}-2}=\frac{4}{33}
$$

B1.

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
p &= \frac{3}{q}+2 \\
pq &= 3+2q \\
q(p-2) &= 3 \\
q &= \frac{3}{p-2}
\end{aligned}
$$

It is inaccurate as $q$ is undefined only when $p=2$. B1: Make $q$ the subject. B1.

OR

When $p=3$,

$$
3=\frac{3}{q}+2
$$

$$
1=\frac{3}{q}
$$

Thus $q=3$ instead of undefined when $p=3$. B1: Substitute $p=3$. B1.

> **Markers Report:** Well done with most using the alternative method.
