## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Find the equation of the straight line passing through $(7,-7)$ and $(-4,15)$.

## Answer Scheme

$m = \dfrac{15-(-7)}{-4-7} = \dfrac{22}{-11} = -2$.

$-7 = (-2)(7)+c$, so $c = 7$.

$y = -2x+7$.
