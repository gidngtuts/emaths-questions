## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Simplify $5(3^{3}\times 5^{4})^{2}$.
Give your answer in the form $3^{a}\times 5^{b}$.

**(b)** Use laws of indices to find the value of $k$.
Show your working.

$$
2^{100}-4\times 2^{97} = 2^{k}
$$

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $5(3^{3}\times 5^{4})^{2}=5(3^{6}\times 5^{8})=3^{6}\times 5^{9}$ B1

**(b)** $2^{100}-4\times 2^{97}=2^{k}$

$2^{100}-2^{2}\times 2^{97}=2^{k}$

$2^{100}-2^{99}=2^{k}$ M1

$2^{99}(2-1)=2^{k} \Rightarrow k=99$ A1
