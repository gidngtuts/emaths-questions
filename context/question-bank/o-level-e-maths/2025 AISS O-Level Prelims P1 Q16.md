## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

Solve these simultaneous equations.

$$
3x+5y+8=0
$$

$$
4x+13y-2=0
$$

You must show your working. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Solve these simultaneous equations.

$$
3x+5y+8=0 \quad (1)
$$

$$
4x+13y-2=0 \quad (2)
$$

You must show your working.

$$
(1)\times 4:\quad 12x+20y+32=0 \quad (3)
$$

$$
(2)\times 3:\quad 12x+39y-6=0 \quad (4)
$$

M1 for making the coefficient of $x$ or $y$ the same, or for making $x$ or $y$ the subject of the equation.

$$
(4)-(3):\quad 19y-38=0
$$

$$
y=2
$$

M1 for eliminating $x$ or $y$, or for correct substitution.

Substitute $y=2$ into $(1)$:

$$
3x+10+8=0
$$

$$

$$

$$
x=-6
$$

Answer: $x=-6$ and $y=2$. A1 for getting both $x$ and $y$ correct.
