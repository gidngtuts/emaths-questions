## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 14

$P$ is the point $(-3,1)$ and $Q$ is the point $(3,3)$.

**(a)** Find the length of the $PQ$.

**(b)** The point $R$ is such that $PQR$ forms an isosceles triangle.
The angle bisector of angle $PQR$ has an equation of $y=x$.
Find the coordinates of $R$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $PQ=\sqrt{(-3-3)^{2}+(1-3)^{2}}$ (M1)

$=6.32$ units (3sf) (A1)

**(b)** $R$ is $(1,-3)$ (B1)

By sketching and counting,
From $Q$ to $P$: horizontally $-6$ and vertically $-2$.
Since $y=x$ is the reflection line,
From $Q$ to $R$: horizontally $-2$ and vertically $-6$.
