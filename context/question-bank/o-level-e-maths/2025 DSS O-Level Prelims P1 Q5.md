## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 5

**(a)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{27a^{12}}{b^{3}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}$. [2]

**(b)** $13\times 9^{2}-4\times 3^{4}=3^{p}$

Use the laws of indices to find the value of $p$.
Show your working. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\left(\frac{27a^{12}}{b^{3}}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}}=\left(\frac{b^{3}}{27a^{12}}\right)^{\frac{2}{3}}=\frac{b^{2}}{9a^{8}}
$$

**(b)**

$$
13\times 9^{2}-4\times 3^{4}=3^{p}
$$

$$
13\times 3^{4}-4\times 3^{4}=3^{p}
$$

$$
3^{4}(13-4)=3^{p}
$$

$$
3^{4}\times 3^{2}=3^{p}
$$

$$
p=6
$$
