### Q26

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Sub-topic:** Vectors
> **Page:** 20

**(a)**

$$
\overrightarrow{QS} = \overrightarrow{QR}+\overrightarrow{RS} = -\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{a}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{PT} = \overrightarrow{PS}+\overrightarrow{ST} = -\mathbf{b}+\mathbf{a}
$$

Therefore $\overrightarrow{QS} = \overrightarrow{PT}$.

**(b)** Since $PQRS$ is a parallelogram, $PS \parallel QR$, $PQ \parallel SR$, $PS = QR$ and $PQ = SR$.

From (a), $PT = QS$. Also $PT = QS = PS = QR$ and $PQ = SR = TS$.

By the SSS congruency test, triangles $PTS$, $PQS$ and $QSR$ are congruent, therefore the trapezium is made up of three isosceles triangles.
