## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 3–4

(a) Solve $\dfrac{2x+7}{2}-\dfrac{7-x}{5} = 0$. [2]

(b) Solve the equation $(2x-3)(x-4) = 2$. [4]

(c) Simplify $\dfrac{15x^{2}}{2y}\div\dfrac{3x^{2}y}{8}$, leaving your answer in positive index form. [1]

(d) Simplify $\dfrac{ax+3bx-2a-6b}{a^{2}+ab-6b^{2}}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $5(2x+7) = 2(7-x)$ [M1]

$10x+35 = 14-2x$

$12x = -21$

$x = -1\dfrac{3}{4}$, also accept $-\dfrac{7}{4}$. [A1]

Alternative: $5(2x+7)-2(7-x) = 0$.

(b) $(2x-3)(x-4) = 2$

$2x^{2}-11x+10 = 0$ [M1]

$x = \dfrac{11\pm\sqrt{(-11)^{2}-4(2)(10)}}{2(2)}$ [M1]

$x = \dfrac{11\pm\sqrt{41}}{4}$

$x = 1.15$ or $4.35$ [A1, A1]

(c) $\dfrac{15x^{2}}{2y}\times\dfrac{8}{3x^{2}y} = \dfrac{20}{y^{2}}$ [B1]

(d) $\dfrac{(a+3b)(x-2)}{(a+3b)(a-2b)} = \dfrac{x-2}{a-2b}$ [M1 numerator, M1 denominator, A1]
