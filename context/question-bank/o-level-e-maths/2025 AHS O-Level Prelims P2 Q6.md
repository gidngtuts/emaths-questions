## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 8

In the Singapore F1 Grand Prix, the drivers must complete 62 laps around a circuit that is $4.940$ km long. One F1 driver completed 32 laps in 65 minutes.

What must be the minimum average speed, in km/h, for the remaining laps if he wants to complete the race in at most two hours? [3]

## Answer Scheme

In the Singapore F1 Grand Prix, the drivers must complete 62 laps around a circuit that is $4.940$ km long. One F1 driver completed 32 laps in 65 minutes.

What must be the minimum average speed for the remaining laps if he wants to complete the race in at most two hours?

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Distance travelled in the first 32 laps}&=32\times 4.940\\
&=158.08\ \mathrm{km}\\
\text{Time taken}&=\dfrac{65}{60}\ \text{hours}\\
\text{Distance to travel in the next 30 laps}&=30\times 4.940\\
&=148.2\ \mathrm{km}\\
\dfrac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}&\leq\dfrac{55}{60}\\
\dfrac{148.2}{\text{Speed}}&\leq\dfrac{55}{60}\\
148.2&\leq\text{Speed}\times\dfrac{55}{60}\\
\text{Speed}&\geq 148.2\div\dfrac{55}{60}\\
\text{Speed}&\geq 161.67\ \text{km per hour}
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** for calculating the distance and times for the first 32 laps. **M1** for setting up the inequality or equation to calculate the minimum speed in the next 30 laps. **A1**
