## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 8

(a) Factorise $a^{2}b^{4}-ab^{2}$. [1]

(b) Expand and simplify $(3x+2)^{2}+4x(2-x)$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $ab^{2}(ab^{2}-1)$ — B1.

(b)

$$
(3x+2)^{2}+4x(2-x) = 9x^{2}+12x+4+8x-4x^{2} = 5x^{2}+20x+4
$$

M1 for $9x^{2}+12x+4$. M1 for $8x-4x^{2}$. A1.
