## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 4

Rearrange the formula to make $c$ the subject.

$$
D=(a-c)^{2}-a^{2}
$$

[3]

## Answer Scheme

Rearrange the formula to make $c$ the subject. $D=(a-c)^{2}-a^{2}$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
D&=(a-c)^{2}-a^{2}\\
(a-c)^{2}&=D+a^{2}\\
a-c&=\pm\sqrt{D+a^{2}}\\
c&=a\pm\sqrt{D+a^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
