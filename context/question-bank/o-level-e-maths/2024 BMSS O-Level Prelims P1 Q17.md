## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 16–17

The cumulative frequency graph shows the distribution of the marks of $200$ students for a Biology test.

![Cumulative frequency graph of Biology marks for 200 students](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BMSS/images/Bendemeer-2024_Prelim-Exam_4E5N_EMath_P1-QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Use the curve to estimate

**(i)** the median mark for the test, [1]

**(ii)** the interquartile range for the test, [1]

**(iii)** the percentage of the students scored more than $70$ marks for the test. [2]

**(b)** The box-and-whisker plot represents the distribution of marks of the same $200$ students for a Chemistry test.

![Box-and-whisker plot of Chemistry marks](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BMSS/images/Bendemeer-2024_Prelim-Exam_4E5N_EMath_P1-QP-pages/page-17.png)

Make two comparisons between the marks for the two tests. Use figures to support your answers. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)** Median $=30$ [B1]

**(ii)** $\operatorname{IQR}=46-22=24$ [B1]

**(iii)**

$$
\frac{200-185}{200}\times 100\%
$$

[M1]

$$
=7.5\%
$$

[A1]

**(b)**

1. Students did better in the Chemistry test on average since the median of Chemistry ($38$) is greater than the median of Biology ($30$). [A1]

2. Students have more consistent marks in the Biology test since the IQR of Biology ($24$) is smaller than the IQR of Chemistry ($32$). [A1]
