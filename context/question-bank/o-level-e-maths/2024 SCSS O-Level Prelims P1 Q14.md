## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 9

**(a)** A regular polygon has interior angles of $140^{\circ}$. Find the number of sides of the polygon.

**(b)** The diagram shows a sketch of a regular octagon and an equilateral triangle. The triangle is attached outwardly to one side of the octagon. The angle $x^{\circ}$ is the angle between that shared side’s neighbouring octagon side and the adjacent side of the triangle.

![Regular octagon with an equilateral triangle and angle x](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/SCSS/images/SCSS-2024-EM-Prelim-P1-QP-pages/page-09.png)

Calculate $x$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Exterior angle $=180-140=40$. Number of sides $=360\div 40=9$ (M1, A1)

Or $(n-2)\times 180=140n$ (M1), $180n-360=140n$, $40n=360$, $n=9$ (A1)

**(b)** Exterior angle of octagon $=360\div 8=45$ (M1)

Exterior angle of equilateral triangle $=180-60=120$

$x=120+45=165$ (A1)
