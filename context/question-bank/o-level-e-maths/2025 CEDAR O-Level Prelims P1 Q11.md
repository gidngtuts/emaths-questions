## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 9

The table shows the income tax rates for different tax brackets.

| Chargeable income | Income tax rate (%) |
| --- | --- |
| First &#36;20 000 | 0 |
| Next &#36;10 000 | 2 |
| Next &#36;10 000 | 3.5 |
| Next &#36;40 000 | 7 |
| Next &#36;40 000 | 11.5 |

In 2022, given that David’s chargeable income is &#36;60,500, find his income tax payable for YA 2022. [2]

## Answer Scheme

In 2022, given that David’s chargeable income is &#36;60,500, find his income tax payable for YA 2022.

$$
\text{Income tax payable} = &#36;10000\times\frac{2}{100}+&#36;10000\times\frac{3.5}{100}+&#36;20500\times\frac{7}{100} = &#36;1985
$$
