## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 8

**(a)** Factorise $a^{4}-b^{2}$.

**(b)** Factorise completely $8xy-1-4x+2y$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $a^{4}-b^{2}=(a^{2}+b)(a^{2}-b)$ (B1)

**(b)** $8xy-1-4x+2y=8xy-4x+2y-1=4x(2y-1)+2y-1$ (M1)

$=(2y-1)(4x+1)$ (A1)
