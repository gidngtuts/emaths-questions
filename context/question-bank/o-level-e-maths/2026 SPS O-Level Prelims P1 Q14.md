## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 10

$A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on a circle with centre $O$. $AOC$ and $ABD$ are straight lines,
Angle $CAB=28^{\circ}$ and Angle $BDC=60^{\circ}$.

![Question 14 circle with centre O](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/SPS/images/SPS-EM-2026-4G3-P1-Final-pages/page-10.png)

**(a)** Find angle $DCB$.
Give a reason for each step of your working. [2]

**(b)** Explain why $DC$ is **not** a tangent to the circle. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle ABC=90^{\circ}$ (angle in semicircle)

$\angle DBC=180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}=90^{\circ}$ (angles on straight line $ABD$)

$$
\angle DCB=180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-60^{\circ}=30^{\circ}
$$

(angle sum of $\triangle BDC$)

**(b)** If $DC$ were a tangent at $C$, then $\angle DCB$ would equal $\angle BAC=28^{\circ}$ (tangent–chord, alternate segment).
But $\angle DCB=30^{\circ}\neq 28^{\circ}$, so $DC$ is not a tangent.
