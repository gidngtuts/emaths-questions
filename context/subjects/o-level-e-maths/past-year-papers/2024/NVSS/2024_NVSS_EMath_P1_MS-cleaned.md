### Q6

> **Marks:** 2
> **Sub-topic:** Geometry
> **Page:** 5

Size of each exterior angle $=\dfrac{180}{9}\times 4=80^{\circ}$ B1

Since the number of sides $=\dfrac{360^{\circ}}{80^{\circ}}=4.5$ is not a positive integer, it is not possible to form a regular polygon. B1

Alternative: let $n$ be the number of sides.

$(n-2)\times 180 : 360$, so $(n-2):2$, and with the given ratio this leads to $n=4.5$.

Or exterior $+$ interior $=180^{\circ}$, and $\dfrac{(n-2)\times 180}{n}=\dfrac{5}{9}\times 180$, so $n=4.5$.
