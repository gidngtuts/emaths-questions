## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 15

**(a)** 8 men working 6 hours a day, can build a cupboard in 2 days.

Find the additional number of days required to build a cupboard by 3 men working 4 hours a day at the same rate. [2]

**(b)** $y^{3}$ is inversely proportional to the square of $x$.
The difference between the values of $y^{3}$ when $x = 4$ and when $x = 8$ is 3.

Find the values of $x$ when $y = 1$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** 1 cupboard : 96 man-hours

$$
\begin{aligned}
96\div(3\times 4) &= 8\text{ days} \\
8-2 &= 6\text{ days}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: total number of days. A1.

**(b)** $y^{3}=\dfrac{k}{x^{2}}$ where $k$ is a constant.

$$
\begin{aligned}
k\left(\frac{1}{4^{2}}-\frac{1}{8^{2}}\right) &= 3 \\
k\left(\frac{1}{16}-\frac{1}{64}\right) &= 3 \\
\frac{3}{64}k &= 3 \\
k &= 64 \\
x &= 8\text{ or }-8
\end{aligned}
$$

M1. M1: Find $k$. A1.

> **Markers Report:** Generally quite well done if they managed to get the first equation. Some mistakes include not having a constant ($k$) in the equation. If there are 2 sets of workings, we will pick the one that results in lower marks and there would be no marks if there is no $k$.
