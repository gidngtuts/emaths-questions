## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

The diagram represents a plot of land, $ABCD$, which is to be used for a park.

![Plot of land ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ACSBR/images/ACSBR-2024-4E5N-Prelim-Math-P1-QP-pages/page-06.png)

(a) Construct the bisector of angle $ABC$.

(b) Construct the perpendicular bisector of $AD$.

(c) A toilet is to be built in the park, nearer to $A$ than to $D$ and nearer to $BC$ than to $AB$. Shade the region where the toilet is to be built.

## Answer Scheme

Construction arcs are to be clearly seen.

- Correct angle bisector of $\angle ABC$.
- Correct perpendicular bisector of $AD$.
- Correct region shaded: on the $A$ side of the perpendicular bisector of $AD$, and on the $BC$ side of the angle bisector of $\angle ABC$.
