## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 24–25

The lines $ABC$ and $CDE$ are tangents to the circle at $B$ and $D$ respectively.
$F$, $G$ and $H$ are points on the circle and the centre of the circle is $O$.
The straight lines $BD$ and $HF$ intersect at $J$.
It is given that $\angle ACE=62^{\circ}$ and $\angle DHF=40^{\circ}$.

![Circle with centre O. Tangents from C touch at B and D and continue to A and E. Chord BD meets HF at J. Angle at C is 62 degrees and angle DHF is 40 degrees.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_Prelim_P1_QP-pages/page-24.png)

Find, giving reasons for each answer,

**(a)** $\angle FGD$, [1]

**(b)** $\angle CDO$, [1]

**(c)** $\angle BOF$, [2]

**(c)** $\angle FJD$. [2]

The second part is printed as (c) again.

## Answer Scheme

The question paper prints the last part as (c) again. The scheme labels it (d).

**(a)**

$$
\angle FGD=180^{\circ}-\angle FHD=180^{\circ}-40^{\circ}=140^{\circ}
$$

(angles in opposite segments)

**(b)**

$$
\angle CDO=90^{\circ}
$$

(tangent perpendicular to radius)

**(c)**

$$
\angle ODH=40^{\circ}
$$

(base angle of an isosceles triangle)

$$
\angle FOD=40^{\circ}+40^{\circ}=80^{\circ}
$$

(exterior angle of a triangle)

$$
\angle BOF=360^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-62^{\circ}-80^{\circ}=38^{\circ}
$$

(angle sum of quadrilateral $BODC$)

**(d)**

$$
\angle BDF=\frac{38^{\circ}}{2}=19^{\circ}
$$

(angle at the centre $=2\times$ angle at the circumference)

$$
\angle OFD=\frac{180^{\circ}-80^{\circ}}{2}=50^{\circ}
$$

(base angle of an isosceles triangle)

$$
\angle FJD=180^{\circ}-50^{\circ}-19^{\circ}=111^{\circ}
$$
