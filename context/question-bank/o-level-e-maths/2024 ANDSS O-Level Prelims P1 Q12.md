## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

$$
\xi = \{(x,y): x\text{ and }y\text{ are integers, }-2\leq x<1\text{ and }-1\leq y\leq 1\}
$$

$$
P = \{(x,y): x^{2}+y^{2}<2\}
$$

$$
Q = \{(x,y): xy<0\}
$$

**(a)** Find the number of elements in the universal set $\xi$. [1]

**(b)** List the elements of the following in set notation.

**(i)** $P$ [1]

**(ii)** $Q$ [1]

## Answer Scheme

The scheme labels the second list as (c). On the question paper it is (b)(ii).

**(a)**

$$
n(\xi)=3\times 3=9
$$

**(b)**

$$
P=\{(-1,0),\ (0,-1),\ (0,0),\ (0,1)\}
$$

**(c)**

$$
Q=\{(-2,1),\ (-1,1)\}
$$
