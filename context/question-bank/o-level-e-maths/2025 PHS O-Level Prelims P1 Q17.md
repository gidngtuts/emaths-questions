## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 15

Given that $2x-y=8$ and $xy=4$, without solving for $x$ and $y$, find the value of $4x^{2}+y^{2}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
(2x-y)^{2}=64 \Rightarrow 4x^{2}+y^{2}=64+4xy=80
$$
