## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** Expand and simplify $-(5x-2y)(-3x+4y)$. [2]

**(b)** Factorise completely $9ab-12ac+28bc-21b^{2}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
-(5x-2y)(-3x+4y)
$$

$$
=(-5x+2y)(-3x+4y)\text{ or }-(-15x^{2}+20xy+6xy-8y^{2})
$$

$$
=15x^{2}-20xy-6xy+8y^{2}
$$

$$
=15x^{2}-26xy+8y^{2}
$$

**(b)**

$$
9ab-12ac+28bc-21b^{2}
$$

$$
=3a(3b-4c)-7b(3b-4c)\text{ or }3a(3b-4c)+7b(4c-3b)
$$

$$
=(3b-4c)(3a-7b)
$$
