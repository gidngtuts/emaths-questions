## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 11

In a regular polygon, the ratio of an interior angle to an exterior angle is $14:1$.

(a) Find the number of sides of the polygon. [3]

(b) Find the sum of the interior angles of the polygon. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) 15 units correspond to $180^{\circ}$, so 1 unit corresponds to $12^{\circ}$. Number of sides $= \dfrac{360^{\circ}}{12^{\circ}} = 30$. M1, M1, A1.

(b) Sum of interior angles $= (30-2)\times 180^{\circ} = 5040^{\circ}$. A1, with error carried forward.
