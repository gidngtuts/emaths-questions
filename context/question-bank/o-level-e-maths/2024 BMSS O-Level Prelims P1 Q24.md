## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 24

The diagram shows a closed L-shaped rectangular structure with dimensions stated.

![Closed L-shaped rectangular structure](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BMSS/images/Bendemeer-2024_Prelim-Exam_4E5N_EMath_P1-QP-pages/page-24.png)

**(a)** Calculate the volume of the L-shaped rectangular structure. [2]

**(b)** Edison wants to put a thin and long metal rod inside the structure. What is the longest length of the metal rod that Edison can put in? [3]

End of Paper

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
80\times 20\times 10+40\times 20\times 10
$$

[M1]

$$
=24000\text{ mm}^{3}
$$

[A1]

**(b)**

$$
\frac{x}{20}=\frac{80}{60}
$$

$$
x=26\frac{2}{3}
$$

[M1]

$$
l=\sqrt{\left(26\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}+80^{2}+10^{2}}
$$

[M1]

Longest length of the metal rod, $l=84.9\text{ mm}$. [A1]
