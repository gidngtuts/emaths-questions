## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 18

A train travels at a constant speed of $40\text{ m/s}$ for $50$ seconds.
It then slows down at a constant rate until it comes at rest in $20$ seconds.

**(a)** On the axes, draw the speed-time graph for the journey. The vertical axis is speed in m/s, marked at $0$, $10$, $20$, $30$ and $40$. The horizontal axis is time in seconds, marked at $10$, $20$, $30$, $40$, $50$, $60$, $70$ and $80$. [1]

**(b)** Calculate the distance travelled by the train during the first $60$ seconds. [2]
