## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Solve $\dfrac{x-3}{3}-\dfrac{1-x^{2}}{2-3x}=1$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{x-3}{3}-\frac{1-x^{2}}{2-3x}=1
$$

$$
(x-3)(2-3x)-3(1-x^{2})=3(2-3x)
$$

$$
2x-3x^{2}-6+9x-3+3x^{2}=6-9x
$$

$$
11x-9=6-9x
$$

$$
20x=15
$$

$$
x=\frac{3}{4}\text{ or }0.75
$$
