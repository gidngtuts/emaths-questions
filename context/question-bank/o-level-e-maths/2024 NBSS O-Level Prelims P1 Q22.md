## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 19

$A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on the circle, centre $O$.
$DE$ is a tangent to the circle at $A$.
Angle $AOC = 4x^{\circ}$ and angle $ACB = x^{\circ}$.

![Circle with centre O, tangent DE and angles 4x and x](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-_Prelim_P1_2024-v2_QP-pages/page-19.png)

Find, in terms of $x$, the following.
Give a reason for each step of your answer.

**(a)** angle $ABC$,

**(b)** angle $BAE$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Reflex $\angle AOC = 360^{\circ}-4x^{\circ}$ (angles at a point).

$$
\angle ABC = \frac{360^{\circ}-4x^{\circ}}{2} = 180^{\circ}-2x^{\circ}
$$

(angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference).

**(b)** $\angle BAC = 180^{\circ}-x^{\circ}-(180^{\circ}-2x^{\circ}) = x^{\circ}$ (sum of angles in a triangle).

$\angle BAE = 90^{\circ}-(90^{\circ}-2x^{\circ})-x^{\circ} = x^{\circ}$ (radius perpendicular to tangent).
