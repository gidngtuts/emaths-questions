\frac{x}{4}-\frac{3x-2}{5} = -1.
$$

[3]

## Answer Scheme

Solve the equation $\dfrac{x}{4}-\dfrac{3x-2}{5}=-1$.

$$\frac{x}{4}-\frac{3x-2}{5}=-1$$

$$5x-12x+8=-20$$

$$-7x=-28$$

$$x=4$$
