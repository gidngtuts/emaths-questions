## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 3–4

(a) $b = \dfrac{a}{a-1}+\dfrac{2}{c}$

(i) Find the value of $b$ when $a = 3$ and $c = 4$. [1]

(ii) Rearrange the formula to make $a$ the subject. [3]

(b) Solve these simultaneous equations. [3]

$$
4x-y = -11
$$

$$
5x+3y = -1
$$

(c) Solve the equation $\dfrac{x}{3x-1}-\dfrac{5}{2x+3} = 1$. Give your solutions correct to three decimal places. [4]

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) $b = \dfrac{3}{3-1}+\dfrac{2}{4} = 2$. [B1]

(ii) $bc(a-1) = ac+2(a-1)$. [M1, common denominator]

$a(bc-c-2) = bc-2$. [M1]

$a = \dfrac{bc-2}{bc-c-2}$ or $\dfrac{2-bc}{2-bc+c}$. [A1]

(b) $12x-3y = -33$. Add to $5x+3y = -1$ to get $17x = -34$, so $x = -2$. [M1, A1]

$4(-2)-y = -11$, so $y = 3$. [A1]

(c) $\dfrac{x(2x+3)-5(3x-1)}{(3x-1)(2x+3)} = 1$. [M1]

$4x^{2}+19x-8 = 0$. [M1]

$x = \dfrac{-19\pm\sqrt{489}}{8}$. [M1]

$x = 0.389$ or $x = -5.139$ (3 d.p.). [A1]
