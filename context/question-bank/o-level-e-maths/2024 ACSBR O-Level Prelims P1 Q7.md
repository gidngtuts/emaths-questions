## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

(a) Solve the inequalities $-8 \le 2-3x < 7-\dfrac{1}{2}x$.

(b) Write down all the integers that satisfy $-8 \le 2-3x < 7-\dfrac{1}{2}x$.

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
-8 \le 2-3x \quad \text{and} \quad 2-3x < 7-\frac{1}{2}x
$$

$$
-10 \le -3x \quad \text{and} \quad -5 < \frac{5}{2}x
$$

$$
-2 < x \le 3\frac{1}{3}
$$

Do not accept $3.33$.

(b) $-1, 0, 1, 2, 3$
