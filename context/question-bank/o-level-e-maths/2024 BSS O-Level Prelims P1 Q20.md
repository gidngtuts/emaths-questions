## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

Simplify $\dfrac{2x^{2}-5xy-12y^{2}}{x^{2}-16y^{2}}$.

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{2x^{2}-5xy-12y^{2}}{x^{2}-16y^{2}}$

$=\dfrac{(x-4y)(2x+3y)}{(x+4y)(x-4y)}$ (M1, M1)

$=\dfrac{2x+3y}{x+4y}$ (A1)
