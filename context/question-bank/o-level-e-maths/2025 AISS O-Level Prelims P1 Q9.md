## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

Factorise $6x^{2}y^{2}+xy-3x^{2}y-2xy^{2}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Factorise $6x^{2}y^{2}+xy-3x^{2}y-2xy^{2}$.

$$
6x^{2}y^{2}+xy-3x^{2}y-2xy^{2}=xy(6xy+1-3x-2y)
$$

M1 for extracting common term.

$$
=xy\bigl(3x(2y-1)-(2y-1)\bigr)=xy(3x-1)(2y-1)
$$

M1 for factorising by grouping. A1.
