## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

During a school's anniversary, Joey sold $x$ cookies at 50 cents each and $(x+30)$ chocolates at 90 cents each. Form an inequality in $x$ and solve it to find the minimum value of $x$ in order for her to receive at least ＄152 from the sales. [3]

## Answer Scheme

In cents: $50x+90(x+30)\ge 15200$ [M1, correct inequality]

$50x+90x+2700\ge 15200$

$140x\ge 12500$

$x\ge 89.285$ [M1, for simplification]

$x = 90$ [A1]

Alternative in dollars: $0.5x+0.9(x+30)\ge 152$, so $1.4x\ge 125$ and $x\ge 89.285$, hence $x = 90$.

Penalise under presentation on the cover page if students write $x\ge 90$.
