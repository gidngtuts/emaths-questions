## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Mdm Siti took a bank loan of &#36;12 000 where the bank charges an annual compound interest of $7\%$ compounded half-yearly.
Find the interest earned at the end of 2 years. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$P=&#36;12000$, $R=\dfrac{7}{2}=3.5\%$, $n=4$

$$
A=P\left(1+\frac{R}{100}\right)^{n}=12000\left(1+\frac{3.5}{100}\right)^{4}=13770.27601=&#36;13770.28
$$

Compound interest

$$
=13770.27601-&#36;12000=&#36;1770.27601=&#36;1770.28\ (2\text{ d.p.})
$$
