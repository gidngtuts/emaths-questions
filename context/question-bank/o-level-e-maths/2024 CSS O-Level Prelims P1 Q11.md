## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 8

**(a)** The angles of a quadrilateral taken in order are $3x$, $6x$, $5x$ and $4x$.
Find the value of $x$. [1]

**(b)** Explain whether the quadrilateral is a parallelogram or a trapezium. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $18x = 360^{\circ}$, so $x = 20^{\circ}$.

**(b)** $3x+6x = 9x = 180^{\circ}$. The angles are interior angles.

$6x+5x = 11x \neq 180^{\circ}$.

The quadrilateral is a trapezium and not a parallelogram as it has 2 parallel sides and 2 non-parallel sides.
