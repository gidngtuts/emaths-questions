## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

The expression $2+px-x^{2}$ can be written in the form $q-(x-3)^{2}$.

(a) Find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [2]

(b) Explain why when $x = 3$, the expression has its maximum value. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $2+px-x^{2} = q-(x^{2}-6x+9) = (q-9)+6x-x^{2}$.

Comparing coefficients, $p = 6$ and $2 = q-9$, so $q = 11$. [2]

(b) $(x-3)^{2}$ is least when $x = 3$, and the coefficient of $x^{2}$ is negative, so $-(x-3)^{2}$ (and the expression) is greatest when $x = 3$. [1]
