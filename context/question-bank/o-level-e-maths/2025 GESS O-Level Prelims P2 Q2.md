## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 3, (d)(i) 1, (d)(ii) 1; total 7
> **Page:** 5–6

The organiser of the SG60 events held SG60 concerts on two specific weekends in June 2025.
The seats in the concert hall were divided into three sections. The table below summarises the number of tickets sold on Saturday and Sunday in week 1.

|  | Section A | Section B | Section C (obstructed view) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Saturday | 164 | 144 | 50 |
| Sunday | 128 | 90 | 40 |

**(a)** Write down a $2\times 3$ matrix $A$ to represent the information shown in the above table. [1]

The price per ticket for each section is as shown in the table below.

|  | Section A | Section B | Section C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price per ticket | &#36;30 | &#36;25 | &#36;20 |

**(b)** Represent the price of the tickets in a $3\times 1$ column matrix $P$. [1]

**(c)** Evaluate the matrix $(1\ 1)AP$ and interpret the element(s) in the matrix. [2] [1]

**(d)** In week 2, the number of tickets sold changes for the various sections on Saturday and Sunday.

The number tickets sold for section A increased by $25\%$.
The number of tickets sold for section B increased by $50\%$.
The number to tickets sold for section C decreased to $80\%$.

**(i)** Write down a matrix $M$ such that the elements of matrix $F$, where $F=AM$, represents the number of tickets sold for sections A, B and C respectively on Saturday and Sunday in week 2. [1]

**(ii)** State what each element of matrix $FP$ represents. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{pmatrix}164&144&50\\ 128&90&40\end{pmatrix}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\begin{pmatrix}30\\ 25\\ 20\end{pmatrix}
$$

**(c)**

$$
AP=\begin{pmatrix}164&144&50\\ 128&90&40\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}30\\ 25\\ 20\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}9520\\ 6890\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
(1\ 1)AP=(1\ 1)\begin{pmatrix}9520\\ 6890\end{pmatrix}=(16410)
$$

The element $16410$ represents the total amount collected from the sales of tickets for Saturday and Sunday for the 1st weekend.

**(d)(i)**

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1.25&0&0\\ 0&1.5&0\\ 0&0&0.8\end{pmatrix}
$$

**(d)(ii)** Each element in matrix $FP$ represents the total amount collected from the sales of tickets for Saturday and Sunday respectively for the 2nd weekend.
