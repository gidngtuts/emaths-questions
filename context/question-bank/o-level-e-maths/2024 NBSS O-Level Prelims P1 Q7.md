## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 6

Given that $81^{2x}\times 9^{x} = 27$, find $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
81^{2x}\times 9^{x} &= 27 \\
(3^{4})^{2x}\times (3^{2})^{x} &= 3^{3} \\
8x+2x &= 3 \\
x &= 0.3
\end{aligned}
$$

Or $9^{4x}\times 9^{x} = 9^{1.5}$, so $5x = 1.5$ and $x = 0.3$.
