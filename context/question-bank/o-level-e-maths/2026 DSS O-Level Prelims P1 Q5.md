## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Solve the equation $x^{2}-7x+4=0$ by completing the square.
Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left(x-\frac{7}{2}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^{2}+4&=0\\
x-\frac{7}{2}&=\pm\sqrt{\frac{33}{4}}\\
x&=6.37\text{ or }x=0.63
\end{aligned}
$$
