## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 15

In triangle $ABD$, $AC$ is perpendicular to $BD$.
Angle $ADC = 48^{\circ}$, $AC = 8.4$ cm and $BC = 5$ cm.

![Triangle ABD with altitude AC to BD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-_Prelim_P1_2024-v2_QP-pages/page-15.png)

Calculate

**(a)** angle $ABC$,

**(b)** $AD$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\tan\angle ABC = \dfrac{8.4}{5}$, so $\angle ABC = 59.2^{\circ}$.

**(b)** $\sin 48^{\circ} = \dfrac{8.4}{AD}$, so $AD = 11.3$ cm.
