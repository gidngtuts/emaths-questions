## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 1, (d) 1, (e) 3; total 11
> **Page:** 8–9

Jennie travels from Town $A$ to Town $B$ at a speed of $x$ km/h.
The journey takes $1$ hour.
She then spends $30$ minutes having her lunch in Town $B$, before setting off to Town $C$, which takes $x$ minutes at $(x-20)$ km/h.

The total distance of the journey is $120$ km.

**(a)** Show that $x^{2}+40x-7200=0$. [3]

**(b)** Solve the equation $x^{2}+40x-7200=0$.
Give your solutions correct to $2$ decimal places. [3]

**(c)** Explain why one of the solutions in part (b) must be rejected. [1]

**(d)** Calculate the difference between the times Jennie spent driving from Town $A$ to Town $B$ and from Town $B$ to Town $C$.
Give your answer in minutes and seconds, correct to the nearest second. [1]

**(e)** Calculate Jennie's average speed for the entire journey. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Town $A$ to Town $B$: speed $x$ km/h, time $1$ h. Town $B$ to Town $C$: speed $(x-20)$ km/h, time $x$ minutes.

$$
\text{Total distance}=x(1)+(x-20)\left(\frac{x}{60}\right)=x+\frac{x(x-20)}{60}
$$

M1 for the correct distance from Town $B$ to Town $C$, $\dfrac{x(x-20)}{60}$.

$$
x+\frac{x(x-20)}{60}=120
$$

M1 for equating the total distance to $120$.

$$
60x+x(x-20)=7200
$$

$$
x^{2}+40x-7200=0\ (\text{shown})
$$

A1.

**(b)**

$$
x=\frac{-40\pm\sqrt{40^{2}-4(1)(-7200)}}{2}
$$

M1 for substitution into the quadratic formula correctly.

$$
x=67.18\quad\text{or}\quad x=-107.18\ (2\text{ d.p.})
$$

A1 for each correct $x$. Deduct one mark if not to $2$ d.p.

**(c)** $x=-107.18$ is rejected as time/speed cannot be negative. B1.

**(d)** Difference in time

$$
=67.17797887-60=7.17797887\text{ minutes}
$$

$$
=7\text{ minutes }11\text{ seconds}
$$

B1.

**(e)** Total distance $=120$ km.

$$
\text{Total time}=1+0.5+\frac{67.17797887}{60}=2.619632981\text{ h}
$$

M1 for the correct calculation of total time.

$$
\text{Average speed}=\frac{120}{2.619632981}=45.8\text{ km/h}
$$

M1 for substitution of total distance over total time. A1.
