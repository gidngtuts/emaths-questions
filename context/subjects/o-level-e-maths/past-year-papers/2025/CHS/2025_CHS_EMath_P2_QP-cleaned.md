$$

$$

$$
\text{Arc length} = r\theta,\text{ where }\theta\text{ is in radians}

$$
\text{Sector area} = \frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta,\text{ where }\theta\text{ is in radians}

$$
\text{Mean} = \frac{\sum fx}{\sum f}
