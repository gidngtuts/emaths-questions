## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 2, (d)(i) 2, (d)(ii) 2, (d)(iii) 3; total 12
> **Page:** 8–9

**(a)** The variables $x$ and $y$ are connected by the equation $y = \dfrac{7}{x}+2x-11$.
Some corresponding values are given in the table below.
Values are given to $1$ decimal place where appropriate.

| $x$ | $0.5$ | $1$ | $1.5$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ | $6.5$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $y$ | | $-2$ | $-3.3$ | $-3.5$ | $-2.7$ | $-1.25$ | $0.4$ | $2.2$ | $3.1$ |

Fill in the missing value in the table. [1]

**(b)** On the grid below, draw the graph of $y = \dfrac{7}{x}+2x-11$ for $0.5 \leq x \leq 6.5$. [2]

![Question 4 graph grid](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CEDAR/images/CEDAR-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-08.png)

**(c)** By drawing a tangent, find the gradient of the curve at $x = 2$. [2]

**(d)** Using your graph,

**(i)** explain why the equation $2x+\dfrac{7}{x}-7 = 0$ has no solution. [2]

**(ii)** find the $x$-coordinate of the points where the line $2y = 4-x$ intersects the curve. [2]

**(iii)** These values of $x$ are the solutions of the equation $Ax^{2}+Bx+14 = 0$.

Find the value of $A$ and of $B$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** When $x = 0.5$, $y = 4$.

**(b)** Draw $y = \dfrac{7}{x}+2x-11$.

![Question 4 drawn curve](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CEDAR/images/CEDAR-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-MS-pages/page-08.png)

**(c)** By drawing a tangent,

$$
\text{Gradient} = \frac{-3-(-3.5)}{4-2} = 0.25
$$

($\pm 0.13$)

**(d) (i)**

$$
2x+\frac{7}{x}-11 = -4
$$

The line $y = -4$ is below the minimum point of / lies entirely below the curve $y = 2x+\dfrac{7}{x}-11$ and therefore, will not intersect the curve at any point.

**(ii)** Draw $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$.

From graph, $x$-coordinate of points $= 0.6$ and $4.6$ ($\pm 0.1$).

**(iii)**

$$
-\frac{1}{2}x+2 = 2x+\frac{7}{x}-11
$$

Multiply by $2x$:

$$
-x^{2}+4x = 4x^{2}+14-22x
$$

$$
0 = 5x^{2}-26x+14
$$

By comparing coefficients, $A = 5$ and $B = -26$.
