## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 4, (b) 6; total 10
> **Page:** 20–23

A pottery artist in Singapore makes a clay sculpture consisting of two identical solid hemispheres and a solid cylinder as shown in the diagram below.
The hemisphere has a radius of 14 cm.
The cylinder has a radius of 2.5 cm and height of 6 cm.

The two hemispheres sit at the ends of the cylinder. The similar sculpture uses the same arrangement.

The artist made another geometrically similar clay sculpture.
The radius of the hemisphere of the second sculpture is 7 cm.

**(a)** Given that the clay used has a density of $1.5\text{ g/cm}^{3}$, show that the total mass of the two sculptures is 19.59 kg correct to 4 significant figures.

After completing the two sculptures, he has to deliver them to Australia within 2 weeks via postage. The following tables show the mailing prices and other costs at the post office.

| Destinations (Zones) | Airmail Rate (Delivery in 2–9 days): 1st 5 kg | Airmail additional 1 kg or part thereof | Surface Mail Rate (Delivery in 15–30 days): 1st 5 kg | Surface additional 1 kg or part thereof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zone A Malaysia | ＄16 | ＄3 | – | – |
| Zone B Brunei, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Philippines, Taiwan and Thailand | ＄30 | ＄5 | ＄18 | ＄2 |
| Zone C China, India, South Korea, rest of Asia | ＄30 | ＄5 | ＄18 | ＄2 |
| Zone D Australia, Japan and New Zealand | ＄40 | ＄7 | ＄20 | ＄2 |

| Carton Size | Dimension (cm) | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XS | $20 \times 15 \times 9$ | ＄3.90 |
| S | $30 \times 25 \times 15$ | ＄4.90 |
| M | $35 \times 25 \times 15$ | ＄5.90 |
| L | $40 \times 22 \times 22$ | ＄6.90 |
| XL | $45 \times 30 \times 30$ | ＄7.90 |
| XXL | $55 \times 32 \times 32$ | ＄8.90 |

| Items | Dimensions | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaging foam peanut (to fill empty space in the carton) | Small bag – $1500\text{ cm}^{3}$ | ＄2 |
| | Medium bag – $7500\text{ cm}^{3}$ | ＄5 |
| | Large bag – $60000\text{ cm}^{3}$ | ＄39 |

How to Pack and Seal Packages

1. Items may be packed in one carton or two separate cartons.
2. Fill all empty space with foam peanuts to protect the contents.
3. The weight of the carton box and foam peanuts is negligible.

**(b)** The cost of the clay he uses is ＄41.50 per pack. Each pack contains 5 kg of clay.
Given that the cost of delivery should be kept as low as possible, decide on the carton size(s) and suggest a sensible selling price of the sculptures.

Justify any decisions you make and show your calculations clearly.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Volume of the first sculpture $=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(14)^{3}+\pi(2.5)^{2}(6)=11611.85\text{ cm}^{3}$ M1

Volume of the second sculpture $=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{3}\times 11611.85=1451.481\text{ cm}^{3}$ M1

Mass $=(11611.85+1451.481)\times 1.5=19594\text{ g}=19.594\text{ kg}$ (at least 5 s.f.) M1

$=19.59\text{ kg}$ (shown) A1

The A1 is not awarded if the mass is not shown to at least 5 significant figures before rounding to 4 significant figures.

**(b)** Packs of clay $=\dfrac{19.5975}{5}=3.9195$, so 4 packs.

Cost of clay $=4\times 41.50=\text{＄}166$ M1

Airmail to Australia (Zone D), mass about 20 kg: $40+(20-5)\times 7=\text{＄}145$ M1

One carton, size XXL. Empty space $=(55\times 32\times 32)-\left(11611+\dfrac{11611}{8}\right)=43257.625\text{ cm}^{3}$ M1

Foam: six medium bags. Total $=166+145+8.90+(6\times 5)=\text{＄}349.90$

Two cartons, S and XL. Empty space $=(30\times 25\times 15)+(45\times 30\times 30)-\left(11611+\dfrac{11611}{8}\right)=38687.625\text{ cm}^{3}$ M1

Total $=166+145+4.90+7.90+(5\times 5+2)=\text{＄}350.80$

XXL is cheaper, so use the XXL carton. M1 for the comparison and the decision.

Selling price: any value above the total cost, with a reason such as profit, labour, or covering materials and delivery. A1. No A1 without a justification.
