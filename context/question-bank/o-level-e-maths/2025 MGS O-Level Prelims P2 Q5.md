## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (a)(iii) 1, (b) 2, (c) 2; total 7
> **Page:** 14–15

In the diagram, $ABC$ is a triangle such that $\overrightarrow{AB}=5\mathbf{b}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}=2\mathbf{a}$.
It is given that $\dfrac{BD}{DA}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, $\overrightarrow{BN}=\dfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BM}$ and $M$ is the midpoint of $AC$.

![Question 5 vector diagram](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/MGS/images/MGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-14.png)

**(a)** Write each of the following as simply as possible in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$,

**(i)** $\overrightarrow{AD}$, [1]

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{BM}$, [1]

**(iii)** $\overrightarrow{CD}$. [1]

**(b)** Show that $\overrightarrow{CN}=\dfrac{5}{3}(2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})$. [2]

**(c)** Explain why $C$, $N$ and $D$ lie in a straight line. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

**(i)** $\overrightarrow{AD}=4\mathbf{b}$ B1

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{BM}=\mathbf{a}-5\mathbf{b}$ B1

**(iii)** $\overrightarrow{CD}=-2\mathbf{a}+4\mathbf{b}$ B1

**(b)** Show that $\overrightarrow{CN}=\dfrac{5}{3}(2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})$.

$$
\overrightarrow{CN}=\overrightarrow{CM}+\overrightarrow{MN}=-\mathbf{a}+\frac{2}{3}(5\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})
$$

M1

$$
=-\mathbf{a}+\frac{10}{3}\mathbf{b}-\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}=-\frac{5}{3}\mathbf{a}+\frac{10}{3}\mathbf{b}=\frac{5}{3}(2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})
$$

A1

**(c)** Explain why $C$, $N$ and $D$ lie in a straight line.

$$
\overrightarrow{CD}=2(2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{CN}=\frac{5}{3}(2\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})=\frac{5}{6}\overrightarrow{CD}
$$

M1

Since $\overrightarrow{CN}=\dfrac{5}{6}\overrightarrow{CD}$, and $C$ is a common point, $\therefore C$, $N$ and $D$ lie in a straight line. A1
