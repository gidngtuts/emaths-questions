## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 14

A plot of land $ABCD$ is given below.

![Quadrilateral plot of land ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NVSS/images/North-Vista-4E-EM-Prelim-P1-2024-QP-pages/page-14.png)

A playground $E$, inside the quadrilateral $ABCD$, is equidistant from $DA$ and $CD$ and closer to $B$ than to $A$.

By construction, using compass and ruler, mark and label a possible position of the playground $E$.

## Answer Scheme

The scheme draws the angle bisector of $\angle ADC$ and the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. Their intersection inside the quadrilateral, on the side of the bisector nearer $B$, is $E$.

Perpendicular bisector B1. Angle bisector B1. Correct position of $E$ B1.
