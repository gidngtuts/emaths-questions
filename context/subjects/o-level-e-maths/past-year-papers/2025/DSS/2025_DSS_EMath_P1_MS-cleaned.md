$$
5y=6x-2\times 2:\quad 10y=12x-4 \quad (1)

$$
y=\frac{3}{p}x-\frac{1}{p}
$$

$$
\frac{3}{p}=\frac{6}{5}\Rightarrow p=\frac{5}{2}
