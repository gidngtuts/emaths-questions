## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 9

The diagram shows the cross section of a plastic pipe. The arc $AB$ is part of the circle with centre $O$ and radius 45 mm. The pipe has a uniform thickness of $x$ mm.
The shaded area represents the cross-sectional area that is filled with water.
$AB=65.32$ mm.

![Pipe cross-section. Chord AB = 65.32 mm on a circle of radius 45 mm centred at O. The pipe wall has thickness x mm. The region inside the pipe below chord AB is shaded.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_-Prelim-P2_QP-pages/page-09.png)

**(i)** Show that $\angle AOB=1.6243$ radians. [2]

**(ii)** Given that the cross-sectional area of the pipe is $1398.29\text{ mm}^{2}$, find $x$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(i)** Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$.

$$
MB=\frac{1}{2}(65.32)=32.66\text{ mm}
$$

(property of a chord)

$$
\angle MOB=\sin^{-1}\left(\frac{32.66}{45}\right)=0.81216\text{ rad}
$$

M1 for using sine.

$$
\angle AOB=2\angle MOB=1.624328=1.6243\text{ rad}
$$

(shown)

**(ii)** The scheme prints the area as $1389.29$, then divides as if the area were $1398.29$. Both lines are transcribed as printed.

$$
\pi(45+x)^{2}-\pi(45)^{2}=1389.29
$$

$$
(45+x)^{2}-(45)^{2}=445.08953
$$

$$
x=4.699=4.70\text{ mm (3 s.f.)}
$$
