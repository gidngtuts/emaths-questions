## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 1, (d) 2, (e) 1, (f) 2; total 10
> **Page:** 18–19

Solutions to this question by accurate drawing will not be accepted.
The diagram below shows a triangle $ABC$, with $A(3, 6)$ and $B(-11, 0)$.
Triangle $ABC$ has an area of 22.5 units$^{2}$. $E$ is a point on the line $AB$ passing through the $y$-axis. $C$ lies on the $x$-axis between $B$ and the origin.

![Triangle ABC with A(3, 6), B(-11, 0) and C on the x-axis](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/OPSS/images/OPSS_Prelims_2024_4E_Math_P1_QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Find the length $AB$.

**(b)** Find the equation of the line $AB$.

**(c)** State the coordinates of $E$.

**(d)** Find the coordinates of $C$.

**(e)** Given that $ABDC$ is a parallelogram, find the coordinates of $D$.

**(f)** The line $l$ has equation $7y-3x+15=0$.
Explain whether line $l$ will ever cut the line $AB$ when both lines are extended.
Show your workings clearly.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $AB=\sqrt{(3-(-11))^{2}+(6-0)^{2}}=\sqrt{232}=15.2$ units

**(b)** Gradient $=\dfrac{6-0}{3-(-11)}=\dfrac{3}{7}$

At $(3, 6)$, $6=\dfrac{3}{7}(3)+C$, so $C=4\dfrac{5}{7}$

$y=\dfrac{3}{7}x+4\dfrac{5}{7}$

**(c)** $E=\left(0,\ 4\dfrac{5}{7}\right)$

**(d)** $\dfrac{1}{2}(BC)(6)=22.5$, so $BC=7.5$

$C=(-3.5,\ 0)$

**(e)** The shift from $A$ to $C$ is $(-6.5,\ -6)$.

$D=(-11-6.5,\ 0-6)=(-17.5,\ -6)$

**(f)** $7y-3x+15=0$ gives $y=\dfrac{3}{7}x-\dfrac{15}{7}$.

The gradient equals the gradient of $AB$, so the lines are parallel and will not meet.
