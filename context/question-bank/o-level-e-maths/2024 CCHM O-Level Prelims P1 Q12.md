## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Factorise completely $4mn-16n^{2}-4m^{2}n+64n^{3}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
4mn-16n^{2}-4m^{2}n+64n^{3} = 4n(m-4n)-4n(m^{2}-16n^{2}) = 4n(m-4n)(1-m-4n)
$$
