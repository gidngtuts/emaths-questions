## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 3

Given that $p:16 = 3:20$, find the value of $p$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{p}{16} = \frac{3}{20}
$$

$$
p = \frac{3}{20} \times 16 = 2.4 \text{ or } \frac{12}{5}
$$
