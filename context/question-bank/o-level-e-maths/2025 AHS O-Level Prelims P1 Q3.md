## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3–4

Factorise completely $ax-2cy-cx+2ay$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Factorise completely $ax-2cy-cx+2ay$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
ax-2cy-cx+2ay&=ax-cx-2cy+2ay\\
&=x(a-c)+2y(-c+a)\\
&=x(a-c)+2y(a-c)\\
&=(x+2y)(a-c)
\end{aligned}
$$

**M1** Factorise $x$ and $2y$. **A1**
