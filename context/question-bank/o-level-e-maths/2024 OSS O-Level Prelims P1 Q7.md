## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** Paul is 25% heavier than Mike. Mike is 25% lighter than Oscar.
Find the ratio of the weight of Paul to that of Oscar.

**(b)** Rachel bought 8 watches for ＄900 each. She sold 4 watches at a profit of 80%, and 3 watches at a loss of 50%. She kept one watch for herself.

Calculate Rachel’s net profit.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Paul $=125\%$ of Mike, so Mike $=80\%$ of Paul. Mike $=75\%$ of Oscar.

$0.8$ Paul $=0.75$ Oscar, so Paul $:$ Oscar $=15:16$

**(b)** Total cost $=900\times 8=7200$

Total received $=4\times\dfrac{180}{100}\times 900+3\times\dfrac{50}{100}\times 900=7830$

Profit $=\text{＄}630$
