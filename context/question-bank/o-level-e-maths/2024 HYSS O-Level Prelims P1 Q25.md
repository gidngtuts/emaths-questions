## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 22

$ORS$ is a triangle. $J$ is a point on $RS$ such that $RJ:JS = 3:2$. $\overrightarrow{OR} = \mathbf{r}$ and $\overrightarrow{OS} = \mathbf{s}$.

(a) Show that $\overrightarrow{OJ} = \dfrac{1}{5}(2\mathbf{r}+3\mathbf{s})$. [2]

(b) $X$ is a point such that $\overrightarrow{RX} = \dfrac{1}{5}(\mathbf{r}+9\mathbf{s})$. Explain why $O$, $J$ and $X$ lie on a straight line. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\overrightarrow{RS} = -\mathbf{r}+\mathbf{s}$. M1.

$$
\overrightarrow{OJ} = \overrightarrow{OR}+\overrightarrow{RJ} = \mathbf{r}+\frac{3}{5}(\mathbf{s}-\mathbf{r}) = \frac{2}{5}\mathbf{r}+\frac{3}{5}\mathbf{s} = \frac{1}{5}(2\mathbf{r}+3\mathbf{s})
$$

M1. The scheme’s last printed bracket is $(2\mathbf{r}+3\mathbf{r})$; the shown result required by the question is $\dfrac{1}{5}(2\mathbf{r}+3\mathbf{s})$.

(b)

$$
\overrightarrow{RX} = \frac{1}{5}(\mathbf{r}+9\mathbf{s})
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{OX} = \overrightarrow{OR}+\overrightarrow{RX} = \mathbf{r}+\frac{1}{5}\mathbf{r}+\frac{9}{5}\mathbf{s} = \frac{6}{5}\mathbf{r}+\frac{9}{5}\mathbf{s} = 3\left(\frac{2}{5}\mathbf{r}+\frac{3}{5}\mathbf{s}\right) = 3\overrightarrow{OJ}
$$

M1. Since $\overrightarrow{OX}$ and $\overrightarrow{OJ}$ differ by the scalar factor $3$ and share the point $O$, the vectors are parallel, so $O$, $J$ and $X$ lie on the same straight line. A1.
