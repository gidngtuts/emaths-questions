## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Given that $z=\dfrac{x^{2}+y}{2}$, express $x$ in terms of $z$ and $y$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
z=\frac{x^{2}+y}{2}
$$

$$
2z=x^{2}+y
$$

$$
4z^{2}=x^{2}+y
$$

$$
x^{2}=4z^{2}-y
$$

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{4z^{2}-y}
$$

Minus 1 mark if no $\pm$.
