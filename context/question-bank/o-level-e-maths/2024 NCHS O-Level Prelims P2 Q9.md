## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) [2]; (a)(ii) [1]; (a)(iii) [2]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 18–19

$K$, $L$, $M$, $N$ and $H$ are points on the circle, centre $O$.
$GJ$ is a tangent to the circle at $H$ and $LOH$ and $LKI$ are straight lines.
Angle $NHG = 15^{\circ}$, angle $OML = 47^{\circ}$ and angle $LIJ = 118^{\circ}$.

![Circle with centre O, tangent GJ and the labelled angles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NCHS/images/2024-NCHS-Prelim-Math-P2-QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Find, giving reasons for each answer,

**(i)** angle $MNH$,

**(ii)** angle $MOH$,

**(iii)** angle $KNH$.

**(b)** Explain whether $MN$ and $LI$ are parallel lines.

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)** $\angle MLO = 47^{\circ}$ (base angle in an isosceles triangle).

$\angle MNH = 180^{\circ}-47^{\circ} = 133^{\circ}$ (angle in the opposite segment).

**(ii)** $\angle MOH = 47^{\circ}\times 2 = 94^{\circ}$ (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference).

**(iii)** $\angle LHJ = 90^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius).

$\angle HLI = 118^{\circ}-90^{\circ} = 28^{\circ}$ (exterior angle of a triangle).

$\angle KNH = 28^{\circ}$ (angles in the same segment).

**(b)** $\angle LHG = 90^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius), so $\angle OHN = 75^{\circ}$.

$\angle OMN = 360^{\circ}-(133^{\circ}+75^{\circ}+94^{\circ}) = 58^{\circ}$.

$\angle NML+\angle MLI = (58^{\circ}+47^{\circ})+(47^{\circ}+28^{\circ}) = 180^{\circ}$.

Interior angles sum to $180^{\circ}$, so $MN$ and $LI$ are parallel.
