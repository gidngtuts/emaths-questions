## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Given that $3^{x}+3^{x+2}=90$, find $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
3^{x}+3^{x+2}=90
$$

$$
3^{x}+3^{x}\times 3^{2}=90
$$

$$
3^{x}(1+9)=90
$$

$$
3^{x}=9
$$

$$
3^{x}=3^{2}
$$

$$
x=2
$$
