## Question

**(a)** $2p^{4}\times 6p^{-5}$ [1]

**(b)** $4(3x-2)-5$ [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
2p^{4}\times 6p^{-5}=12p^{-1}=\frac{12}{p}
$$

**(b)**

$$
4(3x-2)-5=12x-8-5=12x-13
$$
