## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 18

Solve the equation $a^{2}+17a-30=0$ by completing the square.
Give your solutions correct to 2 decimal places. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Solve the equation $a^{2}+17a-30=0$ by completing the square. Give your solutions correct to 2 decimal places.

$$
a^{2}+17a-30=0
$$

$$
\left(a+\frac{17}{2}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{17}{2}\right)^{2}-30=0
$$

M1 for completing the square correctly.

$$
\left(a+\frac{17}{2}\right)^{2}=102.25
$$

$$
a+\frac{17}{2}=\pm\sqrt{102.25}
$$

M1 for taking the square root.

$$
a=1.61\quad\text{or}\quad a=-18.61\ (2\text{ d.p.})
$$

A1 for both correct answers.
