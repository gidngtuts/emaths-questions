## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 11

The figure below shows a circle with centre $O$. Points $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on the circle. Angle $ABC = 100^{\circ}$. Find angle $OAC$, giving reasons for each step of your working.

![Circle centre O with chord AC, radii OA and OC, and angle ABC = 100 degrees at B on the circumference](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E-Math-Prelim-Paper-1-FINAL-pages/page-11.png)

[4]

## Answer Scheme

Reflex angle $AOC = 100\times 2 = 200^{\circ}$ (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference).

Obtuse angle $AOC = 360^{\circ}-200^{\circ} = 160^{\circ}$ (angles at a point).

$$
\text{Angle } OAC = \frac{180^{\circ}-160^{\circ}}{2} = 10^{\circ}
$$

(angles of an isosceles triangle $OAC$).

M1 with correct reasoning for the reflex angle. M1 for $160^{\circ}$. A1.
