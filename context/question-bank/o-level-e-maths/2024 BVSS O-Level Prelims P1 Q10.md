## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

$x$ is $35\%$ of $y$.

$$
2x+45 = y.
$$

Find the value of $x$ and of $y$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
x = 0.35y \quad [1]
$$

$$
2x+45 = y \quad [2]
$$

Substitute $[1]$ into $[2]$:

$$
2(0.35y)+45 = y \quad\text{M1}
$$

$$
0.7y+45 = y
$$

$$
0.3y = 45
$$

$$
y = 150 \quad\text{A1}
$$

$$
x = 52.5 \quad\text{A1}
$$
