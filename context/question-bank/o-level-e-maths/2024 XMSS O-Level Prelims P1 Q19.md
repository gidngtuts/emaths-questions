## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 16

The mass of some crystals were measured and the results are shown in the stem-and-leaf diagram.

| Stem | Leaf | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 | 7 | 7 | 8 |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | | |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 5 | 8 |
| 4 | 0 | 1 | | |

Key: $2 \mid 4$ means $240$ g.

(a) Find the median mass. [1]

(b) Find the standard deviation of the mass. [1]

(c) A crystal is chosen at random. Calculate the probability of the crystal having a mass of at least $350$ g. [1]

(d) It was discovered that the mass has been measured incorrectly. The correct mass were all $20$ g more than those recorded. Explain how the median and standard deviation of the mass have been affected by this error. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Median $= \dfrac{230+320}{2} = 275$ g. [B1]

(b) $94.6$ g. [B1]

(c) $\dfrac{4}{12} = \dfrac{1}{3}$. [B1]

(d) The median mass increases by $20$ g, to $295$ g, but the standard deviation remains unchanged. Either statement is accepted; the increase must be stated. [B2]
