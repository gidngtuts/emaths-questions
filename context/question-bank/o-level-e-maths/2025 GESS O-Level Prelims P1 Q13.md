## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 2, (d) 2, (e) 2; total 10
> **Page:** 11–12

$A$, $B$ and $C$ are the points $A(-6,5)$, $B(9,5)$ and $C(-3,-2)$.

![Question 13 points A, B, C and P](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-11.png)

Find

**(a)** the equation of line $AC$, [2]

**(b)** the equation of the line that is parallel to $AC$ and passes through $B$, [2]

**(c)** area of triangle $ABC$, [2]

**(d)** the shortest distance from $A$ to $BC$, [2]

**(e)** the coordinate of point $P$ as the line $AC$ intersects the $x$-axis at $P$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $(-6,5)$ and $(-3,-2)$

$$
\text{Gradient}=\frac{5-(-2)}{-6-(-3)}=\frac{7}{-3}
$$

$$
y=\frac{7}{-3}x+c
$$

Sub $(-6,5)$:

$$
5=\frac{7}{-3}(-6)+c,\quad c=-9
$$

$$
y=\frac{7}{-3}x-9
$$

**(b)**

$$
y=\frac{7}{-3}x+c
$$

Sub $(9,5)$:

$$
5=\frac{7}{-3}(9)+c,\quad c=26
$$

$$
y=\frac{7}{-3}x+26
$$

**(c)**

$$
9-(-6)=15,\quad 5-(-2)=7
$$

$$
\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\times 15\times 7=52.5
$$

**(d)** $(9,5)$, $(-3,-2)$

$$
BC=\sqrt{(9-(-3))^{2}+(5-(-2))^{2}}=\sqrt{144+49}=\sqrt{193}
$$

$$
\text{Area}=\frac{1}{2}\times d\times\sqrt{193}=52.5
$$

$$
d=\frac{52.5}{\frac{1}{2}\times\sqrt{193}}=7.558065=7.56\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

**(e)**

$$
y=\frac{7}{-3}x-9
$$

Sub $y=0$:

$$
\frac{7}{3}x=-9,\quad x=\frac{-27}{7}
$$

$$
P\left(\frac{-27}{7},0\right)
$$
