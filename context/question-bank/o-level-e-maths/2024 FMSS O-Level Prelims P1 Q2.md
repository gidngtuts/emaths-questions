## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Factorise completely $2a^{2}c-ad-2abc+bd$. [2]

**(b)** Expand and simplify $(7x-4y)(x+3y)$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
2a^{2}c-ad-2abc+bd = a(2ac-d)-b(2ac-d) = (a-b)(2ac-d)
$$

or $(b-a)(d-2ac)$. B1, B1 or B2.

**(b)**

$$
(7x-4y)(x+3y) = 7x^{2}+21xy-4xy-12y^{2} = 7x^{2}+17xy-12y^{2}
$$

M1, A1.
