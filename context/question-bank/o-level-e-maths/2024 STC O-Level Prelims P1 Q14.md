## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 10

$P$, $Q$, $R$, $S$ and $T$ are points on the circumference of a circle, centre $O$.

$PS$ intersects $TR$ at $O$ and $AB$ is a tangent to the circle at $S$. $TR$ produced meets $AS$ produced at point $B$. $\angle RSB = 34^{\circ}$ and $\angle QTR = 40^{\circ}$.

Giving reasons for each step of your working, find

![Circle with centre O, tangent AB at S, and chords through P, Q, R, S and T](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/STC/images/CHIJ-STC-2024-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-10.png)

(i) $\angle RBS$, [2]

(ii) $\angle PQR$, [1]

(iii) reflex $\angle POR$, [1]

(iv) $\angle QRP$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(i)

$\angle OSB=90^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius)

$\angle OSR=90^{\circ}-34^{\circ}=56^{\circ}$

$\angle ORS=56^{\circ}$ (isoceles triangle)

$\angle BRS=180^{\circ}-56^{\circ}=124^{\circ}$ (adj. $\angle$s on a straight line)

$\angle RBS=180^{\circ}-124^{\circ}-34^{\circ}=22^{\circ}$ (angle sum of triangle)

(ii) $\angle PQR=180^{\circ}-56^{\circ}=124^{\circ}$ ($\angle$s in opposite segment)

(iii) Reflex $\angle POR=124^{\circ}\times 2=248^{\circ}$ ($\angle$ at centre $=$ twice $\angle$ at circumference)

(iv)

$\angle QPR=40^{\circ}$ ($\angle$s in the same segment)

$\angle QRP=180^{\circ}-124^{\circ}-40^{\circ}=16^{\circ}$ (angle sum of triangle)
