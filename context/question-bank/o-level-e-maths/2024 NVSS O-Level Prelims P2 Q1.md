## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c)(i) 1, (c)(ii) 3, (d) 3; total 12
> **Page:** 3–5

**(a)** Solve the inequality $-5 < 3x-2 < 13$.

**(b)** Solve these simultaneous equations.

$$
\frac{1}{2}x+y=5
$$

$$
2x-3y=13
$$

You must show your working.

**(c)**

$$
p=\sqrt[3]{\frac{r+q}{4r-1}}
$$

**(i)** Find $p$ when $r=7$ and $q=-15$.

**(ii)** Rearrange the formula to make $r$ the subject.

**(d)** Solve $\dfrac{15}{2x-1}=x+3$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $-5 < 3x-2 < 13$

$-3 < 3x < 15$ M1

$-1 < x < 5$ A1

**(b)** $\dfrac{1}{2}x+y=5$ (1), $2x-3y=13$ (2)

(1) $\times 4$: $2x+4y=20$ (3) M1

(2) $-$ (3): $-7y=-7$, so $y=1$ A1

When $y=1$, $2x-3=13$, so $x=8$ A1

**(c)** **(i)** $p=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{7+(-15)}{4(7)-1}}=\sqrt[3]{-\dfrac{8}{27}}=-\dfrac{2}{3}$ B1

**(ii)** $p^{3}=\dfrac{r+q}{4r-1}$

$p^{3}(4r-1)=r+q$ M1

$4p^{3}r-p^{3}=r+q$

$r(4p^{3}-1)=p^{3}+q$ M1

$$
r=\frac{p^{3}+q}{4p^{3}-1}
$$

A1

**(d)** $(2x-1)(x+3)=15$ M1

$2x^{2}+5x-18=0$

$(2x+9)(x-2)=0$ M1

$x=-4\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x=2$ A1
