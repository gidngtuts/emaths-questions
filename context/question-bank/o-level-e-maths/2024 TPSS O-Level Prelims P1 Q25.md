## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 19

The diagram shows a solid formed by joining two hemispheres and a cylinder.
The radius of the small hemisphere and the radius of the cylinder are both 3.6 cm.
The length from the centre to the bottom of the large hemisphere is 5.4 cm.
The diagram also labels the cylinder height as 6.5 cm.

Calculate the total surface area of the solid.

![Solid of a small hemisphere, a cylinder of height 6.5 cm, and a large hemisphere of radius 5.4 cm](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/TPSS/images/2024-TPSS-EMATH-P1-PRELIM-QP-pages/page-19.png)

## Answer Scheme

Curved surface of the small hemisphere

$$
= 2\pi(3.6)^{2} = 25.92\pi \text{ cm}^{2} \quad \text{M1}
$$

Curved surface area of the cylinder

$$
= 2\pi(3.6)(6.5) = 46.8\pi \text{ cm}^{2} \quad \text{M1}
$$

Total surface area of the large hemisphere

$$
= 2\pi(5.4)^{2}+\pi(5.4^{2}-3.6^{2}) = 74.52\pi \text{ cm}^{2} \quad \text{M1, M1}
$$

Total surface area of the solid

$$
25.92\pi+46.8\pi+74.52\pi = 462.56 \approx 463 \text{ cm}^{2} \quad \text{A1}
$$
