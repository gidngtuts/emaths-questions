## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (a)(iii) 3, (b) 1, (c) 3; total 9
> **Page:** 10–11

In the diagram, $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are points on the circle with centre $O$.
$CT$ is tangent to the circle and $AC$ is parallel to $OT$.
Angle $AOC=100^{\circ}$.

![Circle, centre O, with tangent CT and points A, B, C, D](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BSS/images/Broadrick-EM-2024-Maths-Sec-Prelim-P2-pages/page-10.png)

**(a)** Giving reason(s) for your workings, find

**(i)** angle $ABC$,

**(ii)** angle $ADC$,

**(iii)** angle $OTC$.

**(b)** Explain whether a circle can be drawn passing through the points $A$, $O$, $C$ and $D$.

**(c)** Given $CT=4.8\text{ cm}$, find the area of the minor segment $ADC$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $\angle ABC=\dfrac{100^{\circ}}{2}=50^{\circ}$ (angle at centre $=2\times$ angle at circumference) (B1)

**(a)(ii)** $\angle ADC=180^{\circ}-50^{\circ}=130^{\circ}$ (angles in opposite segments) (B1)

**(a)(iii)** $\angle ACO=\dfrac{180^{\circ}-100^{\circ}}{2}=40^{\circ}$ (base angles of isosceles triangle) (M1)

$\angle COT=40^{\circ}$ (alternate angles, $AC\parallel OT$)

$\angle OCT=90^{\circ}$ (tangent perpendicular to radius) (M1)

$\angle OTC=180^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-40^{\circ}=50^{\circ}$ (angle sum of triangle) (A1)

**(b)** $\angle ADC+\angle AOC=130^{\circ}+100^{\circ}=230^{\circ}\neq 180^{\circ}$ (B1)

They are not angles in opposite segments.
A circle cannot be drawn passing through the points $A$, $O$, $C$ and $D$.

**(c)** $\tan 50^{\circ}=\dfrac{OC}{4.8}$ (M1)

$OC=5.72042$

Minor segment = minor sector $-$ triangle

$=\dfrac{100}{360}\times\pi(5.72042)^{2}-\dfrac{1}{2}(5.72042)^{2}\sin(100^{\circ})$ (M1)

$=12.4\text{ cm}^{2}$ (A1)
