## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 10

Find five positive integers that satisfy all four of the following conditions.

(1) Mode $= 5$

(2) Median $= 5$

(3) Mean $= 6$

(4) Range $= 7$

## Answer Scheme

$3, 5, 5, 7, 10$ or $4, 5, 5, 5, 11$. B1
