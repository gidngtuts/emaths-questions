## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 7

The table shows the times, $t$ minutes, taken by $40$ students to complete a Mathematics quiz.

| Time, $t$ (minutes) | Frequency |
| --- | --- |
| $30\leq t<40$ | $6$ |
| $40\leq t<50$ | $10$ |
| $50\leq t<60$ | $16$ |
| $60\leq t<70$ | $8$ |

**(a)** Calculate an estimate for

**(i)** the mean time, [1]

**(ii)** the standard deviation of the times. [1]

**(b)** Each recorded time was $5$ minutes less than the actual time, so $5$ minutes is added to each time to correct it.

Explain how the mean and standard deviation of the corrected times compare with the values in (a). [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** Midpoints: $35$, $45$, $55$, $65$

$$
\text{Mean}=\frac{6(35)+10(45)+16(55)+8(65)}{40}=\frac{2060}{40}=51.5\text{ min}
$$

**(a)(ii)**

$$
\text{s.d.}=\sqrt{\frac{109800}{40}-51.5^{2}}=\sqrt{92.75}=9.63\text{ min}
$$

**(b)** Adding $5$ to every value increases the mean by $5$, to $56.5\text{ min}$.
The standard deviation is unchanged ($9.63\text{ min}$): adding a constant does not change the spread.
