## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 2, (c) 3, (d) 2; total 10
> **Page:** 14–15

$ABCD$ is a trapezium.
$AB$ is parallel to $DC$.
$\overrightarrow{AB}=16\mathbf{p}$ and $\overrightarrow{AD}=5\mathbf{q}$.
$P$ is the point on $BD$ such that $DP:DB=1:4$ and $4AB=3DC$.

![Question 5 trapezium](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/NASS/images/NASS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-14.png)

**(a)** Express in terms of $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$,

**(i)** $\overrightarrow{DB}$, [1]

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{AP}$. [2]

**(b)** Show that line $BC$ is parallel to line $AP$. [2]

**(c)** Find the ratio area triangle $APB$ : area triangle $BCD$. [3]

**(d)** Find the ratio area triangle $APD$ : area trapezium $ABCD$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $\overrightarrow{DB}=16\mathbf{p}-5\mathbf{q}$

**(a)(ii)**

$$
\overrightarrow{DP}=\frac{1}{4}(16\mathbf{p}-5\mathbf{q})
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{AP}=\overrightarrow{AD}+\overrightarrow{DP}
$$

$$
=5\mathbf{q}+\frac{1}{4}(16\mathbf{p}-5\mathbf{q})
$$

$$
=\frac{15}{4}\mathbf{q}+4\mathbf{p}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\overrightarrow{DC}=\frac{4}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{BC}=\overrightarrow{BA}+\overrightarrow{AP}+\overrightarrow{DC}
$$

$$
=-16\mathbf{p}+5\mathbf{q}+\frac{64}{3}\mathbf{p}
$$

$$
=\frac{16}{3}\mathbf{p}+5\mathbf{q}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{BC}=\frac{4}{3}\left(\frac{15}{4}\mathbf{q}+4\mathbf{p}\right)
$$

$$
=\frac{4}{3}\overrightarrow{AP}
$$

Since $\overrightarrow{BC}=\dfrac{4}{3}\overrightarrow{AP}$, they are parallel.

**(c)** $\angle APB=\angle CBD$ (alternate $\angle$, $AP\parallel BC$)

$\angle ABP=\angle CDB$ (alternate $\angle$, $AB\parallel DC$)

Triangle $APB$ is similar to Triangle $CBD$ by AA similarity.

Triangle $APB$ : Triangle $BCD$

$$
3^{2}:4^{2}
$$

$$
9:16
$$

OR

$$
\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }APB}{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}=\frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}{\text{Area of Triangle }BCD}=\frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }APB}{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}\times\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}{\text{Area of Triangle }BCD}
$$

$$
=\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{3}{4}=\frac{9}{16}
$$

**(d)** Since Triangle $APD$ and Triangle $APB$ have common height,

Triangle $APD$ : Triangle $APB$

$$
1:3
$$

Triangle $APD$ : Trapezium $ABCD$

$$
3:28\text{ (Since }16+9+3=28\text{)}
$$

OR

$$
\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }APD}{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}\times\frac{\text{Area of Triangle }BAD}{\text{Area of Trapezium }ABCD}
$$

$$
=\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{3}{7}=\frac{3}{28}
$$
