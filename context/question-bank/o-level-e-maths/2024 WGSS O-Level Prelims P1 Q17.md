## Question

> **Marks:** 7
> **Page:** 15

(a) Simplify.

(i) $\dfrac{a^{3}}{3a^{2}}\times 27a^{6}$ [2]

(ii) $\sqrt[4]{\dfrac{4}{16x^{3}}}$ [2]

(b) $\dfrac{49^{2a}}{7^{b}} = 343^{4c}$. Find an expression for $b$ in terms of $a$ and $c$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) $\dfrac{a^{3}}{3a^{2}}\times 27a^{6} = \dfrac{9a^{3}}{a^{2}}\times a^{6}$ [M1]

$= 9a^{7}$ [A1]

(ii) $\sqrt[4]{\dfrac{4}{16x^{3}}} = 4\left(\dfrac{4}{x^{3}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ [M1]

$= 4x^{\frac{2}{3}}$ [A1]

(b) $\dfrac{49^{2a}}{7^{b}} = 343^{4c}$

$\dfrac{7^{4a}}{7^{b}} = 7^{12c}$ [M1]

$4a-b = 12c$ [M1]

$b = 4a-12c$ [A1]
