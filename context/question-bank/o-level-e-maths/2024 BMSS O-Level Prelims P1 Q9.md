## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 10

Explain why $(2n+3)^{2}-(4n+3)(n-6)$ is a multiple of $3$ for all integer values of $n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
(2n+3)^{2}-(4n+3)(n-6)=4n^{2}+12n+9-(4n^{2}+3n-24n-18)
$$

[M1]

$$
=33n+27=3(11n+9)
$$

[A1]

Hence it is a multiple of $3$ for all integer values of $n$.
