## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Ahmad, Simon and Bala share a sum of money in the ratio $3 : 2 : 5$.
If Bala has ＄900 more than Simon, what is the sum of money being shared by the three of them? [2]

## Answer Scheme

Total sum of money

$$
= \frac{900}{3}\times 10 = 3000
$$

＄3000.
