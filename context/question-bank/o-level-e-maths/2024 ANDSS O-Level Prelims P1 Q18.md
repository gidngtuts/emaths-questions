## Question

> **Marks:** 7
> **Page:** 16–17

The diagram shows two congruent isosceles triangles, $PQS$ and $RSQ$.
$PQ$ and $RS$ are parallel to the $x$-axis.

![Coordinate diagram. P is (-2, 1), Q is to the right of P on the same horizontal line, S is above the segment PQ, and R is (13, 4) on the same horizontal line as S.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_Prelim_P1_QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Write down the coordinates of the point $Q$. [1]

**(b)** Find the equation of the line $PR$. [2]

**(c)** $W$ is a point on the $x$-axis such that $RW$ is perpendicular to $RS$.
Write down the coordinates of the point $W$. [1]

**(d)** Find the perimeter of $PQRS$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
15\div 3=5
$$

$$
Q=(-2+5\times 2,\ 1)=(8,1)
$$

**(b)** Gradient of $PR$:

$$
\frac{4-1}{13-(-2)}=\frac{1}{5}
$$

Let the equation of the line $PR$ be $y=mx+c$.

$$
4=\frac{1}{5}\times 13+c
$$

$$
c=\frac{7}{5}
$$

$$
y=\frac{1}{5}x+\frac{7}{5}
$$

**(c)** $W=(13,0)$

**(d)** Length of $QR$:

$$
\sqrt{(13-8)^{2}+(4-1)^{2}}=\sqrt{34}
$$

$$
\text{Perimeter of }PQRS
= 2\times\sqrt{34}+2\times 10
= 31.7\text{ units (3 s.f.)}
$$
