## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 16

$ABC$ is an arc of a circle, centre $O$ and radius $8\text{ cm}$.
The tangents at $A$ and $C$ meet at $P$. Angle $AOC$ is $\dfrac{2\pi}{9}$ radians and $AP = 2.91\text{ cm}$.

![Question 21 circle and tangents](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Show that angle $APC = \dfrac{7\pi}{9}$ radians.
(Give reasons for each step of your working.) [2]

**(b)** Find the area of the shaded region. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Tangents are perpendicular to the radii, so $\angle OAP = \angle OCP = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$.

$$
\angle APC = 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{2\pi}{9} = \frac{7\pi}{9}
$$

**(b)** $0.951\text{ cm}^{2}$
