## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

Simplify $\dfrac{5}{2-x}+\dfrac{4x-8}{(x-2)^{2}}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Simplify $\dfrac{5}{2-x}+\dfrac{4x-8}{(x-2)^{2}}$.

$$
\frac{5}{2-x}+\frac{4(x-2)}{(x-2)^{2}} = \frac{5}{-(x-2)}+\frac{4}{x-2} = \frac{1}{x-2}
$$

or

$$
\frac{5}{2-x}+\frac{4}{2-x} = \frac{1}{2-x}
$$
