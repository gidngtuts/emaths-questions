## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 16

$$
12\quad 24\quad 8\quad 21\quad 28\quad 17\quad 2p\quad 4p^{2}
$$

The list shows information about the number of text messages Mei received each day for 8 days.
The total number of text messages is $140$.

**(a)** Show that $p=2.5$. [3]

**(b)** The standard deviation for Mei's data is $7.89$.

For the same 8 days, Mei's brother also received some text messages.
For his data, the mean is $19$ and the standard deviation $8.51$.

Make two comparisons between the distributions for the number of text messages received by Mei and her brother. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
12\quad 24\quad 8\quad 21\quad 28\quad 17\quad 2p\quad 4p^{2}
$$

The list shows information about the number of text messages Mei received each day for 8 days. The total number of text messages is $140$.

**(a)** Show that $p=2.5$.

$$
12+24+8+21+28+17+2p+4p^{2}=140
$$

M1 for showing the correct set-up of the equation.

$$
4p^{2}+2p+110=140
$$

$$
2p^{2}+p-15=0
$$

$$
(p+3)(2p-5)=0
$$

M1 for correct factorisation or correct application of the quadratic formula.

$$
p+3=0\quad\text{or}\quad 2p-5=0
$$

$$
p=-3\ (\text{rejected because }p>0)\quad\text{or}\quad p=2.5\ (\text{shown})
$$

M1 for applying the zero product rule and a sound rejection reason.

**(b)** The standard deviation for Mei's data is $7.89$. For the same 8 days, Mei's brother also received some text messages. For his data, the mean is $19$ and the standard deviation $8.51$. Make two comparisons between the distributions for the number of text messages received by Mei and her brother.

1. Mei's brother received more text messages on average because the mean is higher at $19$ compared to Mei's mean which is $17.5$. B1.

2. The number of text messages Mei's brother received is less consistent since the standard deviation is higher at $8.51$ compared to Mei's which is $7.89$. B1.
