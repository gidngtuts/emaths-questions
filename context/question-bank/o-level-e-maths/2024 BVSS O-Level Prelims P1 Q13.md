## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 9

(a) By completing the square, find the coordinates of the turning point of the curve $y = x^{2}+4x+7$. [2]

(b) Hence, sketch the graph of $y = x^{2}+4x+7$ in the space below, showing your $y$-intercept and turning point clearly. Blank axes labelled $x$ and $y$ are given. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
x^{2}+4x+7 = (x+2)^{2}-4+7 = (x+2)^{2}+3 \quad\text{M1 (this must be evident)}
$$

Turning point is $(-2, 3)$. A1.

(b) U-shaped curve with turning point $(-2, 3)$. B1 (u-shaped with correct turning point). The curve meets the $y$-axis at $7$. B1 ($y$-intercept), no ECF.
