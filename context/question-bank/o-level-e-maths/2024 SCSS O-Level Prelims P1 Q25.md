## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1, (c) 2, (d) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 22–23

The diagram shows the speed-time graph for a car journey. Speed rises linearly from $0\text{ m/s}$ at $t=0$ to $20\text{ m/s}$ at $t=10\text{ s}$, stays at $20\text{ m/s}$ until $t=20\text{ s}$, then falls linearly to $0\text{ m/s}$ at $t=40\text{ s}$.

**(a)** Find the speed of the car when $t=8$.

**(b)** Find the acceleration of the car when $t=8$.

**(c)** Find the total distance travelled on the journey.

**(d)** Complete the distance-time graph for the journey on the grid. The grid has time from $0$ to $40\text{ s}$ and distance from $0$ to $600\text{ m}$.

![Blank distance-time grid for the journey](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/SCSS/images/SCSS-2024-EM-Prelim-P1-QP-pages/page-23.png)

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $16\text{ m/s}$ (B1)

**(b)** $2\text{ m/s}^{2}$ (B1)

**(c)** Distance $=$ area under the graph $=100+200+200$ (M1) $=500\text{ m}$ (A1)

**(d)** Straight section from the origin while the car accelerates and travels at constant speed, then a curve while the car decelerates. B1 for the straight section correct. B1 for the curved section correct.

The completed graph is on the same mark-scheme page as Q24(c)(ii).
