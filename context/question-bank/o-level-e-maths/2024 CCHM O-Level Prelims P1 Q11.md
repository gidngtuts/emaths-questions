## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 7

Given that $a^{2}-\dfrac{6a}{b}+\dfrac{9}{b^{2}} = 0$, find the value of $ab$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
a^{2}-\frac{6a}{b}+\frac{9}{b^{2}} = 0
$$

$$
\left(a-\frac{3}{b}\right)^{2} = 0, \quad ab = 3
$$

Alternative: $a^{2}b^{2}-6ab+9 = 0$, $(ab-3)^{2} = 0$, $ab = 3$.
