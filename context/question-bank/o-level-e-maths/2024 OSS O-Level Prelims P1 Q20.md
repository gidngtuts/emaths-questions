## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 1, (d) 1; total 5
> **Page:** 17

A concert was held over a particular weekend. The matrix $M$ shows the number of tickets sold on Saturday and Sunday respectively.

$$
M=\begin{pmatrix} 84 & 51 \\ 135 & 160 \\ 72 & 87 \end{pmatrix}
$$

The rows are children, adults and senior citizens. The columns are Saturday and Sunday.

**(a)** The concert tickets were priced at ＄6 for a child, ＄15 for an adult and ＄8 for a senior citizen.
Represent this information in a $1\times 3$ matrix $P$.

**(b)** Evaluate the matrix $T=PM$.

**(c)** State what each of the elements in matrix $T$ represents.

**(d)** The elements of the matrix $N$, where $N=QM$, represent the number of tickets sold on each day of the concert.
Write down the matrix $Q$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $P=\begin{pmatrix} 6 & 15 & 8 \end{pmatrix}$

**(b)** $T=\begin{pmatrix} 3105 & 3402 \end{pmatrix}$

**(c)** The total money collected from the ticket sales on Saturday and Sunday respectively.

**(d)** $Q=\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
