## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 23

In the diagram, $ABC$, $CDE$ and $AFE$ are straight lines. $BD$ is parallel to $AE$. $BD=15\text{ cm}$, $CD=12\text{ cm}$, $DE=8\text{ cm}$ and $EF=10\text{ cm}$.

Point $C$ is above $D$, with $CD=12\text{ cm}$. $B$ is to the left of $D$, with $BD=15\text{ cm}$. $E$ is to the right of and below $D$, with $DE=8\text{ cm}$. $F$ lies on $AE$ with $EF=10\text{ cm}$. Arrows on $BD$ and $AE$ show that the lines are parallel.

**(a)** Show that triangles $CDB$ and $DEF$ are similar. Give a reason for each statement you make. [2]

**(b)** Find the ratio of area of triangle $CDB$ : area of triangle $DEF$. [1]

**(c)** Show that $ABDF$ is a parallelogram. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle BDC=\angle FED$ (corresponding angles, $BD\parallel AE$).

$$
\frac{BD}{FE}=\frac{15}{10}=\frac{3}{2}\quad\text{and}\quad\frac{CD}{DE}=\frac{12}{8}=\frac{3}{2}
$$

[B1]

Therefore $\triangle CDB$ and $\triangle DEF$ are similar (ratio of corresponding sides and included angle are equal / SAS similarity test). [B1]

**(b)** $(12:8)^{2}=9:4$ [B1]

**(c)** $\angle CBD=\angle DFE$ (from part (a), $\triangle CDB$ and $\triangle DEF$ are similar). [B1]

$\angle BAF=\angle CBD$ (corresponding angles, $BD\parallel AE$) and so $\angle BAF=\angle DFE$. [B1]

Therefore $AB\parallel FD$ and hence $ABDF$ is a parallelogram.
