## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 2; total 7
> **Page:** 6–7

$A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and $E$ are points on a circle.
$AE = AB$, $AE$ is parallel to $BD$, angle $BEC = 22^{\circ}$ and angle $EAC = 80^{\circ}$.

![Question 3 circle ABCDE](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-06.png)

**(a)** Find angle $AEB$. [2]

**(b)** Find angle $DEC$. [3]

**(c)** Angle $AFE = 78^{\circ}$.
Determine the position of point $F$.
Justify your answer. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Angle $AEB = 39^{\circ}$

**(b)** Angle $DEC = 41^{\circ}$

**(c)** Point $F$ lies on the centre of the circle.
