## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 2; total 7
> **Page:** 18–19

$OABC$ is a right pyramid with vertex $O$ vertically above its base, an equilateral triangle $ABC$.
A small similar pyramid $OGFH$ is removed from the top of pyramid $OABC$.
$BC=8\text{ cm}$. $ONP=20\text{ cm}$, where line $ONP$ is a straight line perpendicular to the base.

![Right pyramid OABC with a smaller pyramid removed](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/OSS/images/2024-Outram-Prelim-P2-QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Show that the volume of pyramid $OABC$ is $185\text{ cm}^{3}$, correct to the nearest $\text{cm}^{3}$.

**(b)** Given that the volume of pyramid $OGFH$ is $39.96\text{ cm}^{3}$, find the value of

$$
\frac{\text{area of triangle }FGH}{\text{area of triangle }BAC}.
$$

**(c)** The remaining vertical height of the solid, $NP$, is 8 cm.
Calculate the area of the top $FGH$ of the remaining solid.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Volume $=\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\times 8\times 8\times\sin 60^{\circ}\right)\times 20=184.75\text{ cm}^{3}$, which is $185\text{ cm}^{3}$ to the nearest $\text{cm}^{3}$.

**(b)** $\dfrac{V_{1}}{V_{2}}=\dfrac{39.96}{185}=\dfrac{27}{125}$, so $\dfrac{l_{1}}{l_{2}}=\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $\dfrac{A_{1}}{A_{2}}=\dfrac{9}{25}$

**(c)** $HF=\dfrac{3}{5}\times 8=4.8$

Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}\times 4.8\times 4.8\sin 60^{\circ}=9.98\text{ cm}^{2}$

Alternatively, $\dfrac{9}{25}\times\dfrac{1}{2}\times 8\times 8\sin 60^{\circ}=9.98\text{ cm}^{2}$. The scheme writes the similar-triangle factor with side 6 in one line; the numerical result is $9.98\text{ cm}^{2}$.
