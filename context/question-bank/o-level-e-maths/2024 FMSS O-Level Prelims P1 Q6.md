## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

A map has a scale of $1:2\,000\,000$.
The area of Johor Bahru on the map is $0.55\text{ cm}^{2}$.
Calculate the actual area, in square kilometres of Johor Bahru. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$1\text{ cm} : 2\,000\,000\text{ cm}$, so $1\text{ cm} : 20\text{ km}$.

$1^{2}\text{ cm}^{2} : 20^{2}\text{ km}^{2}$, so $1\text{ cm}^{2} : 400\text{ km}^{2}$.

$$
400 \times 0.55 = 220\text{ km}^{2}
$$

M1 for either $20^{2}$ or $400$. A1.
