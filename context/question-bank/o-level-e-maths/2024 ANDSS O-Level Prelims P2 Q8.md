## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 10–11

![Points A, B and C at sea level. AB = 4.83 km, BC = 7.24 km. The bearing of B from A is 064 degrees and the bearing of C from B is 127 degrees.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_-Prelim-P2_QP-pages/page-10.png)

The diagram shows the positions of 3 points $A$, $B$, and $C$, at sea level.
$AB=4.83$ km and $BC=7.24$ km. The bearing of $B$ from $A$ is $064^{\circ}$ and the bearing of $C$ from $B$ is $127^{\circ}$.

**(i)** Show that $AC=10.368$ km. [3]

**(ii)** Hence find the bearing of $A$ from $C$. [3]

**(iii)** A boat travels in a straight line from point $B$ to reach the line $AC$. Find the shortest distance that the boat needs to travel. [2]

**(iv)** An object was detected directly below point $C$. The angle of depression of the object from $B$ is $12^{\circ}$. Find the distance of the object from $C$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(i)**

$$
\angle ABN_{B}=180^{\circ}-64^{\circ}=116^{\circ}
$$

(interior angles, $N_{A}$ parallel to $N_{B}$). The reason must be given.

$$
\angle ABC=360^{\circ}-127^{\circ}-116^{\circ}=117^{\circ}
$$

(angles at a point)

$$
AC=\sqrt{4.83^{2}+7.24^{2}-2(4.83)(7.24)\cos 117^{\circ}}=10.368\text{ km}
$$

(shown). M1 for the cosine rule.

**(ii)**

$$
\frac{\sin 117^{\circ}}{10.368}=\frac{\sin\angle BCA}{4.83}
$$

$$
\angle BCA=24.52443^{\circ}\ (5\text{ d.p.})
$$

M1 for the sine rule.

Bearing of $A$ from $C$:

$$
\text{Reflex }\angle N_{C}CB=360^{\circ}-24.52443^{\circ}-(180^{\circ}-127^{\circ})=282.47557^{\circ}
$$

$$
\text{Bearing of }A\text{ from }C=282.5^{\circ}\ (1\text{ d.p.})
$$

**(iii)**

$$
\text{Shortest distance}=7.24\times\sin 24.52443^{\circ}=3.00518=3.01\text{ km (3 s.f.)}
$$

**(iv)**

$$
\text{Distance of the object from }C=7.24\times\tan 12^{\circ}=1.54\text{ km (3 s.f.)}
$$
