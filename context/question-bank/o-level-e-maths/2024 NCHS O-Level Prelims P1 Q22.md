## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [3]; (b) [1]
> **Page:** 19

The diagram shows a triangle $ABC$ with $AC = 8$ cm, $AB = 6.4$ cm and $BC = 4.8$ cm.
$DAB$ is a straight line.

![Triangle ABC with D on the extension of BA](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NCHS/images/2024-NCHS-Prelim-Math-P1-QP-pages/page-19.png)

**(a)** Without calculating the value of angles in the triangle, find the value of $\sin\angle ACB$.

**(b)** State the value of $\cos\angle DAC$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
AB^{2}+BC^{2} &= 6.4^{2}+4.8^{2} = 64 \\
AC^{2} &= 8^{2} = 64
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $AB^{2}+BC^{2} = AC^{2}$, by the converse of Pythagoras’ theorem, $\angle ABC = 90^{\circ}$.

$$
\sin\angle ACB = \frac{6.4}{8} = \frac{4}{5}
$$

**(b)** $\cos\angle DAC = -\dfrac{4}{5}$
