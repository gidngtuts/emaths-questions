## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

(i) Solve the inequalities $2x+11<4x+5<137-2x$. [2]

(ii) Hence, write down the largest prime number value of $x$ which satisfies $2x+11<4x+5<137-2x$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(i)

$$
2x+11<4x+5\quad\text{and}\quad 4x+5<137-2x
$$

$$
-2x<-6\quad\text{and}\quad 6x<132
$$

$$
x>3\quad\text{and}\quad x<22
$$

$$
3<x<22
$$

(ii) Largest prime number $=19$.
