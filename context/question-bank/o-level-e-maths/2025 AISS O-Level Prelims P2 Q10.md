## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1, (c) 7; total 10
> **Page:** 20–21

A new $52$-level private condominium, with units starting from the first level, has just been launched.

The sale price of a unit depends on the floor area and the level the unit is on.
The price of each similar unit increases by a specific amount per level.
Below is some information of the different types of units available.

| Type of unit | 2-room | 3-room | 4-room | 5-room |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Number of toilets | $1$ | $2$ | $2$ | $2$ |
| Floor area (m$^{2}$) | $40$ | $60$ | $90$ | $110$ |
| Price per square feet (&#36;/feet$^{2}$) | $890$ | $990$ | $1100$ | $1300$ |
| Increase in price per level (&#36;) | $13000$ | $16000$ | $19000$ | $22000$ |

**(a)** Given that $1$ m $= 3.28$ feet, show that the floor area of a $3$-room unit is $646$ square feet, correct to $3$ significant figures. [2]

**(b)** Calculate the sale price of a $3$-room unit on the first level. [1]

Lisa is planning to buy a $3$-room unit.
To purchase the unit, she decides to pay $45\%$ of the price of the unit and take a housing loan from the bank to pay the remaining amount by monthly instalments.
The bank charges a simple interest rate of $3\%$ per year for $25$ years.
Lisa has a gross monthly salary of &#36;7500, and she does not plan to spend more than $30\%$ of her gross monthly salary to pay for the monthly instalment.

**(c)** Suggest the maximum level that Lisa can afford to purchase.
Justify the decisions you make and show your calculations clearly. [7]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $1$ m $=3.28$ feet, so $1$ m$^{2}=3.28^{2}$ feet$^{2}$. M1 for the correct ratio of m$^{2}$ to feet$^{2}$.

$$
60\times 3.28^{2}=645.504\text{ feet}^{2}=646\text{ feet}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

A1 for $60\times 3.28^{2}$ and $645.504$ (more than $3$ s.f.).

**(b)** $&#36;990\times 645.504=&#36;639048.96$. B1. The exact floor area is used.

Or $&#36;990\times 646=&#36;639540$ if the rounded floor area from part (a) is used.

**(c)** Let &#36;x be the cost of the unit. Amount covered by the housing loan $=&#36;0.55x$.

$$
\text{Simple interest}=\frac{0.55x\times 3.0\times 25}{100}=0.4125x
$$

M1 for calculating simple interest.

$$
\text{Total to be repaid}=0.55x+0.4125x=0.9625x
$$

M1 for the total loan amount to be repaid.

$$
\text{Each monthly instalment}=\frac{0.9625x}{12\times 25}=\frac{77x}{24000}
$$

M1 for one monthly instalment.

$$
30\%\times 7500=&#36;2250
$$

$$
\frac{77x}{24000}\leq 2250
$$

$$
x\leq 701298.70\ (2\text{ d.p.})
$$

M1 for the inequality between the monthly instalment and $30\%$ of gross monthly income.

$$
\text{Amount for additional levels}=701298.70-639048.96=&#36;62249.74
$$

M1 for calculating the amount for additional levels.

$$
\frac{62249.74}{16000}=3.89060875=3
$$

(round down to the nearest integer). M1 for rounding down.

Maximum level $=1+3=4$th level. A1.

Alternative method 2 uses the first-level price &#36;639048.96, monthly instalment &#36;2050.28, and an increase of &#36;51.33 per level, giving $2050.28+51.33x\leq 2250$ and $x=3$, so the 4th level.

Method 3 (guess and check): a 4th-level unit costs &#36;687048.96 and the monthly instalment is &#36;2204.28, which is less than &#36;2250. A 5th-level unit costs &#36;703048.96 and the monthly instalment is &#36;2255.62, which is more than &#36;2250. Therefore the maximum level is the 4th level.

Method 4: the maximum total of all monthly instalments is $&#36;2250\times 25\times 12=&#36;675000$. Then $\dfrac{7}{4}P=675000$, so $P=&#36;385714.2857$ and the total cost is &#36;701297.7013. Additional levels $=\dfrac{701298.7013-639048.96}{16000}=3$ after rounding down, so the maximum level is the 4th level.
