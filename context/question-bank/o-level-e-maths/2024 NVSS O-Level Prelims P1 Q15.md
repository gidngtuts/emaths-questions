## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 10

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form

$$
\frac{x}{x+9}-\frac{4x+3}{x^{2}-81}.
$$

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{x}{x+9}-\frac{4x+3}{x^{2}-81}=\frac{x(x-9)-(4x+3)}{(x+9)(x-9)}
$$

M1

$$
=\frac{x^{2}-9x-4x-3}{(x+9)(x-9)}=\frac{x^{2}-13x-3}{(x+9)(x-9)}
$$
