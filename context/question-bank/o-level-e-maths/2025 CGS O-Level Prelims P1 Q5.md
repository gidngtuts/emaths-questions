## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Express $\dfrac{1}{2x^{2}-3x-2}-\dfrac{2}{x-2}$ as a single fraction in its simplest form. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{1}{(2x+1)(x-2)}-\frac{2}{x-2} = \frac{-1-4x}{(2x+1)(x-2)}
$$
