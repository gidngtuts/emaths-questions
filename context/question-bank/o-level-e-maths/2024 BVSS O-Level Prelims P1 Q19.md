## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 13

A straight line meets the curve $y = x^{2}-2x-3$ at two points $A(-3, k)$ and $B(4, 5)$.

(i) Show that $k = 12$. [1]

(ii) Find the equation of the straight line. [2]

(iii) Find the length of the line segment $AB$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(i) Substitute $A(-3, k)$ into the equation.

$$
k = (-3)^{2}-2(-3)-3 = 12 \quad\text{B1 (substituting)}
$$

(ii)

$$
\text{Gradient} = \frac{12-5}{-3-4} = -1 \quad\text{M1}
$$

$$
y = -x+c
$$

$$
5 = -4+c
$$

$$
c = 9
$$

$$
y = -x+9 \quad\text{A1}
$$

(iii)

$$
\text{Length of } AB = \sqrt{(12-5)^{2}+(-3-4)^{2}} = \sqrt{98} = 9.89949 = 9.90\text{ units (3 s.f.)} \quad\text{B1}
$$

A more accurate approximation is accepted.
