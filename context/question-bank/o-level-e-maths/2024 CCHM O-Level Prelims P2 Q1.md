## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (a)(iii) 1, (b)(i) 2, (b)(ii) 2; total 8
> **Page:** 4–5

**(a)** The table shows the total electricity consumption in Singapore over three years.

| Year | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total electricity consumption in gigawatt hours (GWh) | 50 779 | 53 483 | 54 884 |

**(i)** In 2021, the electricity consumed by households took up 15.5% of the total electricity consumption. Calculate the amount of electricity consumed by households in 2021, correct to two significant figures. [1]

**(ii)** Calculate the percentage increase in the total electricity consumption from 2020 to 2022. [2]

**(iii)** Express the 2020 electricity consumption in kilowatt hours (kWh), leaving your answer in standard form, correct to two significant figures. [1]

**(b)** A microprocessor is in the shape of a cube where the sides are 5 mm in length.

**(i)** Find the maximum number of microprocessors that can be placed into a container with dimensions 10 cm by 2 cm by 8 cm. [2]

**(ii)** A model of the microprocessor was made to a scale of $10:1$. Given that the surface area of the microprocessor is $150\text{ mm}^{2}$, find the surface area of the model microprocessor in square centimetres. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $0.155\times 53483 = 8289.865 = 8300\text{ GWh}$ (2 s.f.).

**(a)(ii)**

$$
\frac{54884-50779}{50779}\times 100\% = 8.08\%
$$

**(a)(iii)** $50779\times 10^{9}\div 10^{3} = 5.1\times 10^{10}\text{ kWh}$ (2 s.f.).

**(b)(i)** Side $5\text{ mm} = 0.5\text{ cm}$.

$$
\frac{10}{0.5}\times\frac{2}{0.5}\times\frac{8}{0.5} = 20\times 4\times 16 = 1280
$$

**(b)(ii)** Linear scale model : actual $= 10:1$, so area scale $= 100:1$.

$$
100\times 150 = 15000\text{ mm}^{2} = 150\text{ cm}^{2}
$$
