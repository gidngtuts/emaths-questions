## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 12–13

A water dispenser is in the shape of a cylinder and a hemisphere both of radius 10 cm.
The height of the dispenser is 90 cm.

Conical disposable cups of diameter 6 cm and height 5.3 cm are provided to drink the water from the dispenser.

![Water dispenser and conical cup](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ACSBR/images/ACSBR-2024-4E5N-Prelim-Math-P2-QP-pages/page-12.png)

(a) Water is filled to the brim of the dispenser.
Show that the amount of water in the dispenser is $8666\dfrac{2}{3}\pi$ cm$^{3}$.

(b) Find the capacity of one conical cup.
Give your answer to the nearest cm$^{3}$.

(c) Find the height of the water remaining in the dispenser after 250 cups of water have been dispensed.

(d) The conical disposable cups are made of a thin material of negligible thickness.
Calculate the cost of 250 conical cups given that the cost of material is 0.003 cents/cm$^{2}$.
Give your answer to the nearest cent.

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\pi(10^{2})(80)+\frac{2}{3}\pi(10^{3}) = 8666\frac{2}{3}\pi
$$

(b)

$$
\frac{1}{3}\pi(3^{2})(5.3) = 50 \text{ cm}^{3}
$$

(c) Volume of water remaining after dispensing 250 cups

$$
= 8666\frac{2}{3}\pi-\left(250 \times \frac{1}{3}\pi(3^{2})(5.3)\right) = 4691\frac{2}{3}\pi \text{ or } 14739 \text{ cm}^{3}.
$$

Volume of water in the cylinder

$$
= 4691\frac{2}{3}\pi-\frac{2}{3}\pi(10^{3}) = 4025\pi \text{ or } 12645 \text{ cm}^{3}.
$$

Height of water in the cylindrical section

$$
= \frac{4025\pi}{\pi(10^{2})} \text{ or } \frac{12645}{\pi(10^{2})} = 40.25.
$$

Height of water remaining in the dispenser $= 40.25+10 = 50.25$ cm or $50.3$ cm.

Alternative: volume dispensed for 250 cups $= 250 \times 15.9\pi$. Height of water dispensed $= \dfrac{250 \times 15.9\pi}{100\pi} = \dfrac{125}{\pi}$. Height remaining $= 90-\dfrac{125}{\pi} = 50.2$ cm (3 s.f.).

(d) Slant height of a cup $= \sqrt{3^{2}+5.3^{2}} = 6.0902$ cm.

Curved surface area $= \pi(3)(6.0902) = 57.399$ cm$^{2}$.

Cost of 250 cups $= 57.399 \times 250 \times 0.003 = 43$ cents. Accept 44 cents.
