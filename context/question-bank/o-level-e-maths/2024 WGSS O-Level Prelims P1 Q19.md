## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 17

The diagram below shows the speed-time graph of a car's journey.

![Speed-time graph of a car's journey](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/WGSS/images/WGSS_2024_Prelim_EMath_4052-1-_QP-pages/page-17.png)

For this journey, calculate

(a) the acceleration during the first $40$ seconds, [1]

(a) The total distance travelled. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $a = \dfrac{24}{40} = 0.6$ [B1]

(a) Total distance travelled $= \dfrac{1}{2}\times 60\times 24 = 720$ [B1]
