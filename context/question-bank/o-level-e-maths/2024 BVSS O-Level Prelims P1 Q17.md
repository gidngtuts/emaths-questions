## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 12

When written as a product of its prime factors,

$$
X = 2^{n}\times a^{b}.
$$

The cube root of $X$ is $14$.

(a) Find the values of $n$, $a$, and $b$. [2]

(b) Find the largest possible number that will divide $X$ and $756$ exactly. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\sqrt[3]{X} = 14
$$

$$
X = 14^{3} = 2^{3}\times 7^{3}
$$

$n = 3$ B1, $a = 7$, $b = 3$ B1.

(b)

$$
X = 2^{3}\times 7^{3}
$$

$$
756 = 2^{2}\times 3^{3}\times 7 \quad\text{M1}
$$

$$
\text{HCF} = 2^{2}\times 7 = 28 \quad\text{A1}
$$

(B2 if the answer is given without the factorisation shown.)
