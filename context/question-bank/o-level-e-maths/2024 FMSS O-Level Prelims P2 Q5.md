## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b)(i) 2, (b)(ii) 3, (b)(iii) 3; total 11
> **Page:** 14–16

**(a)** $P$ is the point $(8, -4)$ and $Q$ is the point $(6, 2)$. $R$ has the coordinates $(2, h)$, and $\overrightarrow{PQ} = k\overrightarrow{PR}$. Find the value of $h$ and $k$. [3]

**(b)**

![Vectors on points O, P, Q, R, T and S](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/FMSS/images/2024-FMSS-EMATH-P2-PRELIM-QP-pages/page-15.png)

In the diagram, $\overrightarrow{OR} = 2\mathbf{a}$, $\overrightarrow{OP} = 4\mathbf{b}$, $\overrightarrow{TQ} = \mathbf{b}$ and $\overrightarrow{PQ} = 5\mathbf{a}$. $PR$ and $OT$ intersect at point $S$ such that $PS:PR = m:13$.

**(i)** Express $\overrightarrow{OS}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$, $\mathbf{b}$ and $m$ as simply as possible. [2]

**(ii)** Given that $ST:OS = 9:4$, find the value of $m$. [3]

**(iii)** $U$ is the point such that $\overrightarrow{RU} = \dfrac{2}{3}(7\mathbf{a}+6\mathbf{b})$. Explain why $O$, $S$ and $U$ lie on a straight line. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\overrightarrow{PQ} = \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 6 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \overrightarrow{PR} = \begin{pmatrix} -6 \\ h+4 \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
-2 = -6k, \quad k = \frac{1}{3}, \quad h = 14
$$

M1, A1, A1.

**(b)(i)**

$$
\overrightarrow{OS} = 4\mathbf{b}+\frac{m}{13}(-4\mathbf{b}+2\mathbf{a}) = \frac{2m}{13}\mathbf{a}+\left(4-\frac{4m}{13}\right)\mathbf{b}
$$

M1, A1.

**(b)(ii)** $ST:OS = 9:4$, so $\overrightarrow{OS} = \dfrac{4}{13}\overrightarrow{OT}$.

$$
\overrightarrow{OS} = \frac{20}{13}\mathbf{a}+\frac{12}{13}\mathbf{b}, \quad m = 10
$$

M1, A1, A1.

**(b)(iii)**

$$
\overrightarrow{OU} = \frac{20}{3}\mathbf{a}+\frac{12}{3}\mathbf{b} = \frac{4}{3}(5\mathbf{a}+3\mathbf{b})
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{OS} = \frac{4}{13}(5\mathbf{a}+3\mathbf{b})
$$

$\overrightarrow{OU}$ and $\overrightarrow{OS}$ are scalar multiples and share the point $O$, so $O$, $S$ and $U$ lie on a straight line. M1, A1, B1.
