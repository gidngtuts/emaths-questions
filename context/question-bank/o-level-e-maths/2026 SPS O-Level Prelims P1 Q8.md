## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Solve the equation $3x^{2}-7x+1=0$, giving your answers correct to $2$ decimal places. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
x=\frac{7\pm\sqrt{(-7)^{2}-4(3)(1)}}{2(3)}=\frac{7\pm\sqrt{37}}{6}
$$

$x=2.18$ or $x=0.15$
