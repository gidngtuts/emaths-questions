## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 3, (b)(ii) 4; total 9
> **Page:** 16–17

The diagram shows two concentric circles with centre $O$.
$PQ$ is the diameter of the larger circle and $RS$ is the diameter of the smaller circle.
$PS$ and $RQ$ are tangents to the smaller circle.

![Question 6 concentric circles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/NASS/images/NASS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-16.png)

**(a)** Show that the triangle $PSO$ is congruent to triangle $QRO$.
Give a reason for each statement you make. [2]

**(b)** The radius of the larger circle is 8 cm and the radius of the smaller circle is 4.36 cm.

**(i)** Calculate the area of triangle $QRO$. [3]

**(ii)** Calculate the shaded area. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle POS=\angle QOR$ (vertically opposite $\angle$)

$OS=OR$ (radii of circle)

$\angle OSP=\angle ORQ$ (tangent $\perp$ radius)

Triangle $PSO$ is congruent to Triangle $QRO$ (ASA).

OR

$\angle ORQ=\angle OSP$ (tan $\perp$ rad)

$OQ=OP$ (radii of circle)

$OR=OS$ (radii of circle)

Triangle $PSO$ is congruent to Triangle $QRO$ (RHS).

**(b)(i)**

$$
QR=\sqrt{8^{2}-4.36^{2}}
$$

$$
=6.707488353
$$

Area of triangle:

$$
\frac{1}{2}\times 4.36\times 6.707488353=14.6\text{ cm}^{2}\text{ (3 sf)}
$$

**(b)(ii)** Shaded circular area:

$$
\pi(8)^{2}-\pi(4.36)^{2}=141.3415101
$$

$$
\angle ROQ=\cos^{-1}\left(\frac{4.36}{8}\right)
$$

$$
=0.9944072121
$$

Area of sector:

$$
\frac{1}{2}(4.36)^{2}(0.9944072121)=9.451641669
$$

Total shaded area:

$$
141.34151-2(14.62232461-9.451641669)+2(9.451641669)
$$

$$
=149.9034\text{ cm}^{2}
$$

$$
=150\text{ cm}^{2}\text{ (3 sf)}
$$
