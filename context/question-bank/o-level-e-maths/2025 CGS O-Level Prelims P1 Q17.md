## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 11

The diagram shows a quadrilateral $ABCD$.
$AB = 5.4\text{ cm}$, $BC = 7.6\text{ cm}$, $CD = 8.3\text{ cm}$, angle $BCD = 75^{\circ}$ and angle $ADB = 32^{\circ}$.

![Question 17 quadrilateral ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-11.png)

**(a)** Find $BD$. [3]

**(b)** Find angle $BAD$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
BD^{2} = 7.6^{2}+8.3^{2}-2(7.6)(8.3)\cos 75^{\circ}
$$

$$
BD = 9.70\text{ cm}
$$

**(b)** $107.9^{\circ}$
