## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [2]; (c) [3]; total [6]
> **Page:** 14

![Question 24 circle diagram](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P1-QP-pages/page-14.png)

$O$ is the centre of the circle through $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, and $E$. Angle $COD = 82^{\circ}$, angle $ABD = 55^{\circ}$, angle $BAE = 70^{\circ}$ and angle $BCO =$ angle $EDO = x^{\circ}$.

Giving a reason for each step of your working, find

**(a)** angle $ODC$, [1]

**(b)** angle $AEC$, [2]

**(c)** $x$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\text{angle } ODC = \frac{180^{\circ}-82^{\circ}}{2} = 49^{\circ}
$$

(base $\angle$s of isos. $\triangle$) [A1]

**(b)**

$$
\text{angle } CED = \frac{\angle COD}{2} = \frac{82^{\circ}}{2} = 41^{\circ}
$$

(angle at the centre $= 2 \times$ angle at circumference) [B1]

angle $AEC = 180^{\circ}-$ angle $ABD-$ angle $CED$ ($\angle$s in opp. segments)

$= 180^{\circ}-55^{\circ}-41^{\circ} = 84^{\circ}$ [A1]

**(c)** Let angle $BDC$ be $y$.

angle $DBC + x^{\circ}+$ angle $OCD + y = 180^{\circ}$ ($\angle$ sum of $\triangle$)

$41^{\circ}+x^{\circ}+49^{\circ}+y = 180^{\circ}$ … (1)

angle $BAE + x^{\circ}+$ angle $CDE - y = 180^{\circ}$ ($\angle$s in opp. segments)

$70^{\circ}+x^{\circ}+49^{\circ}-y = 180^{\circ}$ … (2) [M1]

(1) + (2), $209^{\circ}+2x^{\circ} = 360^{\circ}$ [M1]

$x^{\circ} = 75.5^{\circ}$ [A1]
