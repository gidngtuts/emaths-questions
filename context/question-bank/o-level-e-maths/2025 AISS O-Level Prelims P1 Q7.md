## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** A map has a scale of $1:2500000$.
The distance between Singapore and Phuket is $1314$ km.
Calculate the distance, in centimetres, between Singapore and Phuket on the map. [2]

**(b)** When a ball is dropped, the distance, $d$ metres, it falls is directly proportional to the square of the time, $t$ seconds, from when the ball is released.
The distance from which the ball is dropped is increased by $44\%$.
Calculate the percentage change in the time taken for the ball to reach the ground. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** A map has a scale of $1:2500000$. The distance between Singapore and Phuket is $1314$ km. Calculate the distance, in centimetres, between Singapore and Phuket on the map.

M1 for correctly converting from cm to km.

$25$ km represents $1$ cm.
$1314$ km represents $52.56$ cm.

A1, exact answer only.

**(b)** When a ball is dropped, the distance, $d$ metres, it falls is directly proportional to the square of the time, $t$ seconds, from when the ball is released. The distance from which the ball is dropped is increased by $44\%$. Calculate the percentage change in the time taken for the ball to reach the ground.

$$
d \propto t^{2}
$$

$$
1.44d \propto (1.2t)^{2}=1.44t^{2}
$$

M1 for getting $1.2^{2}t^{2}$ or $1.2t$ as the new time.

$$
\text{Percentage change}=\frac{1.2t-t}{t}\times 100\%=20\%
$$

A1.
