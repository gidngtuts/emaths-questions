## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [1]; (b) [2]; total [3]
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** The sum of three angles of a quadrilateral is $285^{\circ}$. Find the fourth angle. [1]

**(b)** Each interior angle of a regular polygon is $165^{\circ}$. Find the number of sides of this polygon. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Sum of interior angles $= (n-2)(180^{\circ})$

$285^{\circ}+$ fourth angle $= ((4-2)(180^{\circ})$

Fourth angle $= 360^{\circ}-285^{\circ} = 75^{\circ}$ [A1]

**(b)** Given: interior angle of a regular polygon is $165^{\circ}$

1 ext angle $= 180^{\circ}-165^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$ [M1]

$$
1\text{ ext angle} = \frac{360^{\circ}}{n}
$$

$$
n = \frac{360^{\circ}}{15^{\circ}} = 24
$$

Number of sides is 24. [A1]
