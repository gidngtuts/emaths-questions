## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 4

Factorise the following expressions completely.

**(a)** $5m+2n-mn-10$ [2]

**(b)** $x^{2}+4xy-9+4y^{2}$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
5m+2n-mn-10 = 5(m-2)+n(2-m) = -(5-n)(m-2)
$$

or $(n-5)(2-m)$.

**(b)**

$$
x^{2}+4xy+4y^{2}-9 = (x+2y)^{2}-9 = (x+2y+3)(x+2y-3)
$$
