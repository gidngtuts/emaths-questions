## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 11

(a) Given that $\dfrac{2a-b}{a-3b}=\dfrac{2}{9}$, find the value of $\dfrac{a}{b}$. [2]

(b) Consider the equation $(m+5)^{80}+(n-8)^{100}=0$. Determine the value of $m+n$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\frac{2a-b}{a-3b}=\frac{2}{9}
$$

$$
9(2a-b)=2(a-3b)
$$

$$
18a-9b=2a-6b
$$

$$
16a=3b
$$

$$
\frac{a}{b}=\frac{3}{16}
$$

(b)

$$
(m+5)^{80}+(n-8)^{100}=0
$$

$$
(m+5)^{80}\geq 0\quad\text{and}\quad (n-8)^{100}\geq 0
$$

$$
m+5=0\quad\text{and}\quad n-8=0
$$

$$
m=-5,\quad n=8
$$

$$
m+n=-5+8=3
$$
