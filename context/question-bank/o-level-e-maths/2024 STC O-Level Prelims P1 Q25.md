## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 20

It is given that $y$ is inversely proportional to $\sqrt{x-1}$ and that the difference in the values of $y$ is $\dfrac{1}{18}$ when the values of $x$ are 10 and 17. Express $y$ in terms of $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
y=\frac{k}{\sqrt{x-1}}
$$

When $x=10$, $y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{10-1}}=\dfrac{k}{3}$.

When $x=17$, $y=\dfrac{k}{\sqrt{17-1}}=\dfrac{k}{4}$.

$$
\frac{k}{3}-\frac{k}{4}=\frac{1}{18}
$$

$$
k=\frac{12}{18}=\frac{2}{3}
$$

$$
y=\frac{2}{3\sqrt{x-1}}
$$
