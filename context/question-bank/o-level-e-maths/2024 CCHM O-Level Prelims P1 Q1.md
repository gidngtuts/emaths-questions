## Question

> **Marks:** 1
> **Page:** 3

The length of a court is $0.028\text{ km}$ and the width of the court is $16\text{ m}$. Express the ratio of the length to the width of the court in its simplest form.

## Answer Scheme

$28 : 16 = 7 : 4$.
