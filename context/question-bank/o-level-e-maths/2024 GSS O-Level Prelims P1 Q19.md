## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 14

Siti has $x$ number of ＄5 notes. Aisha has $y$ number of ＄2 notes. They have a total of 30 notes with total value ＄111. Form and solve two simultaneous equations to calculate the amount of money Siti has. [4]

## Answer Scheme

$$
5x+2y = 111 \quad (2)
$$

$$
x+y = 30 \quad (1)
$$

$(2)-2\times(1)$: $3x = 51$, so $x = 17$. Substitute into $(1)$: $y = 13$.

Amount of money Siti has $= 17\times 5 =$ ＄$85$.

B1 and B1 for forming the correct equations. M1 for solving. A1.
