## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

A bag contains blue, green and red marbles. There are 3 more green marbles than blue marbles. Half of the marbles in the bag are red.
Given that the probability of choosing a blue marble is $\dfrac{6}{25}$, find the number of green marbles in the bag.

## Answer Scheme

Blue $x$, green $x+3$, red $2x+3$, total $4x+6$

$\dfrac{x}{4x+6}=\dfrac{6}{25}$, so $x=36$ and green $=39$
