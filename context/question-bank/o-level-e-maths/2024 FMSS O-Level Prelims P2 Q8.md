## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 2, (d) 1; total 9
> **Page:** 21–22

![Two triangular plots ABC and ADC with lengths and bearings](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/FMSS/images/2024-FMSS-EMATH-P2-PRELIM-QP-pages/page-21.png)

$ABC$ and $ADC$ are two triangular plots of farmland. $AB = 1.2\text{ km}$, $AC = 1.4\text{ km}$ and $BC = 900\text{ m}$. $\angle ADC = 122^{\circ}$, $\angle DAC = 33^{\circ}$ and $\angle ACD = 25^{\circ}$.

**(a)** Calculate $\angle BAC$. [3]

**(b)** Find the shortest distance from $D$ to a point along the path $AC$. [3]

**(c)** A meteorological tower of 300 m tall is installed vertically at point $D$. Find the largest angle of depression from the top of the tower to a point along the path $AC$. [2]

**(d)** $A$ is due west of $C$. Find the bearing of $A$ from $B$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
0.9^{2} = 1.2^{2}+1.4^{2}-2(1.2)(1.4)\cos\angle BAC
$$

$$
\angle BAC = 39.6^{\circ} \text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$

M1, M1, A1.

**(b)** $AD = 0.697679\text{ km}$ and $CD = 0.899117\text{ km}$. Shortest distance $DX = 0.380\text{ km}$ (3 s.f.), using either $\sin 33^{\circ}$ from $AD$ or $\sin 25^{\circ}$ from $CD$. M1, M1, A1.

**(c)** Tower height $0.3\text{ km}$.

$$
\tan\theta = \frac{0.3}{0.379983}, \quad \theta = 38.3^{\circ} \text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$

M1, A1.

**(d)** Bearing of $A$ from $B$ is $180^{\circ}+(90^{\circ}-39.57^{\circ}) = 230.4^{\circ}$ (1 d.p.). B1.
