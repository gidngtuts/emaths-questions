## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 14–15

$CBEO$ is a parallelogram. The position vectors of $C$ and $E$, relative to $O$, are $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ respectively.

![Parallelogram CBEO. Vector OC is a and vector OE is b. Point A is above the parallelogram. D lies on CB, and A, D and E are collinear.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_-Prelim-P2_QP-pages/page-14.png)

It is given that $3BC=5BD$ and $AD=\dfrac{2}{3}DE$.

**(a)** Find, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$,

**(i)** $\overrightarrow{CD}$, [1]

**(ii)** $\overrightarrow{AD}$. [3]

**(b)** Is $AC$ parallel to $OC$? Justify your answer with appropriate working. [2]

**(c)** Find

**(i)** $\dfrac{\text{area of }\triangle ADB}{\text{area of }\triangle ACD}$, [1]

**(ii)** $\dfrac{\text{area of }\triangle ADC}{\text{area of }\triangle AOE}$, [2]

**(iii)** $\dfrac{\text{area of }\triangle ADB}{\text{area of }CDEO}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)**

$$
\overrightarrow{CD}=\frac{2}{5}\mathbf{b}
$$

**(a) (ii)**

$$
\overrightarrow{DE}=\overrightarrow{DC}+\overrightarrow{CO}+\overrightarrow{OE}
= -\frac{2}{5}\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}
= -\mathbf{a}+\frac{3}{5}\mathbf{b}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{AD}=\frac{2}{3}\left(-\mathbf{a}+\frac{3}{5}\mathbf{b}\right)
= \frac{2}{5}\mathbf{b}-\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\overrightarrow{AC}=\overrightarrow{AD}+\overrightarrow{DC}
= -\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a}
= -\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{OC}
$$

Therefore $AC$ is parallel to $OC$.

**(c) (i)** Let $h$ be the common height of $\triangle ADB$ and $\triangle ACD$ from $A$ to $CB$.

$$
\frac{\text{area of }\triangle ADB}{\text{area of }\triangle ACD}
=\frac{\frac{1}{2}\times DB\times h}{\frac{1}{2}\times CD\times h}
=\frac{3}{2}
$$

**(c) (ii)** Since

$$
\frac{AC}{AO}=\frac{AD}{AE}=\frac{CD}{OE},
$$

$\triangle ADC$ is similar to $\triangle AOE$.

$$
\frac{\text{area of }\triangle ADC}{\text{area of }\triangle AOE}
=\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{2}
=\frac{4}{25}
$$

The scheme writes the left side as area of $\triangle ADB$ over area of $\triangle ACD$ in this step. The ratio used is $\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)^{2}=\dfrac{4}{25}$, which is the answer given for (c)(ii).

**(c) (iii)**

$$
\text{area of }\triangle ADB:\text{area of }\triangle ACD:\text{area of }\triangle AOE
= 6:4:25
$$

$$
\frac{\text{area of }\triangle ADB}{\text{area of }CDEO}
=\frac{6}{25-4}
=\frac{2}{7}
$$

M1 for finding the area of $CDEO$ relative to the area of $\triangle ADB$.
