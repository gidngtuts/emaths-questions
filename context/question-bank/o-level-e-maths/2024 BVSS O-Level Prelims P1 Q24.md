## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 18

In the diagram, $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are points on a circle. $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at $F$. Angle $CBF = x^{\circ}$ and angle $BAC = 35^{\circ}$.

![Circle through A, B, C and D; chords AC and BD meet at F; angle BAC is 35 degrees and angle CBF is x degrees](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BVSS/images/BVSS-2024-Prelim-EM-Paper-1-pages/page-18.png)

(a) Find, in terms of $x$, angle $BCD$. Give reason(s) clearly. [2]

(b) Given that angle $ADB = 55^{\circ}$, show that $AC$ is a diameter of the circle. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Angle $CAD = x$ (angles in the same segment). M1 with reason.

Angle $BCD = 180-(35+x)$ (angles in opposite segments) $= 145-x$. A1 with the reason above.

(b) Angle $BCA =$ angle $ADB$ (angles in the same segment) $= 55^{\circ}$.

Angle $ACD = 145-x-55 = 90^{\circ}-x$.

Angle $ADC = 180-x-(90-x) = 90^{\circ}$. M1 (accept without reason).

Since angle $ADC = 90^{\circ}$, $AC$ is a diameter of the circle (right angle in a semicircle). A1.
