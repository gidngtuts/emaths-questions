## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 8

The points $(4, 20)$ and $(10, -4)$ satisfy the curve given by the equation $y = ax^{2}+bx-4$. Use an algebraic method to determine the values of $a$ and $b$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

$$
20 = a(4)^{2}+b(4)-4 \Rightarrow 24 = 16a+4b \Rightarrow 6 = 4a+b \quad (1)
$$

$$
-4 = a(10)^{2}+b(10)-4 \Rightarrow 100a+10b = 0 \Rightarrow 10a+b = 0 \quad (2)
$$

Solve by elimination or substitution. $a = -1$, $b = 10$. M1 for either equation. M1 for solving. A2.
