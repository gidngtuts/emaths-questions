## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 7

A map of Mount Fuji has a scale of $1:25000$.

(a) The length of a trail on the map is $52.2$ cm. Calculate the actual length, in kilometres, of the trail. [2]

(b) A lake at the base of Mount Fuji has an actual area of $6.57\text{ km}^{2}$. Calculate the area, in square centimetres, of the lake on the map. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $1:25000$ gives $52.2:1305000$. $1305000\text{ cm} = 13.05\text{ km}$. [M1, A1]

(b) $1\text{ cm}:0.25\text{ km}$, so $1\text{ cm}^{2}:0.0625\text{ km}^{2}$. Map area $= \dfrac{6.57}{0.0625} = 105.12\text{ cm}^{2}$. [M1, A1]
