## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (a)(iii) 1, (a)(iv) 1, (b)(i) 3, (b)(ii) 1, (b)(iii) 2; total 10
> **Page:** 14–15

**(a)** A group of 50 patients had their blood pressures taken.
The results are shown in the table.

| Blood pressure (mmHg) | $100<p\leq 120$ | $120<p\leq 140$ | $140<p\leq 160$ | $160<p\leq 180$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frequency | 27 | 12 | 6 | 5 |

**(i)** Find the interval that contains the median blood pressure. [1]

**(ii)** Calculate an estimate of the mean blood pressure. [1]

**(iii)** Calculate an estimate of the standard deviation of the blood pressures. [1]

**(iv)** The standard deviation of the blood pressures taken by a second group of patients was $22.1\text{ mmHg}$.

Use this information to comment on one difference between the two distributions. [1]

**(b)** The heights of two groups of students are measured.
Their heights are shown in the stem-and-leaf diagram.

| Group A | Stem | Group B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $1$ | $14$ | $2$ |
| $9\ 8\ 8\ 2$ | $15$ | $3\ x\ 7\ 8$ |
| $4\ 3\ 3$ | $16$ | $0\ 2\ 4$ |
| $3\ 0$ | $17$ | $8$ |

Key: $1\mid 14\mid 2$ means a height of $141\text{ cm}$ in group A and a height of $142\text{ cm}$ in group B.

**(i)** The interquartile range for Group B is $10\text{ cm}$.

Show that the value of $x$ is $3$. [3]

**(ii)** One of the students from Group A is selected at random.

Find the probability that the student has a height between $152\text{ cm}$ and $164\text{ cm}$. [1]

**(iii)** Two students are selected at random, one from Group A and the other from Group B.

Find the probability that one has a height above $160\text{ cm}$ and the other has a height below $155\text{ cm}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)** Find the interval that contains the median blood pressure.

$$
100<p\leq 120
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Most were able to get the correct interval.

**(a) (ii)** Calculate an estimate of the mean blood pressure.

$$
125.6\text{ mmHg}
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Well done

**(a) (iii)** Calculate an estimate of the standard deviation of the blood pressures.

$$
20.1\text{ mmHg}
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Well done

**(a) (iv)** Comment on one difference between the two distributions.

The blood pressures of the second group of patients is less consistent than that of the first group of patients, since it has a higher SD compared to the first group.

> **Marker's Comments:** Good. Many correctly used the terms 'less consistent' or 'more consistent'.

**(b) (i)** Show that the value of $x$ is $3$.

Group B: $142$, $153$, $y$, $157$, $158$, $160$, $162$, $164$, $178$.

$$
Q_{3}=\frac{162+164}{2}=163
$$

$$
163-Q_{1}=10
$$

$$
Q_{1}=153
$$

$$
Q_{1}=\frac{153+y}{2}=153
$$

$$
y=153
$$

$$
x=3\quad(\text{shown})
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Poorly done. A number of students gave wrong presentations: $15x$ means $15$ multiplied by $x$. Wrong interpretation: $x$ is not the height.

**(b) (ii)** Find the probability that the student has a height between $152\text{ cm}$ and $164\text{ cm}$.

$$
\frac{5}{10}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** $\dfrac{7}{10}$ was a common answer, not realising that lengths $152\text{ cm}$ and $164\text{ cm}$ are not included.

**(b) (iii)** Find the probability that one has a height above $160\text{ cm}$ and the other has a height below $155\text{ cm}$.

$$
\frac{5}{10}\times\frac{3}{9}+\frac{2}{10}\times\frac{3}{9}=\frac{7}{30}
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Many had only one possible outcome, $\dfrac{5}{10}\times\dfrac{3}{9}$, not realizing that there was another possible outcome.
