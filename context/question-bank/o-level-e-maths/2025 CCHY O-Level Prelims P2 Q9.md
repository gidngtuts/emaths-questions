## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 8; total 9
> **Page:** 18–20

Irene is looking to buy furniture from EKAYA.
She identifies the following items from the catalogue that she needs to purchase, and lists down the approximate amount of time that she will take if she assembles each item.

| Item | Retail Price per item (GST included) | Quantity | Approximate time to assemble each item (min) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bed frame, $218 \times 155 \times 40\text{ cm}$, $36.25\text{ kg}$ | SGD 249 | 1 | 95 |
| Storage solution, $12.30\text{ kg}$ | SGD 119 | 2 | 20 |
| Desk with Drawers, $140 \times 78 \times 75\text{ cm}$, $22.10\text{ kg}$ | SGD 149 | 1 | 35 |
| Folding chair, $100 \times 45 \times 47\text{ cm}$, $3.00\text{ kg}$, wall-mountable, requires one hole | SGD 29 | 5 | 10 |
| Bookshelf, $80 \times 28 \times 202\text{ cm}$, $22.50\text{ kg}$ | SGD 129 | 1 | 45 |
| Alarm clock, $15 \times 13 \times 5\text{ cm}$, $0.19\text{ kg}$ | SGD 19 | 1 | Does not require assembly |

**(a)** Calculate the total cost of the furniture that Irene intends to buy. [1]

Irene has SGD 1000 EKAYA credits from lucky draw that allows her to make a one-time purchase on the EKAYA online store.

Irene lives on the 4th floor of an old estate that does not have lift access.
She has back issues and would have difficulties handling weight above $25\text{ kg}$.
She also wants to wall-mount all the folding chairs, but she does not have a drill.
She does not want to pay more than SGD 110 out of her pocket.
She does not want to spend more than 80 minutes assembling on her own.

EKAYA

Delivery — You can take it home yourself – but you don’t have to.

Home delivery is SGD 35 for orders above $15\text{ kg}$. We'll pick and deliver as many products as you want. Delivery typically takes place within 2-5 days from the date of order.

Please note for home delivery, an additional flat rate of SGD 20 will apply if there is no lift access, or if items cannot fit in the lift at the delivery address located in a building from the 3rd floor onwards. This service is applicable up to the 5th floor only.

Assembly — You can assemble it yourself – but you don’t have to.

Service fees

1. All other furniture that requires assembly: $20\%$ of the original product's retail price.
2. Drilling services are included as part of the assembly services.
3. A SGD 5 charge per drilled hole applies to products, which require to be wall-mounted and where assembly service has not been booked.

All costs are subject to $9\%$ GST.

**(b)** Suggest how Irene could minimise both the costs spent and the time for assembly for all the furniture purchased.
Justify all the decisions you make and show your calculations clearly. [8]
