## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 10

Simplify $\dfrac{-9x^{2}+12x-4}{3-\dfrac{1}{x-\dfrac{x}{2}}}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{-9x^{2}+12x-4}{3-\dfrac{1}{x-\dfrac{x}{2}}} &= \frac{-(3x-2)^{2}}{3-\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{2x-x}{2}}} \\
&= \frac{-(3x-2)^{2}}{3-\dfrac{2}{x}} \\
&= \frac{-(3x-2)^{2}}{\dfrac{3x-2}{x}} \\
&= -x(3x-2)
\end{aligned}
$$
