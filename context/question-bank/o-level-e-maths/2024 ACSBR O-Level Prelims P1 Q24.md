## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 19

$A$ is the point $(8, -2)$ and $\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}$.

(a) Find

(i) $AB$,

(ii) the equation of the line $AB$.

(b) The equation of the line $CD$ is $2y-4x = 19$.
Find the coordinates of the point of intersection of $AB$ and $CD$.

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) $AB = 5$

(ii) Gradient of $AB = -\dfrac{3}{4}$.

$$
-2 = -\frac{3}{4}(8)+c, \qquad c = 4
$$

Equation is $y = -\dfrac{3}{4}x+4$.

(b) Or elimination, following through from (a)(ii).

$$
2\left(-\frac{3}{4}x+4\right)-4x = 19
$$

$x = -2$, $y = 5.5$, so $(-2, 5.5)$.
