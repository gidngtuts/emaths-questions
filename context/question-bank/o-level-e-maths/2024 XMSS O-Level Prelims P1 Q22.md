## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 19

The diagram shows a circle with centre $O$. The angle of the minor sector is $\theta$ radians.

The perimeter of the major sector is three times the perimeter of the minor sector.
Show that $\theta = 0.571$, correct to 3 significant figures. [4]

## Answer Scheme

Major sector perimeter $= r(2\pi-\theta)+2r$. [M1]

Minor sector perimeter $= r\theta+2r$. [M1]

$3(r\theta+2r) = r(2\pi-\theta)+2r$ [M1]

$3\theta+6 = 2\pi-\theta+2$

$4\theta+4 = 2\pi$

$\theta+1 = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$

$\theta = 0.57079 = 0.571$ (shown). [A1]
