## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{5x}{18}-\dfrac{5x+12}{54}-\dfrac{2(x-1)}{27}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{5x}{18}-\frac{5x+12}{54}-\frac{2(x-1)}{27}=\frac{15x-(5x+12)-4(x-1)}{54}
$$

$$
=\frac{10x-12-4x+4}{54}=\frac{6x-8}{54}=\frac{3x-4}{27}
$$
