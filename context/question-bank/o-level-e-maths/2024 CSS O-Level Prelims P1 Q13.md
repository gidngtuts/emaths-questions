## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 9

The following stem-and-leaf diagram shows the scores of students in a math test.

| Stem | Leaf |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 8 9 |
| 7 | 0 2 3 3 4 4 4 7 8 |
| 8 | 3 4 5 6 7 7 9 |
| 9 | 1 3 7 |

Key: $6\mid 8$ represents 68 marks.

Find

**(a)** the median score, [1]

**(b)** the interquartile range. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $78$.

**(b)** The interquartile range $= 87-73 = 14$.
