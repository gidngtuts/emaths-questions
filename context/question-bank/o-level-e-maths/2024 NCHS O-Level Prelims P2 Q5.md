## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [4]; (b) [3]; (c) [3]
> **Page:** 10–11

The diagram shows four points $W$, $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ in an open field where $W$ is due north of $Y$.
$WZ = 12.5$ m and $WX = 10.3$ m.
Angle $WZY = 102.6^{\circ}$, angle $WYZ = 34^{\circ}$ and angle $XWY = 45.3^{\circ}$.

![Points W, X, Y and Z with the given lengths and angles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NCHS/images/2024-NCHS-Prelim-Math-P2-QP-pages/page-10.png)

**(a)** Calculate $XY$.

**(b)** Calculate the bearing of $W$ from $Z$.

A vertical tree of height 8 m is situated at point $Z$.
A man whose height is 1.9 m, walks from point $Y$ to point $W$.

**(c)** Find the greatest angle of elevation of the top of the tree from the top of the man’s head.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{WY}{\sin 102.6^{\circ}} &= \frac{12.5}{\sin 34^{\circ}} \\
WY &= 21.815 \\
XY^{2} &= 10.3^{2}+21.815^{2}-2(10.3)(21.815)\cos 45.3^{\circ} \\
XY &= 16.3\text{ m (3 s.f.)}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(b)** $\angle YWZ = 180^{\circ}-(34^{\circ}+102.6^{\circ}) = 43.4^{\circ}$.

Bearing of $W$ from $Z = 360^{\circ}-43.4^{\circ} = 316.6^{\circ}$.

**(c)** Shortest horizontal distance from the path to $Z$ is $l = 12.5\sin 43.4^{\circ} = 8.5886$ m.

$$
\tan\theta = \frac{8-1.9}{8.5886},\quad \theta = 35.4^{\circ}
$$
