## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 15

Solve $\dfrac{4x+5}{x} = 4+\dfrac{3}{x+2}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{4x+5}{x} = \frac{4x+11}{x+2} \quad \text{M1}
$$

$$
(4x+5)(x+2) = x(4x+11) \quad \text{M1}
$$

$$
4x^{2}+13x+10 = 4x^{2}+11x
$$

$$
2x = -10
$$

$$
x = -5 \quad \text{A1}
$$
