## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 12–13

(a) $A$ is the point $(2, 3)$ and $B$ is the point $(3, -5)$.

(i) Find the equation of the line $AB$. [2]

(ii) Find the length of $AB$. [2]

(iii) $\overrightarrow{BC} = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$. Find the coordinates of point $C$. [2]

(b) $OABC$ is a parallelogram. $\overrightarrow{OA} = 2\mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OC} = 3\mathbf{c}$. $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$. $T$ is the point on $OB$ such that $OT:TB = 2:1$.

(i) Find $\overrightarrow{OB}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{c}$. [1]

(ii) Find $\overrightarrow{OT}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{c}$. [1]

(iii) Determine if the points $A$, $T$ and $M$ lie on a straight line. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) Gradient $= -8$. $3 = -8(2)+c$, so $c = 19$ and $y = -8x+19$. M1, A1.

(ii) $\sqrt{(2-3)^{2}+(3-(-5))^{2}} = 8.06$ units. M1, A1.

Alternatively, $\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}-\begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -8 \end{pmatrix}$ and $AB = \sqrt{1^{2}+(-8)^{2}} = 8.06$.

(iii)

$$
\overrightarrow{OC} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}
$$

$C(-1, -2)$. M1, A1.

(b) (i) $\overrightarrow{OB} = 2\mathbf{a}+3\mathbf{c}$. B1.

(ii) $\overrightarrow{OT} = \dfrac{2}{3}(3\mathbf{c}+2\mathbf{a})$. B1.

(iii)

$$
\overrightarrow{AT} = -2\mathbf{a}+\frac{2}{3}(3\mathbf{c}+2\mathbf{a}) = 2\mathbf{c}-\frac{2}{3}\mathbf{a} = \frac{2}{3}(3\mathbf{c}-\mathbf{a})
$$

$\overrightarrow{AM} = 3\mathbf{c}-\mathbf{a}$, so $\overrightarrow{AT} = \dfrac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AM}$.

M1 for $\overrightarrow{AT}$, $\overrightarrow{AM}$ or $\overrightarrow{TM}$. M1 for $\overrightarrow{AT} = k\overrightarrow{AM}$. A1: $AT$ is parallel to $AM$ and $A$ is common, so $A$, $T$ and $M$ lie on a straight line.
