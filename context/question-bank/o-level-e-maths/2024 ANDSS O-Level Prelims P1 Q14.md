## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

$W=\{0,\{0\}\}$.

**(a)** Find $n(W)$. [1]

**(b)** List all possible subsets of $W$. [1]

**(c)** Write down the set represented by the following shaded region. [1]

![Venn diagram. The rectangle and all of set A are shaded. The part of set B that is outside A is unshaded.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_Prelim_P1_QP-pages/page-13.png)

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $n(W)=2$

**(b)** $\varnothing$, $\{0\}$, $\{\{0\}\}$, $\{0,\{0\}\}$

**(c)** $A\cup B'$, or $(A'\cap B)'$, or $(A\cup B)'\cup A$, or $(A\cap B)\cup B'$.
