## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 4; total 10
> **Page:** 14–15

The diagram shows a circle $ABCD$, centre $O$.
$AT$ and $CT$ are tangents to the circle.
Angle $ATC=32^{\circ}$ and angle $ACB=38^{\circ}$.

![Question 6 circle](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-14.png)

**(a)** Find angle $ADC$.
Give a reason for each step of your working. [3]

**(b)** Determine if $OC$ parallel to $AB$.
Give a reason for each step of your working. [3]

**(c)** The radius of the circle is 10 cm.

Calculate the area of the shaded region. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle OCT=90^{\circ}$ (tangent $\perp$ radius)

$$
\angle AOC=360^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-32^{\circ}=148^{\circ}
$$

($\angle$ sum of quadrilateral)

$$
\angle ADC=148^{\circ}\div 2=74^{\circ}
$$

($\angle$ at centre $=2\angle$ at circumference)

**(b)**

$$
\angle CBA=180^{\circ}-74^{\circ}=106^{\circ}
$$

($\angle$s in opposite segment)

$$
\angle OCA=\frac{180^{\circ}-148^{\circ}}{2}=16^{\circ}
$$

(base $\angle$s of isosceles triangle)

$$
\angle OCB=16^{\circ}+38^{\circ}=54^{\circ}
$$

$$
\angle OCB+\angle CBA=106^{\circ}+54^{\circ}=160^{\circ}\neq 180^{\circ}
$$

Therefore, by converse of interior angles, $OC$ is not parallel to $AB$.

**(c)**

$$
\angle AOB=38^{\circ}\times 2=76^{\circ}
$$

($\angle$ at centre $=2\angle$ at circumference)

$$
\text{Area of sector}=\frac{76}{360}\times\pi(10^{2})=\frac{190}{9}\pi
$$

$$
\text{Area of triangle}=\frac{1}{2}(10)(10)\sin 76^{\circ}=48.5147\ldots
$$

$$
\text{Area of shaded region}=\frac{190}{9}\pi-48.5147=17.8\text{ cm}^{2}\text{ (3 s.f.)}
$$
