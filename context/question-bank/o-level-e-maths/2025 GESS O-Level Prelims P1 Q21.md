## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 2; total 6
> **Page:** 18–19

The diagram shows a circle $ABCD$ with centre $O$ and $BD$ passing through $O$.
$AC$ intersects $BD$ at $E$. Angle $ACB=67^{\circ}$ and angle $DAC=24^{\circ}$.

![Question 21 circle ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Prove, stating your reasons clearly, that $\triangle AED$ is similar to $\triangle BEC$. [2]

**(b)** Given that $AE=3.5\text{ cm}$, $CE=4\text{ cm}$, $DE=2\text{ cm}$, calculate the length of $EB$. [2]

**(c)** Calculate, as a fraction, the numerical value of the ratio $\dfrac{\text{minor arc length }AD}{\text{minor arc length }BC}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** M1 for one pair of correct corresponding angles with reasons.

$\angle AED=\angle BEC$ (vert. opp. $\angle$s)

$\angle EAD=\angle EBC=24^{\circ}$ ($\angle$s in the same segment)

$\angle EDA=\angle ECB=67^{\circ}$ ($\angle$s in the same segment)

$\triangle AED$ is similar to $\triangle BEC$ (AA similarity test)

**(b)**

$$
\frac{2}{4}=\frac{3.5}{EB}
$$

$$
EB=7
$$

Alternative solution:

$$
\frac{4}{\sin 24^{\circ}}=\frac{EB}{\sin 67^{\circ}}
$$

$$
EB=\frac{4\sin 67^{\circ}}{\sin 24^{\circ}}=9.05\text{ cm}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

**(c)**

$$
\angle AOD=180^{\circ}-67^{\circ}-67^{\circ}=46^{\circ}
$$

$$
\angle BOC=180^{\circ}-24^{\circ}-24^{\circ}=132^{\circ}
$$

$$
\frac{\dfrac{46}{360}\times 2\pi r}{\dfrac{132}{360}\times 2\pi r}=\frac{46}{132}=\frac{23}{66}
$$
