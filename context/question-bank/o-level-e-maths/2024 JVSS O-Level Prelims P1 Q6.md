## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 5

It is given that

$\xi = \{x : x\text{ is an integer and }1 \leq x < 15\}$

$A = \{x : x\text{ is a multiple of }3\}$

$B = \{x : x\text{ is a prime number}\}$

**(a)** Illustrate the information in the Venn diagram of $\xi$ with overlapping sets $A$ and $B$. [2]

**(b)** List the elements of $A' \cap B$. [1]
