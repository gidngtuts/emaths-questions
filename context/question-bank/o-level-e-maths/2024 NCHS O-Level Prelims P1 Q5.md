## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 5

It is given that $y^{3}$ is inversely proportional to the square of $x$.
The difference between the value of $y^{3}$ when $x = 3$ and $x = 6$ is $5$.
Find the values of $x$ when $y = 2$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
y^{3} &= \frac{k}{x^{2}} \\
\frac{k}{3^{2}}-\frac{k}{6^{2}} &= 5 \\
\frac{k}{9}-\frac{k}{36} &= 5 \\
\frac{k}{12} &= 5 \\
k &= 60 \\
8 &= \frac{60}{x^{2}} \\
x^{2} &= 7.5 \\
x &= \pm 2.74
\end{aligned}
$$
