## Question

> **Marks:** 12
> **Page:** 16–17

$ABCDE$ is a right square-based pyramid. $DE = 12$ cm and $DC = 10$ cm.

![Square-based pyramid ABCDE with DE = 12 cm and DC = 10 cm](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/SJI/images/2024-SJI-Prelim-P2-pages/page-16.png)

(a) Show that the vertical height of the pyramid is 9.695 cm. [4]

(b) Angle $DEC = 49.2^{\circ}$.
Calculate angle $EDC$. [2]

(c) $M$ is the midpoint on $EB$ such that angle $DMC = 55.7^{\circ}$.
Angle $DEB = 72.2^{\circ}$.

![Pyramid with M the midpoint of EB](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/SJI/images/2024-SJI-Prelim-P2-pages/page-17.png)

Calculate the area of triangle $DMC$ giving your answer correct to the nearest whole number. [6]
