## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 12

Show that $x^{2}(3x-10)+2(x^{3}+10x-8)-4$ is divisible by 5 for any integer $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$3x^{3}-10x^{2}+2x^{3}+20x-16-4=5(x^{3}-2x^{2}+4x-4)$

The expression has a factor of 5, so it is divisible by 5 for any integer $x$.
