## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Write $\dfrac{2}{x-2}-\dfrac{3}{2x+1}$ as a single fraction in its simplest form. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2}{x-2}-\frac{3}{2x+1} = \frac{2(2x+1)-3(x-2)}{(x-2)(2x+1)} = \frac{4x+2-3x+6}{(x-2)(2x+1)} = \frac{x+8}{(x-2)(2x+1)}
$$

M1 for combining the fractions. A1.
