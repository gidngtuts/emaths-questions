## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 6

In triangle $ABC$, $AB=14.6\text{ cm}$, $BC=7.6\text{ cm}$ and angle $BAC=31^{\circ}$.

Find

**(a)** obtuse angle $ACB$,

**(b)** area of triangle $ABC$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{\sin\angle ACB}{14.6}=\dfrac{\sin 31^{\circ}}{7.6}$ (M1)

$\angle ACB=81.6562^{\circ}$

Obtuse $\angle ACB=180-81.6562=98.3438=98.3^{\circ}$ (1dp) (A1)

**(b)** Angle $ABC=180-31-98.3438=50.6562^{\circ}$ (M1, formula to find area with their angle)

Area $=\dfrac{1}{2}(14.6)(7.6)\sin(50.6562^{\circ})=42.9\text{ cm}^{2}$ (3sf) (A1)
