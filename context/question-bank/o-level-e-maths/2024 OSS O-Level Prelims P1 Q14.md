## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 13

The diagram shows one interior angle of each of the three polygons, $A$, $B$ and $C$.
The polygons fit together at the point $O$.
The interior angle of polygon $A$ is $90^{\circ}$.
The interior angle of polygon $B$ is $130^{\circ}$.

Explain why polygon $C$ can be a regular polygon.

## Answer Scheme

Interior angle of polygon $C=140^{\circ}$, so the exterior angle is $40^{\circ}$.

$\dfrac{360}{40}=9$, or $\dfrac{(n-2)\times 180}{n}=140$ gives $n=9$.

Polygon $C$ can be a regular polygon with 9 equal sides, each interior angle $140^{\circ}$.
