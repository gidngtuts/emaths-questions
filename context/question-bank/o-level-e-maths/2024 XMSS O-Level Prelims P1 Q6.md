## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Rearrange the formula $10+y = \dfrac{x^{2}+k}{x^{2}}$ to make $x$ the subject. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$x^{2}(10+y) = x^{2}+k$ [M1]

$10x^{2}+yx^{2} = x^{2}+k$

$9x^{2}+yx^{2} = k$

$x^{2}(9+y) = k$

$x^{2} = \dfrac{k}{9+y}$ [M1]

$x = \pm\sqrt{\dfrac{k}{9+y}}$ [A1]

Alternative: $10+y = 1+\dfrac{k}{x^{2}}$, so $9+y = \dfrac{k}{x^{2}}$.
