## Question

> **Page:** 9

$$
P = 2w-\frac{x^{2}y}{w}
$$

**(a)** Find $P$ when $w = 86.3$, $x = 21.41$ and $y = 0.872$.
Give your answer correct to 4 significant figures. [1]

**(b)** Rearrange the formula to make $x$ the subject. [3]

**(b)** Rearrange the formula to make $x$ the subject.

$$
\frac{x^{2}y}{w}=2w-P
$$

$$
x^{2}y=2w^{2}-Pw
$$

$$
x^{2}=\frac{2w^{2}-Pw}{y}
$$

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{\frac{2w^{2}-Pw}{y}}
$$

> **Marker's Comments:** Quite a number still weak in algebraic manipulation. Many did not include the $\pm$
