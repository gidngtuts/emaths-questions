## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3, (c) 3, (d) 5; total 13
> **Page:** 6–7

**(a)** Solve $2x-7 = 3(1-3x)$. [2]

**(b)** Solve the inequalities $7x-1 < 13$ and $\dfrac{x+1}{2} \geq -2(x-2)$. [3]

**(c)** Rearrange the formula $y = \dfrac{x^{2}+5}{7x^{2}}$ to make $x$ the subject. [3]

**(d)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{2x}{1-x}-3 = \dfrac{1}{2x-3}$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [5]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $2x-7 = 3-9x$, so $11x = 10$ and $x = \dfrac{10}{11}$.

**(b)** $7x-1 < 13$ gives $x < 2$.

$$
\frac{x+1}{2} \geq -2(x-2), \quad x+1 \geq -4x+8, \quad x \geq 1.4
$$

$$
1.4 \leq x < 2
$$

**(c)**

$$
7x^{2}y = x^{2}+5, \quad x^{2}(7y-1) = 5, \quad x = \pm\sqrt{\frac{5}{7y-1}}
$$

**(d)**

$$
\frac{2x}{1-x}-3 = \frac{1}{2x-3}
$$

$$
2x(2x-3)-3(1-x)(2x-3) = 1-x
$$

$$
10x^{2}-20x+8 = 0, \quad 5x^{2}-10x+4 = 0
$$

$$
x = \frac{10\pm\sqrt{(-10)^{2}-4(5)(4)}}{10}
$$

$x = 1.45$ or $0.55$. The scheme labels these “(2 s.f.)”; the question asks for two decimal places.
