## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 7

$$
T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}
$$

Rearrange the formula to make $g$ the subject.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
T &= 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} \\
\frac{T}{2\pi} &= \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} \\
\frac{L}{g} &= \frac{T^{2}}{(2\pi)^{2}} \\
g &= \frac{4\pi^{2}L}{T^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
