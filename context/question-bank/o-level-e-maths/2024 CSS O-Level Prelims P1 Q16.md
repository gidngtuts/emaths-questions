## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 11

A box contains 15 marbles of which $x$ are green and the rest are yellow.

**(a)** Write down the probability, in terms of $x$, that a marble chosen from the box is yellow. [1]

**(b)** When 5 more green marbles are added to the box, the probability of choosing a yellow marble becomes $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
Find the initial number of green marbles in the box. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{15-x}{15}$.

**(b)** $P(\text{green marbles}) = P(\text{yellow marbles})$

$$
= \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\frac{x+5}{20} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad x = 5
$$
