## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 16

(a) Simplify $\left(\dfrac{4a^{6}}{b^{4}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}}$, giving your answer in positive index.

(b) $\dfrac{2^{k}}{\sqrt[4]{8}} = 4^{2k}$.

Use the laws of indices to find the value of $k$.
Show your working.

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\left(\frac{4a^{6}}{b^{4}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \left(\frac{b^{4}}{4a^{6}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{b^{2}}{2a^{3}}
$$

(b)

$$
\frac{2^{k}}{8^{1/4}} = 4^{2k}
$$

$$
\frac{2^{k}}{2^{3/4}} = 2^{4k}
$$

$$
k-\frac{3}{4} = 4k
$$

$$
k = -\frac{1}{4}
$$
