## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [2]
> **Page:** 17

In triangle $ABC$, angle $ABC = 90^{\circ}$, $BX = 2XC$ and $\sin\angle AXC = \dfrac{3}{5}$.

![Right-angled triangle ABC with X on BC](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-_Prelim_P1_2024-v2_QP-pages/page-17.png)

Express as a fraction in its lowest terms,

**(a)** $\tan\angle AXB$,

**(b)** $\tan\angle ACB$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\sin\angle AXC = \dfrac{3}{5}$ and the scheme takes $BX = 4$, so $\tan\angle AXB = \dfrac{3}{4}$.

**(b)** $\tan\angle ACB = \dfrac{3}{4+2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
