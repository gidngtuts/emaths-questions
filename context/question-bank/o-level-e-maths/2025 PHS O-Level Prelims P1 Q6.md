## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 6

Given that $A:B=\dfrac{1}{6}:\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $B:C=3:7$, find $A:C$ as a ratio of two integers. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$A:B=5:6$, so $A:C=5:14$.
