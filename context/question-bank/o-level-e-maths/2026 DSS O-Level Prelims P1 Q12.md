## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 11

An investment company published a graph on their website to show the total investment returns they have earned for their clients for each of the last three years.

![Question 12 investment graph](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/DSS/images/Dunman-2026-Emaths-Prelims-Paper-1-pages/page-11.png)

State one aspect of the graph that may be misleading and explain how this may lead to a misinterpretation of the graph. [2]

## Answer Scheme

The increase in area is not (directly) proportional to the height. [B1]

This may mislead the readers that the total investment returns in 2025 is more than 3 times that in 2023. [B1]
