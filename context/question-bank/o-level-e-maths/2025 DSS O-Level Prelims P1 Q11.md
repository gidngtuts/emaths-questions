## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 8

In the diagram, $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are points on a circle, centre $O$.
$OE=OF$.
Prove that triangles $OAB$ and $OCD$ are congruent.
Give a reason for each statement you make. [2]

![Question 11 circle](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-1-QP-pages/page-08.png)

## Answer Scheme

$OD=OB$ (radius of circle)

$OC=OA$ (radius of circle)

$CD=AB$ (equal chords)

Therefore, triangles $OAB$ and $OCD$ are congruent by SSS congruency test.
