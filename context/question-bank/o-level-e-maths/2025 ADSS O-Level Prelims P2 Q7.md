## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [3]; (b) [3]; (c) [4]; total [10]
> **Page:** 13–14

A lighthouse of height 24 m stands vertically at $A$ on the ground of an island. Two boats at $B$ and $C$ such that $AB = 240$ m, $AC = 278.9$ m and $BC = 380.4$ m.

![Question 7 lighthouse and boats](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/ADSS/images/ADSS-Prelim-2025-P2-QP-pages/page-13.png)

**(a)** Calculate the angle $BAC$. [3]

**(b)** Calculate the area of triangle $ABC$. [3]

**(c)** The boat at $C$ sails in a straight line towards $B$. Find the greatest angle of depression of the boat from the top of the lighthouse. [4]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\cos \angle BAC = \frac{240^{2}+278.9^{2}-380.4^{2}}{2 \times 240 \times 278.9} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
\cos \angle BAC = -0.06961 \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
\angle BAC = 93.99^{\circ} = 94.0^{\circ} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\text{area of }\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2}(240)(278.9)\sin 93.99^{\circ} \quad \text{[M1][M1]}
$$

$= 33386.88 = 33400\text{ m}^{2}$ (3 sig fig) [A1]

**(c)** Shortest distance from $A$ to $BC$

$$
= \frac{33386.88 \times 2}{380.4} = 175.5356 = 176\text{ m} \quad \text{[M1][B1]}
$$

$$
\tan(\text{greatest angle of depression}) = \frac{24}{175.5356} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

Greatest angle of depression $= 7.785^{\circ} = 7.79^{\circ}$ [A1]
