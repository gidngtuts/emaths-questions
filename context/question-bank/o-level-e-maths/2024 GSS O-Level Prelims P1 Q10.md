## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

$2c+b = \dfrac{b-c}{a}$.

Rearrange the formula to make $b$ the subject. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
2c+b = \frac{b-c}{a}
$$

$$
2ac+ab = b-c
$$

$$
ab-b = -2ac-c
$$

$$
b(a-1) = -(2ac+c)
$$

$$
b = \frac{-(2ac+c)}{a-1} = \frac{2ac+c}{1-a}
$$

M1 for cross-multiplication. M1 for isolating $b$. A1. Answers with the negative sign in the numerator are accepted.
