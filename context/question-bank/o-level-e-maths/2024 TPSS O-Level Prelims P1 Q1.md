## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Find the largest prime number that satisfies the inequality $\dfrac{3x-8}{2} < 28$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
3x-8 < 56 \quad \text{M1}
$$

$$
3x < 64
$$

$$
x < 21.3
$$

The largest prime number is 19. A1
