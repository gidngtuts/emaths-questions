## Question

> **Marks:** 11
> **Page:** 3–4

(a) Express as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{2}{7-3x}-\dfrac{1}{6-x}$. [2]

(b) It is given that $v = \dfrac{3-5w}{w+2}+9$.

(i) Find $v$ when $w = -6$. [1]

(ii) Rearrange the formula to make $w$ the subject. [3]

(c) Solve the equation $\dfrac{5}{x-2}-\dfrac{3}{x^{2}-4} = \dfrac{1}{7}$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [5]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\frac{2}{7-3x}-\frac{1}{6-x} = \frac{2(6-x)-(7-3x)}{(6-x)(7-3x)} = \frac{5+x}{(6-x)(7-3x)}
$$

M1 for expanding the numerator correctly. A1.

(b) (i) $v = \dfrac{3-5(-6)}{-6+2}+9 = \dfrac{3}{4}$ or $0.75$. B1.

(ii)

$$
(w+2)(v-9) = 3-5w
$$

$$
wv-9w+2v-18 = 3-5w
$$

$$
wv-4w = 21-2v
$$

$$
w = \frac{21-2v}{v-4} = -\frac{21-2v}{4-v}
$$

M1 for clearing the denominator. A1 for grouping the $w$ terms. A1.

(c)

$$
\frac{5}{x-2}-\frac{3}{x^{2}-4} = \frac{1}{7}
$$

$$
\frac{5(x+2)-3}{(x+2)(x-2)} = \frac{1}{7}
$$

$$
7(5x+7) = x^{2}-4
$$

$$
x^{2}-35x-53 = 0
$$

$$
x = \frac{35\pm\sqrt{(-35)^{2}-4(1)(-53)}}{2}
$$

$$
x = -1.45\text{ or }x = 36.45
$$

M1 for a common denominator. M1 for an accurate quadratic. M1 for the quadratic formula. A1 and A1, accepting 2 decimal places only.
