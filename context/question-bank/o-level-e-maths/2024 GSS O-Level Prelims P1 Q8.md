## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 6

(a) Convert $40^{\circ}$ into radians. Leave your answers in terms of $\pi$. [1]

(b) Convert $23\text{ km/h}$ into m/s. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $\dfrac{\pi}{180}\times 40 = \dfrac{2\pi}{9}\text{ rad}$ — B1.

(b) $23\text{ km/h} = \dfrac{23\times 1000}{60\times 60} = \dfrac{115}{18} = 6\dfrac{7}{18} = 6.39\text{ m/s}$ — B1.
