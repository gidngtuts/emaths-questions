## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 4

In the diagram, $ABC$ is a triangle. $D$ is the point on $AC$ such that $\angle ABC = \angle ADB$.

**(a)** Show that triangles $ABC$ and $ADB$ are similar. [2]

**(b)** Given that $AB = 8\text{ m}$ and $AD = 5\text{ m}$, find $AC$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle CAB = \angle BAD$ (common angle).

$\angle ABC = \angle ADB$ (given).

Since two pairs of corresponding angles are equal, triangles $ABC$ and $ADB$ are similar. Or by the AA similarity test.

M1 for showing two pairs of corresponding angles are equal. AG1 for the correct reason.

**(b)**

$$
\frac{AC}{AB} = \frac{AB}{AD}, \quad \frac{AC}{8} = \frac{8}{5}, \quad AC = \frac{8}{5} \times 8 = 12.8\text{ m or } 12\frac{4}{5}\text{ m}
$$

B1.
