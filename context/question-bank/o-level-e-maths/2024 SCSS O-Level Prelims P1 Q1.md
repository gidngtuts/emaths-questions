## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

In the triangle, $PQ = QR$, $PR = 15\text{ cm}$ and angle $PQR = 90^{\circ}$. $Q$ is the right angle, with equal marks on $PQ$ and $QR$, and $PR$ the hypotenuse.

Calculate $PQ$.

## Answer Scheme

$PQ^{2}+PQ^{2}=15^{2}$ (M1)

$2PQ^{2}=225$

$PQ=10.6\text{ cm}$ (A1)
