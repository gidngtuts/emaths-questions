## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 11

The number of university graduates for the year $2000$ and year $2020$ are represented by the chart below. Statistics have shown that the number of university graduates in year $2020$ is doubled the number of university graduates in year $2000$.

![Question 12 chart](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CHS/images/CAT-HIGH-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-11.png)

Explain how the above chart may be misleading and suggest a possible way to improve the chart. [2]

## Answer Scheme

The area of the picture for year $2020$ is more than twice the area of the picture for year $2000$, which might mislead people into thinking that the number of graduates in $2020$ is more than twice that of year $2000$.

1. Replace with bar graphs of equal width and the height of the bar of year $2020$ twice the height of the bar of year $2000$.
2. Replace the year $2020$ picture with two identical $2000$ pictures, and stack the two pictures on top of each other.
