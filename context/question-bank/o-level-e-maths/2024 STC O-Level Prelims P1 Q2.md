## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

(a) Given that $6\sin x = 5$, find the two possible values for angle $x$, where $0^{\circ}\leq x\leq 180^{\circ}$. [2]

(b) Convert $138^{\circ}$ into radians. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
6\sin x=5
$$

$$
\sin x=\frac{5}{6}
$$

$$
x=56.4^{\circ}\text{ or }123.6^{\circ}\text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$

(b)

$$
138^{\circ}=\frac{\pi}{180^{\circ}}\times 138^{\circ}\approx 2.41\text{ rad (3 s.f.)}
$$
