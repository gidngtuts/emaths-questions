## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 13

Each term in the following sequence is found by multiplying the same constant to the previous term.

$$
p,\quad 12,\quad q,\quad 48,\quad r,\quad \ldots
$$

**(a)** Write down two possible values of $q$. [2]

**(b)** Write down the value of $\dfrac{p}{r}$. [1]

**(c)** Write down the $n$th term of the sequence. [1]

**(d)** Explain why $400$ is not a term in this sequence. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Let the multiplying constant be $x$.

$$
12x^{2}=48
$$

[M1]

$$
x=\pm 2
$$

Hence $q=12\times 2=24$ or $q=12\times(-2)=-24$ [A1]

**(b)**

$$
\frac{p}{r}=\frac{6}{96}=\frac{1}{16}
$$

[B1]

**(c)** $T_{n}=6\times 2^{n-1}$ or $3\times 2^{n}$ [B1]

**(d)** Either the positive sequence continues $\ldots, 48, 96, 192, 384, 768, \ldots$, or $\dfrac{400}{6}=66.67$ is not a multiple of $2$. Hence $400$ is not a term in this sequence. [B1]
