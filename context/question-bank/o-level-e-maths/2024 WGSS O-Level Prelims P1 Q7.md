## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 6

Solve $\dfrac{2}{x+1}-3 = \dfrac{1}{2x-5}$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{2}{x+1}-3 = \dfrac{1}{2x-5}$

$\dfrac{2-3(x+1)}{x+1} = \dfrac{1}{2x-5}$

$\dfrac{-3x-1}{x+1} = \dfrac{1}{2x-5}$ [M1]

$(-3x-1)(2x-5) = x+1$

$-6x^{2}+15x-2x+5 = x+1$

$-6x^{2}+12x+4 = 0$

$3x^{2}-6x-2 = 0$ [M1]

$x = \dfrac{6\pm\sqrt{(-6)^{2}-4(3)(-2)}}{2(3)} = \dfrac{6\pm\sqrt{60}}{6}$ [M1]

$x = 2.29$ or $-0.291$ (3 s.f.) [A1]
