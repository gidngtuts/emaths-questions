## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 3, (d) 2; total 9
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Express $\dfrac{5}{x-2}-\dfrac{3}{2x+1}$ as a single fraction in its simplest form. [2]

**(b)** Make $r$ the subject of the formula $V=\dfrac{h(R^{2}+r^{2})}{r^{2}}$. [2]

**(c)** Simplify $\dfrac{2x^{2}-xy-6x+3y}{9-x^{2}}$. [3]

**(d)** Simplify $\left(2x^{3}y^{-2}\right)^{4}\div\left(8x^{5}y^{-3}\right)$, giving your answer with positive indices. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{5}{x-2}-\frac{3}{2x+1}=\frac{5(2x+1)-3(x-2)}{(x-2)(2x+1)}=\frac{10x+5-3x+6}{(x-2)(2x+1)}=\frac{7x+11}{(x-2)(2x+1)}
$$

**(b)**

$$
Vr^{2}=h(R^{2}+r^{2})
$$

$$
Vr^{2}-hr^{2}=hR^{2}
$$

$$
r^{2}(V-h)=hR^{2}
$$

$$
r^{2}=\frac{hR^{2}}{V-h}
$$

$$
r=R\sqrt{\frac{h}{V-h}}
$$

**(c)**

$$
\frac{2x^{2}-xy-6x+3y}{9-x^{2}}=\frac{x(2x-y)-3(2x-y)}{(3-x)(3+x)}=\frac{(2x-y)(x-3)}{-(x-3)(x+3)}=\frac{y-2x}{x+3}
$$

**(d)**

$$
\left(2x^{3}y^{-2}\right)^{4}\div\left(8x^{5}y^{-3}\right)=\frac{16x^{12}y^{-8}}{8x^{5}y^{-3}}=2x^{7}y^{-5}=\frac{2x^{7}}{y^{5}}
$$
