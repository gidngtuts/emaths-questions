## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

$y$ is directly proportional to $x^{2}$.
If $x$ is increased by $200\%$, find the percentage increase in $y$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{9kx^{2}-kx^{2}}{kx^{2}} \times 100\% = 800\%
$$
