## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 3

A salesperson earns a fixed weekly salary of &#36;1000 plus $8\%$ commission on the total value of items he sells.

**(a)** In one week, he sold items worth &#36;1750.
Calculate the total amount the salesperson earned that week. [1]

**(b)** The following week, the salesperson earned a total of &#36;1450.
Calculate the total value of items he sold that week. [2]

## Answer Scheme

A salesperson earns a fixed weekly salary of &#36;1000 plus $8\%$ commission on the total value of items he sells.

**(a)** In one week, he sold items worth &#36;1750. Calculate the total amount the salesperson earned that week.

$$
\text{Total amount}=&#36;1000+\frac{8}{100}\times 1750=&#36;1140
$$

B1.

**(b)** The following week, the salesperson earned a total of &#36;1450. Calculate the total value of items he sold that week.

$$
\text{Commission}=&#36;1450-&#36;1000=&#36;450
$$

$8\%$ represents &#36;450. M1 for recognising that $8\%$ represents &#36;450.

$$
100\%\text{ represents }&#36;\frac{450}{8}\times 100=&#36;5625
$$

A1.
