## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Rearrange the formula $y=\dfrac{2x+3}{5x-1}$ to make $x$ the subject.

## Answer Scheme

$$
y=\frac{2x+3}{5x-1}
$$

$$
y(5x-1)=2x+3
$$

$$
5xy-y=2x+3
$$

$$
5xy-2x=y+3
$$

$$
x(5y-2)=y+3
$$

$$
x=\frac{y+3}{5y-2}
$$
