## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

(a) Simplify $24a^{2}b^{5}\div 8a^{6}b^{-2}$. Leave your answer in positive index form. [1]

(b) Given that $9^{k}\times\dfrac{1}{729} = 3^{k}$, find the value of $k$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $24a^{2}b^{5}\div 8a^{6}b^{-2} = 3a^{-4}b^{7} = \dfrac{3b^{7}}{a^{4}}$ [B1]

Penalise if not in positive index form. A note says this is usually not the case and will be own decision.

(b) $(3^{2})^{k}\times\dfrac{1}{3^{6}} = 3^{k}$ [M1, both forms accepted]

$3^{2k}\times 3^{-6} = 3^{k}$

$3^{2k-6} = 3^{k}$

$2k-6 = k$

$k = 6$ [A1]
