## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 12

$P$ is inversely proportional to the square root of $Q$.
The sum of the values of $P$ when $Q = 9$ and when $Q = 16$ is 21.
Find the value of $Q$ when $P = 100$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
P = \frac{k}{\sqrt{Q}}
$$

$$
\frac{k}{\sqrt{9}}+\frac{k}{\sqrt{16}} = 21 \quad \text{M1}
$$

$$
\frac{k}{3}+\frac{k}{4} = 21
$$

$$
\frac{7k}{12} = 21
$$

$$
7k = 252
$$

$$
k = 36
$$

$$
\frac{36}{\sqrt{Q}} = 100 \quad \text{M1}
$$

$$
Q = \left(\frac{36}{100}\right)^{2} = 0.1296
$$

Accept $\dfrac{81}{625}$. A1
