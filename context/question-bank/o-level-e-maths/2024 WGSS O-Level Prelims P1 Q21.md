## Question

> **Marks:** 6
> **Page:** 19

The first four terms in a sequence of numbers, $x_{1}$, $x_{2}$, $x_{3}$, $x_{4}$, $\ldots$ are given below.

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{1} &= 2(0)+1 = 1 \\
x_{2} &= 2(1)+3 = 5 \\
x_{3} &= 2(2)+5 = 9 \\
x_{4} &= 2(3)+7 = 13
\end{aligned}
$$

(a) Write down an expression for $x_{5}$. [1]

(b) Find an expression, in term of $n$, for the $n$th term, $x_{n}$, of the sequence. [2]

(c) Evaluate $x_{20}$ [1]

(d) Explain why $203$ is not a term of this sequence. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $17$ [B1]

(b) $x_{n} = 2(n-1)+1+2(n-1)$ [M1]

$= 2n-2+1+2n-2 = 4n-3$ [A1]

(c) $x_{20} = 4(20)-3 = 77$ [B1]

(d) $x_{n} = 4n-3$, so $203 = 4n-3$, $4n = 206$, $n = 51.5$. Since $n$ is not a positive integer [1], therefore $203$ is not a term of this sequence [1].
