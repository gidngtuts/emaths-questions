## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 8

The figure below shows a graph with $y$-intercept $-4$.
It has a minimum point $(-2, -8)$.
Find the equation of the graph in the form $y = x^{2}+ax+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers. [2]

![Question 11 quadratic graph](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-08.png)

## Answer Scheme

$$
y = (x+2)^{2}-8 = x^{2}+4x-4
$$
