## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 7

Simplify $\dfrac{x^{4}-81}{2x-6}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{x^{4}-81}{2x-6} = \frac{(x^{2})^{2}-9^{2}}{2(x-3)}
$$

$$
= \frac{(x^{2}-9)(x^{2}+9)}{2(x-3)} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{(x-3)(x+3)(x^{2}+9)}{2(x-3)} \quad \text{[M1]}
$$

$$
= \frac{(x+3)(x^{2}+9)}{2} \quad \text{[A1]}
$$
