## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

Solve

(a) $3-4x = 21$. [1]

(b) $2x < 3x+1 \le 13$, [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $3-4x = 21$

$-4x = 18$

$x = -\dfrac{9}{2}$ or $-4\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $-4.5$ [B1]

(b) $2x < 3x+1$ and $3x+1 \le 13$

$-x < 1$ so $x > -1$, and $3x \le 12$ so $x \le 4$ [M1]

$-1 < x \le 4$ [A1]
