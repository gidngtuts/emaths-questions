## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3, (c) 5; total 9
> **Page:** 24–26

Mdm Siti is planning a trip from Singapore to London. She will spend 6 days in London.
Here are the information on her trip.

- The total flight duration is 13 hours 50 minutes, which excludes a 2-hour layover at Doha.

| Singapore to Doha | 7 h 40 min |
| --- | --- |
| Doha to London | 6 h 10 min |

- Her flight departs at 11:15 PM (Singapore time).
- London time is 7 hours behind Singapore time.
- The exchange rate is 1 Pound Sterling (£) $=1.65$ Singapore Dollars (SGD).

**(a)** What is her arrival time in London, in local London time? [1]

Mdm Siti plans to bring a total amount of SGD&#36;700 for meals, transport, sightseeing and emergencies.
She plans to spend the following amount on average for the entire trip in London:
£25 per day on food
£10 per day on transport
£15 per day on sightseeing
$10\%$ of the total amount for emergencies

**(b)** By showing your working clearly, determine whether SGD&#36;700 is sufficient for her meals, transport and sightseeing. [3]

**(c)** Mdm Siti plans to spend four days sightseeing in London and only visit 2 attractions per day. She is considering three sightseeing options:

| Option | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Fixed daily pass | 4-day London Sightseeing Pass, covering unlimited entries for £90, with $5\%$ discount if she stays more than 5 days in London. |
| B | Pay-as-you-go | Each attraction costs £12.50. |
| C | Partial Pass + Pay-as-you-go | She buys a 2-day pass for £35, covering unlimited entries for 2 days, and pays £6 per attraction for the other 2 days. |

Assuming the total amount set aside for sightseeing remains the same for the four days, show your calculations and explain which option would be more cost-effective for Mdm Siti, considering her sightseeing plans and budget. [5]

---

**End of Paper**

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Total time taken $=13\text{ h }50\text{ min}+2\text{ h}=15\text{ h }50\text{ min}$

Time $=8.05$ am

**(b)**

$$
\text{Total per day}=£25+£10+£15=£50
$$

$$
\text{Total for 6 days}=6\times 50=£300
$$

Assuming that she needs to save $10\%$ of the total amount of &#36;700.

$$
\text{Total planned spending}=300(1.65)+\frac{10}{100}\times 700=&#36;565
$$

Sufficient.

Alternative solution: assuming that she needs to save $10\%$ of the total amount planned for 6 days.

$$
\text{Total planned spending}=300(1.65)+\frac{10}{100}\times(300\times 1.65)=&#36;544.50
$$

**(c)**

$$
\text{Budget}=15\times 4=£60
$$

Option A

Full price $=£60$

$$
\text{Discount}=\frac{5}{100}\times 90=£4.50
$$

$$
\text{Final cost}=60-4.50=£55.50
$$

Exceed budget.

Option B

$$
8\text{ attractions}\times 12.50=£100
$$

Exceed budget.

Option C

2 day pass $=£35$

Remaining 2 days $=4$ attractions $\times 6=£24$

$$
\text{Total cost}=£35+£24=£59
$$

Cover all attractions with sufficient budget.

Money left $=£60-£59=£1$

I would recommend C.

It meets all her sightseeing needs at the lowest cost and meeting her budget of £60.
