## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 3; total 5
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** Factorise $a^{3}-2a^{2}b-4a+8b$.

**(b)** Solve $\dfrac{3x+2}{9x}=\dfrac{1}{7x-3}$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $a^{2}(a-2b)-4(a-2b)=(a^{2}-4)(a-2b)=(a-2)(a+2)(a-2b)$

**(b)** $(3x+2)(7x-3)=9x$

$21x^{2}-4x-6=0$

$x=0.638$ or $x=-0.448$
