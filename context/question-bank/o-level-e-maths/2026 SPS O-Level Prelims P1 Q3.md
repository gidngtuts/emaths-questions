## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 1; total 2
> **Page:** 3

**(a)** Express $0.00047$ in standard form. [1]

**(b)** Express $1\text{ km}$ as a percentage of $0.4\text{ nm}$, where $1\text{ nm}=1\times 10^{-9}\text{ m}$.
Give your answer in standard form. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $0.00047=4.7\times 10^{-4}$

**(b)**

$$
1\text{ km}=1\times 10^{3}\text{ m}
$$

$$
0.4\text{ nm}=4\times 10^{-10}\text{ m}
$$

$$
\text{Percentage}=\frac{1\times 10^{3}}{4\times 10^{-10}}\times 100\%=2.5\times 10^{14}\%
$$
