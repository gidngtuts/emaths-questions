## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 3, (d) 2; total 11
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Solve the equation $x^{2}+5x-32=0$ by completing the square.
Give your solutions correct to 2 decimal places. [3]

**(b)** Write as a single fraction in its simplest form

$$
\frac{2x}{x^{2}-4}+\frac{5}{2-x}.
$$

[3]

**(c)** Solve the inequalities $x+5 \leq 3+2x < 21-x$. [3]

**(d)** Simplify

$$
\left(\frac{256x^{12}}{y^{8}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}.
$$

[2]
