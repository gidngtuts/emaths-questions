## Question

> **Marks:** 10


> **Page:** 6–7

![Points A, B, C and D on horizontal ground, with a north line at A](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GMSS/images/GMSS_2024_4E5N_EM_P2_PRELIM-pages/page-06.png)

$A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are four points on horizontal ground.
$AB = 70$ m, $BC = 55$ m, $CA = 60$ m, $AD = 50$ m, angle $ACD = 50^\circ$ and angle $ADC$ is acute.

(a) Calculate angle $BAC$. [3]

(b) Calculate angle $DAC$. [3]

(c) Calculate the bearing of $A$ from $D$. [2]

(d) A man walks northwards from point $A$. A vertical pole is placed at point $D$. The greatest angle of elevation of the pole when viewed along this path is $7^\circ$. Calculate the height of the pole. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$55^2 = 60^2 + 70^2 - 2(60)(70)\cos \angle BAC$$

$$\angle BAC = 49.3^\circ$$

(b)

$$\frac{\sin \angle ADC}{60} = \frac{\sin 50^\circ}{50}$$

$$\angle ADC = 66.817^\circ$$

$$\angle DAC = 180^\circ - 66.817^\circ - 50^\circ = 63.2^\circ$$

(c) North $N_1$ is drawn at $D$.

$$\angle ADN_1 = 63.2^\circ + 49.3^\circ = 112.5^\circ$$

(alternate angles, $AN \parallel DN_1$).

Bearing of $A$ from $D = 360^\circ - 112.5^\circ = 247.5^\circ$ (sum of angles at a point).

(d) Let $X$ be the point on the northward path where the angle of elevation is greatest.

$$\angle XAD = 180^\circ - 63.2^\circ - 49.30^\circ = 67.5^\circ$$

(angles on a straight line).

$$XD = 50 \sin 67.5^\circ = 46.193 \text{ m}$$

$$h = 46.193 \times \tan 7^\circ = 5.67 \text{ m}$$
