## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 3, (c) 5; total 11
> **Page:** 4–5

**(a)** Solve the inequality $\dfrac{3x+1}{3} < \dfrac{2-5x}{4}$. [2]

**(b)** It is given that $a = \dfrac{2b-3c}{b+2c}$.

**(i)** Find $a$ when $b = 0.2$ and $c = -1.5$. [1]

**(ii)** Express $b$ in terms of $a$ and $c$. [3]

**(c)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{5x}{x-3}-\dfrac{2}{2x-3} = 1$.

Give your solutions correct to 3 decimal places. [5]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{3x+1}{3} < \frac{2-5x}{4}
$$

$$
4(3x+1) < 3(2-5x)
$$

$$
12x+4 < 6-15x, \quad 27x < 2, \quad x < \frac{2}{27}
$$

M1, A1.

**(b)(i)**

$$
a = \frac{2(0.2)-3(-1.5)}{0.2+2(-1.5)} = -\frac{7}{4} \text{ or } -1.75
$$

B1.

**(b)(ii)**

$$
a(b+2c) = 2b-3c
$$

$$
ab+2ac = 2b-3c, \quad b(a-2) = -3c-2ac
$$

$$
b = \frac{-3c-2ac}{a-2} = \frac{3c+2ac}{2-a}
$$

M1, M1, A1.

**(c)**

$$
\frac{5x(2x-3)-2(x-3)}{(x-3)(2x-3)} = 1
$$

$$
10x^{2}-17x+6 = 2x^{2}-9x+9, \quad 8x^{2}-8x-3 = 0
$$

$$
x = \frac{-(-8)\pm\sqrt{(-8)^{2}-4(8)(-3)}}{2(8)}
$$

$$
x = 1.291 \text{ or } -0.291 \text{ (3 d.p.)}
$$

M1, M1, M1, A1, A1.
