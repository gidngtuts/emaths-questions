## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 15

$F$ is directly proportional to $v^{2}$.
Find the percentage change in $F$ when $v$ is reduced by 20%. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
F=kv^{2}
$$

$$
F_{\text{new}}=k(v_{\text{new}})^{2}=k(0.8v)^{2}
$$

$$
\text{Percentage change in }F=\frac{k(0.8v)^{2}-kv^{2}}{kv^{2}}\times 100\%=-36\%
$$
