## Question

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Page:** 10

Solve the simultaneous equations.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{4}-\frac{y}{2} &= 2 \\
3x &= 19+4y
\end{aligned}
$$

You must show your working.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{4}-\frac{y}{2} &= 2 &&\text{(1)} \\
3x &= 19+4y &&\text{(2)} \\
x-2y &= 8 \\
x &= 8+2y \\
3(8+2y) &= 19+4y \\
y &= -2.5 \\
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$
