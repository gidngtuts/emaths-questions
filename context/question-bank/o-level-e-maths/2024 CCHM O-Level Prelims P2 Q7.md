## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (a)(iii) 4, (b)(i) 2, (b)(ii) 2, (b)(iii) 2; total 12
> **Page:** 16–17

**(a)** $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are four points such that the coordinates of $A$ and $C$ are $(4, 2)$ and $(10, -34)$ respectively. $\overrightarrow{AB} = \begin{pmatrix} -9 \\ -12 \end{pmatrix}$ and $\overrightarrow{AD} = \begin{pmatrix} 24 \\ -12 \end{pmatrix}$.

**(i)** Find the coordinates of $B$. [1]

**(ii)** Find $AB$. [1]

It is given that $E$ is a point on $BD$ such that $BE = \dfrac{1}{3}BD$.

**(iii)** Show that $A$, $C$ and $E$ are collinear. [4]

**(b)** $A$ is the point $(2, 6)$, $B$ is the point $(-4, -2)$ and $C$ is the point $(6, -2)$. A line $L$ passes through point $C$ and is parallel to $AB$.

**(i)** Find the equation of line $L$. [2]

Point $D$ lies on line $L$ such that $AD \parallel BC$.

**(ii)** Find the coordinates of point $D$. [2]

**(iii)** Show that $ABCD$ is a rhombus. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $B(-5, -10)$.

**(a)(ii)** $AB = \sqrt{9^{2}+12^{2}} = 15$ units.

**(a)(iii)**

$$
\overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{1}{3}\begin{pmatrix} 33 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 11 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
\overrightarrow{AE} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ -12 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \overrightarrow{AC} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \\ -36 \end{pmatrix} = 3\overrightarrow{AE}
$$

Since $\overrightarrow{AE} = \dfrac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}$ and they share point $A$, $A$, $C$ and $E$ are collinear.

**(b)(i)** Gradient of $AB = \dfrac{4}{3}$.

$$
y+2 = \frac{4}{3}(x-6), \quad y = \frac{4}{3}x-10
$$

**(b)(ii)** $D(12, 6)$.

**(b)(iii)** $AB = CD = BC = 10$ units, and $AD = BC$, so $ABCD$ is a rhombus.
