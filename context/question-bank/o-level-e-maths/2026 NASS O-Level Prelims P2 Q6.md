## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 4, (b) 3, (c) 2, (d) 1; total 10
> **Page:** 14–15

An empty water tank has a capacity of $4.8\text{ m}^{3}$.
An inlet pipe pumps water into the tank at a constant rate of $x\text{ cm}^{3}/\text{s}$.
On a particular day, the tank took 1 hour 40 minutes longer than usual to fill because of a crack at the bottom of the tank.
Water leaked through the crack at a constant rate of $1500\text{ cm}^{3}/\text{s}$.

**(a)** Show that $x^{2}-1500x-1200000=0$. [4]

**(b)** Solve the equation $x^{2}-1500x-1200000=0$.
Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [3]

**(c)** Hence calculate the time taken to fill the tank on that day.
Give your answer in hours and minutes, correct to the nearest minute. [2]

**(d)** The water tank is in the shape of an inverted cone with a height of 200 cm.

On the axes, sketch a graph to show the relationship between the height of the water, $h$ cm, and the time, $t$ hours, as the tank was filled that day. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $4.8\text{ m}^{3}=4800000\text{ cm}^{3}$.

Time taken to fill tank without leak $=\dfrac{4800000}{x}$ s.

Time taken to fill tank with leak $=\dfrac{4800000}{x-1500}$ s.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{4800000}{x-1500}-\frac{4800000}{x} &= 6000 \\
800x-800(x-1500) &= x(x-1500) \\
1200000 &= x^{2}-1500x \\
x^{2}-1500x-1200000 &= 0\text{ (shown)}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Markers Report:** Not well done. Students struggle to use the information in forming the required equation. Some could not even convert from m$^{3}$ to cm$^{3}$.

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^{2}-1500x-1200000 &= 0 \\
x &= \frac{-(-1500)\pm\sqrt{(-1500)^{2}-4(1)(-1200000)}}{2} \\
x &= 2077.59\text{ or }-577.59\text{ (2 d.p.)}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Markers Report:** Generally okay. However, some students did not show the actual formula with the actual values of $a$, $b$ and $c$ substituted (as seen above). They show evaluated values of discriminant instead. Some failed to give answers to 2 d.p.

**(c)** Time taken to fill tank that day

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= \frac{4800000}{2077.59-1500} \\
&= 8310.39\text{ s} \\
&= 2\text{ h }19\text{ min (nearest minute)}
\end{aligned}
$$

> **Markers Report:** Students could not do this part as they were not able to do part (a).
