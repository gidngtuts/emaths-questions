## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 16

Gino can paint 6 fence panels in 4 hours, while Danish can paint 7 fence panels in 5 hours. Gino and Danish work together to paint a total of 21 panels. If they continue to paint at the same rate, how long will it take them to paint 21 panels? Give your answer in hours and minutes, correct to the nearest minute. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Gino: $4$ hours for $6$ panels, so $1.5$ panels per hour. Danish: $5$ hours for $7$ panels, so $1.4$ panels per hour. Together, $2.9$ panels per hour. M1.

$$
21\text{ panels take }\frac{21}{2.9} = 7.241379\text{ hours} = 7\text{ h }14\text{ min}
$$

M1 with error carried forward. A1. Also accept $7\text{ h }15\text{ min}$, since the time is $7$ hours $14$ minutes $29$ seconds and some students round up because the work finishes after that second.
