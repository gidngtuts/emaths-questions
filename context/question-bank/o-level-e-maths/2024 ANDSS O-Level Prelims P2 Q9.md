## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 12

The diagram shows a part of a regular $n$-sided polygon.

![Part of a regular polygon. One exterior side is extended. A line from a vertex meets that extension, forming angles a and b with the polygon.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_-Prelim-P2_QP-pages/page-12.png)

Given $\angle a+\angle b=72^{\circ}$, find the value of $n$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

Let an interior angle of the polygon be $x$.

$$
3x+\angle a+\angle b=(5-2)\times 180^{\circ}
$$

$$
3x+72^{\circ}=540^{\circ}
$$

$$
x=156^{\circ}
$$

$$
n=\frac{360^{\circ}}{180^{\circ}-156^{\circ}}=15
$$
