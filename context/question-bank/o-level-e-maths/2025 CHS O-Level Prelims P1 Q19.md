## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3; total 6
> **Page:** 18–19

The diagram shows two sectors $OAB$ and $OCD$ with centre at $O$. $OB$ is $15\text{ cm}$ and $CD$ has an arc length of $5\text{ cm}$. The ratio of area of sector $OCD$ : area of $ABDC$ is $16:65$.

![Question 19 sectors](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CHS/images/CAT-HIGH-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(a)** Find angle $COD$ in radian. [3]

**(b)** Find the area of the shaded region. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\angle COD = \dfrac{3}{4}\text{ rad}$.

One method: area ratio of sector $OCD$ to sector $OAB$ is $16:81$, so $OD:OB = 4:9$ and $OD = 6\dfrac{2}{3}$. Then

$$
\angle COD = \frac{5}{6\frac{2}{3}} = \frac{3}{4}\text{ rad}.
$$

**(b)**

$$
A = \frac{1}{2}(15)^{2}\left(\frac{6}{3}\right)\sin\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{2}\left(6\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{3}{4}\right) = 17.4\text{ cm}^{2}
$$
