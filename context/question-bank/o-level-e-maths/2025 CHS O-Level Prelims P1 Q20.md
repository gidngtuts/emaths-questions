## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 19

In this table, $p$ is inversely proportional to $q^{2}$.

| $p$ | $0.08$ | $0.5$ | $b$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $q$ | $a$ | $4$ | $2$ |

Calculate the value of $a$ and of $b$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
0.5 = \frac{k}{4^{2}} \Rightarrow k = 8
$$

$$
a = 10,\quad b = 2
$$
