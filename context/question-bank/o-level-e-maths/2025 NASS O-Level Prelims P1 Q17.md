## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 14

**(a)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{625x^{4}}{y^{8}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{4}}$. [2]

**(b)** $243^{a}=3^{8}+3^{8}+3^{8}$

Find the value of $a$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\left(\frac{625x^{4}}{y^{8}}\right)^{-\frac{1}{4}}
$$

$$
=5^{-1}x^{-1}y^{2}
$$

$$
=\frac{y^{2}}{5x}
$$

**(b)**

$$
243^{a}=3^{8}+3^{8}+3^{8}
$$

$$
3^{5a}=3^{8}(1+1+1)
$$

$$
3^{5a}=3^{8}(3)
$$

$$
3^{5a}=3^{9}
$$

By comparing indices,

$$
5a=9
$$

$$
a=\frac{9}{5}\text{ or }1\frac{4}{5}\text{ or }1.8
$$
