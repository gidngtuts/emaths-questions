## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

A running route is 42.2 km long and a runner took 4 hours 45 minutes to finish the route.
Calculate the speed of the runner in metres per second.

## Answer Scheme

$42.2\text{ km}=42200\text{ m}$ and $4\text{ h }45\text{ min}=17100\text{ s}$

Speed $=\dfrac{42200}{17100}=2.47\text{ m/s}$

Alternatively, $42.2\div 4.75=8.8842\text{ km/h}$, then $8.8842\times\dfrac{1000}{3600}=2.47\text{ m/s}$.
