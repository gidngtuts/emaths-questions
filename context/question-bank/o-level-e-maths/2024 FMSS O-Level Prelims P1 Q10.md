## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 8

The diagram shows a parallelogram $ABCD$. The perpendicular height is $h\text{ cm}$.

The area of parallelogram is increased by $150\%$ when $AB$ is reduced by $20\%$ and $h$ is increased by $x\%$. Find the value of $x$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

New area $= 2.5 \times$ old area. Old area $= AB \times h$.

$$
\text{new area} = \frac{8}{10}AB \times \frac{100+x}{100}h
$$

$$
\frac{5}{2} \times \text{old area} = \frac{4}{5}AB \times \frac{100+x}{100}h
$$

$$
\frac{5}{2} \times \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{100+x}{100} = 1
$$

$$
\frac{5 \times 4(100+x)}{2 \times 500} = 1, \quad 2500 = 8(100+x), \quad x = 212.5.
$$

M1 for the equation for the new area. A1. Answer $x = 212.5\%$.
