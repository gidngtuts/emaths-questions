## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 9

(a) $y$ is directly proportional to $\sqrt[3]{x}$. Find the percentage increase of $y$ when $x$ increases by $700\%$. [2]

(b) 6 men took 50 hours to paint a mural wall. How long does it take for 4 men to paint the same wall? [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) Original $y = k\sqrt[3]{x}$. New $y = k\sqrt[3]{8x} = 2k\sqrt[3]{x}$.

$$
\text{Percentage change} = \frac{2k\sqrt[3]{x}-k\sqrt[3]{x}}{k\sqrt[3]{x}}\times 100 = 100\%
$$

M1, A1.

(b) 6 men take 50 hours to paint a mural. 4 men take $\dfrac{6}{4}\times 50 = 75$ hours to paint the same mural. M1, A1.
