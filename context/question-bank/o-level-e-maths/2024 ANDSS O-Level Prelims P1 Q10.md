## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 10

$ABCD$ is a rhombus and $RD=CS$.
$SCB$, $DCR$ and $AQR$ are straight lines.

![Rhombus ABCD with point S to the left of C, point R below C, and point Q on AB. Lines SCB, DCR and AQR are straight.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_Prelim_P1_QP-pages/page-10.png)

Prove that triangles $SCD$ and $RDA$ are congruent. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$SC=RD$ (given). No marks for this step.

$\angle SCD=\angle RDA$ (alternate angles). M1

$CD=DA$ (sides of a rhombus are equal). M1

By the SAS congruency test, triangle $SCD$ is congruent to triangle $RDA$. A1
