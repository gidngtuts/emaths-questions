## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

**(a)** Simplify $(2x^{3}y)^{3}$. [1]

**(b)** Use the laws of indices to solve the following equation for $x$. Show your working clearly.

$$
2^{2}\times 5^{3}+\frac{1}{125^{x}}=5^{4}
$$

[2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $(2x^{3}y)^{3}=8x^{9}y^{3}$ [B1]

**(b)**

$$
2^{2}\times 5^{3}+\frac{1}{125^{x}}=5^{4}
$$

$$
5^{-3x}=5^{4}-4\times 5^{3}
$$

$$
5^{-3x}=5^{3}
$$

[M1]

$$
-3x=3
$$

$$
x=-1
$$

[A1]
