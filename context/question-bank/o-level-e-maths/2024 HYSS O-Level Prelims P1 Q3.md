## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

(a) Simplify $y^{0} \div 9x^{-2} \times x^{7}$. [2]

(b) Simplify $(81a^{12})^{\frac{5}{4}}$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $y^{0}\div 9x^{-2}\times x^{7} = 1\times \dfrac{x^{2}}{9}\times x^{7} = \dfrac{x^{9}}{9}$. M1, A1.

(b) $(81a^{12})^{\frac{5}{4}} = 243a^{15}$. B1.
