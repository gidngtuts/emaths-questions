## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Using factorisation, solve $6p^{2}-9p-6 = 0$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
(2p+1)(p-2) = 0
$$

$$
p = -\frac{1}{2} \quad \text{or} \quad p = 2
$$

Accept $(2p+1)(3p-6) = 0$.
