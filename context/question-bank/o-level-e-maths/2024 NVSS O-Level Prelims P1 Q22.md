## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 15

The diagram below shows a solid circular cone and a solid sphere.
The cone has radius $12x\text{ cm}$ and height $17x\text{ cm}$.
The sphere has radius $rx\text{ cm}$.
The cone has the same total surface area as the sphere.

Calculate the value for $r$.

## Answer Scheme

Slant height $=\sqrt{(12x)^{2}+(17x)^{2}}=\sqrt{433}\,x$ M1

$\pi(12x)^{2}+\pi(12x)(\sqrt{433}\,x)=4\pi(rx)^{2}$ M1

$144\pi x^{2}+12\sqrt{433}\,\pi x^{2}=4\pi x^{2} r^{2}$

$4\pi x^{2} r^{2}=(144+12\sqrt{433})\pi x^{2}$ M1

$$
r^{2}=\frac{144+12\sqrt{433}}{4}=98.42595
$$

$r=9.92$ (3 s.f.). Negative value rejected. A1
