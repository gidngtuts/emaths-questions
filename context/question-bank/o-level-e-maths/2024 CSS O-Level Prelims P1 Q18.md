## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 13

A length of 5 cm on a map represents an actual distance of 2.5 km.

Find

**(a)** the actual distance represented by 20 cm on the map, giving your answer in km, [1]

**(b)** the scale of the map in the form $1 : n$, [2]

**(c)** the land area in $\text{km}^{2}$, which is represented by $30\text{ cm}^{2}$ on the map. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $10\text{ km}$.

**(b)** $5\text{ cm} : 2.5\text{ km}$, so $1\text{ cm} : 0.5\text{ km}$, so $1\text{ cm} : 50000\text{ cm}$, so $1 : 50000$.

**(c)** $1\text{ cm}^{2} : 0.25\text{ km}^{2}$, so $30\text{ cm}^{2} : 7.5\text{ km}^{2}$.
