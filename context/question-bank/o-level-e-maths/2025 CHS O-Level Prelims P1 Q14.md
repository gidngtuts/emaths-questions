## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 13

Find $\angle a + \angle b + \angle c + \angle d$.

![Question 14 angles](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CHS/images/CAT-HIGH-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-13.png)

[2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\angle a+\angle c = 60^{\circ}\text{ (exterior angle)}
$$

$$
\angle b+\angle d = 180-60-48 = 72^{\circ}\text{ (exterior angle)}
$$

$$
\angle a+\angle b+\angle c+\angle d = 60+72 = 132^{\circ}
$$
