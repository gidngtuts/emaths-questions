## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 1, (a)(iii) 1, (b) 1; total 4
> **Page:** 19

In the diagram, $ABCDE$ is a regular pentagon.

**(a)** Calculate

**(i)** $\angle BAE$, [1]

**(ii)** $\angle AEB$, [1]

**(iii)** $\angle BEC$. [1]

**(b)** Explain why $BE$ is parallel to $CD$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)**

$$
\angle BAE = \frac{(5-2)180}{5} = 108^{\circ}.
$$

B1.

**(ii)**

$$
\angle AEB = \frac{180-108}{2} = 36^{\circ}
$$

(base angles of an isosceles triangle). B1.

**(iii)** Triangles $AEB$ and $DEC$ are congruent.

$$
\angle BEC = 108-36-36 = 36^{\circ}.
$$

B1.

**(b)**

$$
\angle ECD = \frac{180-108}{2} = 36^{\circ}
$$

(base angle of an isosceles triangle). $\angle BEC = \angle ECD = 36^{\circ}$. By the converse of alternate angles, $BE$ is parallel to $CD$.

Or: they form a pair of alternate angles, so $BE$ is parallel to $CD$. B1.
