## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 4

**(a)** Given that $3\sin x=1$, find the two possible values for angle $x$, where $0^{\circ}\leq x\leq 180^{\circ}$. [2]

**(b)** Convert $26^{\circ}$ into radians. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\sin x=\frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
x=19.47122\ldots\text{ or }160.5287\ldots=19.5^{\circ}\text{ or }160.5^{\circ}\text{ (1 d.p.)}
$$

**(b)**

$$
26^{\circ}=0.45378\ldots=0.454\text{ (3 s.f.)}
$$
