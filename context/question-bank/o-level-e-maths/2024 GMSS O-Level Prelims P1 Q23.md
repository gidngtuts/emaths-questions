## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 16

The diagram below shows a square $S$, a regular hexagon $H$ and a part of a regular $n$-sided polygon $P$.

![Square, regular hexagon and part of polygon P](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GMSS/images/GMSS_2024_4E5N_EM_P1_PRELIM-pages/page-16.png)

The regular hexagon $H$ sits on the top side of the square $S$. Two sides of polygon $P$ leave the figure: one from the right-hand vertex where $H$ meets $S$, drawn up and to the right, and one from the bottom-right vertex of $S$, drawn down and to the right. Both continue as dashed lines.

Find the number of sides, $n$, of the regular polygon $P$.

## Answer Scheme

Interior angle of the regular hexagon $= \dfrac{(6-2)\times 180^{\circ}}{6} = 120^{\circ}$.

Interior angle of the square $= 90^{\circ}$.

Interior angle of $P$ at the common vertex $= 360^{\circ}-120^{\circ}-90^{\circ} = 150^{\circ}$ (angles at a point).

Exterior angle of $P = 180^{\circ}-150^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}$.

$n = \dfrac{360^{\circ}}{30^{\circ}} = 12$.
