## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

Jessica invests ＄4540 at a rate of $r\%$ per year compound interest.
At the end of 10 years, she has earned ＄1328.54 in interest.
Calculate the value of $r$.

## Answer Scheme

$4540+1328.54=4540\left(1+\dfrac{r}{100}\right)^{10}$ M1

$\left(1+\dfrac{r}{100}\right)^{10}=1.2926299$

$1+\dfrac{r}{100}=1.2926299^{0.1}$ M1

$r=2.6000 \approx 2.60$ (3 s.f.) A1
