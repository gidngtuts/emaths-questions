## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

Triangle $ABC$ is such that $AB = 16\text{ cm}$, $BC = 63\text{ cm}$, $AC = 65\text{ cm}$. Do points $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on the circumference of a circle? Explain with working. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
16^{2}+63^{2} = 4225 = 65^{2}
$$

By the converse of Pythagoras’ Theorem, triangle $ABC$ is a right-angled triangle. $A$, $B$ and $C$ are also points on a circle by the angle in a semicircle property. Yes, $A$, $B$ and $C$ lie on the circumference of a circle.

M1 for showing Pythagoras. A1 for Pythagoras. A1 for the circle property.
