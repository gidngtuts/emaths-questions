## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

Solve the inequality $\dfrac{2x-3}{4} \leq \dfrac{5-x}{5}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2x-3}{4} \leq \frac{5-x}{5}
$$

$$
5(2x-3) \leq 4(5-x)
$$

$$
10x-15 \leq 20-4x
$$

$$
14x \leq 35
$$

$$
x \leq \frac{35}{14}
$$

$$
x \leq 2.5
$$

M1 for multiplying both sides by 20. A1.
