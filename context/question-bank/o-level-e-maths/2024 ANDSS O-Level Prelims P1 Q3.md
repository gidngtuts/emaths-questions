## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 4

Simplify $\dfrac{2x^{2}+4xy-3x-6y}{2x^{2}+xy-6y^{2}}$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2x^{2}+4xy-3x-6y}{2x^{2}+xy-6y^{2}}
$$

$$
= \frac{2x(x+2y)-3(x+2y)}{2x^{2}+xy-6y^{2}}
$$

M1 for factorising the numerator by grouping.

$$
= \frac{(x+2y)(2x-3)}{2x^{2}+xy-6y^{2}}
$$

$$
= \frac{(x+2y)(2x-3)}{(2x-3y)(x+2y)}
$$

M1 for factorising the denominator.

$$
= \frac{2x-3}{2x-3y}
$$
