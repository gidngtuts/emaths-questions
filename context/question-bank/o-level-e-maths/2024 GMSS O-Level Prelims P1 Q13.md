## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 9

Factorise completely $8-2x-24y+6xy$.

## Answer Scheme

$8-2x-24y+6xy = 2(4-x-12y+3xy) = 2(4-x)(1-3y)$.

Equivalent form $2(x-4)(3y-1)$ is accepted.
