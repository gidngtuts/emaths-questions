## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 13

A map is drawn to a scale of $1 : 300\,000$.

**(a)** The length of a bridge measures $5.2\text{ km}$.
Calculate the length of the bridge on the map in centimetres. [1]

**(b)** The area of a forest measures $7.5\text{ cm}^{2}$ on the map.
Find the actual area of the forest in square kilometres. [2]
