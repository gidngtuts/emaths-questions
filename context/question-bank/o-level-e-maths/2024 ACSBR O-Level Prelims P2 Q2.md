## Question

> **Marks:** 8
> **Page:** 4–5

(a) It is given that $sq^{2} = r-3q^{2}$.

(i) Evaluate $s$ when $q = -3$ and $r = 5$.

(ii) Express $q$ in terms of $r$ and $s$.

(b) (i) Show that $(7n-1)^{2}-(n-1)^{2}$ is a multiple of 12 for all integer values of $n$.

(ii) Simplify

$$
\frac{p-3n+12n^{2}-4np}{(7n-1)^{2}-(n-1)^{2}}.
$$

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) $s = [5-3(-3)^{2}] \div (-3)^{2}$

$$
s = -2\frac{4}{9} \quad \text{or} \quad s = -\frac{22}{9}
$$

Do not accept 3 significant figures.

(ii)

$$
sq^{2} = r-3q^{2}
$$

$$
sq^{2}+3q^{2} = r
$$

$$
q^{2}(s+3) = r
$$

$$
q = \pm \sqrt{\frac{r}{s+3}}
$$

(b) (i)

$$
(7n-1)^{2}-(n-1)^{2} = [(7n-1)+(n-1)][(7n-1)-(n-1)] = 6n(8n-2) = 12n(4n-1)
$$

$12(4n^{2}-n)$ is also accepted.

(ii)

$$
\frac{p-3n+12n^{2}-4np}{(7n-1)^{2}-(n-1)^{2}} = \frac{p(1-4n)-3n(1-4n)}{12n(4n-1)} = \frac{(3n-p)(4n-1)}{12n(4n-1)} = \frac{3n-p}{12n}
$$
