## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 4, (d) 1; total 9
> **Page:** 8–9

A piece of wire of length 120 cm is cut into two parts.
One part of the wire is bent to form a square of side $x$ cm.
The remaining part of the wire is bent to form a circle with radius, $r$ cm.

**(a)** Show that the radius of the circle, $r$, is given by $\dfrac{60-2x}{\pi}$ cm.

**(b)** If the area of the square is equal to the area of the circle, form an equation in $x$ and show that it reduces to

$$
(4-\pi)x^{2}-240x+3600=0.
$$

**(c)** Solve the equation $(4-\pi)x^{2}-240x+3600=0$, giving your solutions correct to two decimal places.

**(d)** Explain why one of the solutions has to be rejected.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $2\pi r=120-4x$ M1

$$
r=\frac{120-4x}{2\pi}=\frac{60-2x}{\pi}
$$

A1 (shown)

**(b)** $x^{2}=\pi\left(\dfrac{60-2x}{\pi}\right)^{2}$ M1

$\pi x^{2}=3600-240x+4x^{2}$

$(4-\pi)x^{2}-240x+3600=0$ A1 (shown)

**(c)**

$$
x=\frac{240\pm\sqrt{(-240)^{2}-4(4-\pi)(3600)}}{2(4-\pi)}
$$

M1

$$
x=\frac{240\pm\sqrt{45238.93421}}{2(4-\pi)}
$$

M1

$x=263.68$ or $x=15.90$ A1, A1

**(d)** Reject $x=263.68$ because the perimeter of the square ($4x$) must be less than the length of the wire, or because the radius of the circle cannot be negative. B1
