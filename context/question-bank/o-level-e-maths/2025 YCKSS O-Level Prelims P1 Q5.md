## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 4

Factorise.

**(a)** $24pq^{2}+15pq-9p^{2}q$ [1]

**(b)** $5+2a^{2}bc-5ac-2ab$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Factorise $24pq^{2}+15pq-9p^{2}q$.

$$
=3pq(8q+5-3p)
$$

**(b)** Factorise $5+2a^{2}bc-5ac-2ab$.

$$
=5-5ac+2a^{2}bc-2ab
$$

$$
=5(1-ac)-2ab(1-ac)
$$

$$
=(1-ac)(5-2ab)
$$

Accept $(ac-1)(2ab-5)$.
