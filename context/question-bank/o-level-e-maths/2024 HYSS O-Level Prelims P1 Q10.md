## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 7

(a) Express $9-8x+x^{2}$ in the form $a+(x+b)^{2}$. Find the value of $a$ and of $b$. [2]

(b) Explain why when $x = 4$, the expression $9-8x+x^{2}$ has its minimum value. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $9-8x+x^{2} = (x-4)^{2}+9-16 = -7+(x-4)^{2}$. $a = -7$, $b = -4$. B1, B1.

(b) For any perfect square, the smallest value is zero or greater than zero. When $x = 4$, $(x-4)^{2} = 0$, so $(x-4)^{2}-7 = -7 < 0$, and the coefficient of $x^{2}$ is positive, so the quadratic has a minimum value at $x = 4$. B1.
