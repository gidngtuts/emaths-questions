## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3; total 4
> **Page:** 14

The masses, in kilograms, of bags of tomatoes in a shipment are shown in the stem-and-leaf diagram below.

| Stem | Leaf |
| --- | --- |
| 18 | 2 4 7 |
| 19 | 0 2 5 7 8 8 9 |
| 20 | 1 2 5 6 6 6 7 9 9 |
| 21 | 3 5 5 5 7 8 8 9 |

Key: $18\mid 2$ represents $18.2\text{ kg}$.

**(a)** Find the number of bags of tomatoes in the shipment. [1]

**(b)** The data above can be represented by a box-and-whisker diagram. The left whisker is at $18.2$, the left edge of the box is labelled $p$, a line inside the box is labelled $20.6$, the right edge of the box is labelled $q$, and the right whisker is labelled $r$. Given that the mass of the lightest bag is $18.2\text{ kg}$, find the value of $p$, $q$ and $r$. [3]
