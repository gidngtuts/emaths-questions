## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 7

Factorise completely

(a) $2p^{4}-32s^{4}$, [3]

(b) $12cd-9cx+6xy-8dy$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
2p^{4}-32s^{4} = 2\left[(p^{2})^{2}-(4s^{2})^{2}\right] = 2(p^{2}+4s^{2})(p^{2}-4s^{2}) = 2(p^{2}+4s^{2})(p+2s)(p-2s)
$$

M1 for the HCF. M1 for a difference of squares. A1 for the second difference of squares.

(b) $3c(4d-3x)+2y(3x-4d) = 3c(4d-3x)-2y(4d-3x) = (3c-2y)(4d-3x)$. M1 for factoring out the negative sign. A1.
