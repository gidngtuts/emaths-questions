## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 10

The expression $x^{2}-4x+7$ can be written in the form of $(x-a)^{2}+b$

(a) Find the value of $a$ and $b$. [2]

(b) Explain why the expression gives a minimum value. [2]

(c) Write down the minimum value. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $x^{2}-4x+7 = (x-2)^{2}-4+7 = (x-2)^{2}+3$

$a = 2$ [B1], $b = 3$ [B1]

(b) $(x-2)^{2} \ge 0$ [M1]. The smallest it can have is $0$. Therefore the expression gave a minimum values [A1].

(c) $3$ [B1]
