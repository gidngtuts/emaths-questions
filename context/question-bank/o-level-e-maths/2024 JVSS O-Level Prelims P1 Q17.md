## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 12

The kinetic energy, $E$ Joules, of an object is directly proportional to $(v+0.5)^{2}$, where $v$ is the velocity in m/s.

Given that the difference between the values of $E$ when $v = 10$ and when $v = 13$ is $1296$ Joules, express $E$ in terms of $v$ and hence, find the value of $E$ when $v = 20$. [3]
