## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Solve the simultaneous equations.

$$
x+3y = 29
$$

$$
2x = 5y-30
$$

[3]

## Answer Scheme

Elimination: $2x+6y = 58$ and $2x-5y = -30$. Subtract to get $11y = 88$, so $y = 8$ and $x = 5$. [M1 for elimination, A1]

Substitution: $x = 29-3y$, then $2(29-3y) = 5y-30$, so $y = 8$ and $x = 5$. [M1 for substitution]
