## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 6

Express $\dfrac{2}{d+3e}-\dfrac{d-15e}{d^{2}-9e^{2}}$ as a single fraction in its simplest form.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2}{d+3e}-\frac{d-15e}{d^{2}-9e^{2}} = \frac{2}{d+3e}-\frac{d-15e}{(d+3e)(d-3e)} \quad\text{M1 (factorise denominator)}
$$

$$
= \frac{2(d-3e)-(d-15e)}{(d+3e)(d-3e)} \quad\text{M1 (single fraction or better)}
$$

$$
= \frac{2d-6e-d+15e}{(d+3e)(d-3e)} = \frac{d+9e}{(d+3e)(d-3e)} \quad\text{A1}
$$

Accept $\dfrac{d+9e}{d^{2}-9e^{2}}$.
