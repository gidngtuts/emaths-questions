## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 1, (a)(ii) 2, (b) 3; total 6
> **Page:** 5

**(a)** The position vector of $A$ is $\begin{pmatrix}-1\\ 4\end{pmatrix}$.

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\ 5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

**(i)** Find the coordinates of $B$. [1]

**(ii)** Find the equation of line $AB$. [2]

**(b)** $C$ is the point $(-2,\ 5)$.
$D$ is a point that lies on the line $x=3$.
The length of $CD$ is $13$ units.
Find the two possible pairs of coordinates of $D$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)** $(-4,\ 9)$. B1.

**(a)(ii)**

$$
\text{Gradient}=\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=\frac{5}{-3}=-\frac{5}{3}
$$

M1 for the correct gradient.

$$
y=-\frac{5}{3}x+c
$$

When $x=-1$ and $y=4$,

$$
4=-\frac{5}{3}(-1)+c,\quad c=\frac{7}{3}
$$

$$
y=-\frac{5}{3}x+\frac{7}{3}
$$

A1.

**(b)** $D(3,\ y)$. M1 for identifying that the $x$-coordinate of $D$ is $3$.

$$
(3-(-2))^{2}+(y-5)^{2}=13^{2}
$$

M1 for the correct formula for the length of a line segment.

$$
5^{2}+(y-5)^{2}=13^{2}
$$

$$
(y-5)^{2}=144
$$

$$
y-5=\pm 12
$$

$$
y=-7\quad\text{or}\quad y=17
$$

$$
D(3,\ -7)\quad\text{and}\quad D(3,\ 17)
$$

A1 for both correct answers.
