## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 4

A number has exactly fifteen factors.
Two of the factors are 81 and 108.

Find this number. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
81 &= 3^{4} \\
108 &= 2^{2}\times 3^{3} \\
\text{LCM: } 2^{2}\times 3^{4} &= 324
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: either prime factorisation. A1 (to give 2 marks if no method).
