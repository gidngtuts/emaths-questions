## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 13

The sketch shows the graph of $y=ka^{x}$, where $a>0$.

![Question 19 exponential graph](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/DSS/images/Dunman-Sec-2025-Prelims-4EXP-Math-Paper-1-QP-pages/page-13.png)

Find the values of $k$ and $a$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
16=ka^{0}\Rightarrow k=16
$$

$$
64=16a^{-2}
$$

$$
a^{2}=\frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
a=\frac{1}{2}
$$

(reject negative)
