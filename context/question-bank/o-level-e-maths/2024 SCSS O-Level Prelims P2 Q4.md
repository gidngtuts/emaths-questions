## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3, (c) 3, (d) 1; total 8
> **Page:** 11

Here are the first four terms of a sequence.

$$
\frac{13}{5},\quad \frac{10}{10},\quad \frac{7}{15},\quad \frac{4}{20}
$$

**(a)** Find the fifth term of the sequence. [1]

**(b)** $T_{n}$ is the $n$th term of the sequence. Find an expression, in terms of $n$, for $T_{n}$. [3]

**(c)** The difference between the two consecutive terms of the sequence is $T_{n+1}-T_{n}$. Show that

$$
T_{n+1}-T_{n} = \frac{-16}{5n(n+1)}.
$$

[3]

**(d)** Explain why the difference between the two consecutive terms of the sequence is always negative. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $\dfrac{1}{25}$. B1.

**(b)** $T_{n} = \dfrac{16-3n}{5n}$. M1 for $-3n$, M1 for $5n$, A1.

**(c)**

$$
T_{n+1}-T_{n} = \frac{16-3(n+1)}{5(n+1)}-\frac{16-3n}{5n}
$$

$$
= \frac{n(13-3n)-(16-3n)(n+1)}{5n(n+1)} = \frac{-16}{5n(n+1)} \text{ (shown)}
$$

M1, M1, A1.

**(d)** For $n > 0$, $5n(n+1) > 0$, so $\dfrac{-16}{5n(n+1)} < 0$ and $T_{n+1}-T_{n} < 0$. B1.
