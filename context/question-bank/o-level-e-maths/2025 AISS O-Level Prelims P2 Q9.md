## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3, (c) 2, (d) 3; total 9
> **Page:** 18–19

**(a)** Complete the table of values for $y=3x^{2}-x^{3}-1$. [1]

| $x$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1.5$ | $2$ | $2.5$ | $3$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $y$ | $3$ |  | $1$ | $2.4$ | $3$ | $2.1$ |  |

**(b)** On the grid, draw the graph of $y=3x^{2}-x^{3}-1$ for $-1\leq x\leq 3$. [3]

![Question 9 graph grid](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/AISS/images/AISS-Prelim-EMath-Paper-2-QP-pages/page-18.png)

**(c)** By drawing a tangent, find the gradient of the graph when $x=1$. [2]

**(d)** Using your graph, solve $3-x=\dfrac{1}{x^{2}}-\dfrac{1}{x}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $-1,\ -1$. B1.

The missing $y$-values are $-1$ when $x=0$ and $-1$ when $x=3$.

**(b)** B1 for $4$ correctly plotted points. B2 for $7$ correctly plotted points. B1 for a smooth curve.

Deduct one mark if the graph goes beyond $-1$ or $3$.

**(c)** Draw a tangent. M1. Gradient $=3\pm 0.5$. A1. M1 for drawing a tangent where $x=1$.

**(d)**

$$
3-x=\frac{1}{x^{2}}-\frac{1}{x}
$$

$$
3x^{2}-x^{3}=1-x
$$

$$
3x^{2}-x^{3}-1=-x
$$

M1 for obtaining $3x^{2}-x^{3}-1=-x$.

Draw the line $y=-x$. M1.

Acceptable range: $x=-0.55$ to $-0.8$ and $x=0.35$ to $0.55$. A1 for both correct answers.
