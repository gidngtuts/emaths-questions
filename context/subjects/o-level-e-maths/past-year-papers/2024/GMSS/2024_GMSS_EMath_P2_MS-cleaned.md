### Q9

> **Marks:** 10

> **Sub-topic:** Real-world context

> **Page:** 18–20

(a) Monthly salary $= \dfrac{108000}{12} = \text{＄}9000$, which is more than ＄$8729$ from the research, so Ann buys a car (YES).

$$27\% \times \text{＄}9000 = \text{＄}2430$$

(b) (i) Used car, $1800$ cc:

$$[475 + 0.75 \times (1800 - 1600)] \times 0.782 = \text{＄}488.75$$

(ii) New car, $1500$ cc:

$$[250 + 0.375 \times (1500 - 1000)] \times 0.782 = 342.125 = \text{＄}342.13$$

(c) Used car. Loan after the downpayment:

$$\frac{60}{100} \times 98000 = \text{＄}58800$$

Interest for years $1$ and $2$: $\dfrac{58800 \times 2.28 \times 2}{100} = \text{＄}2681.28$.

Interest for years $3$ to $5$: $\dfrac{58800 \times 3 \times 3}{100} = \text{＄}5292.00$.

Monthly loan repayment $= \dfrac{58800 + 2681.28 + 5292}{60} = \text{＄}1112.888$.

Other monthly expenses $= 110 + 340 + \dfrac{360}{12} + \dfrac{1800}{12} + \dfrac{650}{6} = \text{＄}738\frac{1}{3}$.

Monthly road tax $= \dfrac{488.75}{12} = \text{＄}40\frac{35}{48}$.

Total $= \text{＄}1891.95$, which is within the ＄$2430$ budget.

New car. Loan $= \dfrac{70}{100} \times 150000 = \text{＄}105000$.

Interest $= \dfrac{105000 \times 2.88 \times 5}{100} = \text{＄}15120$.

Monthly loan repayment $= \dfrac{120120}{60} = \text{＄}2002$.

Other monthly expenses $= 110 + 270 + \dfrac{360}{12} + \dfrac{1600}{12} + \dfrac{500}{6} = \text{＄}626\frac{2}{3}$.

Monthly road tax $= \dfrac{342.125}{12} = \text{＄}28\frac{49}{96}$.

Total $= \text{＄}2657.18$, which is above the ＄$2430$ budget.

Ann should purchase the used car. The total expenses are within $27\%$ of her monthly salary. The new car incurs higher monthly expenses.
