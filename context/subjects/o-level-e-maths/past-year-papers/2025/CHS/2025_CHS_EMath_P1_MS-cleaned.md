### Q1

> **Marks:** [3]
> **Sub-topic:** Algebraic fractions
> **Page:** 3

Simplify $\dfrac{5}{2-x}+\dfrac{4x-8}{(x-2)^{2}}$.

$$
\frac{5}{2-x}+\frac{4(x-2)}{(x-2)^{2}} = \frac{5}{-(x-2)}+\frac{4}{x-2} = \frac{1}{x-2}
$$

or

$$
\frac{5}{2-x}+\frac{4}{2-x} = \frac{1}{2-x}
$$

### Q23

> **Marks:** [4]
> **Sub-topic:** Congruence and isosceles triangles
> **Page:** 22

Since $\angle EDC = \angle ECD = 55^{\circ}$, $\triangle EDC$ is isosceles, $ED = EC$.

$$
\angle ADE = \angle BCE = 90^{\circ}-55^{\circ} = 35^{\circ}
$$

$AD = BC$ (sides of square).

$\triangle ADE \equiv \triangle BCE$ (SAS).

Therefore $AE = BE$, so $\triangle AEB$ is an isosceles triangle.
