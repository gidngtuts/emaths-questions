## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{3}{2a-1}-\dfrac{2}{1-2a}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{3}{2a-1}-\frac{2}{1-2a} = \frac{3}{2a-1}+\frac{2}{2a-1} = \frac{5}{2a-1}
$$
