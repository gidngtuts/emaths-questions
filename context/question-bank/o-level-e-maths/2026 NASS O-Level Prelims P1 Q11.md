## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 8

$OABC$ is a quadrilateral where $AB$ and $BC$ are tangents to a circle with centre $O$ and radius of 6 cm.

![Question 11 circle and tangents](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/NASS/images/NASS-2026-MA-O-S4-5-Prelim-P1-QP-pages/page-08.png)

**(a)** Explain why angle $OAB$ is $90^{\circ}$. [1]

**(b)** Calculate the shaded area. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Angle $OAB$ is $90^{\circ}$ because tangent is perpendicular to the radius. B1 (accept abbreviation).

**(b)** Area of quadrilateral $-$ Area of sector

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= 6\times 11-\frac{126}{360}\times\pi(6)^{2} \\
&= 26.4\text{ cm}^{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: either area. A1.
