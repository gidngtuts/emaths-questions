## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b)(i) 1, (b)(ii) 1; total 4
> **Page:** 5

**(a)** Express $x^{2}+6x+10$ in the form $(x+h)^{2}+k$.

**(b)** Using your answer in (a),

**(i)** write down the equation of the line of symmetry of the curve $y=x^{2}+6x+10$,

**(ii)** explain why the equation $x^{2}+6x+10=0$ does not have any solutions.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $x^{2}+6x+10=(x+3)^{2}+1$

**(b)** **(i)** $x=-3$

**(ii)** The minimum point of the graph is above the $x$-axis.
