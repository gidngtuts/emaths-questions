## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 6

**(a)** Given that $\sqrt{a}\times a^{3}\div a^{x} = 1$, find the value of $x$. [1]

**(b)** Simplify $4\div\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)^{-2}$, giving your answer in positive index. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $3.5$.

**(b)**

$$
4\div\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)^{-2} = 4\div\left(\frac{2}{x}\right)^{2} = 4\times\left(\frac{x^{2}}{4}\right) = x^{2}
$$
