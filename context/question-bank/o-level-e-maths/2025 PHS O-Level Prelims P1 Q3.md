## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 4

**(a)** Expand and simplify $(p+2)\left(1+3p-p^{2}\right)$. [2]

**(b)** Write as a single fraction in its simplest form $\dfrac{3}{(5-2x)^{2}}-\dfrac{1}{5-2x}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $(p+2)\left(1+3p-p^{2}\right)=2+7p+p^{2}-p^{3}$

**(b)** $\dfrac{2x-2}{(5-2x)^{2}}$
