## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 9

A square pyramid $P$ has base with side $x$ m, height $y$ m and a volume of $300\text{ m}^{3}$.

Find

**(a)** the volume of a similar square pyramid, which has a height double that of the pyramid $P$. [1]

**(b)** the volume of another square pyramid with side $3x$ m and height $\dfrac{2}{3}y$ m. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{V_{L}}{300} = \left(\frac{2}{1}\right)^{3}, \quad V_{L} = 2400\text{ m}^{3}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\frac{1}{3}x^{2}y = 300, \quad x^{2}y = 900
$$

Volume of pyramid

$$
= \frac{1}{3}(3x)^{2}\times\frac{2}{3}y = 2x^{2}y = 2\times 900\text{ m}^{3} = 1800\text{ m}^{3}
$$
