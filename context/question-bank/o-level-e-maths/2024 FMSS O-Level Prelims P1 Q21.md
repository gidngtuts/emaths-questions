## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 2, (a)(ii) 1, (b) 2; total 5
> **Page:** 18

**(a)** In the diagram below, $DCB$ is a straight line.
$AD = 8\text{ m}$, $AC = 10\text{ m}$, $CD = 6\text{ m}$ and $AB = 17\text{ m}$.

**(i)** Show that $\angle ADC$ is a right angle. [2]

**(ii)** Express the value of $\cos \angle ACB$ as a fraction in its lowest term. [1]

**(b)** $\sin x^{\circ} = 0.8929$
Find two possible values of $x$ in the range $0^{\circ} \leq x \leq 180^{\circ}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a) (i)**

$$
AD^{2}+DC^{2} = 8^{2}+6^{2} = 100, \quad AC^{2} = 10^{2} = 100.
$$

Since $AD^{2}+DC^{2} = AC^{2}$, by the converse of Pythagoras' theorem, $\angle ADC = 90^{\circ}$.

Or, by the cosine rule,

$$
\angle ADC = \cos^{-1}\left(\frac{10^{2}-8^{2}-6^{2}}{-2(8)(6)}\right) = 90^{\circ}.
$$

M1, AG1.

**(ii)**

$$
\cos \angle ACB = -\frac{6}{10} = -\frac{3}{5}.
$$

B1 for the lowest term.

**(b)** The scheme prints

$$
x = \sin^{-1} 0.8829
$$

and then

$$
x = 63.239 \text{ or } 180-63.239 = 63.2 \text{ or } 116.8 \text{ (1 d.p.)}.
$$

B1, B1. The question states $\sin x^{\circ} = 0.8929$.
