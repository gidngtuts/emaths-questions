## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 14

The diagram shows a semi-circle $BAC$, with diameter $BC=34$ cm.
$AD$ and $BCD$ are straight lines.

![Semicircle with diameter BC of 34 cm and A on the arc. B, C and D are collinear, and A, D is a straight line.](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/ANDSS/images/ANDSS-4E2024EM_Prelim_P1_QP-pages/page-14.png)

Given that $\cos\angle ABC=\dfrac{8}{17}$, without solving for angle $ABC$ and angle $ACD$, find the exact value of $\cos\angle ACD$. [4]

## Answer Scheme

$\angle BAC=90^{\circ}$ (right angle in a semicircle). M1 is awarded only with the relevant values substituted in the cosine step.

$$
\cos\angle ABC=\frac{AB}{BC}
$$

$$
\frac{8}{17}=\frac{AB}{34}
$$

$$
AB=16\text{ cm}
$$

$$
AC=\sqrt{34^{2}-16^{2}}=30
$$

$$
\cos\angle ACD=-\cos\angle ACB
$$

$$
\cos\angle ACD=-\frac{AC}{BC}=-\frac{30}{34}=-\frac{15}{17}
$$
