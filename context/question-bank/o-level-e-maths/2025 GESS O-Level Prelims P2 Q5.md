## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 3, (c) 1, (d) 2, (e) 3; total 10
> **Page:** 12–13

**(a)** Complete the table of values for $y=\dfrac{x^{2}}{7}+\dfrac{2}{x}-2$.
Give your answer to 2 decimal places. [1]

| $x$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $y$ | 0.14 | $-0.42$ |  | 0.79 | 1.97 | 3.48 |

**(b)** Using a scale of $2\text{ cm}$ to $1$ unit, draw a horizontal $x$-axis for $0<x\leq 6$.
Using a scale of $4\text{ cm}$ to $1$ unit, draw a vertical $y$-axis for $-1\leq y\leq 5$.
On your grid opposite, plot the points given in the table and join them with a smooth curve. [3]

**(c)** Use your graph to explain why the equation $\dfrac{x^{2}}{7}+\dfrac{2}{x}-2=0$ has two solutions for $0<x\leq 6$. [1]

**(d)** By drawing a tangent, find the gradient of the curve at $(4, 0.79)$. [2]

**(e)** By drawing a suitable straight line on the grid, solve the equation $x^{3}+7x^{2}-28x+14=0$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $y=-0.05$ when $x=3$.

**(b)** Refer to graph paper.

**(c)** The graph $y=\dfrac{x^{2}}{7}+\dfrac{2}{x}-2$ cuts the $x$-axis at 2 points. Hence $\dfrac{x^{2}}{7}+\dfrac{2}{x}-2=0$ has 2 solutions.

**(d)** Accurate tangent line drawn.

$$
\text{Gradient}=\frac{1.8}{1.8}=1
$$

Accepts $1.02\pm 0.05$.

**(e)**

$$
x^{3}+7x^{2}-28x+14=0
$$

$$
x^{2}+7x-28+\frac{14}{x}=0
$$

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{7}+x-4+\frac{2}{x}=0
$$

$$
\frac{x^{2}}{7}+\frac{2}{x}-2=-x+2
$$

Line $y=2-x$ drawn.

From graph, $x=2.35\pm 0.05$.
