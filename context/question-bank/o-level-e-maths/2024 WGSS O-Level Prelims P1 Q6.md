## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 5

Simplify $\dfrac{9x^{2}-4}{12x^{2}-x-6}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{9x^{2}-4}{12x^{2}-x-6} = \dfrac{(3x-2)(3x+2)}{(3x+2)(4x-3)}$ [M2]

$= \dfrac{3x-2}{4x-3}$ [A1]
