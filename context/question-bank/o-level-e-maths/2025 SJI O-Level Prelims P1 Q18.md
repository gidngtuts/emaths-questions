## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 15

**(a)** Factorise $x^{2}-169$. [1]

**(b)** Use your answer to part (a) to find the two factors of 1431 other than 1 and 1431. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $x^{2}-169=(x+13)(x-13)$

**(b)**

$$
1431=40^{2}-169=(40+13)(40-13)=(53)(27)
$$

2 factors are 27 and 53.
