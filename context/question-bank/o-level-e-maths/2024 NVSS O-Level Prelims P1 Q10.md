## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 6

It is given $P(-4, 2)$, $Q(2, 10)$ and $R(-4, -5)$.

**(a)** Write down the equation of the line $PR$.

**(b)** The line $5y+10=mx$ has the same gradient as $QR$.
Find the value of $m$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $x=-4$ B1

**(b)** $5y+10=mx$, so $y=\dfrac{m}{5}x-2$

Gradient of $QR$ is $\dfrac{5}{2}$, so $\dfrac{m}{5}=\dfrac{5}{2}$ M1

$m=12.5$ A1
