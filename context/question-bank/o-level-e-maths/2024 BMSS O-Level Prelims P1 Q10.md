## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 10

Simplify $\dfrac{2x+3}{x^{2}+3x+2}-\dfrac{5}{x+1}$ as a single fraction. [3]

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{2x+3}{(x+1)(x+2)}-\frac{5}{x+1}
$$

[M1]

$$
=\frac{2x+3-5(x+2)}{(x+1)(x+2)}
$$

[M1]

$$
=\frac{2x+3-5x-10}{(x+1)(x+2)}=\frac{-3x-7}{(x+1)(x+2)}
$$
