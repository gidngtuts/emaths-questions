## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 15

A composite solid is made from a cylinder and two hemispheres. The cylinder has radius $p$ and length $2p$, while the hemispheres have radius $p$. A hemisphere is attached at each end of the cylinder.

The total surface area of the solid is six times the total surface area of a solid cone with radius $p$ and slant height $l$. Find $l$ in terms of $p$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

The cone is a solid, so it is closed.

$$
4\pi p^{2}+2\pi p(2p) = 6(\pi pl+\pi p^{2})
$$

$$
8\pi p^{2}-6\pi p^{2} = 6\pi pl
$$

$$
l = \frac{2\pi p^{2}}{6\pi p} = \frac{p}{3}
$$

M1 for the surface-area equation. M1 for isolating $l$. A1. The curved surface of the cylinder together with the two hemispheres is the sphere surface $4\pi p^{2}$ plus the cylinder curved surface.
