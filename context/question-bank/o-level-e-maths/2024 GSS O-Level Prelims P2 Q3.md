## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 7–9

(a) $\xi = \{x : x\text{ is an integer, } 2 \leq x \leq 14\}$, $A = \{x : x\text{ is a prime number}\}$, $B = \{x : x\text{ is a multiple of } 3\}$.

(i) List the elements in $(A \cup B)'$. [1]

(ii) List the elements in $A \cap B$. [1]

(iii) Given that $C \subset A$, $n(C) = 3$ and $B \cap C = \varnothing$, list the elements of a possible set $C$. [1]

(b) (i) The diagram shows a cone $P$ of height $24\text{ cm}$, vertex downward, with liquid filled from the vertex to a depth of $d\text{ cm}$.

![Cone P, vertex down, height 24 cm, liquid depth d cm from the vertex](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E5N-Prelim-Paper-2-as-on-5-Aug-pages/page-08.png)

The volume of the liquid in the cone is half the volume of the cone. Calculate the depth, $d\text{ cm}$, of the liquid. [2]

(ii) The volume of cone $P$ is $v\text{ cm}^{3}$. Cone $T$ has radius which is double that of the cone $P$ and its height is one-third that of the cone $P$. Express the volume of cone $T$ in terms of $v$. [2]

(c) $A$, $B$ and $C$ are points on the circumference of a circle with centre $O$. Angle $CAB =$ angle $CBA$. $DA$ and $DB$ are tangents to the circle from the point $D$.

![Circle with centre O, chord AB, point C on the circle, and tangents DA and DB from an external point D](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/GSS/images/2024-GSS-4E5N-Prelim-Paper-2-as-on-5-Aug-pages/page-09.png)

Prove that triangle $ACD$ and triangle $BCD$ are congruent. [3]

## Answer Scheme

(a) (i) $A = \{2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13\}$, $B = \{3, 6, 9, 12\}$, $(A \cup B)' = \{4, 8, 10, 14\}$. B1.

(ii) $A \cap B = \{3\}$. B1.

(iii) Any subset with 3 elements from $\{2, 5, 7, 11, 13\}$. B1.

(b) (i)

$$
\left(\frac{d}{24}\right)^{3} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
d = 19.0
$$

M1, A1.

(ii) Let $r$ be the radius of cone $P$.

$$
v = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^{2}(24) = 8\pi r^{2}\text{ cm}^{3}
$$

Volume of cone $T$

$$
= \frac{1}{3}\pi (2r)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{3}\times 24\right) = \frac{4}{3}\times 8\pi r^{2} = \frac{4}{3}v\text{ cm}^{3}
$$

M1, A1.

(c) $CD$ is common. $DA = DB$ (tangents from an external point). B1.

Since angle $CAB =$ angle $CBA$, triangle $CAB$ is isosceles, hence $AC = BC$. B1.

Therefore triangle $ACD$ and triangle $BCD$ are congruent (SSS). B1.
