## Question

> **Marks:** (a) [2]; (b) [1]; (c)(i) [1]; (c)(ii) [3]; (d) [5]; total [12]
> **Page:** 5–7

**(a)** Solve $7(x-2)-3(2x-1)=8(3x+5)-6$. [2]

**(b)** Solve the inequality $-3x+4<-2x-6$. [1]

**(c)** It is given that

$$
p=\dfrac{r^{2}q-2}{q-r^{2}}.
$$

**(i)** Find $p$ when $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $q=-1$. [1]

**(ii)** Express $r$ in terms of $p$ and $q$. [3]

**(d)** Solve the equation

$$
\dfrac{x+3}{2x^{2}-8x+6}+\dfrac{5x}{3-x}=\dfrac{2}{x-1}.
$$

Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [5]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** Solve $7(x-2)-3(2x-1)=8(3x+5)-6$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
7x-14-6x+3&=24x+40-6\\
x-11&=24x+34\\
-23x&=45\\
x&=-\dfrac{45}{23}=-1\dfrac{22}{23}
\end{aligned}
$$

Answer must be exact and shown as a mixed number.

**(b)** Solve the inequality $-3x+4<-2x-6$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-x&<-10\\
x&>10
\end{aligned}
$$

**(c)** It is given that $p=\dfrac{r^{2}q-2}{q-r^{2}}$.

**(i)** Find $p$ when $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $q=-1$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p&=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{2}(-1)-2}{-1-\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}\\
&=\dfrac{-\dfrac{9}{4}}{-\dfrac{5}{4}}\\
&=\dfrac{9}{5}=1\dfrac{4}{5}
\end{aligned}
$$

Or $1.8$. Show substitution of values.

**(ii)** Express $r$ in terms of $p$ and $q$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
p(q-r^{2})&=r^{2}q-2\\
pq-pr^{2}&=qr^{2}-2\\
pq+2&=r^{2}(p+q)\\
r^{2}&=\dfrac{pq+2}{p+q}\\
r&=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{pq+2}{p+q}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(d)** Solve the equation $\dfrac{x+3}{2x^{2}-8x+6}+\dfrac{5x}{3-x}=\dfrac{2}{x-1}$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dfrac{x+3}{2(x-3)(x-1)}-\dfrac{5x}{x-3}&=\dfrac{2}{x-1}\\
\dfrac{x+3}{2(x-3)(x-1)}-\dfrac{5x(2)(x-1)}{2(x-3)(x-1)}&=\dfrac{2(2)(x-3)}{2(x-3)(x-1)}\\
x+3-10x(x-1)&=4(x-3)\\
10x^{2}-7x-15&=0\\
x&=\dfrac{-(-7)\pm\sqrt{(-7)^{2}-4(10)(-15)}}{2(10)}\\
x&=1.62\quad\text{or}\quad -0.92\quad(2\text{ d.p.})
\end{aligned}
$$

Note: $2x^{2}-8x+6=2(x-3)(x-1)$ and $3-x=-(x-3)$.
