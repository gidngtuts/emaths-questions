## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 5

In the following graph, write down a possible value of $n$ and the corresponding value of $b$ for the equation.

![Curve of y = x^n + b through (1, -1)](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BSS/images/Broadrick-EM-2024-Maths-Sec-Prelim-P1-pages/page-05.png)

The curve $y=x^{n}+b$ passes through $(1,-1)$. It lies in the third and first quadrants and is increasing through that point.

## Answer Scheme

$n=3$ or $5$ (any odd integer more than 1) (B1)

$-1=(1)^{3}+b$

$b=-2$ (B1)
