## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 12

**(a)** Simplify $(a+2b)(a-2b)-a^{2}$.

**(b)** Hence evaluate $12\,349\times 12\,341-12\,345^{2}$.

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $(a+2b)(a-2b)-a^{2}=a^{2}-4b^{2}-a^{2}$ (M1, correct expansion)

$=-4b^{2}$ (A1)

**(b)** $12\,349\times 12\,341-12\,345^{2}$

$=(12\,345+4)(12\,345-4)-12\,345^{2}$ (M1)

$=-4(4)=-16$ (A1)
