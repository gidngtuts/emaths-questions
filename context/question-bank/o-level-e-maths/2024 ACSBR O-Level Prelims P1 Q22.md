## Question

> **Marks:** 5
> **Page:** 17

The first three terms in a sequence of numbers are given below.

$$
\begin{align*}
T_{1} &= 1 \times 2 + 10 = 12 \\
T_{2} &= 2 \times 3 + 6 = 12 \\
T_{3} &= 3 \times 4 + 2 = 14 \\
T_{4} &= 4 \times 5 - 2 = 18
\end{align*}
$$

(a) Show that the $n$th term of the sequence, $T_{n}$, is given by $T_{n} = n^{2}-3n+14$.

(b) Evaluate $T_{50}$.

(c) Explain why every term in the sequence is even.

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
T_{n} = n(n+1)+10-4(n-1) = n^{2}+n+10-4n+4 = n^{2}-3n+14
$$

(b) $T_{50} = 2364$

(c) $n^{2}-3n+14 = n(n-3)+14$.

When $n$ is even, $n(n-3)$ is even $\times$ odd $=$ even.

When $n$ is odd, $n(n-3)$ is odd $\times$ even $=$ even.

Adding 14, which is also even, $T_{n}$ is always even.
