## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 3, (b) 3, (c) 2, (d) 3; total 11
> **Page:** 3–4

**(a)** Find all the prime numbers that will satisfy the inequality $-5\leq 2x-1<x+3$. [3]

**(b)** Simplify $\dfrac{30x^{2}y^{2}}{11z}\div\dfrac{25xz^{3}}{22y}$. [3]

**(c)** Simplify $\left(\dfrac{p^{12}}{81q^{16}}\right)^{-\dfrac{3}{4}}$. [2]

**(d)** Solve $\dfrac{x}{x-1}+2=\dfrac{4}{2-x}$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $-5\leq 2x-1<x+3$

$$
-5\leq 2x-1\quad\text{and}\quad 2x-1<x+3
$$

$$
-2\leq x\quad\text{and}\quad x<4
$$

$$
-2\leq x<4
$$

M1 for $-2\leq x$. M1 for $x<4$. A1.

Primes that satisfy the inequality: $2,\ 3$.

**(b)**

$$
\frac{30x^{2}y^{2}}{11z}\div\frac{25xz^{3}}{22y}=\frac{30x^{2}y^{2}}{11z}\times\frac{22y}{25xz^{3}}=\frac{12xy^{3}}{5z^{4}}
$$

Four terms to simplify: $\dfrac{12}{5}$, $x$, $y^{3}$, $z^{4}$.

B1 for one correct term in the correct position (numerator or denominator). B2 for two or three correct terms. B3 for all four correct terms.

**(c)**

$$
\left(\frac{p^{12}}{81q^{16}}\right)^{-\frac{3}{4}}=\left(\frac{81q^{16}}{p^{12}}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}=\frac{27q^{12}}{p^{9}}
$$

B1 for numerator, B1 for denominator.

**(d)**

$$
\frac{x}{x-1}+2=\frac{4}{2-x}
$$

$$
\frac{x+2(x-1)}{x-1}=\frac{4}{2-x}
$$

$$
\frac{3x-2}{x-1}=\frac{4}{2-x}
$$

$$
(3x-2)(2-x)=4(x-1)
$$

$$
6x-3x^{2}-4+2x=4x-4
$$

$$
-3x^{2}+4x=0
$$

M1 for the correct quadratic equation.

$$
x(-3x+4)=0
$$

M1 for correct factorisation or correct substitution into the quadratic formula.

$$
x=0\quad\text{or}\quad x=\frac{4}{3}
$$

A1 for both correct answers.
