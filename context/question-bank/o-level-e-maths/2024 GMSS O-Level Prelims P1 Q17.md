## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 11

$N = \dfrac{a^{6}}{b^{3}}$ where $a$ and $b$ are prime numbers.

(a) Explain why $N$ is a perfect cube. [1]

(b) Show that $M$ is divisible by $6$, given that $\dfrac{MN}{2} = (3ab)^{2}$ and $M$ is a natural number. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a) $N = \dfrac{a^{6}}{b^{3}} = \left(\dfrac{a^{2}}{b}\right)^{3}$, so $N$ is a perfect cube. [1]

(b) $\dfrac{MN}{2} = (3ab)^{2}$, so $MN = 2\times 9(ab)^{2} = 18(ab)^{2}$ and $M = 6\left(\dfrac{3a^{2}b^{2}}{N}\right)$. Hence $M$ is a multiple of $6$. [2]
