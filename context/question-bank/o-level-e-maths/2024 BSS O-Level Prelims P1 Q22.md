## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 15

Regular pentagons and squares of the same sides are placed together in a pattern as shown in the diagram.
Caleb claims that if he continues the pattern, a closed loop will form.
Explain whether his claim is true, showing your working clearly.

![Chain of regular pentagons alternating with squares](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/BSS/images/Broadrick-EM-2024-Maths-Sec-Prelim-P1-pages/page-15.png)

## Answer Scheme

Interior angle of pentagon $=\dfrac{(5-2)\times 180^{\circ}}{5}=108^{\circ}$ (M1)

Interior angle of square $=90^{\circ}$

Interior angle of regular polygon $=360^{\circ}-90^{\circ}-108^{\circ}=162^{\circ}$ (M1, interior or exterior of polygon)

Exterior angle $=180^{\circ}-162^{\circ}=18^{\circ}$

$n=\dfrac{360}{18}=20$

Since $n$ is a positive integer, it is possible to form a regular polygon, hence a closed loop. (A1)
