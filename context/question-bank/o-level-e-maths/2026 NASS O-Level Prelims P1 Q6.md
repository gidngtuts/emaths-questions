## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2; total 3
> **Page:** 5

Factorise completely.

**(a)** $15\pi-24\pi b+3\pi^{2}$ [1]

**(b)** $4x^{2}+9y^{2}-12xy-64$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
15\pi-24\pi b+3\pi^{2}=3\pi(5-8b+\pi)
$$

B1.

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
4x^{2}+9y^{2}-12xy-64 &= (2x-3y)^{2}-64 \\
&= (2x-3y-8)(2x-3y+8)
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: $(2x-3y)^{2}$. A1.
