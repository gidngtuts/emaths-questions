## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1, (c) 1, (d) 2, (e) 3; total 9
> **Page:** 8–9

In the diagram, the perpendicular from $B$ to $AC$ meets $AC$ produced at $D$.
It is given that $AC = BC$ and the gradient of $BC$ is $-\dfrac{3}{4}$.

![Question 4 coordinate diagram](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CGS/images/CGS-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P2-QP-pages/page-08.png)

**(a)** Find the equation of line $BC$. [2]

**(b)** Find the value of $n$. [1]

**(c)** Show that $m = -3$. [1]

**(d)** Find the area of triangle $ABC$. [2]

**(e)** Hence, find the length of $BD$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
y-5 = -\frac{3}{4}(x-5)
$$

$$
y = -\frac{3}{4}x+\frac{35}{4}
$$

**(b)**

$$
n = -\frac{3}{4}(13)+\frac{35}{4} = -1
$$

**(c)** $BC = \sqrt{(13-5)^{2}+(-1-5)^{2}} = 10$

$$
(m-5)^{2}+(-1-5)^{2} = 100
$$

$$
m = -3
$$

(the solution $m = 13$ is the point $B$)

**(d)** Base $AB = 16$ and height $6$, so area $= \dfrac{1}{2}\times 16\times 6 = 48$ units$^{2}$.

**(e)**

$$
48 = \frac{1}{2}\times 10\times BD
$$

$$
BD = 9.6\text{ units}
$$
