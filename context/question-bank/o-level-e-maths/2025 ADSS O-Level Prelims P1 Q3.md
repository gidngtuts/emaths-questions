## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 3

Find the smallest value of $x$ such that the lowest common multiple (LCM) of $x$ and 15 is 45. [2]

## Answer Scheme

$15 = 3 \times 5$

$45 = 3 \times 3 \times 5$ [M1]

Thus, the smallest value of $x = 9$. [A1]
