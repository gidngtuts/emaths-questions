## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 3; total 7
> **Page:** 24–25

![Question 26 quadrilateral ABCD](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2026/NASS/images/NASS-2026-MA-O-S4-5-Prelim-P1-QP-pages/page-24.png)

$ABCD$ is a quadrilateral.
$E$ is the point on $BD$ such that $BE:BD=3:4$.
$F$ is the midpoint of $BC$.
$\overrightarrow{AB}=4\mathbf{a}$, $\overrightarrow{AD}=8\mathbf{b}$ and $\overrightarrow{EF}=2(\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b})$.

**(a)** Express $\overrightarrow{BE}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible. [2]

**(b)** Express $\overrightarrow{BC}$ in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, as simply as possible. [2]

**(c)** What type of quadrilateral is $ABCD$?
Explain your answer using vectors. [3]

---

**END OF PAPER**

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{BD} &= \overrightarrow{BA}+\overrightarrow{AD} \\
&= 8\mathbf{b}-4\mathbf{a} \\
\overrightarrow{BE} &= \frac{3}{4}(8\mathbf{b}-4\mathbf{a}) \\
&= 6\mathbf{b}-3\mathbf{a}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: Find $\overrightarrow{BD}$ (even without the tilde). A1.

**(b)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{BF} &= \overrightarrow{BE}+\overrightarrow{EF} \\
&= -3\mathbf{a}+6\mathbf{b}+2\mathbf{a}-2\mathbf{b} \\
&= 4\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a} \\
\overrightarrow{BC} &= 2(4\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}) \\
&= 8\mathbf{b}-2\mathbf{a}
\end{aligned}
$$

M1: Find $\overrightarrow{BF}$ (even without the tilde). A1.

**(c)**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overrightarrow{DC} &= \overrightarrow{DB}+\overrightarrow{BC} \\
&= 4\mathbf{a}-8\mathbf{b}+8\mathbf{b}-2\mathbf{a} \\
&= 2\mathbf{a}
\end{aligned}
$$

Since $\overrightarrow{AB}=2\overrightarrow{DC}$, $AB$ is parallel to $DC$. M1: Find $\overrightarrow{DC}$. M1.

$ABCD$ is a trapezium because it has only one pair of parallel lines. A1.

> **Markers Report:** Well done but the students need to learn that they need to express vector as a scalar multiplication of another vector to draw that the 2 vectors are parallel. To gather that it has only one pair of parallel lines, they can compare the other 2 vectors or they can highlight that the parallel lines have different lengths.
