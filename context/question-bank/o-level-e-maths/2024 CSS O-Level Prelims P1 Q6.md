## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 1; total 3
> **Page:** 5

The quadratic curve $y = x^{2}+4x-21$ can be written as $y = (x-a)(x+b)$.

**(a)** Find the value of $a$ and the value of $b$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. [2]

**(b)** Explain why $y$ will not have a value smaller than $-25$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $a = 3$, $b = 7$.

**(b)** Since the minimum point of $y$ is at $(-2, -25)$, $y$ will not have a value smaller than $-25$.
