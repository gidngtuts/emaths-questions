## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 9

It is given that $2(mn)^{2}-2mn = 3mn+3$.
Find $n$ in terms of $m$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
2(mn)^{2}-2mn &= 3mn+3 \\
2m^{2}n^{2}-5mn-3 &= 0 \\
(mn-3)(2mn+1) &= 0 \\
mn &= 3 \text{ or } mn = -\frac{1}{2} \\
n &= \frac{3}{m} \text{ or } n = -\frac{1}{2m}
\end{aligned}
$$
