## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2, (c) 3, (d) 3; total 10
> **Page:** 12–13

Line $l$ passes through the points $(-4, 4)$ and $(8, -2)$.

**(a)** Find the equation of line $l$. [2]

**(b)** The equation of line $m$ is $6y = 18-3x$. Show how you can tell that the line $m$ does not intersect the line $l$. [2]

**(c)** The equation of line $n$ is $2y = 3x-4$. Line $n$ and line $l$ intersect at point $A$. Find the coordinates of $A$. [3]

**(d)** Line $l$ intersects the $y$-axis at point $B$ and line $n$ intersects the $y$-axis at point $C$. Calculate the area of triangle $ABC$. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\text{Gradient of line } l = \frac{-2-4}{8-(-4)} = -\frac{1}{2}
$$

Substitute $(-4, 4)$: $c = 2$. $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x+2$. M1, A1.

**(b)** Line $m$: $6y = 18-3x$, so $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}x+3$.

The gradients are equal $\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ and the $y$-intercepts are not equal, so the lines are parallel. Therefore line $m$ does not intersect line $l$. M1, A1.

**(c)**

$$
2\left(-\frac{1}{2}x+2\right) = 3x-4, \quad x = 2, \quad y = 1
$$

$A(2, 1)$. M1, M1, A1.

**(d)** $B$ is $(0, 2)$ and $C$ is $(0, -2)$, so $BC = 4$ and the height from $A$ is $2$.

$$
\text{Area of }\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2}\times 4\times 2 = 4\text{ units}^{2}
$$

M1, M1, A1.
