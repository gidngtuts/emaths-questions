## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 13

In the diagram, $BCD$ is a straight line and $BC:CD = 3:5$.

![Triangles ABC and ABD sharing AB, with C between B and D](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/NBSS/images/NBSS-EM-_Prelim_P1_2024-v2_QP-pages/page-13.png)

Calculate the area of triangle $ABC$ as a percentage of the area of triangle $ABD$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{3}{5+3}\times 100\% = 37.5\%
$$
