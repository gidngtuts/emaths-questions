## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 6

Calculate the total surface area of a solid hemisphere with radius $6\text{ cm}$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Total surface area

$$
= \frac{1}{2}\times 4\pi(6)^{2}+\pi(6)^{2} = 72\pi+36\pi = 108\pi = 339\text{ cm}^{2}\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

M1 for the hemisphere together with the circular base. A1.
