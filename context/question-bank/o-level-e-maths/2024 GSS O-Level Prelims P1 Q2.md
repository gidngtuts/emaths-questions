## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 3

(a) Given that $9^{12} = 27^{x}$, find $x$. [2]

(b) The number of people owning cars in a country increased by $p\%$ every year, from 5000 in the year 2020, to 6500 in the year 2024. Find the value of $p$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
9^{12} = 27^{x}
$$

$$
(3^{2})^{12} = (3^{3})^{x}
$$

$$
3^{24} = 3^{3x}
$$

$$
24 = 3x
$$

$$
x = 8
$$

M1 for expressing all terms as powers of 3. A1.

(b)

$$
6500 = 5000\left(1+\frac{p}{100}\right)^{4}
$$

$$
\left(1+\frac{p}{100}\right)^{4} = \frac{6500}{5000}
$$

$$
\frac{p}{100} = 0.06778997
$$

$$
p = 6.78\%\ (3\text{ s.f.})
$$

M1, A1.
