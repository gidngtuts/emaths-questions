## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 3

Factorise each of the following completely.

**(a)** $4a^{2}-b^{2}$ [1]

**(b)** $5x-1+10x^{2}y-2xy$ [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** $4a^{2}-b^{2}=(2a+b)(2a-b)$ [B1]

**(b)**

$$
5x-1+10x^{2}y-2xy=(5x-1)+2xy(5x-1)
$$

[M1]

$$
=(5x-1)(1+2xy)
$$

[A1]
