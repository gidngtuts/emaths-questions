## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

The position vectors of points $A$ and $B$ are $\begin{pmatrix}-9\\ 3\end{pmatrix}$ and $\begin{pmatrix}1\\ -12\end{pmatrix}$ respectively.

**(a)** Find the column vector $\overrightarrow{BA}$. [1]

**(b)** Find $\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\overrightarrow{BA}=\overrightarrow{BO}+\overrightarrow{OA}
=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\ 12\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}-9\\ 3\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-10\\ 15\end{pmatrix}
$$

**(b)**

$$
\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|=\sqrt{(-10)^{2}+(15)^{2}}=18.0\text{ units (3 s.f.)}
$$
