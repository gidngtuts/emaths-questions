## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

Simplify $\dfrac{x^{3}-4x}{2x^{2}-7x+6}$.

## Answer Scheme

$\dfrac{x^{3}-4x}{2x^{2}-7x+6} = \dfrac{x(x^{2}-4)}{(x-2)(2x-3)} = \dfrac{x(x+2)(x-2)}{(x-2)(2x-3)} = \dfrac{x(x+2)}{2x-3}$.
