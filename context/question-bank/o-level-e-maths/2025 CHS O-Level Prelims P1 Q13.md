## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 12

The vertices of $A$ and $B$ are $(-3,8)$ and $(7,2)$.

The line $BC$ is parallel to the $x$-axis.

The area of triangle $ABC$ is $12$ square units.

![Question 13 triangle](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/CHS/images/CAT-HIGH-2025-EMATH-PRELIM-P1-QP-pages/page-12.png)

**(a)** Find the coordinates of $C$. [2]

**(b)** Find the exact value of $\cos \angle ACB$. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
\frac{1}{2}(b)(6) = 12 \Rightarrow b = 4
$$

$$
C(3,2)
$$

**(b)**

$$
AC = \sqrt{6^{2}+6^{2}} = 6\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\cos \angle ACB = -\frac{6}{6\sqrt{2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$
