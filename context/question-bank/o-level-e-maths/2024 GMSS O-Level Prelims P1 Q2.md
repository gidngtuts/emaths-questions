## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

(a) Find the lowest common multiple (LCM) of $108$ and $140$. [1]

(b) Find the highest common factor (HCF) of $108$ and $140$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a) By prime factorisation, $\text{LCM} = 2^{2}\times 3^{3}\times 5\times 7 = 3780$. [1]

(b) $\text{HCF} = 2^{2} = 4$. [1]
