## Question

> **Marks:** 4
> **Page:** 16

In the diagram, $PQ$ is parallel to $RST$. $QBS$ and $PABR$ are straight lines. It is given that $PA=AB$ and $5\,PA=3\,BR$.

![Triangles with PQ parallel to RST, and straight lines QBS and PABR](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/STC/images/CHIJ-STC-2024-Math-Prelim-P1-pages/page-16.png)

(a) Explain why triangles $PBQ$ and $RBS$ are similar. [2]

(b) Calculate

(i) $\dfrac{\text{Area of triangle }QPA}{\text{Area of triangle }QBA}$, [1]

(ii) $\dfrac{\text{Area of triangle }PBQ}{\text{Area of triangle }RBS}$. [1]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$\angle QPB=\angle BRS$ (alternate $\angle$s, $PQ\parallel RS$)

$\angle PBQ=\angle RBS$ (vertically opposite $\angle$s)

Triangles $PBQ$ and $RBS$ are similar (AA).

(b) (i)

$$
\frac{\text{Area of triangle }QPA}{\text{Area of triangle }QBA}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}\times PA\times h}{\frac{1}{2}\times AB\times h}=\frac{PA}{AB}=1
$$

(ii)

$$
5\,PA=3\,BR
$$

$$
PA=\frac{3}{5}BR
$$

$$
PB=\frac{6}{5}BR
$$

$$
\frac{\text{Area of triangle }PBQ}{\text{Area of triangle }RBS}=\left(\frac{PB}{BR}\right)^{2}=\left(\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}=\frac{36}{25}
$$
