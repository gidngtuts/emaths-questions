**(a)** On the Venn diagram, shade the region that represents $A' \cup B$. [1]

**(b)** Use set notation to describe the shaded region. [1]

![Venn diagrams for Q5: blank intersecting sets A and B inside the universal set, and a shaded ring A with B inside A](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2024/CSS/images/Canberra-EM-2024-Prelims-Paper-1-QP-pages/page-04.png)

## Answer Scheme

**(a)** The rectangle outside $A \cup B$ is shaded, circle $A$ (excluding $A \cap B$) is unshaded, and circle $B$ including $A \cap B$ is shaded.

**(b)** $A \cap B'$.
