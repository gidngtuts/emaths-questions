## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 2, (b) 2; total 4
> **Page:** 8–9

**(a)** The diagram below shows a pentagon with two of its interior angles as right angles, and the remaining interior angles are equal.
Find the value of $\dfrac{y}{x}$. [2]

![Question 10 pentagon](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-08.png)

**(b)** The diagram shows three regular polygons, $A$, $B$ and $C$, joined together.
Polygon $A$ has 12 sides and polygon $B$ has 4 sides.
Find the number of sides in polygon $C$. [2]

![Question 10 polygons A, B and C](../../subjects/o-level-e-maths/past-year-papers/2025/GESS/images/GESS-2025-EM-PRELIM-P1-pages/page-09.png)

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
(5-2)\times 180^{\circ}=90^{\circ}+90^{\circ}+3y
$$

$$
540^{\circ}=180^{\circ}+3y
$$

$$
3y=360^{\circ},\quad y=120^{\circ}
$$

$$
x=360^{\circ}-120^{\circ}=240^{\circ}
$$

$$
\frac{y}{x}=\frac{120^{\circ}}{240^{\circ}}=\frac{1}{2}
$$

**(b)**

$$
(12-2)\times 180^{\circ}=1800^{\circ}
$$

$$
\frac{1800^{\circ}}{12}=150^{\circ}
$$

$$
360^{\circ}-150^{\circ}-90^{\circ}=120^{\circ}
$$

$$
180^{\circ}-120^{\circ}=60^{\circ}
$$

$$
\frac{360^{\circ}}{60^{\circ}}=6\text{ sides}
$$
