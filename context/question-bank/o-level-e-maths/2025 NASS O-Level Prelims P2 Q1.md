## Question

> **Marks:** (a)(i) 2, (a)(ii) 3, (b) 3, (c) 3; total 11
> **Page:** 3–5

**(a)**

$$
x=\frac{3h^{2}(y+b)}{b}-1
$$

**(i)** Find $h$ when $b=5$, $x=2$ and $y=0$. [2]

**(ii)** Rearrange the formula to make $b$ the subject. [3]

**(b)** Solve these simultaneous equations.

$$
6x-3y=16
$$

$$
9x+2y=11
$$

[3]

**(c)** Solve $\dfrac{19x+3}{2x^{2}-5x-3}+\dfrac{2}{x-3}=7$.
Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [3]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)(i)**

$$
x=\frac{3h^{2}(y+b)}{b}-1
$$

$$
2=\frac{3h^{2}(0+5)}{5}-1
$$

$$
3=3h^{2}
$$

$$
h^{2}=1
$$

$$
h=\pm 1
$$

**(a)(ii)**

$$
x=\frac{3h^{2}(y+b)}{b}-1
$$

$$
xb=3h^{2}(y+b)-b
$$

$$
xb=3h^{2}y+3h^{2}b-b
$$

$$
xb+b-3h^{2}b=3h^{2}y
$$

$$
b(x+1-3h^{2})=3h^{2}y
$$

$$
b=\frac{3h^{2}y}{x+1-3h^{2}}
$$

**(b)**

$$
6x-3y=16 \quad (1)
$$

$$
9x+2y=11 \quad (2)
$$

$(1)\times 2$

$$
12x-6y=32
$$

$(2)\times 3$

$$
27x+6y=33
$$

$$
39x=65
$$

$$
x=1\frac{2}{3}
$$

$$
6\left(1\frac{2}{3}\right)-3y=16
$$

$$
y=-2
$$

**(c)**

$$
\frac{19x+3}{2x^{2}-5x-3}+\frac{2}{x-3}=7
$$

$$
\frac{19x+3}{(2x+1)(x-3)}+\frac{2}{x-3}=7
$$

$$
19x+3+2(2x+1)=7(2x^{2}-5x-3)
$$

$$
19x+3+4x+2=14x^{2}-35x-21
$$

$$
14x^{2}-58x-26=0
$$

$$
x=\frac{58\pm\sqrt{(-58)^{2}-4(14)(-26)}}{2(14)}
$$

$$
x=-0.41\text{ or }x=4.55
$$
