## Question

> **Marks:** (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 1; total 4
> **Page:** 7

When $n$ is a positive integer, $(2n+3)$ is an odd number.

**(a)** Write down an expression, in term of $n$, for the next consecutive odd number. [1]

**(b)** Find and simplify an expression, in terms of $n$, for the difference between the squares of these two odd numbers. [2]

**(c)** Hence explain why the difference between the squares of two consecutive odd numbers is always a multiple of $8$. [1]
