## Question

> **Marks:** [2]
> **Page:** 4

Solve $7^{9-x^{2}}-1 = 0$. Find the values of $x$.

## Answer Scheme

$$
\begin{aligned}
7^{9-x^{2}}-1 &= 0 \\
7^{9-x^{2}} &= 1 \\
7^{(3-x)(3+x)} &= 7^{0} \\
x &= 3 \text{ or } x = -3
\end{aligned}
$$
