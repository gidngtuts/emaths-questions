## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 13

Let $A$ be the set of integers and $B$ be the set of rational numbers.
Alice claims that $B\subset A$.

Do you agree with Alice? Explain your answer. [2]

## Answer Scheme

Disagree. [B1]

Not all rational numbers are integers (give an example).

Or: there are elements in set $B$ that are not in $A$. For example, $\dfrac{1}{2}\in B$ but $\dfrac{1}{2}\notin A$. [B1]
