## Question

> **Marks:** 2
> **Page:** 3

Write as a single fraction in its simplest form

$$
\frac{7x}{(x-5)^{2}}+\frac{1}{5-x}.
$$

## Answer Scheme

$$
\frac{7x}{(x-5)^{2}}+\frac{1}{5-x} = \frac{7x}{(x-5)^{2}}-\frac{1}{x-5} = \frac{7x-(x-5)}{(x-5)^{2}} = \frac{6x+5}{(x-5)^{2}}
$$

Also accept $\dfrac{7x}{(5-x)^{2}}+\dfrac{1}{5-x} = \dfrac{6x+5}{(5-x)^{2}}$.
