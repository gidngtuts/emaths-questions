## Question

> **Marks:** 3
> **Page:** 8

**(a)** Factorise $9x^{2}+24xy+16y^{2}$ completely. [1]

**(b)** Hence factorise $144a^{8}-(9a^{8}+24a^{4}y+16y^{2})$ completely. [2]

## Answer Scheme

**(a)**

$$
9x^{2}+24xy+16y^{2}=(3x+4y)^{2}
$$

**(b)** Let $x=a^{4}$.

$$
144a^{8}-(9a^{8}+24a^{4}y+16y^{2})
= 144x^{2}-(9x^{2}+24xy+16y^{2})
$$

$$
= (12x)^{2}-(3x+4y)^{2}
$$

M1 for an attempt to factorise an expression in the form $a^{2}-b^{2}$ after using part (a).

$$
= (12x+3x+4y)(12x-3x-4y)
$$

$$
= (15x+4y)(9x-4y)
$$

$$
= (15a^{4}+4y)(9a^{4}-4y)
$$
