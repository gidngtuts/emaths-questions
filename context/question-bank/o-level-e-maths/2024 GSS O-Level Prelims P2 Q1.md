## Question

> **Marks:** 10
> **Page:** 3–4

(a) Simplify $\dfrac{5a^{3}}{6b} \div \dfrac{2ab}{4a^{2}b}$. [1]

(b) Solve $\dfrac{x}{8} = \dfrac{50}{x}$. [2]

(c) Simplify $\dfrac{4v^{2}-1}{2pv+p-10v-5}$. [3]

(d) Solve $2x^{2} = 3(3x-1)$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [4]

## Answer Scheme

(a)

$$
\frac{5a^{3}}{6b} \div \frac{2ab}{4a^{2}b} = \frac{5a^{3}}{6b}\times \frac{4a^{2}b}{2ab} = \frac{5a^{4}}{3b}
$$

B1.

(b) $\dfrac{x}{8} = \dfrac{50}{x}$, so $x^{2} = 400$ and $x = \pm 20$. B2.

(c)

$$
\frac{4v^{2}-1}{2pv+p-10v-5} = \frac{(2v-1)(2v+1)}{(p-5)(2v+1)} = \frac{2v-1}{p-5}
$$

M1 for $(2v-1)(2v+1)$. M1 for $(p-5)(2v+1)$. A1.

(d)

$$
2x^{2} = 3(3x-1)
$$

$$
2x^{2} = 9x-3
$$

$$
2x^{2}-9x+3 = 0
$$

$$
x = \frac{-(-9)\pm\sqrt{(-9)^{2}-4(2)(3)}}{2(2)}
$$

$$
x = 4.14\text{ or }0.36
$$

M1 for forming the quadratic equation. M2, with M1 if $b^{2}-4ac$ is correct. A1.
